## Reading the scorecard

A contribution is the strongest signal because it improves the public record for the next visitor. Shares, opens, directions, saves, and self-reported passport markings are useful intent signals, not proof of a visit. Never replace an unknown with zero. Compare loops only after they have similar distribution and measurement windows. Passport marks per open and referral opens per share can exceed 100% because one passport contains several places and one shared link can reach several recipients.
