# North Carolina Expansion Punchlist

This is AuditMap's durable work plan for mapping public destinations across North Carolina. The complete place-by-place list is in `data/nc-expansion-punchlist.csv`; the detailed status record is in `data/nc-expansion-punchlist.json`.

## Coverage Map

- 553 incorporated cities, towns, and villages
- 224 census-designated communities, including Cherokee
- 100 counties for rural and unincorporated coverage
- Statewide workstreams for state parks, federal and Tribal lands, greenways, water access, and public cultural destinations

County assignments are grouped into 12 working districts. This gives us a practical way to finish neighboring places together while still preserving a record for every individual community.

## Rollout Waves

| Wave | Places | Initial depth |

| 0 | 1 | Raleigh full-buildout model, complete |
| 1 | 9 | Full city buildout |
| 2 | 33 | Major and intermediate public sites |
| 3 | 49 | Major local destinations first |

Wave 1 is Charlotte, Greensboro, Durham, Winston-Salem, Fayetteville, Cary, Wilmington, Concord, and High Point. Its 58 anchor destinations are seeded and live; supporting-place expansion remains available as later city-deepening work.

Wave 2 begins with Asheville, Greenville, Gastonia, Apex, Jacksonville, Huntersville, Chapel Hill, Burlington, Mooresville, and Wake Forest, then continues through every municipality with at least 25,000 residents. The first-batch destination inventory and resumable statuses are stored in `data/nc-wave-2-rollout.json`; Asheville's Blue Ridge Parkway listing is already seeded.

## Completion Standard

A full-buildout destination should have a live canonical page and map point, verified identity and arrival details, hours and seasonal rules, parking, fees, restrooms, accessibility, at least three real source-attributed images, at least ten useful visitor questions, and subsites where they materially help orientation.

Ordinary municipal offices, private residential amenities, duplicate listings, and superseded names remain excluded.

## How To Continue

Future work can reference any stable slice of this punchlist:

- `Continue NC Wave 1`
- `Continue NC Wave 2`
- `Build out Durham`
- `Continue NC District 6`
- `Run county discovery for Dare County`
- `Continue western NC census-designated communities`
- `Finish North Carolina state parks`

Each run should update the place's discovery, inventory, enrichment, image, subsite, live-page, and overall status fields so progress remains visible and resumable.

## Sources

The municipality inventory and 2024 population estimates come from the North Carolina Office of State Budget and Management. Census-designated communities come from the current U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb Places layer. Working districts follow the North Carolina Association of Municipal Clerks county district map.
