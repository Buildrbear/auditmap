Park

Deering Oaks Park

Deering Avenue at Park Avenue, Portland, ME 04102
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Deering Oaks is generally available from 6:30 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. under Portland's city park rules. The splash area, restrooms, Castle, athletic fields, reservations, and Saturday farmers market follow separate or seasonal schedules.
Navigate

What people say

Deering Oaks is Portland's central 55-acre historic park, with a pond, playground and seasonal splash area, baseball and multipurpose fields, basketball and tennis courts, walking paths, the Castle, and the Saturday outdoor farmers market.

What address should I use for Deering Oaks Park? +

Use 45 Park Avenue, Portland, ME 04103 for a practical park arrival. Deering Oaks is bordered by Park Avenue, State Street Extension, Forest Avenue, and Deering Avenue, so route to the playground, pond, ballfield, or market area when you know your destination.

Where should I park for Deering Oaks Park? +

Use legal street parking around the park or the lot off State Street Extension. The City specifically lists street parking for Quinn Field and the State Street Extension lot plus street parking for the baseball field; never park or drive on park grass.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Are there public restrooms at Deering Oaks Park? +

The City lists a vault toilet at Kings Court. A portable restroom has also historically been placed across from the playground, but seasonal availability can change, so do not rely on the playground unit without checking current conditions.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Portland ↗
Is there a playground at Deering Oaks Park? +

Yes. The playground is near the baseball field and the Castle area. The park also has basketball and tennis courts, baseball and multipurpose fields, paths, lawns, and the pond, making it useful for both short play visits and longer park outings.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Portland ↗
Is there a splash pad at Deering Oaks Park? +

Yes. Deering Oaks has a seasonal splash and shallow-water play area near the playground. Because water features can close for weather, water quality, maintenance, or the season, confirm the current City splash-pad status before making it the purpose of your trip.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can I walk through Deering Oaks Park? +

Yes. Paths connect the pond, playground, sports areas, lawns, and entrances around this compact downtown park. Conditions vary between paved paths, field edges, and lawn, especially after rain or snow.

What can affect access at Deering Oaks Park? +

Farmers market days, permitted events, field reservations, maintenance, winter weather, and seasonal water-feature operations can change parking or access to individual areas. Check City notices and posted signs for a time-sensitive visit.

What hours is Deering Oaks Park open? +

Portland city park rules generally close parks between 10:00 p.m. and 6:30 a.m. The splash area, restrooms, athletic fields, Castle, and farmers market have narrower schedules and can close independently.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Deering Oaks Park? +

Dogs may visit on leash under Portland park rules, but must remain controlled and away from the splash area and active playground equipment. Deering Oaks is not a designated off-leash dog park, and temporary event restrictions may apply.

Can I picnic at Deering Oaks Park? +

Yes. The park has open lawns, benches, and gathering areas around the pond and recreation facilities. Organized events, reserved athletic use, tents, vendors, or amplified sound require City approval; Saturday market operations occupy part of the park.

When is the Deering Oaks farmers market held? +

The Portland Farmers' Market uses Deering Oaks on Saturdays during its outdoor season. Vendor access and crowds can change nearby parking and paths, so check the market's current City-listed schedule before planning a combined park and market visit.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Portland ↗
Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Deering Oaks Park? +

Deering Oaks' mature tree canopy provides substantial shade around many paths, picnic areas, the pond, and interior lawns. The Castle and other historic park structures have limited program or operational access and should not be assumed open as public refuge; the playground, splash area, farmers-market space, tennis courts, and open lawns can remain exposed. During lightning, dangerous wind, or extreme heat or cold, leave the park and use a confirmed-open substantial building or enclosed vehicle rather than sheltering beneath large trees.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Portland ↗
Which bus stops near Deering Oaks Park? +

Greater Portland Metro Route 5 serves Park Avenue beside Deering Oaks, with stops near Deering Avenue and the Iris Network. Several downtown and South Portland routes also pass the Congress Street and Forest Avenue corner. Use the exact park side or playground as your destination and check live arrivals before traveling.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Greater Portland Metro ↗
How accessible is Deering Oaks Park? +

Developed paths connect the pond, playground, splash area, Castle, courts, fields, and park edges, but historic grades, winter conditions, event setups, and grass routes vary. Use the nearest destination-specific entrance and do not assume the restroom or splash area is open whenever the outdoor park is open.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 8, 2026
City of Portland ↗
How much does Deering Oaks Park cost? +

General park access is free; organized field use, permits, events, and market purchases may have separate costs.
