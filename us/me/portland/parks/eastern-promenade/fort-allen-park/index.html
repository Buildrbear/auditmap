Street parking serves the upper promenade, while Cutter Street and East End Beach lots serve the beach, launch, and lower trail. Boat-trailer spaces and event restrictions differ, and July 4, races, food-truck activity, and snow bans can sharply change availability. How accessible is Fort Allen Park? +

The upper park and lower waterfront trail sit at different elevations. Paved routes and designated parking serve major areas, but stairs, slopes, rail crossings, winter conditions, and the beach approach can change the easiest route. Start from the destination-specific access closest to Fort Allen, the playground, or East End Beach.

Are dogs allowed at Fort Allen Park? +

From Memorial Day through Labor Day, designated off-leash areas are available before 9 a.m. and after 5 p.m. From the day after Labor Day to Memorial Day, off-leash use is allowed during park hours in designated areas. Dogs are prohibited from playgrounds and athletic fields. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.
