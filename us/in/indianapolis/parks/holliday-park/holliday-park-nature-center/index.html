Where exactly is Holliday Park Nature Center? +

The Nature Center is inside Holliday Park at 6363 Spring Mill Road. The pin uses the reviewed OpenStreetMap building centroid rather than the inaccurate camera location embedded in one interior photograph.

Where should I park for Holliday Park Nature Center? +

Use the free lot near the Nature Center. The Ruins and playground have separate lots, so follow signs for the staffed building after entering from Spring Mill Road.

Are there restrooms near Holliday Park Nature Center? +

The staffed Nature Center is the park's most reliable restroom location during building hours. Do not assume indoor access on Sunday, before 9:00 a.m., after 5:00 p.m. or during a closure.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 11, 2026
Indy Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Holliday Park Nature Center free? +

The operator does not post general admission for ordinary Nature Center and Habitat Hall visits, and parking is free. Programs, camps, rentals and special events may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 11, 2026

How accessible is Holliday Park Nature Center? +

The Nature Center is a developed indoor visitor facility, but the operator does not publish a route-by-route guarantee on this page. Contact staff before relying on a specific mobility, sensory or program accommodation.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 11, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Holliday Park Nature Center? +

Outdoor park rules require leashes, but the Nature Center is an indoor wildlife-education facility. Confirm service-animal and pet access with staff rather than assuming ordinary pets may enter.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 11, 2026

Is Holliday Park Nature Center good for children? +

Habitat Hall's interactive exhibits and nature programming are intended for varied ages. Check the calendar for program registration and supervise children around exhibits.
