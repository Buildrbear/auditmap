What people say

A natural outdoor amphitheater on the east bank of the White River at the west end of Celebration Plaza, with tiered limestone seating and a private waterfall. Check the current park alert and events pages on the day of the visit. The visit page still displays an August 15 closure notice that explicitly says the space reopened August 16, so that notice is expired rather than evidence of a current closure.

Where exactly is Celebration Plaza Amphitheater? +

The amphitheater is tucked into the hillside on the east bank of the White River at the west end of Celebration Plaza. The pin is the reviewed OpenStreetMap point for the named Celebration Plaza and Amphitheater feature.

Where should I park for Celebration Plaza Amphitheater? +

Use the White River State Park underground garage or another downtown parking option, then approach through Celebration Plaza. The operator currently lists garage rates beginning at $10 for up to two hours and warns that special-event rates can differ.

Are there restrooms near Celebration Plaza Amphitheater? +

The operator does not identify a dedicated amphitheater restroom on the venue or park-map pages. Plan around restrooms inside nearby staffed attractions during their own hours, and confirm event facilities when access is important.

Is Celebration Plaza Amphitheater free? +

The operator lists no general admission charge for ordinary access to this outdoor park space. Reserved, private or ticketed events can restrict entry or charge separately; garage parking is paid.

How accessible is Celebration Plaza Amphitheater? +

The venue is built into a hillside with tiered limestone seating. The operator does not publish a route-by-route step-free access description, so visitors who need a specific entrance or seating route should contact park staff before arrival.

Are dogs allowed at Celebration Plaza Amphitheater? +

White River State Park prohibits animals off leash and leashes longer than six feet. Events may impose additional animal restrictions, so check the listing or signs before entering an event setup.

Is Celebration Plaza Amphitheater good for children? +

The outdoor setting can support a short park stop, but children need close supervision around the waterfall, tiered stone seating and any event equipment or restricted areas.
