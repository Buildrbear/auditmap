Where should I park for Fort Harrison Visitor Center? +

Enter from North Post Road and follow signs to the Visitor Center, Delaware Lake, Camp Glenn, Lawrence Creek or other named trailhead. Parking is included after gate admission.

Is Fort Harrison Visitor Center free? +

Current daily vehicle admission is $7 for Indiana plates and $9 for out-of-state plates; pedestrian and bicycle entry is $2 per person age five and older. Other activities may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Indiana Department of Natural Resources ↗
How accessible is Fort Harrison Visitor Center? +

Parts of the paved 3.2-mile Harrison Trace are wheelchair accessible. Natural trails, creek banks and sledding terrain vary substantially.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Indiana Department of Natural Resources ↗
Are dogs allowed at Fort Harrison Visitor Center? +

Leashed dogs are allowed under DNR rules; the dog park requires its own annual membership, application and waiver.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Indiana Department of Natural Resources ↗
Is Fort Harrison Visitor Center good for children? +

Harrison Trace, Delaware Lake, the Visitor Center, playgrounds and picnic areas are the simplest family combination.
