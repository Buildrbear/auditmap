Park directory

Current city pages

Broad Ripple Park

Indianapolis

Park grounds are open dawn to dusk. The Family Center is open Monday-Friday 9:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m., Saturday 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m., and closed Sunday.

Eagle Creek Park

Indianapolis

Park hours are dawn to dusk. The Earth Discovery Center and Ornithology Center are open Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m., Sunday 1:00-5:00 p.m., and closed Monday.

Fort Harrison State Park

Indianapolis

The state park is generally open daily; gate, visitor center, museum, inn and program hours vary. Confirm same-day hours with Indiana DNR.

Garfield Park

Indianapolis

Outdoor grounds are generally available daily. The Family Center currently operates Monday-Friday 11:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m. and is closed weekends; every major facility keeps its own schedule.

Holliday Park

Indianapolis

Park grounds are open dawn to dusk. The Nature Center is open Monday-Saturday 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m.

Monon Trail

Indianapolis

The outdoor trail follows Indy Parks and posted trail hours; use daylight and extra visibility around crossings even when a segment remains legally open later.

Riverside Regional Park

Indianapolis

Outdoor park and trail areas are generally available daily. The Family Center is open Monday-Friday 11:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m., Saturday 10:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m., and closed Sunday.

White River State Park

Indianapolis

Park grounds and the Canal Walk are open daily 5:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Museums, zoo, rentals, concerts and visitor facilities keep separate hours.
