What people say

The Eagle Creek Park Ornithology Center is a staffed bird-focused nature center with exhibits, wildlife observation areas, educational programs and non-releasable raptor ambassadors. The Commons file is titled as a nature center but its description identifies the building as the Ornithology Center. Weekly program times are not a substitute for checking the current calendar; arrive before the building's posted closing time.

Where exactly is Eagle Creek Park Ornithology Center? +

The center is at 6515 Delong Road in the northern part of Eagle Creek Park. The exact pin uses the Commons photograph's camera geotag, which depicts this exterior and matches the official facility address.

Where should I park for Eagle Creek Park Ornithology Center? +

Use the 71st Street park entrance at 7100 North Eagle Creek Parkway, then follow the park map toward 6515 Delong Road and the Ornithology Center lot. Park admission is collected at the gate.

Are there restrooms near Eagle Creek Park Ornithology Center? +

The staffed center is the reliable restroom location during building hours. Do not assume indoor restroom access before opening, after closing or during an unplanned facility closure.

Center admission is free with paid Eagle Creek Park admission. Special programs, group bookings and other park activities may charge separately.

Dogs must remain leashed in Eagle Creek Park, but the center and raptor areas are wildlife-focused facilities. Leave pets outside the building and aviary unless staff or posted rules explicitly allow an accommodation.
