Creve Coeur Water Trail

13725 Marine Avenue, Maryland Heights, MO 63043
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open daily from 30 minutes before official sunrise until 30 minutes after official sunset; vehicle gates generally open at 7:00 a.m. Rentals, water access, splash play, restrooms, concessions and events use separate schedules.

What people say

The marked water trail supports paddlers on the lake under current launch, wind and safety rules; rentals and life-jacket requirements apply independently. Creve Coeur Lake is managed for boating and fishing, not ordinary swimming. Flooding, water quality, heat, high water and events can close trails, launches or features independently; check county notices before a water-focused visit.

Creve Coeur Lake

The 320-acre Creve Coeur Lake supports paddling, sailing and fishing under posted rules; ordinary swimming is not allowed and water conditions can close access.

Lakeview Loop Trail

The roughly 3.8-mile paved Lakeview Loop is a flat shared route around the lake with substantial sun exposure, busy weekend traffic and several shoreline access points.
View guide →
Photo: Fletcher Commons
3 destination

Sailboat Cove

Sailboat Cove is the primary lower-lake arrival for boating, rentals, shelters and shoreline activity; parking fills quickly during races and warm weekends.
View guide →

Mallard Lake

Mallard Lake is a smaller wetland and fishing destination connected to the larger trail system, with quieter habitat and flood-sensitive approaches.

Creve Coeur Connector Trail

Creve Coeur Connector Trail climbs from the lake system toward regional greenway and Missouri River connections, adding distance and grade beyond the lake loop.

Creve Coeur Disc Golf Course

The disc-golf course occupies a separate upper-park recreation area; watch for flying discs and do not use active fairways as picnic or play space.

The seasonal splash pad and nearby playground provide free family play away from lake water; confirm operation, exact parking and restroom availability before visiting.

Where should I park for Creve Coeur Water Trail? +

Use Sailboat Cove on Marine Avenue for lake access or the exact upper-park lot for trails, fields, disc golf, playgrounds and shelters. Busy weekends and races can fill shoreline lots.

Are there restrooms near Creve Coeur Water Trail? +

Modern restrooms are available at selected lake, shelter and recreation areas during posted or seasonal hours, but long trail segments do not have frequent facilities.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Is Creve Coeur Water Trail free? +

General park, trails, playgrounds, beach area, splash play when operating, fishing access and disc golf are free. Rentals, permits, shelters, concessions and organized activities cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
St. Louis County Parks ↗
How accessible is Creve Coeur Water Trail? +

The paved Lakeview Loop and developed shoreline areas provide accessible choices, while natural trails, beach sand, launch edges, steep upper-park connections and flood debris vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
St. Louis County Parks ↗
Are dogs allowed at Creve Coeur Water Trail? +

Leashed dogs are welcome in ordinary park areas. Keep pets out of splash play, swimming-restricted areas, buildings, crowded events and posted habitat zones.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
St. Louis County Parks ↗
Is Creve Coeur Water Trail good for children? +

Playgrounds, seasonal splash play, the flat lake loop, picnic shelters and boats make this a strong family destination. The sand beach is not a supervised swimming beach; obey posted water-contact rules.
