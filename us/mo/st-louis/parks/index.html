Park directory

Current city pages

Citygarden Sculpture Park

St. Louis

Open daily sunrise-10:00 p.m., year-round. The Children's Spray Plaza operates seasonally, generally Memorial Day weekend-Labor Day from 10:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m., subject to weather and maintenance.

Forest Park

St. Louis

Park grounds are open daily 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Museums, zoo, visitor center, Muny, Jewel Box, concessions, rentals and recreation facilities keep separate schedules.

Gateway Arch National Park

St. Louis

Arch grounds and Luther Ely Smith Square are open daily 5:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. The Visitor Center and museum are generally 9:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m., with extended summer Friday-Saturday hours; the Old Courthouse is generally 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Holiday exceptions apply.

Missouri Botanical Garden

St. Louis

Generally open daily 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. with last entry at 4:30 p.m. Early walking, resident-free periods, Children's Garden, conservatories, historic buildings, tram tours and special events use separate schedules.

Tower Grove Park

St. Louis

Open daily 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Splash pad, Nee Kee Nee stream, farmers' market, carriage rides, events, offices and reservable pavilions use separate or seasonal schedules.
