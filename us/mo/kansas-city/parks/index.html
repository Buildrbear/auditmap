Kansas City

Outdoor riverfront spaces are generally available daily, subject to posted closures, events and construction. Stadium, transit and programmed areas keep separate schedules.

Kansas City

Park grounds are open daily 5:00 a.m.-midnight. In 2026 the sprayground operates May 23-September 7 daily 11:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m.; gardens and programmed facilities may keep separate hours.
