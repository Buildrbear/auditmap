Where should I park for Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Arch? +

There is no public parking inside the park. Metered perimeter parking is paid Monday-Friday from 8 a.m.-6 p.m. and normally free at other times, including weekends. Nearby garages and surface lots are alternatives; always follow current signs and event restrictions. How accessible is Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Arch? +

Bushnell Park is a compact 37-acre downtown landscape with paved curving paths, a formal northern promenade, and sidewalk connections to Union Station, the Capitol, monuments, carousel, playground, pond, and East Coast Greenway. Bicycles are allowed on park paths only with caution and must yield to pedestrians. Lawns, temporary event layouts, winter ice, and historic features such as the Memorial Arch's 96-step tower route differ from the principal step-free paths; use the paved promenade and main walks for the most predictable rolling route.

Are dogs allowed at Soldiers and Sailors Memorial Arch? +

Yes, but Hartford requires every dog in a city park to remain on a leash; Bushnell Park has no designated off-leash enclosure. Keep pets controlled on the paths and lawns, remove waste, and follow temporary pet restrictions around the carousel, playground, food service, festivals, races, and other permitted events. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.
