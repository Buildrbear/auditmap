What people say

East Rock Park is a 425-acre New Haven and Hamden park with wooded trails, College Woods, playground, athletic fields, ranger station, seasonal restrooms and water, summit overlooks, Soldiers and Sailors Monument, and restricted vehicle access to the summit.

Which entrance should I use for East Rock Park? +

Use College Woods at Cold Spring and Orange Streets for the playground, ranger station, and lower trails; use Davis Street and Summit Drive for the summit route; or choose Rice Field and other mapped entrances for sports and trail access.

Where should I park at East Rock Park? +

Park only in designated lots. College Woods parking is available along Orange, Cold Spring, and Livingston Streets, and limited accessible stalls are distributed at parking areas for vehicles displaying the required permit.

What hours is East Rock Park open? +

The park closes at sunset and remains closed until sunrise. Lower drives are open year-round, but summit gates, ranger facilities, restrooms, water, fields, and programs have separate or seasonal schedules.

What are East Rock Park trails like? +

Routes range from paved park roads and sidewalks to steep, rocky, rooted woodland trails. Summit approaches gain significant elevation, and rain, leaves, snow, ice, and erosion can make natural surfaces difficult.

Where is the playground at East Rock Park? +

The College Woods playground is by the ranger station area near Cold Spring and Orange Streets. It is separate from summit parking and the monument, so route to College Woods for family play.

Where are restrooms at East Rock Park? +

Seasonal portable toilets are at College Woods, the summit, and Rice Field. Ranger Station restrooms at College Woods are available for permitted events rather than guaranteed general daily use.

Can I drive to the East Rock summit? +

Vehicle summit access is restricted and changes by day or season; walking and bicycling access is more consistently available. Confirm the current gate notice before relying on a car route, and display the required permit for accessible stalls.

Can weather close East Rock summit access? +

Yes. Snow, ice, fallen trees, fire risk, maintenance, events, and gate schedules can restrict summit roads or trails. Check posted gates and City notices on the day of a summit-dependent visit.

Are dogs allowed in East Rock Park? +

Yes, but dogs must remain leashed at all times and owners must remove waste. East Rock Park itself is not an off-leash area; the separate East Rock Dog Park is at 107 Mechanic Street and has its own sunrise-to-sunset schedule.

Can I reserve a picnic pavilion at East Rock Park? +

Yes. English Shelter is at the summit, and a second pavilion is in College Woods beside the Trowbridge Environmental Center. Tables and charcoal grills are also available in designated areas; unreserved facilities are first come, first served, and ground fires are prohibited.

Can I reach East Rock Park by bus? +

Yes. New Haven lists buses traveling State Street, Orange Street, or Whitney Avenue as access options. Choose the stop nearest College Woods, Rice Field, or your trailhead because the park is large and summit vehicle access is separate.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Where can I find shade or weather shelter at East Rock Park? +

Forested trails and College Woods provide substantial natural shade, and two picnic pavilions offer open-air cover: English Shelter at the summit and the Picnic Pavilion beside Trowbridge Environmental Center in College Woods. Rice Field, North Meadow, summit overlooks, athletic areas, and exposed rock can remain open to sun and wind. The environmental center and ranger facilities follow separate hours, and pavilions are not severe-weather shelters; during lightning, dangerous wind, or extreme heat or cold, use a confirmed-open substantial building or vehicle.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of New Haven ↗
Is there a splash pad or swimming area at East Rock Park? +

Yes, Cedar Playground near the south end of Rice Field has a seasonal spray pool. Confirm the City's current splash-pad status before visiting because operation depends on season, maintenance, and weather. Swimming is prohibited in the Mill River. The Wilbur Cross High School pool may offer separate free summer public hours, but it is not general river or park water access and should be checked directly before a trip.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of New Haven ↗
What is the monument at the top of East Rock Park? +

The 112-foot Soldiers and Sailors Monument crowns East Rock's summit and honors New Haven residents who died in four nineteenth-century wars. It was erected in 1887 and is visible across much of the city. Reach the summit by trail, including the steep Giant Steps route, or use the Davis Street gate when the seasonal summit road is open. Interior monument tours occur only on announced dates; the overlook remains the main everyday destination.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

How much does East Rock Park cost? +

General park, trails, summit views, playground, fields, and picnic access are free. Reservations, permitted events, and programs may charge.
