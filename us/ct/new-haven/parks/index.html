East Rock Park

New Haven

Use College Woods at Cold Spring and Orange Streets for the playground, ranger station, and lower trails; use Davis Street and Summit Drive for the summit route; or choose Rice Field and other mapped entrances for sports and trail access.

Lighthouse Point Park

New Haven

Park gates are generally open from 7 a.m. to sunset year-round. Summer vehicle-gate and beach operations may run later under current City schedules, while the carousel, concessions, lifeguards, and splash pad use separate seasonal hours.
