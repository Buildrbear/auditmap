What people say

Middle Sioux Falls is the central mapped waterfall within Falls Park, where the Big Sioux River descends across exposed pink Sioux Quartzite beside the park's paved walking routes and overlooks. Stay on designated paths and behind barriers. The City warns that the quartzite is slippery when wet and that people have died after entering the rocks and water during flood events; keep children close and never climb down for a photograph or rescue attempt.

Falls Park Viewing Tower

Falls Park Viewing Tower is a five-story enclosed tower attached to the Visitor Information Center, with an elevator and an outdoor observation deck overlooking the falls and downtown skyline.

2 destination

Queen Bee Mill Ruins

Queen Bee Mill Ruins preserve the visible remains of the seven-story flour mill that opened beside the falls in 1881 and was later damaged by fire, leaving historic stonework on the river's east bank.

3 destination

Falls Overlook Cafe

Falls Overlook Cafe occupies a restored 1908 hydroelectric plant on the east bank of the Big Sioux River, with indoor and outdoor dining, historic exhibits, local ice cream, gifts, and views of the falls.

Are there restrooms near Middle Sioux Falls? +

Restrooms are available at developed facilities including the Visitor Center area during operating periods, with event or seasonal facilities elsewhere. Identify an open building before visiting early morning, late evening, or in winter.

What fees apply at Middle Sioux Falls? +

The City does not list a general admission charge for Falls Park or its public waterfall overlooks. Food, shopping, reserved shelters, tours, and ticketed or permitted activities can cost separately.

How accessible is Middle Sioux Falls? +

Yes. Developed parking and paved paths reach several waterfall viewpoints, and the Visitor Center provides an observation-tower experience during seasonal hours. Individual overlooks, snow, ice, and slopes vary.

Are dogs allowed at Middle Sioux Falls? +

Pawsible Dog Park is at adjacent Jacobson Plaza in Falls Park West, not beside the main waterfall overlooks. Dogs must be leashed while entering and leaving designated dog areas and remain under control; ordinary Falls Park paths are not an off-leash zone. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.
