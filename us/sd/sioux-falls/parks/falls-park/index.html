Park

Falls Park

131 E Falls Park Drive, Sioux Falls, SD 57103
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Falls Park is open daily from 5:00 a.m. to midnight. The Visitor Information Center and observation tower use seasonal hours; Falls Overlook Cafe, farmers market, shelter, and holiday lights use separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

Falls Park is Sioux Falls's namesake 128-acre Big Sioux River park, with multiple waterfall overlooks, five-story Visitor Center observation tower, paved recreation trail, historic Queen Bee Mill ruins, Stockyards Ag Experience, Falls Overlook Cafe in a former hydroelectric plant, farmers market, open-air shelter, public art, picnic areas, and winter holiday lights.

Middle Sioux Falls

Middle Sioux Falls is the central mapped waterfall within Falls Park, where the Big Sioux River descends across exposed pink Sioux Quartzite beside the park's paved walking routes and overlooks.

Falls Park Viewing Tower

Falls Park Viewing Tower is a five-story enclosed tower attached to the Visitor Information Center, with an elevator and an outdoor observation deck overlooking the falls and downtown skyline.

Queen Bee Mill Ruins

Queen Bee Mill Ruins preserve the visible remains of the seven-story flour mill that opened beside the falls in 1881 and was later damaged by fire, leaving historic stonework on the river's east bank.

Falls Overlook Cafe

What address should I use for Falls Park? +

Use 131 E. Falls Park Drive for the main park and shelter or route directly to the Visitor Information Center for maps and the observation tower. Several free lots serve different overlooks and attractions.

Is parking free at Falls Park? +

Yes. Multiple free surface lots serve the Visitor Center, overlooks, cafe, shelter, and park attractions. Farmers market, marathon, holiday lights, and major events can fill or redirect individual lots.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can I bike through Falls Park? +

Yes. Paved paths connect the overlooks and join Sioux Falls's 45-plus-mile recreation trail system. Falls Park and the Downtown River Greenway remain open until midnight, later than most trail sections.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Sioux Falls ↗
Are there restrooms at Falls Park? +

Restrooms are available at developed facilities including the Visitor Center area during operating periods, with event or seasonal facilities elsewhere. Identify an open building before visiting early morning, late evening, or in winter.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can I see the falls without climbing stairs? +

Yes. Developed parking and paved paths reach several waterfall viewpoints, and the Visitor Center provides an observation-tower experience during seasonal hours. Individual overlooks, snow, ice, and slopes vary.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

What should I see at Falls Park besides the waterfalls? +

Visit the five-story observation tower, Queen Bee Mill ruins, Falls Overlook Cafe's historic hydroelectric building, Stockyards Ag Experience, public art, farmers market, river trail, and seasonal Winter Wonderland lights.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Sioux Falls ↗
Is Falls Park free? +

General entry, parking, waterfall viewpoints, trails, ruins, public art, Visitor Center, and observation tower are free. Cafe purchases, shelter reservations, vendors, tours, and special events may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Sioux Falls ↗
When is the Falls Park observation tower open? +

Visitor Center and tower hours are seasonal: 9:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. daily May 4-August 31, 9:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. through October, and limited holiday-season hours later in the year. It closes on specified holidays and hours may change.

Can I reserve a picnic shelter at Falls Park? +

Yes. Falls Park has an electrified open-air shelter with 14 tables, plus picnic tables elsewhere. Reservations guarantee use; an unreserved shelter is first come, first served. Grills must remain outside shelters, glass is prohibited, and special setups may require permits.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Sioux Falls ↗
Is there a playground at Falls Park? +

Yes. The City lists an accessible playground in Falls Park, along with nearby restrooms, drinking fountains, walking paths, and picnic facilities. Navigate to the playground rather than the general waterfall pin when play access is the priority.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Is there an off-leash dog park at Falls Park? +

Pawsible Dog Park is at adjacent Jacobson Plaza in Falls Park West, not beside the main waterfall overlooks. Dogs must be leashed while entering and leaving designated dog areas and remain under control; ordinary Falls Park paths are not an off-leash zone.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Sioux Falls ↗
Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Falls Park? +

Falls Park has a reservable 20-by-40-foot picnic shelter and a larger open-air shelter with electricity. The Visitor Center, observation tower, Overlook Cafe, and Stockyards Ag Experience provide indoor or enclosed space only during their own posted hours. Waterfall overlooks, quartzite outcrops, bridges, playground, lawns, and much of the recreation trail are exposed. During lightning, flooding, high wind, or extreme cold or heat, leave the river and falls edge and use a confirmed-open substantial building or enclosed vehicle.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Sioux Falls ↗
Can visitors swim or use a splash pad at Falls Park? +

No current splash pad or designated swimming area is listed at Falls Park. The Big Sioux River, falls, quartzite ledges, and quarry water are not family water-play areas; wet rocks are slippery, currents strengthen rapidly, and people have died during high-water incidents. Renderings in long-range master-plan documents show possible future water features, not operating amenities. Use one of Sioux Falls' currently listed pools or spray parks instead.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Sioux Falls ↗
Which Sioux Area Metro bus stops at Falls Park? +

The Purple Line stops on Weber Avenue at both the east and west Falls Park entrances. It connects with the downtown bus terminal and normally runs hourly, so check live times before leaving and use the entrance closest to the Visitor Information Center, Overlook Cafe, Queen Bee Mill ruins, or Levitt event area.
