1 destination

Middle Sioux Falls

Middle Sioux Falls is the central mapped waterfall within Falls Park, where the Big Sioux River descends across exposed pink Sioux Quartzite beside the park's paved walking routes and overlooks.
View guide →
Photo: Richard N Horne
2 destination

Falls Park Viewing Tower

Falls Park Viewing Tower is a five-story enclosed tower attached to the Visitor Information Center, with an elevator and an outdoor observation deck overlooking the falls and downtown skyline.
View guide →
Photo: Richard N Horne
3 destination

Queen Bee Mill Ruins

Queen Bee Mill Ruins preserve the visible remains of the seven-story flour mill that opened beside the falls in 1881 and was later damaged by fire, leaving historic stonework on the river's east bank.
View guide →
Photo: Tiggr222
4 destination

Falls Overlook Cafe

Falls Overlook Cafe occupies a restored 1908 hydroelectric plant on the east bank of the Big Sioux River, with indoor and outdoor dining, historic exhibits, local ice cream, gifts, and views of the falls.
