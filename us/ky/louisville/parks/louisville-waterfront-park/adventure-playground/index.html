Are there restrooms near Adventure Playground? +

Yes. Restrooms are distributed through the park and generally operate 9 a.m.–9 p.m. from mid-April through mid-November. PlayPort has a dedicated restroom building; off-season availability is more limited.

What fees apply at Adventure Playground? +

Adventure Playground and seasonal Splash Park access are free; parking, vendors, rentals, and events may charge separately.

How accessible is Adventure Playground? +

Yes. Park paths were designed to ADA standards, with ramp alternatives where stairs occur. PlayPort includes all-abilities play and a nearby restroom building; confirm event-specific routes when lawns or paths are fenced.

Are dogs allowed at Adventure Playground? +

Leashed pets are welcome in general park areas but are prohibited on the Big Four Bridge, playgrounds, fountains, and Splash Park. There is no general off-leash area within Waterfront Park. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.

Is Adventure Playground useful for a family visit? +

Adventure Playground is the family play area at 1101 River Road beside the seasonal Splash Park and east of the Big Four Bridge. The original Children's Play Area is near Orange lot; Adventure Playground is by Silver lot and the Big Four Bridge; PlayPort is a 3.5-acre all-abilities STEM-focused play and learning landscape by Aqua lot. Each is a separate destination.
