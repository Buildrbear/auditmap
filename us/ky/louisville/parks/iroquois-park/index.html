What people say

Iroquois Park is Louisville's 725-acre Olmsted scenic reservation, with forested hills and hiking trails, a five-kilometer paved walking loop, North Overlook, wheelchair-accessible playground, seasonal sprayground, picnic facilities, golf, disc golf, courts, archery, horse trails, and the 2,366-seat WPA-era Iroquois Amphitheater.

What hours is Iroquois Park open? +

The park is open daily from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. Amphitheater gates, North Overlook vehicle access, sprayground, restrooms, golf, and organized facilities use narrower schedules.

What address should I use for Iroquois Park? +

Use 5216 New Cut Road, Louisville, KY 40214 for a general arrival. Route directly to Iroquois Amphitheater for a show or use the official map for the playground, golf, trails, picnic areas, and overlook approach.

Where should I park at Iroquois Park? +

General parking is distributed near recreation and trail areas. For amphitheater events, premier parking is sold closest to the venue, while free event parking is available on Rundill Road south of Frisbee Way and at DeSales High School when designated.

What are the walking trails like at Iroquois Park? +

The park has a roughly five-kilometer paved walking loop plus natural hiking and horse trails through steep forested hills. Natural routes can be rocky, rooted, muddy, and much more strenuous than the paved loop.

Is the Iroquois Park playground wheelchair accessible? +

Louisville specifically lists a wheelchair-accessible playground, along with a seasonal sprayground. Use the official park map to distinguish the family recreation area from the amphitheater and hill trails.

Is there a sprayground at Iroquois Park? +

Yes. A sprayground is listed among the park amenities near family recreation. It is seasonal and may close for weather, maintenance, water restrictions, or projects, so check current Metro Parks notices.

Are there restrooms at Iroquois Park? +

Yes. Restrooms serve developed park areas, and the amphitheater has family restrooms with changing stations in its concession tunnel during venue operations. Do not assume trail or overlook restrooms are open at all hours.

Is Iroquois Park free? +

General entry, trails, playground, sprayground, picnic areas, courts, and overlook access are free. Ticketed amphitheater events, golf, premium event parking, permits, and rentals charge separately.

Can I drive to the Iroquois Park North Overlook every day? +

No. Vehicle access through the Uppill Road gate is limited to published open-gate dates, while overlook improvements and road projects may add restrictions. Pedestrian access and conditions should be confirmed on the current project page.

Where can visitors find shade or weather shelter at Iroquois Park? +

The forested hills and many trail sections provide extensive natural shade, and Louisville lists picnic shelters in developed areas. The North Overlook, athletic fields, golf areas, and portions of the paved loop are exposed; the amphitheater opens for scheduled operations rather than as a general refuge. Trees and open shelters are not safe in lightning or dangerous wind, so use an open substantial building or vehicle during severe weather.

Where can dogs go at Iroquois Park, and what rules apply? +

Dogs may accompany visitors on Iroquois Park's ordinary paths and permitted trails, but Louisville requires pets to remain on leash and owners to pick up waste. Iroquois does not list a designated dog run, so it should not be treated as an off-leash park. Use extra care on narrow natural trails shared with faster mountain-bike traffic and obey any event, golf-course, playground, or facility restrictions.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Which picnic shelter can I reserve at Iroquois Park? +

Louisville lists the Sunnyhill shelter as Iroquois Park's reservable picnic shelter. A reservation guarantees exclusive use; if no reservation notice is posted, an otherwise available shelter may be used first come, first served at no charge. Shelters are offered daily from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. year-round. Weddings, alcohol, amplified sound, tents, vendors, inflatables, or large organized events can require additional permits, so contact Metro Parks rather than treating a shelter booking as approval for every activity.

Which bus goes to Iroquois Park? +

TARC Routes 4 and 6 serve Iroquois Park from downtown Louisville, and both list the amphitheater as a park-and-ride location. Route 4 approaches through the University of Louisville and New Cut Road corridor, while Route 6 serves the Sixth Street and Auburndale corridor. Use the live planner for the amphitheater, overlook, golf course, playground, or Sunnyhill shelter because the park's hills and loop roads make some destinations a long walk from another stop.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Transit Authority of River City ↗
What landmarks should I see in Iroquois Park? +

The panoramic North Overlook and the restored 1938 WPA-era Iroquois Amphitheater are the park's two signature destinations. A stair-and-trail route from the lot near Uppill and Rundill Roads connects toward Corbly Trail and the overlook, while the amphitheater uses 1080 Amphitheater Road and its own event access. The old-growth forest, scenic loop, other overlooks, golf and disc-golf areas, playground, sprayground, and shelters fill separate districts across the hilly park, so choose the exact destination before arriving.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

How accessible is Iroquois Park? +

Accessibility varies by entrance, route, terrain, and destination. Review the exact mapped subsite and its official source before relying on a specific accessible route.
