Riverfront Park

Billings

Use the main developed area near Lake Josephine for parking, shelters, picnic areas, fishing, restrooms, and lake paths. River access and trail connections use separate approaches near South Billings Boulevard.

Swords Park

Billings

Use East Airport Road and Swords Park Drive for the main access. Choose the closest trailhead for Boothill Cemetery, rim overlooks, paved jogging, or a longer Black Otter Trail connection.
