Park directory

Current city pages

Ala Moana Regional Park

Honolulu

Open daily from 4:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Individual concessions, lifeguards, pavilions, restrooms and permitted activities keep separate hours.

Diamond Head State Monument

Honolulu

Open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; last entrance to hike is 4:00 p.m. Entry is controlled by reservation windows and the monument can close for weather or safety work.

Hanauma Bay Nature Preserve

Honolulu

Temporarily closed August 3-18, 2026 for a sewer project. Normally open Wednesday-Sunday with entry from 6:45 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.; beach clearance begins at 3:30 p.m. and everyone must leave by 4:00 p.m. Closed Monday and Tuesday.

Kapiolani Regional Park

Honolulu

The outdoor park follows posted City closure hours; the zoo, Waikiki Shell, tennis center and permitted events keep separate schedules. Confirm the gate or facility you plan to use.

Koko Crater Railway Trail

Honolulu

Use the trail only during daylight and obey posted Koko Head District Park closure hours. Heat, maintenance, wildfire risk and severe weather can close access independently.

Pearl Harbor National Memorial

Honolulu

Visitor center and museums are open daily 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. except Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day. Parking lots operate 6:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.; Navy boat access can stop for wind, mechanical or security conditions.
