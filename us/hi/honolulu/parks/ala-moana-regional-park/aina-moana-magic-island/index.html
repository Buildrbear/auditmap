What people say

Aina Moana Magic Island is a distinct mapped destination within Ala Moana Regional Park; navigate to this point instead of the general park pin and check posted conditions on arrival. The Banyan Courtyard and portico at Lester McCoy Pavilion remain under improvement with completion targeted by the end of 2026. Large ceremonies and events can also change parking and ordinary access.

Where should I park for Aina Moana Magic Island? +

Multiple free park lots and roadside spaces run along the long park drive, but they fill at beach peaks and major events. Pick Magic Island, the central beach, courts or a bathhouse before entering.

Are there restrooms near Aina Moana Magic Island? +

Bathhouses and comfort stations with outdoor showers serve the beach and Magic Island areas. Renovation or cleaning can close one facility independently, so note the next closest station.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Aina Moana Magic Island free? +

Park, beach, lagoon, paths, lawns, ordinary picnic use and outdoor courts are free. Food, permits, rentals and ticketed events cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Aina Moana Magic Island? +

Paved park drives and promenades reach the principal areas, and beach-access support is available through Honolulu's accessible beach program. Sand and seawalls still require route planning.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Aina Moana Magic Island? +

Dogs are prohibited under current posted park rules, including Magic Island, except as allowed for trained service animals. Follow entrance signs rather than older proposals for expanded dog access.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Aina Moana Magic Island good for children? +

The protected swimming water, broad lawns, showers and food access support a long family visit. There is no substitute for close ocean supervision even inside the reef-protected beach and lagoon.

How do you reach Aina Moana Magic Island? +

TheBus serves Ala Moana Center and the boulevard opposite the park. Crossing the wide road and walking to the far end of Magic Island adds time, especially with children or beach gear.
