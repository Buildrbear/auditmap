Kahala Tunnel Entrance

Diamond Head Road, Honolulu, HI 96815
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; last entrance to hike is 4:00 p.m. Entry is controlled by reservation windows and the monument can close for weather or safety work. Kahala Tunnel Entrance may keep shorter, reserved, staffed, event-dependent, ocean-dependent or daylight-only hours; verify the current feature status before relying on parent hours.

What people say

Kahala Tunnel Entrance is a distinct mapped destination within Diamond Head State Monument; navigate to this point instead of the general park pin and check posted conditions on arrival. Non-Hawaii residents need advance entry reservations, and parking must be reserved separately. The exposed trail becomes hot early; bring water and sun protection and do not start later than your fitness safely allows.

Where should I park for Kahala Tunnel Entrance? +

Non-Hawaii residents must reserve parking in advance. Parking is limited inside the crater and sold in timed blocks; arriving without the correct reservation can mean turning around.

Are there restrooms near Kahala Tunnel Entrance? +

Restrooms and water are available near the crater trailhead and parking area, not along the upper trail. Use them before beginning the climb.

Is Kahala Tunnel Entrance free? +

Nonresident admission is $5 per person and nonresident vehicle parking is $10, plus applicable reservation fees. Hawaii residents enter and park free with valid ID, subject to availability.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

How accessible is Kahala Tunnel Entrance? +

The visitor area is more accessible than the summit route. The hike is steep and exposed with switchbacks, uneven pavement, long stairs, a tunnel and narrow military structures; the summit is not wheelchair accessible.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hawaii State Parks ↗
Are dogs allowed at Kahala Tunnel Entrance? +

Pets are not permitted on the summit trail. Qualified service animals are governed by applicable access rules, but the physical route remains steep and constrained.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Is Kahala Tunnel Entrance good for children? +

Older children comfortable with heat, heights, tunnels and stairs may enjoy the route. Carry water, pace the ascent and do not bring a stroller expecting summit access.
