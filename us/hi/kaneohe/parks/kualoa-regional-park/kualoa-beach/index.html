What people say

Kualoa Beach is a named shoreline destination at Kualoa Regional Park; check water status, lifeguard coverage, wind and posted beach rules before entering. Do not confuse this free City park with the adjacent commercial ranch. Mokolii is offshore and attempting to wade or paddle there depends on tides, currents, skill, permissions and ocean conditions; the viewpoint is the safe default.

Where should I park for Kualoa Beach? +

Free lots serve the long beach and lawn, but weekend, event and camping demand can fill the closest sections. Choose Campground A, Campground B, the beach or Mokolii viewpoint before entering.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
When is Kualoa Beach open? +

Day-use areas follow posted City park hours, with typical overnight closure from 10:00 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. Campground gates and permits use separate schedules; follow current entrance signs. Kualoa Beach may keep shorter, reserved, staffed, event-dependent, ocean-dependent or daylight-only hours; verify the current feature status before relying on parent hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
Are there restrooms near Kualoa Beach? +

Comfort stations and outdoor rinse showers serve developed beach and campground areas. Conditions and opening can vary, so identify the next facility before walking the shoreline.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Kualoa Beach free? +

Day use, beach access, views and ordinary picnicking are free. Camping requires a paid advance permit; commercial tours, rentals and food cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Kualoa Beach? +

Honolulu's accessible beach program identifies beach wheelchairs and mats for Kualoa. Reserve or confirm equipment and the current mat location before relying on it.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Kualoa Beach? +

Follow posted City pet restrictions; beach, campground and protected cultural or wildlife areas may prohibit dogs. Do not assume an ordinary leash overrides a signed exclusion.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City and County of Honolulu Department of Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Kualoa Beach good for children? +

Broad lawns, shallow-looking beach water and dramatic views suit picnics, but currents, wind, sharp reef, boats and an unfenced shoreline require close supervision.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

How do you reach Kualoa Beach? +

TheBus travels Kamehameha Highway, but service is less frequent than urban Honolulu and the park is long. Check the exact stop and last practical return trip before visiting without a car.
