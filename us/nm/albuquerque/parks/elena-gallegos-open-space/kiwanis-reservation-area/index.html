What people say

Kiwanis Reservation Area is Elena Gallegos' large sheltered group-picnic destination, with room for up to 250 people, three coal grills, a volleyball net, horseshoes, running water, electricity, restrooms, and direct trail access. Reserve the facility before planning exclusive or large-group use. The City identifies 40 designated parking spaces with limited accessible parking. Follow the official map from the entrance booth to the Kiwanis lot rather than stopping at the first picnic shelter. Coal grills can be restricted during high fire danger, so verify current fire rules and do not assume that a reservation guarantees grill use.

Are there restrooms near Kiwanis Reservation Area? +

Restroom facilities serve the developed picnic and reservation areas; the Kiwanis area specifically lists pit toilets. Trail users should use developed facilities before continuing toward remote foothill or wilderness routes.

What fees apply at Kiwanis Reservation Area? +

Exclusive group use requires a reservation and a fee based on group size, day, and applicable deposit. Ordinary Elena Gallegos vehicle entry is charged separately at the park entrance; current City guidance lists $1 on weekdays and $2 on weekends.

How accessible is Kiwanis Reservation Area? +

Yes. The half-mile paved Cottonwood Springs Trail starts at a designated trailhead and leads to a pond and wildlife blind, with shaded rest stops and interpretive artwork. Other foothill and wilderness trails have natural surfaces, elevation change, and rocky terrain.

Are dogs allowed at Kiwanis Reservation Area? +

Yes, but all pets must remain under direct supervision on a leash no longer than eight feet throughout Elena Gallegos and other Albuquerque Open Space lands. There is no off-leash exception on the trails or in picnic areas. Carry out waste, keep dogs away from wildlife, horses, cactus, and cliff or drainage edges, and note that guided wildlife programs may ask participants to leave pets at home even though ordinary leashed access is allowed. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.

Is Kiwanis Reservation Area useful for a family visit? +

Kiwanis Reservation Area is Elena Gallegos' large sheltered group-picnic destination, with room for up to 250 people, three coal grills, a volleyball net, horseshoes, running water, electricity, restrooms, and direct trail access. The City does not list a conventional playground at Elena Gallegos. This is a mountain open-space and picnic destination centered on trails, wildlife viewing, outdoor education, reservable picnic areas, and natural terrain; the Kiwanis reservation area adds a volleyball net and horseshoe pit rather than children's play equipment. For the easiest family outing, use the short concrete Cottonwood Springs Trail and a developed picnic area, while supervising children closely around cactus, wildlife, heat, loose rock, and trail traffic.
