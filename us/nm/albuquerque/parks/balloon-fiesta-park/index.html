Balloon Fiesta Park

5500 Balloon Fiesta Parkway NE, Albuquerque, NM 87113
Hours ⌄
Published schedule General public access varies by day and can be restricted for balloon operations, field users, and special events. City park-use materials list 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. as the general operating window, but visitors should confirm same-day access.
Navigate

What people say

Balloon Fiesta Park is Albuquerque's large ballooning and event campus, centered on the North Launch Field and connected to the Anderson-Abruzzo Albuquerque International Balloon Museum, Golf and Event Center, Sid Cutter Pilots' Pavilion, large parking fields, and the North Diversion Channel Trail. Ordinary recreation access is secondary to scheduled operations and events.

Which entrance should I use for Balloon Fiesta Park? +

Use the northern entrance from I-25 Exit 234 and Balloon Fiesta Parkway for special events and field activities. Use the southern entrance from I-25 Exit 233, Alameda Road, and Balloon Museum Drive for the Golf and Event Center, Sid Cutter Pilots' Pavilion, and Balloon Museum.

Can I visit Balloon Fiesta Park on an ordinary day? +

Usually, but access varies. Remote-control flyers typically have exclusive field use until noon, and events can limit daily users, roads, parking, pedestrian gates, or the launch field. City use rules identify 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. as the general window; call the Golf and Event Center for same-day field access.

Where should I park at Balloon Fiesta Park? +

Parking depends on the destination and event. The southern entrance serves Balloon Museum, Golf and Event Center, and pavilion lots; the northern entrance serves launch-field activities and event parking. Never assume a major-event traffic plan, parking price, or gate is the same as ordinary access.

Can I walk or bike to Balloon Fiesta Park? +

Yes, when event restrictions allow. Pedestrians can approach from the north and south. Walkers and cyclists can connect from the North Diversion Channel Trail through Alameda Boulevard Trail East to Balloon Museum Drive or through gate 21 during Golf and Event Center public hours.

Is Balloon Fiesta Park free? +

Ordinary public use is generally free, but major festivals and events may charge admission or parking. Museum admission, golf, reservations, special activities, and a $25 annual automated vehicle gate card are separate.

Is accessible parking available at Balloon Fiesta Park? +

Accessible parking is provided for major city events in designated north and south lots, but the exact traffic plan and closest accessible route change by event. Check the event-specific access page rather than relying on a general park map.

Can dogs visit Balloon Fiesta Park? +

Do not assume pets are allowed. City park-use rules state that dogs other than service animals require advance permission and must remain leashed; individual events may prohibit pets entirely.

Why might Balloon Fiesta Park be unavailable? +

Balloon operations, remote-control flying, field practice, festivals, private reservations, temporary road plans, and weather can restrict ordinary access. The North Launch Field, pavilion, and Golf and Event Center are reserved for the annual Balloon Fiesta during September and October. Confirm current availability before making a dedicated trip.

Are public restrooms available at Balloon Fiesta Park? +

Yes. Albuquerque lists public restrooms at the field, Event Center, Pilots' Pavilion, and Public Safety Building, normally available daily from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Event controls or facility reservations can still limit the closest route.

Is the Balloon Museum part of Balloon Fiesta Park? +

The Anderson-Abruzzo Albuquerque International Balloon Museum is on the park's southern side at 9201 Balloon Museum Drive. It is a separately operated museum with its own admission and schedule, normally Tuesday through Sunday, and expanded hours during Balloon Fiesta.

Where can visitors find shade or weather shelter at Balloon Fiesta Park? +

The launch field is a broad, high-desert event space with very little dependable natural shade. The Event Center, Sid Cutter Pilots' Pavilion, Public Safety Building, and Balloon Museum are not general-purpose shelters and may be closed, reserved, ticketed, or restricted by an event. Bring water and sun protection for ordinary visits; during lightning, high wind, dust, or dangerous heat, return to an open substantial building or vehicle rather than remaining on the field.

What are the walking and rolling surfaces at Balloon Fiesta Park? +

Paved multi-use routes connect Balloon Museum Drive, the North Diversion Channel Trail, Alameda Trail, museum area, and major park entrances. Inside the park, the 86-acre launch field is mostly broad turf with paved roads and paths around it; grass can be uneven, soft, dusty, muddy, or covered by temporary event flooring and barriers. Balloon Fiesta and other large events create long walks and event-specific accessible routes, so use that event's current map rather than the ordinary daily-use layout.

Is there a playground at Balloon Fiesta Park? +

Balloon Fiesta Park does not advertise a conventional daily-use playground. During the ticketed Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta, family activities include the hands-on Balloon Discovery Center, entertainment, and an event Kids Zone shown on the current guest map; these are temporary event features, not year-round park amenities. Outside major events, use the park for scheduled recreation, trails, fields, and museum-area visits, and choose another Albuquerque park for swings or slides. During Fiesta, follow the current map and keep children close on the active launch field.

Can I bring a picnic to Balloon Fiesta Park? +

During Balloon Fiesta sessions, guests may bring food, folding chairs, blankets, and a soft-sided cooler or bag, but security will inspect belongings. Glass, alcohol, hard-sided coolers, smoking, and pets other than service animals are prohibited. A limited number of picnic tables and benches are on the east side of the Launch Field along Main Street, so bring a chair or blanket rather than relying on a table. Rules for ordinary non-event park use can differ by scheduled activity.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Albuquerque International Balloon Fiesta ↗
Does Balloon Fiesta Park have a splash pad or swimming pool? +

No splash pad or public swimming pool is listed at Balloon Fiesta Park. The site is a large launch field and event venue, and temporary event installations should not be mistaken for permanent water play. Albuquerque's operating spray pads are at separately named locations such as Alamosa, Phil Chacon Park, and Civic Plaza, each with seasonal hours and maintenance closures; check the live City list before visiting.
