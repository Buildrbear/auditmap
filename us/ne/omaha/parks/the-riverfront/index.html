Park

The RiverFront

900 Farnam Street, Omaha, NE 68102
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The RiverFront parks are open daily from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Playgrounds, Skate Ribbon, water features, kiosks, programs, rentals, and special events may use separate or weather-dependent schedules.
Navigate

What people say

The RiverFront is Omaha's connected downtown park system comprising Gene Leahy Mall, Heartland of America Park, and Lewis & Clark Landing, with major accessible playgrounds, Cascades water feature, Skate Ribbon, lake and river views, dog park, sculpture garden, urban beach, lawns, gardens, performance spaces, restrooms, public art, and daily programming.

Which part of The RiverFront should I visit? +

Use Gene Leahy Mall between 8th and 13th Streets for Arches Playground, Cascades, slides, sculpture garden, and dog park; Heartland of America Park for the lake and Skate Ribbon; or Lewis & Clark Landing for the riverfront and Discovery Playground.

Where should I park for The RiverFront? +

Use legal Park Omaha metered spaces or downtown garages nearest the specific park. Gene Leahy Mall, Heartland of America Park, and Lewis & Clark Landing span many blocks, and event traffic can change availability; posted signs always control.

Can I reach The RiverFront without a car? +

Yes. The parks are served by Omaha Metro and ORBT routes, downtown walking routes, bike share, and rideshare drop-offs. Gene Leahy Mall sits between Old Market and north downtown, making it the easiest central transit destination.

Where are restrooms at The RiverFront? +

Each park has permanent accessible restrooms: beside Arches Playground near 11th and Farnam in Gene Leahy Mall, at the Skate Kiosk near 8th and Farnam in Heartland of America Park, and east of Discovery Playground at Lewis & Clark Landing.

Where are The RiverFront playgrounds? +

Arches Playground is in Gene Leahy Mall near 11th and Farnam, while the larger Discovery Playground is at Lewis & Clark Landing beside Kiewit Luminarium. Both include accessible and lower-impact play features.

Is there water play at The RiverFront? +

Yes. Gene Leahy Mall includes the Cascades interactive water feature, and Lewis & Clark Landing includes an urban beach area. Operation is weather and maintenance dependent, and the beach is an designed play landscape rather than Missouri River swimming access.

Where is The RiverFront dog park? +

The fenced dog park is in Gene Leahy Mall near 8th and Douglas, with separate small- and large-dog sides, artificial turf, tunnels, and hills. Dogs must remain leashed elsewhere in the three parks.

Is The RiverFront wheelchair accessible? +

Yes. All three parks use gently sloping routes, accessible wayfinding, companion seating, accessible playground transfer systems, and wheelchair-accessible public and family restrooms. Identifiable routes connect the major amenities.

Is The RiverFront free? +

General park admission, playgrounds, dog park, paths, lawns, sculpture garden, and most public activities are free. Parking, skating or recreation rentals, food, reserved venues, and selected events may charge.

What RiverFront features can close independently? +

Cascades, Skate Ribbon, playground elements, kiosks, lawns, roads, and event spaces can close for weather, maintenance, private rentals, or major programming while the larger park remains open. Follow same-day signs and staff directions.

Where can I find shade or weather shelter at The RiverFront? +

Gene Leahy Mall has a mixed canopy of birch, locust, oak, maple, elm, serviceberry, and hawthorn, plus a performance pavilion and developed play areas. Shade is less dependable across Heartland of America Park's broad lawns, lake, pier, and amphitheater and Lewis & Clark Landing's river edge, beach, prairie garden, and playground. Permanent restrooms are available in each park, but kiosks and pavilions are not guaranteed storm refuge; leave exposed riverfront areas during lightning, high wind, flooding, or dangerous heat.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

What are the walking surfaces and accessible routes at The RiverFront? +

The three-park district uses gradually or gently sloping hard-surface paths, promenades, plazas, crosswalks, and pedestrian bridges, with signs identifying accessible routes to major attractions and restrooms. A designated trailway links Gene Leahy Mall, Heartland of America Park, and Lewis & Clark Landing; lawns, sandy beach, prairie planting, play surfacing, and water-feature edges differ from that route. Wheelchairs and mobility devices are supported throughout, while bicycles and scooters may be ridden only on identified trails rather than interior pedestrian paths.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Where can I picnic at The RiverFront in Omaha? +

Picnic tables, fixed benches, companion seating, lawns, and shaded or covered gathering areas are distributed across Gene Leahy Mall, Heartland of America Park, and Lewis & Clark Landing. Choose the specific park nearest the playground, lake, river, or activity you want rather than using one general pin. Keep food out of water features, leave paths and play areas clear, and check the daily event schedule before relying on a lawn or pavilion for a group. Contact The RiverFront for organized or exclusive use.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
The RiverFront ↗
Which part of Omaha's RiverFront should I visit? +

The RiverFront is three connected downtown parks, not one plaza. Gene Leahy Mall is the central urban-play and event destination; Heartland of America Park centers on its lake, walking paths, and open landscapes; Lewis & Clark Landing reaches the Missouri River beside the Kiewit Luminarium and riverfront attractions. Choose the component park or exact feature before navigating, and check the event calendar because programs can change lawn, pavilion, and parking access.
