What people say

The Rink at Campus Martius Park is Downtown Detroit Partnership's seasonal outdoor ice rink in the center of Campus Martius. The rink is closed for the season as of August 2026. Do not reuse last winter's hours or prices for 2026/2027; wait for the operator's next-season announcement.

Where exactly is The Rink at Campus Martius? +

Use the exact seasonal rink pin in the center of Campus Martius Park at Woodward and Michigan avenues. The rink replaces the warm-season central setup and is not present year-round.

Is The Rink at Campus Martius free? +

The concluded 2025/2026 season charged $12 for adults ages 13-58, $9 for children 12 and under and seniors 59+, $8 for active military and first responders, and $6 for skate rental. Treat those prices as expired until the next season is posted.

How accessible is The Rink at Campus Martius? +

The rink occupies a paved downtown plaza, but ice access, rental fit, crowds and winter surfaces require individual planning. Contact the operator for current adaptive-skating or mobility arrangements.

Are dogs allowed at The Rink at Campus Martius? +

Do not bring ordinary pets onto the ice or into the controlled rink area. Follow posted park rules outside the rink; service-animal arrangements should be confirmed with the operator.
