When is Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park open? +

The park is open daily 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. William Davidson Sport House closes at 8:00 p.m., Delta Dental Play Garden closes at 9:00 p.m., and seasonal Water Wonderland is listed 11:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. when operating.

Where should I park for Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park? +

About 150 free on-street spaces line West Jefferson and Rosa Parks. Paid parking and restrooms are available at Bagley Mobility Hub, 1501 Wabash Street, via Southwest Greenway; Assembly Garage is another paid option. Follow signs and expect event changes.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Detroit Riverfront Conservancy ↗
What is the best entrance for Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park? +

The park spans West Jefferson between Eighth Street and Rosa Parks Boulevard. Walkers can arrive from downtown along the RiverWalk; Southwest Greenway connects from Bagley Mobility Hub, and each internal destination requires additional walking.

Detroit Riverfront Conservancy ↗
Are there restrooms at Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park? +

The park lists public restrooms, with additional facilities at Bagley Mobility Hub. Confirm same-day availability during events, maintenance or winter weather.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

Is Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park free? +

Park entry, play garden, water garden, Sport House courts, lawns and ordinary Conservancy programming are free. Parking, food and selected programs may cost separately; private rentals are not offered.

How accessible is Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park? +

New paved routes connect major features, with inclusive play and seating distributed through the site. The Water Garden includes winding paths, a dock and bridge; visitors should use the exact route and avoid assuming every landscape surface is equivalent.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Detroit Riverfront Conservancy ↗
Are dogs allowed at Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park? +

Dogs and cats are allowed when leashed and under control. Keep pets out of active play, water-play and sport surfaces and away from the Water Garden shoreline.

Is Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park good for children? +

Delta Dental Play Garden, seasonal Water Wonderland and the open-air Sport House make this a strong free family destination, but each feature closes earlier than the surrounding park or operates seasonally.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Detroit Riverfront Conservancy ↗
How do I reach Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park without a car? +

DDOT routes 1, 27 and 29 serve the area. Southwest Greenway connects from Bagley Mobility Hub, and the Water Square People Mover station is about a half-mile east along the RiverWalk.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Detroit Riverfront Conservancy ↗
What should I know before visiting Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park? +

This park opened in October 2025, so feature schedules and programming remain fast-changing. Verify Water Wonderland operation, Sport House hours, weather, parking and the event calendar before a feature-specific trip; fishing is prohibited in the Water Garden.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Detroit Riverfront Conservancy ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park? +

Check Detroit weather, air quality, river conditions and operator alerts. Heat, snow, ice, storms, high water and seasonal operations can close paths or facilities independently.
