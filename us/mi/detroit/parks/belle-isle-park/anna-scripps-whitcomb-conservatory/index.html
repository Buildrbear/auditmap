What people say

Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory is Belle Isle's restored glass-domed botanical conservatory and formal garden complex beside the aquarium. The conservatory is a staffed attraction, not an all-day extension of the park grounds. Arrive before 5:00 p.m., confirm holiday or event notices, and do not expect the aquarium to be open on Wednesday.

Where exactly is Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory? +

Use the exact conservatory building pin at the Aquarium/Conservatory complex on Inselruhe Avenue. The two attractions share an arrival zone but have separate entrances and hours.

Where should I park for Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory? +

Park near the Aquarium/Conservatory complex. Motor vehicles need a Recreation Passport; Route 12 stops at the complex, and a rideshare drop-off is also listed near the aquarium and main entrance.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

When is Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory open? +

The conservatory is open Wednesday through Sunday, 10:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Holiday, event and maintenance notices can shorten that schedule.

Are there restrooms near Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory? +

The Conservancy does not identify a guaranteed restroom inside the conservatory and notes limited accessibility at some island bathrooms. Confirm the current facility before a visit that depends on one.

Is Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory free? +

Ordinary conservatory admission is free. A Recreation Passport is required for vehicle entry, and private events or professional photography require separate arrangements and fees.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

How accessible is Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory? +

Developed paths reach the conservatory complex, but the Conservancy notes that some garden, green-space and bathroom routes have limitations. Contact the operator for a mobility-specific route rather than assuming every garden path is step-free.

Are dogs allowed at Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory? +

Pets are not allowed in Michigan state park buildings except service animals. Keep leashed pets in permitted outdoor areas and out of the conservatory.

Is Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory good for children? +

The indoor plant houses and adjacent aquarium make a useful weather-flexible pair, but children should remain on paths and adults should compare the two separate weekly schedules before arrival.

How do you reach Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory? +

DDOT Route 12 Conant serves the island approximately hourly and stops at the Aquarium/Conservatory complex. Confirm the current weekday or Sunday schedule and the final walk to other attractions.
