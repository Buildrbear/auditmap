Where should I park for Belle Isle Giant Slide? +

Parking is distributed across the island. A Michigan Recreation Passport is required for motor vehicles, while pedestrians, bicycles and public-transit riders enter without one. Route to the exact attraction.

Are there restrooms near Belle Isle Giant Slide? +

Seasonal modern restrooms and portable toilets are distributed around the island; heated Bus Stop, Casino-area and Flynn facilities provide selected year-round options.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Is Belle Isle Giant Slide free? +

Park entry on foot, bicycle or transit is free. Motor vehicles require a Recreation Passport. Aquarium and conservatory are generally free; Dossin Museum, Giant Slide, rentals, golf and reservations have separate fees.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Michigan Department of Natural Resources ↗
How accessible is Belle Isle Giant Slide? +

Accessible parking, ramps, fishing piers, kayak launches and a rubber-surface playground are available at selected locations. Distances are large and temporary construction changes routes.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Belle Isle Giant Slide? +

Leashed pets are allowed in ordinary outdoor areas under DNR rules but restricted from buildings, designated swim areas and posted facilities.
