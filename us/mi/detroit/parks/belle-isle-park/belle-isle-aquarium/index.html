Belle Isle Aquarium

3 Inselruhe Avenue, Detroit, MI 48207
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The aquarium is open Thursday through Sunday, 10:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m. It remains open during its current renovation, but construction or holiday notices can alter access.

What people say

Belle Isle Aquarium is the historic public aquarium beside the Anna Scripps Whitcomb Conservatory, with freshwater and marine exhibits inside its green-tiled gallery. Do not assume the aquarium follows the island's outdoor hours. It closes at 4:00 p.m., remains under renovation and can develop a wait on busy weekends; confirm the same-day notice before crossing the bridge.

Where exactly is Belle Isle Aquarium? +

Navigate to the named Aquarium/Conservatory complex on Inselruhe Avenue, shortly after entering Belle Isle. The aquarium and conservatory are adjacent but keep different weekly schedules.

Where should I park for Belle Isle Aquarium? +

Use the attraction parking near the Aquarium/Conservatory complex. A Recreation Passport is required for motor vehicles; pedestrians, bicyclists and Route 12 riders do not need one.

When is Belle Isle Aquarium open? +

The aquarium is open Thursday through Sunday, 10:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m. It remains open during its current renovation, but construction or holiday notices can alter access.

Are there restrooms near Belle Isle Aquarium? +

Belle Isle Conservancy does not promise a particular aquarium restroom in its visitor information and cautions that some island bathrooms have limited accessibility. Confirm facility access before relying on an indoor restroom.

Is Belle Isle Aquarium free? +

Aquarium admission is free. Driving onto Belle Isle requires a Recreation Passport; parking, food and other island activities can have separate costs.

How accessible is Belle Isle Aquarium? +

The aquarium is open during work intended to improve site drainage, HVAC and accessibility. Construction can change the entrance route, and the Conservancy documents limitations at some island bathrooms; contact the operator for a specific accommodation.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Belle Isle Aquarium? +

Michigan DNR prohibits pets in park buildings except service animals. Leashed pets may use permitted outdoor park areas but should not be brought into the aquarium.

Is Belle Isle Aquarium good for children? +

The compact fish exhibits make this a practical family stop, especially when paired with the adjacent conservatory. Verify both schedules because the conservatory is open Wednesday through Sunday while the aquarium starts Thursday.

How do you reach Belle Isle Aquarium? +

DDOT Route 12 Conant serves the island approximately hourly and stops at the Aquarium/Conservatory complex. Confirm the current weekday or Sunday schedule and the final walk to other attractions.
