Park directory

Current city pages

Belle Isle Park

Detroit

Park grounds generally follow Michigan state-park day-use hours, 8:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Attractions keep separate schedules; Oudolf Garden and selected outdoor landmarks are listed 5:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m.

Campus Martius Park

Detroit

The public space is generally accessible daily, while the Beach, Rink, Shop, restaurants and programmed events keep seasonal schedules and may close for weather or setup.

Dequindre Cut

Detroit

Open daily 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Seasonal Freight Yard, events and portable-restroom service use shorter schedules.

Detroit RiverWalk

Detroit

The RiverWalk, Conservancy parks and greenways are open daily 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Extended event hours and temporary closures may apply.

Hart Plaza

Detroit

Hart Plaza is open year-round under city park rules, generally 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m., but festivals, setup, security and private production zones can replace ordinary access.

Palmer Park

Detroit

City park hours are generally 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Splash play, nature programming, cabin access and events have seasonal or scheduled hours.

Ralph C. Wilson Jr. Centennial Park

Detroit

The park follows Detroit Riverfront Conservancy hours, daily 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Seasonal Water Wonderland is listed 11:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. when operating.

Rouge Park

Detroit

City park hours are generally 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Trails should be used in daylight; golf, camps and future recreation facilities keep separate schedules.
