Hudson River Park

353 West Street, New York, NY 10014, New York City
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open daily 6 AM-1 AM unless posted otherwise. Individual piers, rooftops, playgrounds, restrooms, concessions, and partner attractions have separate hours.

What people say

Hudson River Park spans four miles and 550 acres along Manhattan's west side, connecting piers, playgrounds, lawns, sports, beaches, food, science, and river access.

When is Hudson River Park open? +

Open daily 6 AM-1 AM unless posted otherwise. Individual piers, rooftops, playgrounds, restrooms, concessions, and partner attractions have separate hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
Where should I park for Hudson River Park? +

There is no single park lot and west-side parking is expensive. Transit, walking, biking, and choosing a cross street for the intended pier are usually best.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
What is the best entrance for Hudson River Park? +

Plan by pier number and cross street rather than using the general park address. The park extends from Tribeca to West 59th Street, so arriving at the wrong section can add miles.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
Are there restrooms at Hudson River Park? +

Accessible restrooms are distributed at Pier 25, Pier 40, Pier 45, Pier 51, Gansevoort Peninsula, Little Island, Pier 57, Chelsea Waterside, Pier 84, Pier 97, and other facilities; hours vary by location and season.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
Is Hudson River Park free? +

Park entry, public lawns, many playgrounds, and many programs are free. Commercial attractions, food, rentals, and some activities charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
How accessible is Hudson River Park? +

Major park paths and public restrooms are accessible, but some piers and partner spaces have distinct routes, elevators, and schedules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
Are dogs allowed at Hudson River Park? +

Leashed dogs are permitted on designated paths and dog runs are available, but dogs are excluded from lawns, playgrounds, beaches, and some piers or partner spaces.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
Is Hudson River Park good for children? +

Pier 25, Pier 51, Chelsea Waterside, Gansevoort Peninsula, Pier 57, Little Island, sports fields, and river ecology programs provide varied family options.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
How do I reach Hudson River Park without a car? +

Multiple subway lines and crosstown buses reach neighborhoods east of the park. Expect an additional walk west to the river after leaving transit.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
What should I know before visiting Hudson River Park? +

The adjacent Hudson River Greenway bikeway is not part of the park and moves quickly; cross it carefully at marked locations. Pier-specific rules and restroom hours should be checked before a long visit.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Hudson River Park Trust ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Hudson River Park? +

Check New York City's hourly forecast, heat index, rain, wind, air quality, and sunset. Waterfront, island, and elevated parks can feel windier and more exposed than nearby streets.
