Riverside Skate Park

Riverside Drive from West 59th to West 181st Streets, New York, NY, New York City
Hours ⌄

What people say

Riverside Skate Park is a dedicated wheel-sport area in the northern park corridor; surfaces, supervision, and seasonal operations should be checked before a special trip. Riverside Park is six miles long and split across elevations. Search by named playground, pier, or cross street instead of navigating to the park's center point.

Where should I park for Riverside Skate Park? +

There is no single park lot. Street parking is limited and entrances are separated by steep grades, rail lines, and the highway; arrive near the intended cross street.

Are there restrooms near Riverside Skate Park? +

Restrooms are distributed at Pier I, Classic, Elephant, River Run, Hippo and Dinosaur playgrounds, clay tennis courts, Ellington, Grant's Tomb area, and several northern playgrounds.

Is Riverside Skate Park free? +

Park entry, playgrounds, lawns, gardens, and most outdoor areas are free. Tennis permits, food, rentals, and some organized programs cost extra.

How accessible is Riverside Skate Park? +

The park's multiple levels and steep slopes make route selection important. Riverside Park South is broadly accessible, while some historic upper-park paths use stairs or steep ramps.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Riverside Park Conservancy and NYC Parks ↗
Are dogs allowed at Riverside Skate Park? +

Dogs must be leashed except in designated runs or posted off-leash areas. Quiet lawns, playgrounds, sports areas, and gardens have additional restrictions.
