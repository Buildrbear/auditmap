Brooklyn, New York City, NY
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Prospect Park is open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m. Playgrounds, restrooms, zoo, carousel, Lakeside, sports facilities, visitor services, concessions, and events use separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

The Marquis de Lafayette monument honors the French general who supported the American Revolution. Find it near the park's Ninth Street and Prospect Park West entrance, on the western perimeter. This is a free outdoor monument with no dedicated facilities. Enter from Prospect Park West rather than crossing the full park, and use the exact pin. Combine it with the Long Meadow, Bandshell, or Children's Corner depending on your route.

Which Marquis de Lafayette Monument entrance should I use? +

Choose the entrance nearest your destination: Grand Army Plaza for the north end, West 9th or 15th Street for Long Meadow and Picnic House, Ocean and Parkside for Lakeside, or Lincoln Road for the southeast. The park is too large for one useful arrival pin.

Is there parking inside Marquis de Lafayette Monument? +

There is no general central visitor lot. Limited destination-specific and perimeter street parking exists, but transit is usually more reliable. Choose a subway or bus stop nearest the feature you plan to visit.

Is Marquis de Lafayette Monument free? +

General park entry, paths, lawns, playgrounds, woodlands, lake views, and the seasonal Lakeside splash pad are free. Zoo, carousel, skating, boating, rentals, food, programs, and ticketed events charge separately.
