The Battery

State Street and Battery Place, New York, NY 10004, New York City
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The outdoor park is generally open daily 6 AM-1 AM. Battery Playscape, Castle Clinton, restrooms, SeaGlass Carousel, and ferry facilities keep shorter schedules.

What people say

The Battery is a 25-acre harbor park with perennial gardens, Battery Playscape, SeaGlass Carousel, Castle Clinton, lawns, monuments, and ferry connections.

Battery Playscape

Battery Playscape is a large flood-resilient playground with five ecology zones. It is free and generally open 7 AM-6 PM, shorter than the surrounding park.

When is The Battery open? +

The outdoor park is generally open daily 6 AM-1 AM. Battery Playscape, Castle Clinton, restrooms, SeaGlass Carousel, and ferry facilities keep shorter schedules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Where should I park for The Battery? +

There is no park parking. Lower Manhattan garages are costly and street parking is extremely limited; subway or ferry connections are the practical choice.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
The Battery Conservancy and NYC Parks ↗
What is the best entrance for The Battery? +

Bowling Green serves the north edge, Whitehall serves the Staten Island Ferry side, and Battery Maritime Building is east of the park. Statue of Liberty ferries depart near Castle Clinton.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
The Battery Conservancy and NYC Parks ↗
Are there restrooms at The Battery? +

Public restrooms are outside the restaurant, at Castle Clinton, and near the Battery Bikeway on Battery Place, generally 9 AM-4:30 PM; ferry terminals have separate facilities.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
The Battery Conservancy and NYC Parks ↗
Is The Battery free? +

Park entry, gardens, Playscape, and Castle Clinton entry are free. SeaGlass rides cost $6 and Statue Cruises tickets are separate.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
The Battery Conservancy and NYC Parks ↗
How accessible is The Battery? +

Main promenades and Playscape routes are accessible. Ferry security, historic structures, and carousel boarding have their own procedures.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are dogs allowed at The Battery? +

Leashed dogs are allowed on designated paths but not in playgrounds or planted areas. Follow posted rules near ferry queues and monuments.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Is The Battery good for children? +

Battery Playscape, SeaGlass Carousel, harbor views, lawns, gardens, and ferries make a strong family stop, but attraction hours do not match the full park day.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

How do I reach The Battery without a car? +

Use 1 at South Ferry, R/W at Whitehall, 4/5 at Bowling Green, or nearby J/Z. The Staten Island Ferry terminal and Governors Island ferry are close but separate.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
The Battery Conservancy and NYC Parks ↗
What should I know before visiting The Battery? +

Statue of Liberty ferry tickets and security are not operated by the park; use the authorized operator and arrive early. Carousel hours are seasonal and weather-sensitive.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
The Battery Conservancy and NYC Parks ↗
What weather should I check before visiting The Battery? +

Check New York City's hourly forecast, heat index, rain, wind, air quality, and sunset. Waterfront, island, and elevated parks can feel windier and more exposed than nearby streets.
