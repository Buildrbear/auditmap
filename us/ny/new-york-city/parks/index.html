Current city pages

Brooklyn Bridge Park

New York City

Open daily 6 AM-1 AM. Playgrounds are sunrise-sunset and individual piers, restrooms, Water Lab, sports, and seasonal attractions keep shorter hours.

Bryant Park

New York City

May-September: daily 7 AM-11 PM. October and January-April: 7 AM-10 PM. November-December hours extend to midnight Thursday-Sunday.

Central Park

New York City

Choose the entrance nearest your destination: 59th Street for the Pond and Heckscher area, 72nd Street for Bethesda Terrace and Strawberry Fields, 79th Street for Belvedere and the Ramble, or the north end for Harlem Meer and the Davis Center. Do not route to the park center.

Flushing Meadows Corona Park

New York City

Open daily 6 AM-1 AM; museums, recreation centers, sports venues, zoo, and playground facilities have separate schedules.

Governors Island

New York City

Open daily year-round. Current hours are 7 AM-10 PM Sunday-Thursday and 7 AM-11 PM Friday-Saturday; South Island Park closes at dusk.

Hudson River Park

New York City

Open daily 6 AM-1 AM unless posted otherwise. Individual piers, rooftops, playgrounds, restrooms, concessions, and partner attractions have separate hours.

Prospect Park

New York City

Open daily 6 AM-1 AM; individual attractions and facilities keep separate hours.

Riverside Park

New York City

Open daily 6 AM-1 AM. Playgrounds, cafés, courts, restrooms, and programmed areas have shorter or seasonal hours.

The Battery

New York City

The outdoor park is generally open daily 6 AM-1 AM. Battery Playscape, Castle Clinton, restrooms, SeaGlass Carousel, and ferry facilities keep shorter schedules.

The High Line

New York City

Summer hours are generally daily 7 AM-11 PM; closing times change seasonally and weather or maintenance can close sections.
