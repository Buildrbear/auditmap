The Lake, Bow Bridge & Loeb Boathouse

Central Park, New York City, NY
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Central Park is open daily 6 a.m.-1 a.m.; this destination may have shorter or seasonal hours.
Navigate

What people say

The Lake links rowboat views, Bow Bridge, the Ramble shoreline, Cherry Hill, and the Loeb Boathouse dining and boating area in Central Park's busiest scenic cluster. Use West 72nd Street for Bow Bridge and Cherry Hill or East 72nd to 74th Street for the Boathouse. Choose the side for your first stop rather than crossing the Ramble by accident. Rowboats, gondola service, dining, and concessions use separate seasonal schedules and fees. Bow Bridge is pedestrian-only and extremely crowded at peak photo times.
