destination

Bryant Park Lawn

Between 40th and 42nd Streets and Fifth and Sixth Avenues, New York, NY 10018, New York City
Hours ⌄
Published schedule May-September: daily 7 AM-11 PM. October and January-April: 7 AM-10 PM. November-December hours extend to midnight Thursday-Sunday.
Navigate

What people say

The central lawn is open when turf and event conditions allow. A closed lawn does not mean the surrounding promenades, chairs, food, or activities are closed. The lawn is not guaranteed to be open every day because rain, turf care, and large events require closures. Check the daily lawn message and event calendar.

Where should I park for Bryant Park Lawn? +

There is no park parking and Midtown driving is slow and expensive. Subway, bus, walking, or nearby commercial garages are the realistic options.

Are there restrooms near Bryant Park Lawn? +

Staffed public restrooms are on the 42nd Street and 40th Street sides, with changing facilities and accessible fixtures. Summer hours generally extend to 11 PM but maintenance can shorten them.

Is Bryant Park Lawn free? +

Park entry, chairs, lawn access, reading room, and many games and events are free. Carousel, food, rentals, and some seasonal services charge.

How accessible is Bryant Park Lawn? +

Level perimeter entrances and paved promenades make most areas accessible. The lawn can close after rain or for event setup.

Are dogs allowed at Bryant Park Lawn? +

Leashed dogs are permitted on paved areas but not on the central lawn. Owners must follow posted event and seasonal restrictions.
