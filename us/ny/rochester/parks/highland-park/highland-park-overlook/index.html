What people say

Highland Park Overlook is a hillside viewpoint within the Olmsted-designed arboretum, reflecting the park plan's use of glacial terrain and elevated views. Use the exact pin within the park's upper hillside and reservoir area, reached from the Reservoir Avenue side rather than routing to the Lilac Arches. This is an outdoor viewpoint, not a staffed observation deck. Trees, foliage, weather, and ongoing reservoir planning can change the view and approach. Do not confuse it with Cobb's Hill Reservoir overlook, which is a different Rochester park and is generally known for the broader skyline view. Expect slopes and ordinary park paths, and check local notices before relying on a specific accessible route.

What address should I use for Highland Park Overlook Rochester? +

Use 171 Reservoir Avenue, Rochester, NY 14620 for the administrative and central park area. Navigate directly to Lamberton Conservatory, Warner Castle, Highland Bowl, Lily Pond, Lilac Arches, playgrounds, or Olmsted Lodge for the closest approach.

Is Highland Park Overlook free? +

General park, arboretum, gardens, paths, playgrounds, memorials, and ordinary recreation are free. Lamberton Conservatory admission is $4 for adults, $2 for youth ages 6-18 and seniors 62+, and free for children five and under.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

What is accessible at Highland Park Overlook? +

The memorial area now called Beikirch Park is specifically identified as accessible. Accessibility elsewhere varies among paved garden paths, sloped lawns, historic structures, natural surfaces, and event detours; contact the county for a required route.
