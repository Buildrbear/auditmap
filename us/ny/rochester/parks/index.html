Genesee Valley Park

Rochester

The park has practical entrances from Elmwood Avenue at Wilson Boulevard, East River Road, and Crittenden Road. Use Elmwood for the sports complex and central park, Crittenden for several ball fields, or East River Road for soccer, cricket, and additional fields.

Highland Park

Rochester

Use 171 Reservoir Avenue, Rochester, NY 14620 for the administrative and central park area. Navigate directly to Lamberton Conservatory, Warner Castle, Highland Bowl, Lily Pond, Lilac Arches, playgrounds, or Olmsted Lodge for the closest approach.
