Park

Corning Preserve

10 Quay Street, Albany, NY 12207
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Corning Preserve follows Albany park hours: sunrise to 11:00 p.m. from May through October and sunrise to 10:00 p.m. from November through April. Restrooms, docks, boat launch, amphitheater events, and garage access may have separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

Corning Preserve and Jennings Landing form Albany's downtown Hudson River park, with paved walking and biking routes, Empire State Trail connection, playground, picnic tables, fishing, public boat launch and docks, a 1,000-seat amphitheater, and seasonal events.

What address should I use for Corning Preserve? +

Use 10 Quay Street for Corning Riverfront Park and Jennings Landing, or route to the Corning Preserve Public Boat Launch near the Colonie Street and I-787 Exit 4A area when launching a boat. The riverfront is linear, so the exact destination matters.

Where should I park for Corning Preserve and Jennings Landing? +

The Riverfront Garage is the most predictable downtown option and is open 24 hours; use the pedestrian connection toward Jennings Landing and the Preserve. Event pricing can apply, and Quay Street curb space should not be assumed to be legal visitor parking.

Is the Corning Preserve trail paved? +

Yes. A paved, shared walking and biking route follows the Hudson and forms part of the Mohawk-Hudson Bike-Hike and Empire State Trail network. Event areas, docks, and side paths may differ from the main paved route.

Is there a playground at Corning Preserve? +

Yes. Albany's park inventory lists a playground at Corning Riverfront Park and Jennings Landing, along with picnic tables, fishing, biking, walking, and restrooms.

Are there restrooms at Jennings Landing? +

Albany's park inventory lists restrooms at Corning Riverfront and Jennings Landing. Historical event guidance ties staffed restroom availability to approximately mid-May through October 1, so confirm seasonal status before relying on them.

Are dogs allowed at Corning Preserve? +

Leashed dogs are welcome on the paved riverfront trail. Keep pets controlled around the playground, events, docks, cyclists, and crowded amphitheater areas, and follow temporary event rules.

Concerts and festivals at Jennings Landing, permitted runs, river flooding, boat-launch work, and trail or bridge projects can affect parking and paths. Check City event and construction notices when access is time-sensitive.

Albany parks are open from sunrise to 11:00 p.m. May through October and sunrise to 10:00 p.m. November through April. Restrooms, docks, launch access, concerts, and the Riverfront Garage each follow their own operating conditions.

Can I reach Corning Preserve without driving? +

Yes. Downtown pedestrians can use the Hudson River Way connection from the Broadway and Clinton Avenue area toward Jennings Landing. The paved Empire State Trail also reaches the preserve from north and south; event closures may alter the final approach.

Can I picnic at Corning Preserve? +

Yes. Albany lists picnic tables at Corning Riverfront Park and Jennings Landing. Casual use is free, but organized gatherings, walks, runs, amphitheater use, vendors, or exclusive areas require a City special-event permit.

Are accessible restrooms available at Corning Preserve? +

Albany's current portable-restroom plan provides accessible units at both Corning Preserve Boat Launch and Jennings Landing. Placement and service can change around events or flooding, so check posted conditions before relying on a specific unit.

Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Corning Preserve? +

Scattered trees provide limited shade near parts of the path and picnic landscape, but Jennings Landing, the amphitheater seating, playground, boat launch, docks, and Hudson River trail are broadly exposed. The preserve has no dependable general-purpose indoor visitor building; seasonal restrooms and event infrastructure do not provide storm refuge. During lightning, flooding, high wind, or dangerous heat or cold, leave the riverfront and use a confirmed-open downtown building or enclosed vehicle; event organizers sometimes relocate programming under the I-787 structure, but visitors should not treat an underpass as severe-weather shelter.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Is there a splash pad or swimming area at Corning Preserve? +

No splash pad, pool, or designated swimming area is listed at Corning Preserve. The Hudson River frontage supports walking, bicycling, events, docks, rowing, and boat launching, but the shoreline is not presented as a supervised family swim beach. Keep children back from the river edge, use life jackets for boating, and choose one of Albany's separately listed spray pads or pools for water play.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Albany ↗
What are the main landmarks at Corning Preserve? +

Jennings Landing and its riverfront amphitheater are the preserve's main gathering landmark. From downtown, the Hudson River Way pedestrian bridge links Broadway directly to Jennings Landing; the Albany Skyway provides another elevated connection into the riverfront district. Follow the waterfront path north for the playground and boat-launch area. This is primarily a river, trail, and event landscape rather than a concentrated public-art park.

How much does Corning Preserve cost? +

General riverfront, trail, playground, picnic, and boat-launch access is free; garage parking, events, permits, and programs may charge separately.
