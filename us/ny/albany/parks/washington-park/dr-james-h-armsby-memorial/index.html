Dr. James H. Armsby Memorial

City of Albany, NY
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Washington Park is open sunrise-11:00 p.m. from May through October and sunrise-10:00 p.m. from November through April. Lake House, restrooms, playground, courts, skating, and events use separate schedules.

What people say

The Dr. James H. Armsby Memorial is an 1879 bust honoring a co-founder of Albany Medical College and was the first memorial installed in Washington Park. Use the exact AuditMap pin together with the Washington Park Conservancy monument guide; this outdoor bust has no separate entrance or street address. The memorial is free to view during park hours and has no indoor exhibit, admission desk, or dedicated restroom. Treat it as one stop on a monument walk and use the Lake House for public restrooms when open. Dogs must remain leashed, and weekday parking is generally limited to 90 minutes from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., with special-event exceptions.

Where are restrooms in Dr. James H. Armsby Memorial? +

Public restrooms are in the Lake House. Their availability can follow building, maintenance, event, or seasonal schedules, and the Lake House is more than 600 feet from the playground.
