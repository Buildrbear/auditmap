Buffalo AKG Art Museum

North Buffalo, NY
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Delaware Park is open for general public use under posted City of Buffalo park hours. Golf, Hoyt Lake boating, Marcy Casino and Terrace, Parkside Lodge, sports, seasonal bathrooms, events, and rentals keep separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

Buffalo AKG Art Museum is a separately operated modern and contemporary art campus beside Delaware Park, spanning the Knox, Wilmers, and Gundlach buildings. Route to 1285 Elmwood Avenue, adjacent to Delaware Park; vehicle drop-off is at the Knox Building's west entrance from the museum driveway. The collection galleries require museum admission and the museum is closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays; allow about two hours for a first visit. The underground garage is paid, while marked street parking may be available nearby. All galleries and entrances are wheelchair accessible, wheelchairs and walkers are free first-come loans, and a personal care attendant receives complimentary admission with the visitor's ticket.
