What hours is Delaware Park in Buffalo open? +

Delaware Park opens at sunrise and closes at 10:00 p.m. Individual restaurants, restrooms, golf, boats, shelters, museums, the zoo, events, and reserved facilities follow their own schedules and can close earlier.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 8, 2026
City of Buffalo Parks and Recreation ↗
How much does Delaware Park cost? +

General outdoor park access is free; individual attractions, parking, rentals, reservations, and programs may charge separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 8, 2026
OpenStreetMap contributors ↗
How accessible is Delaware Park? +

Accessibility varies by entrance, route, terrain, and destination. Review the exact mapped subsite and its official source before relying on a specific accessible route.
