Where should I park for Civil War Memorial? +

Use legal street parking around the park and read meters, street-sweeping, residential, loading, and event restrictions carefully. Large events and the Saturday market can make the closest blocks impractical.

Are there restrooms near Civil War Memorial? +

Restroom availability varies by facility and event. Do not assume a restroom beside the fountain is continuously open; identify the nearest staffed or event facility before a long visit.

Does it cost money to visit Civil War Memorial? +

Ordinary park, fountain, lawn, path, playground, and splash-pad access is free. Parking, food, markets, permitted events, rentals, and programs may cost.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 8, 2026

How accessible is Civil War Memorial? +

Broad paved walks connect major areas, and the city is planning additional accessibility improvements. Historic paving, roots, lawns, crowds, wet splash-pad surfaces, and temporary event layouts vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 8, 2026
City of Savannah Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Civil War Memorial? +

Leashed dogs may use ordinary outdoor areas under posted city rules. Keep pets out of splash-pad and playground activity, respect event restrictions, and clean up immediately.

Is Civil War Memorial good for children? +

The playground, splash pad, fountain, lawns, shaded paths, market activity, and open space offer several visit styles. Keep children close around streets, crowds, monuments, and wet surfaces.
