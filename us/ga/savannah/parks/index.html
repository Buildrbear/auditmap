Current city pages

Bonaventure Cemetery

Savannah

Open to the public daily 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Registered after-hours activities may be approved between 6:00-8:00 a.m. or 5:00-8:00 p.m.; ordinary visitors should not rely on those extended periods.

Daffin Park

Savannah

Use 1 Waring Drive for the general park, 1301 East Victory Drive for the public pool, or 1401 East Victory Drive for Grayson Stadium. The park spans Victory Drive, Bee Road, Washington Avenue, and Waters Avenue, so route to the exact facility.

Forsyth Park

Savannah

The City does not publish one dependable gate schedule for every outdoor area on the referenced park material. Treat playground, splash pad, bandshell, market, courts, restrooms, and permitted events as separately scheduled and verify current notices.

Skidaway Island State Park

Savannah

Park grounds and buildings may keep different schedules; confirm the current gate and visitor-center hours before leaving. The Avian Trail and observation tower are currently closed because of bridge damage, and a partial campground renovation is scheduled through early 2027.
