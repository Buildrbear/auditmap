Navigate

What people say

The grand overlook frames the protected reservoir and city skyline; it is a viewpoint, not a water-access area. The reservoir is protected drinking-water infrastructure: swimming, boating, shoreline access, and climbing barriers are prohibited. Bring water and sun protection because long paved sections are exposed.

Use the free public lots at the Johnson Road or Grove Park entrances. Lots can fill for sunsets, weekends, and events, and the playground is a walk from parking.

How accessible is Grand overlook and reservoir view? +

Wide paved paths serve major areas, but the park is very large and includes long grades. Visitors with limited stamina should route to the closest entrance and expect distance between parking, playground, and overlooks.
