North Avenue entrance and promenade

680 Dallas Street NE, Atlanta, GA 30308
Hours ⌄
Published schedule City park hours are generally 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. The splash pad is listed for 10:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m. from May 1 through October 1, subject to weather, maintenance, and seasonal changes.
Navigate

What people say

The North Avenue side is a practical arrival from Ponce City Market and the BeltLine, with a paved promenade and busy street crossings nearby. The lake is stormwater infrastructure, not a swimming pond. The park's upper and lower areas feel separate, so route directly to the playground or skatepark rather than using only the general park pin.
