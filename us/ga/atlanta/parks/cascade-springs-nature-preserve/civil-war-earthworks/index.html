Hours ⌄
Published schedule The preserve is open daily during city park hours, but the parking gate may close much earlier than the park; current visitor guidance has listed 3:00 p.m. weekdays and 7:30 p.m. weekends for the vehicle gate.

What people say

Historic earthworks remain in the forest; protect the site by staying on established paths. Rocks near Cascade Falls and the creek can be slick, and water can move quickly after rain. Wear traction, avoid climbing wet rocks, and note the parking-gate closing time before leaving the lot.

Where should I park for Civil War earthworks? +

Use the free lot at 2852 Cascade Road and read the gate-closing sign before hiking. A late return can leave a vehicle behind a locked gate.

How accessible is Civil War earthworks? +

The entrance area and some boardwalk portions are improved, but natural trails include roots, mud, slopes, creek edges, and potentially slippery surfaces; not every landmark has a step-free route.

Are dogs allowed at Civil War earthworks? +

Leashed dogs are welcome; keep them out of waterways and under control around wildlife and narrow trails.

A memorable short nature outing for children who can manage uneven trails, but close supervision is essential around slippery rocks, water, and drop-offs.

What should I know before visiting Civil War earthworks? +

Historic earthworks remain in the forest; protect the site by staying on established paths. Rocks near Cascade Falls and the creek can be slick, and water can move quickly after rain. Wear traction, avoid climbing wet rocks, and note the parking-gate closing time before leaving the lot.
