Current city pages

Atlanta Beltline Eastside Trail

Atlanta

The trail follows City of Atlanta park hours, generally 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Adjacent businesses, parks, restrooms, and access gates use their own schedules.

Cascade Springs Nature Preserve

Atlanta

The preserve is open daily during city park hours, but the parking gate may close much earlier than the park; current visitor guidance has listed 3:00 p.m. weekdays and 7:30 p.m. weekends for the vehicle gate.

Centennial Olympic Park

Atlanta

Open daily 7:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Fountain of Rings shows are scheduled daily at noon, 2:30 p.m., 6:30 p.m., and 9:00 p.m., subject to events, weather, and maintenance.

Chastain Memorial Park

Atlanta

General city park hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. The amphitheater may operate until 1:00 a.m. on performance nights; pool, tennis, golf, arts, and other facilities have separate schedules.

Freedom Park

Atlanta

General city park hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Adjacent institutions, restrooms, events, and parking lots have separate schedules.

Grant Park

Atlanta

General city park hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. The recreation center, seasonal outdoor pool, Zoo Atlanta, fields, and reserved facilities use separate schedules.

Historic Fourth Ward Park

Atlanta

City park hours are generally 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. The splash pad is listed for 10:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m. from May 1 through October 1, subject to weather, maintenance, and seasonal changes.

Perkerson Park

Atlanta

General city park hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Splash pad, fields, courts, reservations, and programs use seasonal or separate schedules.

Piedmont Park

Atlanta

Open daily 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Restrooms generally operate 8:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m.; pool, splash pad, courts, concessions, events, and other facilities use separate schedules.

Shirley Clarke Franklin Park (Westside Park)

Atlanta

General city park hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; gates, parking lots, reservoir overlooks, and reserved areas may close earlier or for operations.
