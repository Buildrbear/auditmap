Piedmont Park Aquatic Center and Pool

1320, Atlanta, GA
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The park is open daily 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Public restrooms generally operate 8:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m.; the pool, splash pad, dog parks, courts, concessions, offices, and events use separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

Piedmont Park Aquatic Center is a seasonal outdoor pool with lap swimming, family recreation, a children's splash area, and an accessible beach-style entry. The pool is in the Greystone complex near the park's northeast side; use the Monroe Drive parking garage and lowest-level park entrance. Pool admission, operating dates, sessions, and lap-swim schedules are separate from free park access and change seasonally. Check the current pool calendar before visiting. The facility includes an accessible entry and recently added lift, but ask staff about the available water-access method. Outside food, flotation devices, and age supervision follow posted aquatic rules.

Where should I park for Piedmont Park Aquatic Center and Pool? +

The shared Atlanta Botanical Garden and Piedmont Park garage is at 1322 Monroe Drive. It is cashless, time-priced, and the first 30 minutes are free; use the lowest floor for park access. Event days can change rates and availability.

Can I take MARTA to Piedmont Park Aquatic Center and Pool? +

Yes. Midtown and Arts Center MARTA stations are the usual rail approaches, followed by a walk or local bus connection. The Atlanta BeltLine also enters the park from the east. Choose the entrance nearest your destination because the park is large.

Where are bathrooms in Piedmont Park Aquatic Center and Pool? +

Restrooms are near Greystone, Noguchi Playscape, Legacy Fountain, the tennis center, dog park, Charles Allen Gate, and behind Magnolia Hall. The Conservancy lists general restroom hours as 8 a.m.-6 p.m. daily, subject to maintenance and events.

How much does Piedmont Park Aquatic Center and Pool cost? +

General park, trails, playground, dog parks, lawns, Lake Clara Meer, and Legacy Fountain splash pad access are free. The pool, parking garage, rentals, food, programs, and many events charge separately.
