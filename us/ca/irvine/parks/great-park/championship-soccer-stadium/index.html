What people say

Championship Soccer Stadium is the ticketed match venue inside Great Park used by Orange County SC, with a west-side main entrance, concourse concessions and restrooms, family facilities, accessible seating, and a matchday Fan Zone. Use the west-side match entrance and arrive early for screening. The Stadium is cashless, restricts bags to approved clear bags under 14 by 14 by 6 inches or small clutches, does not provide bag check, and permits only service animals. One sealed plastic water bottle per person is allowed; outside food, coolers, umbrellas, and many other items are prohibited.

Where should I park for Championship Soccer Stadium? +

Free Great Park parking is available first come, first served. Orange County SC recommends Lots 2, 4, and 5; accessible parking is in Lots 4 and 5, oversized vehicles should use Lot 2, and current construction or event traffic can change the approach.

When is Championship Soccer Stadium open? +

The Stadium opens for scheduled matches and events rather than following Great Park's general hours. For Orange County SC matches, public gates and the ticket office open one hour before kickoff; confirm the event schedule before leaving.

Are there restrooms near Championship Soccer Stadium? +

Restrooms are on the stadium concourses, with men's facilities near Sections 6 and 10, women's and family restrooms near Sections 2 and 12, accessible fixtures, and diaper-changing stations in all public restrooms.

What fees apply at Championship Soccer Stadium? +

Admission depends on the scheduled match or event and selected seat. Children age 3 or younger do not need a ticket when seated on an adult's lap in a reserved area; other visitors need the ticket required for that event.

How accessible is Championship Soccer Stadium? +

Wheelchair, semi-ambulatory, and companion seating is available across multiple sections and price levels. Accessible parking is in Great Park Lots 4 and 5; contact the ticket office before the match when a seating exchange or accommodation is needed.

Are dogs allowed at Championship Soccer Stadium? +

Only service animals required because of a disability are allowed inside Championship Soccer Stadium.

Is Championship Soccer Stadium useful for a family visit? +

Orange County SC matchdays include family restrooms, diaper-changing stations, and a weather-dependent Fan Zone with soccer activities, lawn games, and face painting. Children age 4 and older need a ticket.
