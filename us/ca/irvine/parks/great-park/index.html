Great Park

8000 Great Park Boulevard, Irvine, CA 92618
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Great Park attractions and facilities use separate schedules. The Balloon and Carousel currently operate Thursday through Sunday, while sports fields, playgrounds, arts spaces, gardens, events, and leased attractions may have different hours.

What people say

Great Park is Irvine's evolving 1,300-acre destination on the former Marine Corps Air Station El Toro, with the tethered Great Park Balloon, free Carousel, Kids Rock Playground, Sports Complex and children's play area, Palm Court Arts Complex, Farm + Food Lab, walking and fitness routes, lawns, historic aviation exhibits, events, and extensive parking.

Great Park Balloon

The Great Park Balloon is Irvine's tethered helium observation balloon, rising as high as 400 feet above the park for a weather-dependent panoramic ride near the Visitors Center and Carousel.

Championship Soccer Stadium

What address should I use for Great Park? +

Use 8000 Great Park Boulevard, Irvine, CA 92618 as the general destination, then follow the current map to your named attraction or sports field. The campus is very large and has several entry roads and parking lots.

Where should I park at Great Park? +

Parking is free in the general Great Park lots. Use Lot 3 for the Balloon and follow the official map for the Sports Complex, playgrounds, arts campus, gardens, ice facility, or event venue; tournaments and concerts can change traffic patterns.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

How much is the Great Park Balloon? +

Balloon rides are $10 for adults and free for children. Flights are first come, first served, require same-day check-in and waiver, can fill quickly, and are never guaranteed because wind and weather control operation.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Irvine ↗
When does the Great Park Balloon operate? +

Current regular sessions are Thursday-Friday 10 a.m.-3 p.m. and 7-10 p.m., and Saturday-Sunday 9 a.m.-3 p.m. and 7-10 p.m.; it is closed Monday-Wednesday. Holiday hours and weather can change operation.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Irvine ↗
What playgrounds are at Great Park? +

Kids Rock near the Balloon uses climbing rocks, tunnels, misting shade structures, and water-cycle play themes. A separate Sports Complex play area has slides, swings, climbing equipment, ropes, and open space near the athletic fields.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

What can I do at Great Park besides sports? +

Visit the Balloon and free Carousel, Palm Court Arts Complex, historic aircraft and military-base exhibits, Farm + Food Lab, Giving Grove, Terraced Lawn, Kids Rock Playground, and scheduled cultural events.

Are there restrooms at Great Park? +

Yes. The Visitors Center near the Balloon and Carousel includes restrooms, and additional facilities serve the Sports Complex and other developed zones. Choose the closest destination before arriving because facilities are widely separated.

Will the Great Park Balloon fly today? +

Call the Visitors Center at 949-724-6247 for current flight status. Flights depend on weather, may stop without notice, and often reach capacity; completing a waiver does not reserve a ride.

Are there picnic areas at Irvine Great Park? +

Yes. The City's current park inventory lists four picnic areas with approximately 78 tables across Great Park. The site is very large, so choose the picnic area nearest your playground, sports field, arts complex, or event rather than navigating only to the park address.

What accessible facilities are at Great Park? +

Great Park has accessible parking and developed paths near major public facilities, while individual sports fields, playgrounds, arts spaces, leased attractions, and construction zones have destination-specific routes. Contact the Visitors Center when a particular mobility need depends on exact access.

Can I walk or bike around Irvine Great Park? +

Yes. Developed walking and fitness routes connect major public areas, but the 1,300-acre property remains under phased construction and not every district is continuously connected. Use the current Great Park map and follow detours rather than relying on a straight-line route.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Irvine ↗
Where can visitors find shade or weather shelter at Irvine Great Park? +

Palm Court Arts Complex has a shaded outdoor plaza beneath 54 palms, and Kids Rock Playground includes misting shade structures. Picnic areas, the Sports Complex, lawns, Balloon zone, and long walking connections can otherwise be exposed. The Visitors Center, Great Park Gallery, Hangar 244, and other buildings follow their own program hours and are not guaranteed refuge. During extreme heat, lightning, or dangerous wind, use an open substantial building or vehicle; Balloon flights stop when weather is unsafe.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Irvine ↗
Where can dogs go at Great Park, and what rules apply? +

Dogs may accompany visitors in ordinary Great Park outdoor areas when controlled on a leash no longer than 6 feet, and owners must remove waste. Great Park is not Irvine's off-leash dog park; Central Bark at 6405 Oak Canyon is the City's designated facility for off-leash exercise. Sports fields, playgrounds, buildings, shuttles, and major events may prohibit pets or limit access, including events that admit only service animals.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Irvine ↗
Is there water play at Great Park in Irvine? +

Yes. The Kids Rock children's play area includes water-play features and shaded misting structures, but it is not a swimming pool. Operation can be affected by maintenance, weather, and programming, so confirm the feature's status with Great Park before making water play the main purpose of a visit. Bring sun protection and a change of clothes; the surrounding Great Park campus is large and many other attractions are exposed.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Irvine ↗
Can I take a bus to Irvine Great Park? +

OCTA Route 90 provides the closest regular bus approach, with a stop near Irvine Center Drive and Hubble. Great Park is a very large campus, so this may still leave a substantial walk to the balloon, sports complex, Kids Rock, ice facility, or cultural terrace. Check OCTA's live planner and consider a rideshare connection from Irvine or Tustin station when the final distance is impractical.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
