Autry Museum of the American West

Los Angeles, Los Angeles, CA
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Griffith Park is open daily from 5:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. Griffith Observatory, Los Angeles Zoo, museums, Greek Theatre, trains, golf, pools, recreation centers, and other attractions use separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

The Autry Museum of the American West presents art, history, and cultures of the American West in a separately operated museum across from the Los Angeles Zoo. Route to 4700 Western Heritage Way on Griffith Park's northeast side. The museum is across Zoo Drive from the Los Angeles Zoo, not near Griffith Observatory. The museum is closed Mondays, charges admission, and has shorter hours than Griffith Park. Parking directly in front of the Autry is free for visitors. Large bags over 13 by 12 by 4 inches are not allowed, there are no lockers, and back-worn child carriers are prohibited. Wheelchairs, strollers, sensory kits, and assistive listening devices are available.

Where should I park for Griffith Observatory? +

Parking around the Observatory is paid and limited, with enforcement noon-10 p.m. weekdays and 10 a.m.-10 p.m. weekends. When the Greek Theatre has no show, its lot may provide free parking with a low-cost DASH ride uphill.

Can I take transit to Griffith Observatory? +

Yes. DASH Observatory/Los Feliz runs daily from 10 a.m.-10 p.m. every 15-20 minutes, connecting the Sunset/Vermont Metro station, Los Feliz, Greek Theatre, Mt. Hollywood trail access, and the Observatory lawn.

General park, trails, playgrounds, picnic areas, and Griffith Observatory building admission are free. Observatory parking and planetarium shows, Zoo, museums, golf, trains, concessions, Greek Theatre events, and reservations charge separately.
