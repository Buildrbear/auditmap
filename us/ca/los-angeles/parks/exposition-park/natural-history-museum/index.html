Natural History Museum of Los Angeles County

700 Exposition Park Drive, Los Angeles, CA 90037
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Buy or reserve tickets before busy weekends. Seasonal pavilions and special exhibitions can sell separately from general admission.
Navigate

What people say

A ticketed natural-history museum with dinosaurs, mammals, gems, cultural collections, seasonal butterfly and spider pavilions, and family programming. The main entrance is west of the Rose Garden; use NHM-specific signs from the parking areas or Metro. Buy or reserve tickets before busy weekends. Seasonal pavilions and special exhibitions can sell separately from general admission.
