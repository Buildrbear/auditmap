Navigate

What people say

A reservable community lodge with indoor gathering space, restrooms, and an adjacent group picnic area high in Elysian Park. Use 1025 Elysian Park Drive and do not route to the general Academy Road park address. The lodge and group area can be reserved or closed for private use; confirm access before treating it as a public facility stop.
