Venice Skatepark

1800 Ocean Front Walk, Venice, CA 90291, Los Angeles
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Helmets and protective gear are strongly advised. Keep non-skaters and small children outside the active riding area.
Navigate

What people say

A free oceanfront concrete skatepark with bowls and street elements, as visually compelling for spectators as it is demanding for skaters. Enter Ocean Front Walk near Windward Avenue and follow the beach path to the concrete bowls. Helmets and protective gear are strongly advised. Keep non-skaters and small children outside the active riding area.

Where should I park for Venice Skatepark? +

City and private paid lots line the beach, with metered and restricted neighborhood streets inland. Summer weekends and sunsets fill early; verify closing time before leaving a car in a beach lot.

Is Venice Skatepark free? +

Beach, boardwalk, skatepark, outdoor courts, pier, and general recreation access are free. Parking, rentals, food, programs, and Muscle Beach weight passes can charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

How accessible is Venice Skatepark? +

Paved Ocean Front Walk and the beach bike path are accessible, with designated beach access and parking at selected locations. Sand access requires the correct mobility route or equipment.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026
City of Los Angeles Department of Recreation and Parks ↗
Are dogs allowed at Venice Skatepark? +

Dogs are not allowed on the sand or most beach facilities except service animals; leashed dogs may use permitted boardwalk areas under posted city rules. Check signs at each zone.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026
City of Los Angeles Department of Recreation and Parks ↗
Is Venice Skatepark good for children? +

Use the quieter playground and beach areas for younger children, and supervise closely around the skatepark, bike path, surf, crowds, and performers. The boardwalk can be intense at peak times.

How do you reach Venice Skatepark? +

Metro and Big Blue Bus routes serve Venice and Main. Rideshare drop-off inland avoids beach-lot queues, but plan the final walk to the exact activity.
