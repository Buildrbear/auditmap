What people say

A 32-acre state park beside Chinatown with broad lawns, a mile-long walking route, downtown views, habitat gardens, railroad and Indigenous history, family programs, and Metro access.

When is Los Angeles State Historic Park open? +

Open daily 8 a.m. to sunset. Welcome Center, Cargo Snack Shack, programs, and events use separate schedules.

Where should I park for Los Angeles State Historic Park? +

The official lot is $2 per hour up to $8 daily. Free street parking exists nearby, but anti-gridlock restrictions apply from 4-6 p.m.; read every sign.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026
California State Parks ↗
What is the best entrance for Los Angeles State Historic Park? +

The main address is 1245 N. Spring Street; the paid lot entrance is at 1501 N. Spring. Chinatown station is adjacent, and Union Station is about one mile away.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

Are there restrooms at Los Angeles State Historic Park? +

Accessible public restrooms and drinking water are available in the developed park. Building and program hours may differ from park hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

Is Los Angeles State Historic Park free? +

Park entry is free. The official lot costs $2 per hour up to $8 daily; cafe purchases and special permitted activities cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

How accessible is Los Angeles State Historic Park? +

California State Parks reports that all pathways and restrooms are accessible. Broad lawns and temporary event layouts may have different surface conditions.

Are dogs allowed at Los Angeles State Historic Park? +

Leashed dogs are allowed throughout permitted outdoor areas. Bring waste bags and keep pets out of habitat restoration areas.

Is Los Angeles State Historic Park good for children? +

Open lawns, skyline views, the interpretive play area, trains passing nearby, and a simple loop make this an easy family picnic stop. Shade can be limited.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

How do I reach Los Angeles State Historic Park without a car? +

Metro A Line's Chinatown station is beside the park. Union Station is roughly one mile away, and protected bike access connects through nearby streets.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026
California State Parks ↗
What should I know before visiting Los Angeles State Historic Park? +

The park is an active event venue. Check the official calendar for farmers markets, concerts, filming, and fenced areas, and remember that closing time changes with sunset.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026
California State Parks ↗
What weather check matters before visiting Los Angeles State Historic Park? +

Check current heat, air quality, wind, rain, surf where relevant, and operator alerts. Los Angeles heat and fire conditions can affect trails and outdoor facilities even when general park hours remain unchanged.
