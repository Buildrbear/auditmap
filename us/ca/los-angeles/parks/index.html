Park directory

Current city pages

Echo Park Lake

Los Angeles

The outdoor lake park generally follows city park hours, 5 a.m.-10:30 p.m. Pedal boats, the boathouse cafe, restrooms, and special events use separate schedules.

Elysian Park

Los Angeles

Open daily from sunrise to sunset. Lodges, athletic fields, restrooms, and reserved areas use their own posted schedules.

Exposition Park

Los Angeles

The park grounds are generally open 8 a.m.-10 p.m., subject to event changes. Museums, the Rose Garden, stadiums, food, and parking facilities keep separate hours.

Gloria Molina Grand Park

Los Angeles

Open daily 5:30 a.m.-10 p.m. The playground is posted 8 a.m.-8 p.m.; splash pad, dog run, cafe, restrooms, and events may close independently.

Griffith Park

Los Angeles

The park is generally open daily 5 a.m.-10:30 p.m. Griffith Observatory, the zoo, museums, golf, trains, concessions, and event venues use separate schedules.

Kenneth Hahn State Recreation Area

Los Angeles

The regional park is closed Monday and Tuesday. March 1-October 31 it is open Wednesday-Sunday 6 a.m.-8 p.m.; seasonal winter closing is earlier. Restrooms and offices have shorter hours.

Los Angeles State Historic Park

Los Angeles

Open daily 8 a.m. to sunset. Welcome Center, Cargo Snack Shack, programs, and events use separate schedules.

Runyon Canyon Park

Los Angeles

Open daily sunrise to sunset. The West Trail is currently closed until further notice due to a recent fire; other posted areas remain open.

Venice Beach & Ocean Front Walk

Los Angeles

The outdoor beach recreation area generally follows posted city beach and park hours. The recreation office is open Monday-Friday 8 a.m.-6 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m.-4 p.m.; Muscle Beach and other facilities differ.
