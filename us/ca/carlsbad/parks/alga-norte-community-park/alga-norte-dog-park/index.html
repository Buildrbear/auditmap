Alga Norte Dog Park

6565 Alicante Road, Carlsbad, CA 92009
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open daily 8 a.m.-10 p.m., subject to posted maintenance or temporary closures.
Navigate

What people say

A 30,000-square-foot fenced dog park with separate large- and small-dog areas, agility equipment, drinking water, waste bags, benches, and a shaded picnic area. Follow the internal Alga Norte signs to the dog-park side of the campus; do not stop at the aquatics or ball-field lot if the fenced area is the destination. Dogs are permitted in the fenced dog park, not throughout the rest of Alga Norte Community Park.
