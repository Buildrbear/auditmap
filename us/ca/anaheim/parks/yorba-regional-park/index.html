Yorba Regional Park

7600 E La Palma Avenue, Anaheim, CA 92807
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Yorba Regional Park is open daily 7:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. in spring and summer and 7:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. in fall and winter. Rental and office hours are shorter.

What people say

Yorba Regional Park is a 140-acre linear park with four lakes and streams, more than 400 picnic tables, shade shelters, playgrounds and restrooms throughout, seven reservable group shelters, fishing, bicycle and walking trails, Santa Ana River Trail access, sports courts and fields, fitness course, barbecues, and seasonal bike and paddleboat rentals.

What address should I use for Yorba Regional Park? +

Use 7600 East La Palma Avenue, Anaheim, CA 92807. The park stretches more than a mile, so use the official map to select the closest numbered parking, playground, shelter, lake, or trail connection.

What hours is Yorba Regional Park open? +

The park opens daily at 7 a.m. and closes at 9 p.m. in spring and summer or 6 p.m. in fall and winter. The office and bike or paddleboat rental counter close earlier.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

How much is parking at Yorba Regional Park? +

Vehicle entry is $3 Monday-Friday and $5 Saturday-Sunday. Some holidays and events have higher rates. Park in the numbered area nearest your selected playground, picnic shelter, lake, or trail.

Are there playgrounds at Yorba Regional Park? +

Yes. Playgrounds and restrooms are distributed throughout the long park. Check the official map before arriving so families can choose the closest entrance and parking area.

Can I reserve a picnic shelter at Yorba Regional Park? +

Yes. The park has seven reservable group shelters, more than 400 picnic tables, many permanent shade structures, and about 200 barbecues. Unreserved facilities may be first come, first served under posted rules.

Can I bike from Yorba Regional Park to the Santa Ana River Trail? +

Yes. Paved bicycle routes meander through the park and connect directly with the Santa Ana River Trail. Walking, multi-use, fitness, and equestrian routes are also mapped, with different permitted uses.

Are dogs allowed at Yorba Regional Park? +

Yes, dogs are permitted on leash. Owners must follow OC Parks rules and keep dogs out of any signed restricted activity or facility area.

Can I rent a bike or paddleboat at Yorba Regional Park? +

Bike and paddleboat rentals operate on their own schedule. The last bike rental goes out one hour before sunset and the last boat rental two hours before sunset on operating days; check the vendor's current hours before visiting.

Where are the restrooms at Yorba Regional Park? +

Restrooms are located throughout the park rather than at one central building. Because Yorba Regional Park is more than a mile long, check the official map and park in the numbered area closest to the restroom, playground, shelter, or lake you plan to use.

Can I fish or swim in the lakes at Yorba Regional Park? +

Fishing is allowed, but California requires a state fishing license for visitors age 16 and older. Swimming and wading are prohibited, and visitors may not launch personal watercraft; model sailboats are limited to the designated lake.

What can be closed or seasonal at Yorba Regional Park? +

Park closing time changes seasonally: 9 p.m. during spring and summer daylight-saving months and 6 p.m. in fall and winter. Bike and paddleboat rentals keep separate operating days and stop rentals before sunset; storms, flooding, maintenance, events, or fire restrictions can also affect trails, lakes, shelters, roads, and barbecues. Check the OC Parks alerts page before a feature-specific trip.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
OC Parks ↗
Where can I find shade or shelter at Yorba Regional Park? +

Many of the park's 400-plus picnic tables have permanent shade structures, and seven group shelters can be reserved. Trees also shade portions of the stream and lake corridor, while ballfields, playgrounds, volleyball areas, open lawns, fishing edges, and long trail sections can be exposed. The park office has limited weekday hours, and picnic roofs are not severe-weather shelters; during lightning, flooding, or high wind, use an open substantial building or vehicle.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
OC Parks ↗
Can visitors swim or use a splash pad at Yorba Regional Park? +

No. Yorba Regional Park has no listed splash pad, and OC Parks prohibits swimming or wading in its lakes and connecting streams. Fishing, designated model sailboats, and concession-operated paddleboats are different activities and do not permit entering the water. Keep children and pets out of the lakes and use a separately listed aquatic facility for swimming or splash play.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can I take a bus to Yorba Regional Park? +

OCTA Route 38 runs along La Palma Avenue toward Anaheim Hills and Yorba Linda. The park is long, so confirm the stop closest to your intended lake, playground, shelter, or trail access and expect a final walk; use OCTA's current trip planner because service times and stop patterns can change.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

What are the main landmarks in Yorba Regional Park? +

Four connected lakes and streams form the park's visual spine and support fishing, model boats, and seasonal paddleboat rentals. The park stretches for more than a mile beside the Santa Ana River, with playgrounds, restrooms, picnic tables, and seven reservable group shelters distributed along its length. Choose a lake, shelter, playground, or trail connection before parking rather than treating the first entrance as the whole park.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Accessibility varies by entrance, route, terrain, and destination. Review the exact mapped subsite and its official source before relying on a specific accessible route.
