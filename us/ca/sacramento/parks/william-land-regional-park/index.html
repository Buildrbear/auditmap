What people say

William Land Regional Park is a 207-acre Sacramento destination with adventure playground, seasonal free wading pool, lakes, jogging paths, rock garden, picnic areas, restrooms, fields and courts, amphitheater, golf course, Sacramento Zoo, Fairytale Town, and Funderland.

Where should I enter William Land Park? +

Use 3800 Land Park Drive for the general park, but navigate directly to Sacramento Zoo, Fairytale Town, Funderland, golf course, wading pool, amphitheater, or your numbered picnic area because each has a different closest approach.

Is there parking at William Land Park? +

Yes. The park has off-street parking distributed near major attractions, fields, and picnic areas, plus limited street parking near the wading pool. Zoo, amusement attractions, events, and peak weekends can fill the closest spaces.

Is there a free playground at William Land Park? +

Yes. The city lists an Adventure Play Area as a general park amenity. Fairytale Town and Funderland are separate family attractions with their own admission and operating schedules.

When is the William Land Park wading pool open? +

The free shallow play pool near 13th Street and 13th Avenue operates on a limited summer schedule. For 2026, the published season is June 13-August 9 with varying afternoon hours and closures on Tuesdays and Fridays.

Are there restrooms at William Land Park? +

Yes. Public restrooms are listed among general park amenities, while Sacramento Zoo, Fairytale Town, Funderland, golf, and other staffed attractions manage their own facilities and hours.

Is William Land Park free? +

General park entry, playground, paths, lakes, rock garden, lawns, and the scheduled summer wading pool are free. Sacramento Zoo, Fairytale Town, Funderland, golf, reservations, concessions, and ticketed events charge separately.

Can I reserve a picnic area at William Land Park? +

Yes. More than 20 numbered picnic areas support groups of different sizes and barbecue needs. William Land Park is treated as a premium facility for permit pricing; reserve through the city and select the exact numbered area.

What operates separately inside William Land Park? +

Sacramento Zoo, Fairytale Town, Funderland, golf course, wading pool, amphitheater, fields, concessions, and reserved picnic sites all have independent hours, fees, and closures. General park access does not confirm that an attraction is open.

Where can visitors find shade or weather shelter at William Land Regional Park? +

William Land Park's mature canopy shades many picnic areas, paths, playground edges, and garden spaces. Some permitted group picnic areas may include shade structures, and the seasonal wading pool has a shaded deck and grass area. The zoo, Fairytale Town, Funderland, and other buildings have separate admission and hours; during lightning, dangerous wind, wildfire smoke, or extreme heat, use an open substantial building or vehicle rather than relying on trees.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Sacramento ↗
What are the walking surfaces and distances at William Land Regional Park? +

The 207-acre park has developed jogging and walking paths, paved roads and sidewalks, parking approaches, and paths linking lakes, picnic areas, Village Green, playground, wading pool, golf, zoo, Fairytale Town, and Funderland. Lawn shortcuts, lake edges, the Rock Garden, and historic amphitheater areas can include grass, decomposed granite, stone steps, roots, and uneven grades. Use the park amenities map and navigate to the exact destination; the nearby Del Rio Trail provides a separate paved bicycle route with an adjacent pedestrian path.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Is there an off-leash dog park at William Land Park? +

Not yet. Sacramento is evaluating possible dog-park locations within William Land Park, but the official park listing describes a proposal rather than an open fenced facility. Keep dogs leashed on general park grounds, clean up waste, and do not use lawns as an informal off-leash area; the zoo, Fairytale Town, Funderland, golf course, pool, and events have separate pet restrictions.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Which bus goes to William Land Park? +

SacRT Route 11 Land Park stops in front of the Sacramento Zoo and serves the family-attraction side of William Land Park. From light rail, transfer at Sacramento City College station for the roughly one-mile bus connection. Use the live planner for Fairytale Town, Funderland, the zoo, golf course, pool, amphitheater, or a picnic area because the park is large and each destination has a different best stop.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Sacramento Regional Transit ↗
What are the main attractions in William Land Park? +

The best-known family cluster contains Sacramento Zoo, Fairytale Town, and Funderland, each with its own entrance and operating rules. Elsewhere in the 207-acre park are the WPA-era Rock Garden, William A. Carroll Amphitheater, lakes, Village Green, golf course, sports fields, picnic areas, and seasonal wading pool. Route to the named attraction or amenity rather than the broad park address when timing or walking distance matters.

Accessibility varies by entrance, route, terrain, and destination. Review the exact mapped subsite and its official source before relying on a specific accessible route.
