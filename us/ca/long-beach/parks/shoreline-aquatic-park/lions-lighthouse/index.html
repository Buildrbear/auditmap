Are there restrooms near Lions Lighthouse? +

Do not rely on a restroom within the open lawn or surface parking area. City planning documents identify a lack of public restrooms at the Shoreline Aquatic Park surface lot; nearby Aquarium, marina, restaurant, and event facilities have their own access and hours.

What fees apply at Lions Lighthouse? +

The City lists the lighthouse as an outdoor Shoreline Aquatic Park landmark and does not list an admission charge. Nearby parking, the Aquarium, harbor excursions, restaurants, and special events can cost separately.

How accessible is Lions Lighthouse? +

Use the paved Aquarium Way side and choose legal waterfront parking closest to your destination. The park is an open lawn bordered by paved harbor promenades, but event fencing and marina activity can alter the most direct route.

Are dogs allowed at Lions Lighthouse? +

Yes, on a secure leash no longer than 6 feet and held continuously by someone able to control the dog. Shoreline Aquatic Park is not a designated off-leash exercise area, so keep the leash attached throughout the lawn, promenade, lighthouse, and beach-edge visit and remove all waste. Use a specifically designated Long Beach dog park or dog beach for off-leash time. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.
