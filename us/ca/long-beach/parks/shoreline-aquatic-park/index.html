What people say

Shoreline Aquatic Park is a waterfront green space on Aquarium Way between the Long Beach Arena and Downtown Shoreline Marina, with picnic lawns, a small sandy beach, Lions Lighthouse, Shoreline walking and bicycle paths, and views of Rainbow Harbor, Shoreline Village, Queen Mary, and the downtown skyline.

Use 200 Aquarium Way, Long Beach, CA 90802 for the waterfront park near the Aquarium of the Pacific. Navigate to Lions Lighthouse or the Aquarium parking structure when that is your exact destination.

What can I see at Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

The peninsula offers Lions Lighthouse, grassy lawns, a small sandy beach, Shoreline walking and bicycle paths, and views of Rainbow Harbor, Shoreline Village, Queen Mary, and the downtown Long Beach skyline.

Can I picnic at Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

Yes. The city describes it as a broad open green area used for picnics and special events. A permitted event may occupy or restrict part of the lawn, so check the waterfront event calendar before a major visit.

Can I walk or bike at Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

Yes. The park connects with the paved Shoreline Park walking and bicycle route around the harbor. Expect shared paths, event detours, marina activity, and exposed waterfront conditions.

Where should I park for Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

Use the public parking serving the Aquarium of the Pacific and Aquarium Way area, then walk onto the peninsula. Rates, access, and available lots can change during conventions, festivals, races, and waterfront events.

Is there a playground at Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

The official park description does not list a children's playground or splash pad. This is primarily an open lawn, picnic, harbor-view, lighthouse, beach-edge, walking, and event destination.

Is Shoreline Aquatic Park free? +

Yes. General access to the park, lighthouse area, lawns, and paths is free. Parking, Aquarium admission, marina services, food, and ticketed or permitted events are separate.

Do events close Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

The park is a major permitted-event site and can accommodate several thousand people, so festivals and private setups may restrict lawns, paths, parking, or lighthouse access. Check City of Long Beach event notices before traveling for a quiet visit.

Are there public restrooms at Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

Do not rely on a restroom within the open lawn or surface parking area. City planning documents identify a lack of public restrooms at the Shoreline Aquatic Park surface lot; nearby Aquarium, marina, restaurant, and event facilities have their own access and hours.

What is the easiest accessible route into Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

Use the paved Aquarium Way side and choose legal waterfront parking closest to your destination. The park is an open lawn bordered by paved harbor promenades, but event fencing and marina activity can alter the most direct route.

Is there shade or weather shelter at Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

Plan for a mostly exposed visit. Long Beach describes Shoreline Aquatic Park as a wide-open green event and picnic area, and the lighthouse hill, harbor promenade, lawn, and beach edge receive direct sun and wind. Nearby Aquarium, marina, restaurant, and convention buildings have their own admission or hours and are not park shelters. Bring portable sun protection where permitted, and use an open substantial building or enclosed vehicle during lightning, strong coastal wind, or dangerous heat.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can I bring a dog to Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

Yes, on a secure leash no longer than 6 feet and held continuously by someone able to control the dog. Shoreline Aquatic Park is not a designated off-leash exercise area, so keep the leash attached throughout the lawn, promenade, lighthouse, and beach-edge visit and remove all waste. Use a specifically designated Long Beach dog park or dog beach for off-leash time.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can visitors swim at Shoreline Aquatic Park? +

Shoreline Aquatic Park is a lawn and event space between the arena and Downtown Shoreline Marina; the City does not list a pool, splash pad, or designated swimming area there. Do not enter Rainbow Harbor or the marina from the park. For supervised seasonal water play, use a currently operating Long Beach beach or aquatic playground such as Alamitos Beach or Bayshore Beach and follow posted lifeguard rules, swim tests, and water-quality advisories.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Long Beach Parks, Recreation and Marine ↗
How do I reach Shoreline Aquatic Park without parking? +

Long Beach Transit's free Passport service connects downtown waterfront destinations and stops near the Shoreline area; check its operating hours because return service may end before a late event finishes. Metro A Line riders can transfer or walk from Downtown Long Beach, while seasonal AquaLink or AquaBus service reaches nearby Rainbow Harbor docks. Use the park or marina entrance as the destination because Shoreline Aquatic Park, the arena, Aquarium, and Shoreline Village use different final approaches.
