Sloat Boulevard south entrance

Hours ⌄
Published schedule Park hours are 5 a.m. to midnight. Temporary restrooms, programs, construction areas, and pop-up amenities use separate schedules.

What people say

Sloat Boulevard is the south entrance, connecting the car-free park with southern Ocean Beach and west-side transit. Expect wind, fog, sand, limited shelter, and construction changes. Shipwreck Cove installation began in July 2026 with expected completion around September; confirm opening before promising it to children.

Are dogs allowed at Sloat Boulevard south entrance? +

Dogs must follow posted leash, waste, habitat, and event rules. Keep pets away from restored dunes, wildlife, children's play, and fast bicycle traffic.
