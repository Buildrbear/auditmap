destination

Sloat Boulevard promenade access

Ocean Beach, Great Highway, San Francisco, CA 94122
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The beach follows posted GGNRA access; City-managed parking lots close at 10 p.m. Seasonal fires must end by 9:30 p.m. and are prohibited November through February.
Navigate

What people say

The Sloat Boulevard area provides southern promenade and beach access near the end of Sunset Dunes, with changing road circulation. Do not treat Ocean Beach as a casual swimming beach. Check surf, tides, fog, wind, cold, air quality, and plover rules. Fires are only in provided rings March through October, with no glass or alcohol.

Where should I park for Sloat Boulevard promenade access? +

Lots and curb areas are spread along the shoreline and close at 10 p.m. Sunset Dunes changed vehicle circulation on the former Upper Great Highway, so route to the chosen stairwell.

The beach follows posted GGNRA access; City-managed parking lots close at 10 p.m. Seasonal fires must end by 9:30 p.m. and are prohibited November through February.

Are there restrooms near Sloat Boulevard promenade access? +

Restroom coverage is limited and widely spaced. Identify an open facility near the chosen stairwell or adjacent developed area before settling on the beach.

Is Sloat Boulevard promenade access free? +

Beach and promenade access are free. Parking, food, rentals, permits, and transit fares may cost.

How accessible is Sloat Boulevard promenade access? +

Accessible routes connect selected parking areas to the promenade, and beach wheelchairs are available by request. Sand and dynamic shoreline conditions remain limiting.

Are dogs allowed at Sloat Boulevard promenade access? +

Dog rules vary by season and zone. The snowy plover protection area south of stairwell 21 requires a leash no longer than six feet from July 1 through May 15.

Is Sloat Boulevard promenade access good for children? +

The beach is excellent for walking, kites, and sand play, but the water is frigid with dangerous currents and no assumption of lifeguard protection.
