destination

Alamo Square playground

Hayes and Steiner Streets, San Francisco, CA 94117
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Park hours are 5 a.m. to midnight. Restrooms are 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in spring and summer and 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. in fall and winter.
Navigate

What people say

The dual-level accessible playground is the park's primary children's destination, reached most accessibly from Hayes and Scott. The famous view attracts tours and crowds. Protect valuables, respect residential stoops, check restroom hours, and bring layers for exposed hilltop wind.

Where should I park for Alamo Square playground? +

There is no park lot. Street parking is scarce and tour activity affects loading; use Muni, walking, cycling, or rideshare when possible.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
San Francisco Recreation and Parks ↗
When is Alamo Square playground open? +

Park hours are 5 a.m. to midnight. Restrooms are 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in spring and summer and 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. in fall and winter.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
San Francisco Recreation and Parks ↗
Are there restrooms near Alamo Square playground? +

Accessible public restrooms use seasonal hours: 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. spring/summer and 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. fall/winter.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Is Alamo Square playground free? +

Park, overlook, playground, dog area, lawn, and general recreation are free. Permits, reservations, tours, parking, and nearby food may cost.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
San Francisco Recreation and Parks ↗
How accessible is Alamo Square playground? +

The designated accessible entrance is at Hayes and Scott. The park is a hilltop, so routes from other corners can involve significant grades.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
San Francisco Recreation and Parks ↗
Are dogs allowed at Alamo Square playground? +

A designated off-leash dog play area is provided. Dogs must be leashed elsewhere and controlled around playgrounds, lawns, picnics, and crowds.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Is Alamo Square playground good for children? +

The dual-level accessible playground, lawns, picnic space, and short walking loop make a good neighborhood stop; hills and surrounding streets require supervision.

What should I know before visiting Alamo Square playground? +

The dual-level accessible playground is the park's primary children's destination, reached most accessibly from Hayes and Scott. The famous view attracts tours and crowds. Protect valuables, respect residential stoops, check restroom hours, and bring layers for exposed hilltop wind.

How do you reach Alamo Square playground? +

Muni buses serve the surrounding Western Addition and Hayes Valley streets. Transit avoids limited curb parking.
