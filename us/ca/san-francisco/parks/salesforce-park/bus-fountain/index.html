What people say

The Bus Fountain is a motion-activated artwork whose jets respond to buses moving below; operation can pause for maintenance or weather. Confirm park hours, elevator status, wind, heat, rain, and event occupation. Use a named elevator bank when meeting others because the park spans nearly four blocks.

Where should I park for Bus fountain? +

Use transit or a paid downtown garage; the park has no free visitor lot. Traffic and garage access are difficult during commute hours and events.

Are there restrooms near Bus fountain? +

Public restrooms are inside the Transit Center rather than spread through the rooftop. Confirm the nearest open facility and elevator before settling at the far end.

Is Bus fountain free? +

Park admission and public programming are free unless an event states otherwise. Food, parking, and transit fares cost separately.

How accessible is Bus fountain? +

Elevators connect street and transit levels to the rooftop, and the principal loop is developed. Elevator outages and event setups can change the best entrance.

Are dogs allowed at Bus fountain? +

Pets are not allowed because of the park's delicate plant habitat. Service animals are allowed.

How do you reach Bus fountain? +

The park sits atop the regional Transit Center, served by bus operators and nearby Muni/BART connections. Street-level access varies by operator and time.
