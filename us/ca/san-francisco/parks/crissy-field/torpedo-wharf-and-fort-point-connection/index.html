What people say

Torpedo Wharf and the Fort Point connection bring visitors close to deep cold water, fishing activity, bridge wind, and historic shoreline routes. Bay water is cold and conditions change quickly. Check wind, fog, tides, air quality, event closures, and parking; watch children closely near the shoreline and bicycle traffic.

Where should I park for Torpedo Wharf and Fort Point connection? +

Paid parking serves East Beach and West Bluff, including accessible spaces. Lots fill on warm weekends and major event days; choose the end closest to your activity.

Are there restrooms near Torpedo Wharf and Fort Point connection? +

Accessible restrooms are at East Beach, West Bluff near the Warming Hut, and Golden Gate Bridge Plaza. Drinking water is also available at these developed areas.

Is Torpedo Wharf and Fort Point connection free? +

Beach, promenade, lawns, marsh overlooks, picnic areas, and general access are free. Parking, food, rentals, and programs may charge.

How accessible is Torpedo Wharf and Fort Point connection? +

The 2.3-mile promenade is generally level with accessible parking and restrooms at both ends. East Beach offers a seasonal beach mat and beach wheelchairs by request.

Are dogs allowed at Torpedo Wharf and Fort Point connection? +

Dog access varies by shoreline and habitat zone. Follow posted leash controls, protect marsh wildlife, and use the designated dog-washing station at East Beach.

East Beach combines sand, picnic tables, grills, restrooms, water, and a generally level promenade, making it the easiest family base.
