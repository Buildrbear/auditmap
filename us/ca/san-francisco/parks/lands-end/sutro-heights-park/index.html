What people say

Sutro Heights Park preserves the landscaped grounds and historic remnants above Ocean Beach, with broad lawns, cliff views, and paths separate from the Sutro Baths stairs. Stay on marked trails and away from cliff edges. Expect wind, fog, slippery steps, poison oak, weak cell coverage, cold surf, and rapidly changing coastal weather.

Where should I park for Sutro Heights Park? +

Merrie Way near Lands End Lookout is the primary lot and fills quickly. Legion of Honor and Fort Miley approaches serve different trail sections; do not expect one lot to fit every route.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
When is Sutro Heights Park open? +

Outdoor trails and overlooks follow posted Golden Gate National Recreation Area access. Lands End Lookout, restrooms, parking, and nearby attractions have separate hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Are there restrooms near Sutro Heights Park? +

Restrooms are concentrated near Lands End Lookout and selected developed facilities. There are no dependable facilities on remote trail or beach sections.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Is Sutro Heights Park free? +

Trails, overlooks, Sutro Baths ruins, memorials, and general outdoor access are free. Parking and nearby attractions or food may cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
How accessible is Sutro Heights Park? +

Wheelchair-accessible trails begin at the Merrie Way parking lot, but stairs, steep grades, sand, roots, and cliffside natural paths limit many routes.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Are dogs allowed at Sutro Heights Park? +

Follow current GGNRA leash and habitat rules. Keep dogs controlled near cliffs, stairs, wildlife, crowded overlooks, and narrow trail traffic.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Is Sutro Heights Park good for children? +

The Lookout, Sutro Baths overlook, and short accessible paths work for many families; long stairs to beaches and cliffside trails require close supervision.

How do you reach Sutro Heights Park? +

Muni serves Point Lobos Avenue and nearby Richmond District stops. Transit avoids the most competitive parking at Lands End Lookout.
