Park directory

Current city pages

Alamo Square

San Francisco

Park hours are 5 a.m. to midnight. Restrooms are 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in spring and summer and 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. in fall and winter.

Crissy Field

San Francisco

Outdoor shoreline and promenade access follows posted GGNRA hours. Parking, restrooms, the Warming Hut, programs, and staffed facilities use separate schedules.

Golden Gate Park

San Francisco

The park is open daily from 5 a.m. to midnight. Museums, gardens, playgrounds, restrooms, concessions, roads, shuttles, and events use separate schedules.

Lands End

San Francisco

Outdoor trails and overlooks follow posted Golden Gate National Recreation Area access. Lands End Lookout, restrooms, parking, and nearby attractions have separate hours.

Mission Dolores Park

San Francisco

Park hours are 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Restrooms are 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in spring and summer and 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. in fall and winter.

Ocean Beach

San Francisco

The beach follows posted GGNRA access; City-managed parking lots close at 10 p.m. Seasonal fires must end by 9:30 p.m. and are prohibited November through February.

Salesforce Park

San Francisco

May through October hours are 6 a.m. to 9 p.m.; November through April hours are 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. Hours are subject to change.

Sunset Dunes

San Francisco

Park hours are 5 a.m. to midnight. Temporary restrooms, programs, construction areas, and pop-up amenities use separate schedules.

The Presidio

San Francisco

The Presidio grounds are open 24 hours daily. The Visitor Center is generally open 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; playgrounds, museums, food, beaches, shuttles, and restrooms use separate schedules.

Yerba Buena Gardens

San Francisco

The gardens are open daily 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Public restrooms are open daily 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.; museums, carousel, play areas, food, and events keep separate schedules.
