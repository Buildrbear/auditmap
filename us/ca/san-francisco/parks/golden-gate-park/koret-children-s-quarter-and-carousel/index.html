What people say

Koret Children's Quarter is the park's major playground beside the historic carousel; ride and playground schedules are separate. Expect strong west-side wind, fog, microclimates, event closures, and long distances. Check the exact attraction schedule and current road access before leaving.

Choose parking for the exact destination. Street spaces, attraction garages, and small lots are distributed across a park three miles long; events and car-free roads change access.

Is Koret Children's Quarter and Carousel free? +

General park landscapes, trails, meadows, playgrounds, and lakes are free. Museums, gardens, rentals, food, events, and selected parking charge separately.

How accessible is Koret Children's Quarter and Carousel? +

The City publishes a most-usable-pathways map and operates a free park shuttle. Grades, historic paths, attraction entrances, and event detours vary by destination.
