California Academy of Sciences

San Francisco, San Francisco, CA
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Golden Gate Park is open daily from 5:00 a.m. to midnight. Gardens, museums, playgrounds, restrooms, sports facilities, concessions, roads, shuttles, and events use separate schedules.

What people say

California Academy of Sciences combines Steinhart Aquarium, Osher Rainforest, Morrison Planetarium, natural history exhibits, and a living roof in one ticketed science museum. The Academy is at 55 Music Concourse Drive, directly across from the de Young Museum. Advance tickets are recommended. Public hours are normally 9:30 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Monday-Saturday and 11:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Sunday; Thursday NightLife is 21+. Planetarium passes are first-come after arrival, shows may not suit children under seven, and children under four are not admitted. Expect sensory changes between aquarium, rainforest, and planetarium areas; accessible parking is on garage level B1.

Which entrance should I use for California Academy of Sciences? +

Choose by destination: Stanyan Street for the east end and Conservatory area, 9th Avenue for the Music Concourse and gardens, 25th Avenue for the central park, or the Great Highway for the west end. Route directly to the attraction because the park is about three miles long.

Where can I park in California Academy of Sciences? +

Street spaces, destination lots, and the Music Concourse garage serve different areas. The JFK Promenade and connected west-side route are car-free, while Transverse, Crossover, Chain of Lakes, portions of MLK and JFK, and selected loops remain open to cars. Read current signs and leave nothing visible in vehicles.

Can I reach California Academy of Sciences by transit? +

Yes. Multiple Muni bus and light-rail routes serve the north, south, and east edges. Choose the line nearest your destination; a stop at one end can still leave a multi-mile walk to an attraction at the other.

How much does California Academy of Sciences cost? +

General park, trails, playgrounds, lakes, meadows, and public landscapes are free. Museums, gardens, attractions, events, rentals, food, and selected parking charge separately.
