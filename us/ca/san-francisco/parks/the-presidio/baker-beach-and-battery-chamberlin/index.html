destination

Baker Beach and Battery Chamberlin

210 Lincoln Boulevard, San Francisco, CA 94129
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The Presidio grounds are open 24 hours daily. The Visitor Center is generally open 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.; playgrounds, museums, food, beaches, shuttles, and restrooms use separate schedules.

What people say

Baker Beach has two free lots, restrooms at the north lot, picnic areas, Battery Chamberlin, cold surf, and dramatic bridge views. Main Post access and safety construction runs from July 2026 into winter 2027, bringing detours, noise, and traffic. Check shuttle alerts, wind, fog, beach surf, and destination closures.

Where should I park for Baker Beach and Battery Chamberlin? +

Paid parking is distributed near major destinations, with accessible spaces at Tunnel Tops, the Visitor Center, Crissy Field, and other areas. Baker Beach has two free lots.

Are there restrooms near Baker Beach and Battery Chamberlin? +

Accessible restrooms are available at Presidio Transit Center, Tunnel Tops near the Outpost, the Visitor Center area, beaches, and staffed destinations; hours vary.

Is Baker Beach and Battery Chamberlin free? +

Park admission, Tunnel Tops, the Outpost, trails, overlooks, and Presidio GO are free. Paid parking, museums, lodging, food, golf, camping, and programs cost separately.

How accessible is Baker Beach and Battery Chamberlin? +

Tunnel Tops has flat routes and two wheelchair-accessible north-south paths. Accessible parking and the wheelchair-accessible Presidio GO serve major areas; natural trails vary.

Are dogs allowed at Baker Beach and Battery Chamberlin? +

Leashed pets are allowed in many outdoor areas but not playgrounds or indoor facilities; beaches and habitat areas have location-specific rules.
