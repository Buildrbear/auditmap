Where should I park for History Park? +

Standard parking is $6 per day, collected daily by machines accepting exact cash or major credit cards. Listed holidays cost $10. Discounted and annual passes require eligibility or advance arrangements, and payment does not guarantee re-entry. Navigate to the exact History Park pin rather than the general Kelley Park marker. Are there restrooms near History Park? +

Restrooms are near the Happy Hollow temporary entrance, beside the History Park entrance and parking lot, and near the lower Japanese Friendship Garden entrance. Individual attraction gates and hours can affect access.

How accessible is History Park? +

No. Happy Hollow Park and Zoo, History Park, Japanese Friendship Garden, picnic areas, and open park space have separate entrances, operating schedules, and accessibility conditions. Route to the exact destination and use its nearest parking and restroom facilities.

Are dogs allowed at History Park? +

Do not bring ordinary pets into History Park, Japanese Friendship Garden, or Happy Hollow Park and Zoo; San Jose explicitly prohibits animals in those attractions. Follow posted City rules in general park and picnic areas and confirm service-animal requirements with each operator. Separately operated buildings and attractions can set stricter rules.

Is History Park useful for a family visit? +

History Park is a 14-acre collection of original and reproduction buildings, paved streets, museums, and a weekend trolley at the south end of Kelley Park. Do not confuse the general park with Happy Hollow, the separately operated zoo and family amusement destination. A new Roberts Avenue community-park area with a playground is planned, but visitors should verify its construction status before relying on it.
