What people say

Kelley Park is a 172-acre San Jose destination park with mature-tree picnic grounds, Japanese Friendship Garden, History Park, Happy Hollow Park & Zoo, Leininger Community Center, amphitheater, wedding sites, walking paths, gardens, and multiple reservable picnic areas.

Where should I enter Kelley Park? +

Use 1300 Senter Road for general park and picnic access. Happy Hollow Park & Zoo, History Park, Japanese Friendship Garden, and individual event areas have distinct closest entrances and parking, so navigate to the specific attraction.

What hours is Kelley Park open? +

General park hours are 8:00 a.m. until one-half hour after sunset. Happy Hollow, History Park, Japanese Friendship Garden, community facilities, weddings, and events set their own hours and closure days.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

How much is parking at Kelley Park? +

Standard parking is $6 per day, collected daily by machines accepting exact cash or major credit cards. Listed holidays cost $10. Discounted and annual passes require eligibility or advance arrangements, and payment does not guarantee re-entry.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of San Jose ↗
Is Kelley Park free? +

General park and picnic-ground access is free, but parking normally costs $6. Happy Hollow Park & Zoo, reservations, weddings, programs, and ticketed events can have separate admission or fees; History Park and garden operations should be checked independently.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of San Jose ↗
What attractions are inside Kelley Park? +

The major destinations are Happy Hollow Park & Zoo, Japanese Friendship Garden, History Park, Leininger Community Center, Kelley Amphitheater, Vietnamese Heritage Garden area, and large picnic groves. Each operates as a distinct destination.

Can I reserve a picnic area at Kelley Park? +

Yes. Kelley Park has numerous reservable and first-come areas, including Arbor, Creek Overlook Pavilion, Family Circle, Redwood Grove, and Twin Oaks. Reservation holders must arrive before 10:00 a.m. to retain the site.

Is there a free playground at Kelley Park? +

Do not confuse the general park with Happy Hollow, the separately operated zoo and family amusement destination. A new Roberts Avenue community-park area with a playground is planned, but visitors should verify its construction status before relying on it.

What should I check before visiting Kelley Park? +

Check the exact attraction's hours, seasonal closures, garden maintenance, event reservations, and parking availability. General park opening does not mean Happy Hollow, History Park, Japanese Friendship Garden, or indoor facilities are open.

Where are restrooms in Kelley Park? +

Restrooms are near the Happy Hollow temporary entrance, beside the History Park entrance and parking lot, and near the lower Japanese Friendship Garden entrance. Individual attraction gates and hours can affect access.

Are dogs allowed in Kelley Park attractions? +

Do not bring ordinary pets into History Park, Japanese Friendship Garden, or Happy Hollow Park and Zoo; San Jose explicitly prohibits animals in those attractions. Follow posted City rules in general park and picnic areas and confirm service-animal requirements with each operator.

Is Kelley Park one continuous attraction? +

No. Happy Hollow Park and Zoo, History Park, Japanese Friendship Garden, picnic areas, and open park space have separate entrances, operating schedules, and accessibility conditions. Route to the exact destination and use its nearest parking and restroom facilities.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Which parts of Kelley Park have shade or covered picnic space? +

Mature trees shade many picnic groves, including Twin Oaks, Manual Briar, and Family Circle. Creek Overlook has six covered and six uncovered tables, while Redwood Grove's listing specifically says it has no built shade despite its name. Gardens, paths, parking lots, and event areas vary, and indoor attractions open on separate schedules. Picnic cover is useful for sun or light rain, not lightning or severe wind; use an open substantial building or vehicle in dangerous weather.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of San Jose ↗
What are the walking surfaces and distances at Kelley Park? +

Kelley Park covers 172 acres, with developed paved walks and roads linking parking, picnic areas, Japanese Friendship Garden, History Park, Happy Hollow, Leininger Center, and other major destinations. Garden routes can include bridges, narrower paths, slopes, and wet surfaces, while the disc-golf course and open-space edges use grass, dirt, roots, and uneven terrain. The attractions maintain separate gates and hours, so use the park map and navigate to the exact destination instead of treating the entire park as one continuous accessible loop.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of San Jose ↗
Is there a splash pad or swimming at Kelley Park? +

No general splash pad or swimming facility is listed for Kelley Park. The Japanese Friendship Garden has ornamental ponds, a brook, waterfalls, bridges, and koi; those are display features, not water play. Happy Hollow Park & Zoo is a separately ticketed attraction with its own current amenities and hours. If water play is essential, choose a San Jose facility specifically listed for aquatics rather than assuming the ponds or picnic areas are usable for splashing.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of San Jose ↗
Which VTA bus goes to Kelley Park? +

VTA Route 73 runs along Senter Road and provides the most direct regular bus approach to Kelley Park, History San Jose, and nearby entrances. Use the exact destination, such as Happy Hollow, Japanese Friendship Garden, History Park, or a picnic area, because the campus is large and each attraction uses a different gate or parking zone.
