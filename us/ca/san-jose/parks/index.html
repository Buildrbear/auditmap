Alum Rock Park

San Jose

Use 15350 Penitencia Creek Road. Temporary metered traffic lights control vehicle flow on the damaged approach road, so allow extra time and follow ranger instructions to the open parking areas.

Guadalupe River Park

San Jose

Use 438 Coleman Avenue for the listed park parking and visitor-center area. For a trail trip, select a specific access point along the northern, central, or southern Guadalupe River Trail rather than navigating only to the park name.

Kelley Park

San Jose

Use 1300 Senter Road for general park and picnic access. Happy Hollow Park & Zoo, History Park, Japanese Friendship Garden, and individual event areas have distinct closest entrances and parking, so navigate to the specific attraction.
