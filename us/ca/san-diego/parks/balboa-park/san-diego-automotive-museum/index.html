San Diego Automotive Museum

San Diego, CA
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Outdoor areas use posted City park hours, while museums, gardens, visitor services, zoo, theaters, sports facilities, and attractions keep separate schedules. The free tram normally runs daily from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.

What people say

San Diego Automotive Museum presents changing displays of cars and motorcycles plus interactive automotive experiences in Balboa Park's Palisades area. The museum is at 2080 Pan American Plaza, beside the Air & Space Museum and close to the Palisades tram stop. The museum is normally open 10:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. with last entry at 4:30 p.m. and is closed Thanksgiving and Christmas; verify Monday operations because official pages currently differ. Organizations must reserve at least two weeks ahead. Bring a same-day printed Balboa Park parking receipt for the museum's currently advertised admission discount.

How much is San Diego Automotive Museum parking in 2026? +

Paid parking remains active through December 31, 2026. Road meters are $2.50 per hour, and lots range from $5-$16 depending on residency, location, and duration. Lower Inspiration Point gives nonresidents three free hours; verified San Diego residents receive free or discounted lot parking.

Is there a free shuttle inside San Diego Automotive Museum? +

Yes. The free accessible tram normally runs daily from 8 a.m.-8 p.m. every 10-15 minutes from Inspiration Point, serving Plaza de Panama, the Organ Pavilion, and Pan American Plaza. It may pause for holidays or special events.
