Balboa Park

San Diego

For museums and visitor information, start at the House of Hospitality near Plaza de Panama. For easier parking and the free tram, start at Inspiration Point at Park Boulevard and Presidents Way. Route directly to the zoo, playground, trail, garden, or sports facility when that is your priority.

Mission Bay Park

San Diego

Choose a named destination before navigating: Bonita Cove, Crown Point, De Anza Cove, Fiesta Island, Hospitality Point, Mariner's Point, Mission Point, Ski Beach, Tecolote Shores, Vacation Isle, or another mapped site. The general park pin covers more than 4,200 acres.

Waterfront Park

San Diego

Use 1600 Pacific Highway, San Diego, CA 92101. The north side contains active recreation and dog facilities; the south side contains the children's play zone and picnic areas.
