Waterfront Park

Banker's Hill, San Diego, CA
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Waterfront Park is open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Fountain reflecting pools normally operate 9:30 a.m.-6:00 p.m.; as of July 31, 2026, the jets remain temporarily off for repair.

What people say

Waterfront Park surrounds San Diego's historic County Administration Center with bay views, large civic lawns, an interactive playground, reflecting pools and splash fountains, restrooms, picnic areas, public art, native garden, off-leash dog area, basketball and pickleball courts, fitness equipment, table tennis, bocce, chess, snack bar, and frequent events.

What address should I use for Waterfront Park? +

Use 1600 Pacific Highway, San Diego, CA 92101. The north side contains active recreation and dog facilities; the south side contains the children's play zone and picnic areas.

What hours is San Diego Waterfront Park open? +

The park is open daily from 6 a.m.-10 p.m. The office, information booth, reflecting pools, fountain jets, equipment loans, snack bar, and events use shorter or separate hours.

Are the Waterfront Park fountains working? +

The reflecting pools are currently operating daily from 9:30 a.m.-6 p.m., but the north and south fountain jets remain temporarily off for repairs as of July 31, 2026. Check the official page before traveling specifically for water play.

Where should I park for Waterfront Park? +

Use metered street spaces or nearby paid lots; the County recommends transit. The Little Italy trolley stop and MTS bus routes 280, 290, 923, and 992 serve the area.

Where is the playground at Waterfront Park? +

The children's play zone is on the south side near picnic areas, chess and bocce. The north recreation area has basketball, pickleball, fitness equipment, table tennis, a native garden, dog area, restroom, and information booth.

Is there an off-leash dog area at Waterfront Park? +

Yes. The north recreation area has a designated off-leash enclosure. Dogs must remain on a leash no longer than six feet everywhere else and are not allowed in the fountains.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
San Diego County Parks ↗
Can I reserve a picnic area at Waterfront Park? +

Yes. Picnic areas near the play zone and six Civic Greens can be reserved. Groups of 100 or more, cooking, catering, alcohol, glass, amplified sound, large equipment, or performances require permits.

Do events close Waterfront Park? +

Yes. Concerts, festivals, setup, maintenance, weather, and private reservations can close all or part of the park. Check the official closure notice and event calendar before a special trip.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Are there restrooms at San Diego Waterfront Park? +

Yes. Public restrooms and diaper-changing space are in the park's central fountain and playground area near the County Administration Center. Rinse showers are on the outside wall of the restroom building. Event closures or maintenance can affect access even while other lawns remain open.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
San Diego County Parks ↗
Where can I find shade or weather shelter at San Diego Waterfront Park? +

Trees provide limited shade near portions of the playground, gardens, and picnic areas, but the Civic Greens, fountains, courts, dog park, and much of the waterfront lawn are exposed. Personal shade canopies are allowed on grass when placed within the park's stake restrictions. The County Administration Center is not a guaranteed public refuge, so identify a nearby open substantial building before visiting during extreme heat, lightning, heavy rain, or high wind.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
San Diego County Parks ↗
What are the walking surfaces and accessible routes at San Diego Waterfront Park? +

The downtown civic park is generally flat and developed, with broad paved plazas, sidewalks, promenade routes, and paths connecting the County Administration Center, Civic Greens, playground, fountains, restrooms, courts, dog park, and bayfront. Fountain spray can leave nearby pavement wet and slippery, while lawns, play surfacing, gardens, event fencing, and temporary stages differ from the main hard-surface route. Use the paved perimeter and marked accessible approaches during events or maintenance closures.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
San Diego County Parks ↗
Which trolley station is closest to San Diego Waterfront Park? +

Use County Center/Little Italy station on the UC San Diego Blue Line or Green Line. MTS places Waterfront Park about 0.2 miles from the station, beside the County Administration Center. Santa Fe Depot is another practical connection for the park's southern side and regional rail. Check live service and event notices because downtown festivals and waterfront celebrations can increase crowds or alter access.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
San Diego Metropolitan Transit System ↗
Where are the main features in San Diego Waterfront Park? +

The historic County Administration Center is the park's central landmark, with lawns and the long interactive fountain extending toward the bay. The playground and garden rooms occupy separate areas, while the northeast corner contains the active recreation zone with basketball, pickleball, fitness equipment, and an off-leash dog area. Check the fountain update before visiting because north and south basins can operate independently during maintenance.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
County of San Diego Parks and Recreation ↗
How much does Waterfront Park cost? +

General park, playground, lawns, courts, fitness equipment, dog area, and ordinary fountain access are free. Nearby parking, reservations, concessions, permitted events, and special programs may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026

How accessible is Waterfront Park? +

Accessibility varies by entrance, route, terrain, and destination. Review the exact mapped subsite and its official source before relying on a specific accessible route.
