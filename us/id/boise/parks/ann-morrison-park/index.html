Ann Morrison Park

Hours ⌄

What people say

Ann Morrison Park is a 153-acre Boise River park with one of the city's largest playgrounds, Greenbelt paths, the seasonal river-float takeout, 5.4-acre Dog Island off-leash area, disc golf, sports fields and courts, outdoor fitness, fountain, picnic pavilion, restrooms, and large open lawns.

Where should I enter Ann Morrison Park? +

Use 1000 South Americana Boulevard for the general park. During official Boise River float season, follow signs to the designated takeout and shuttle parking rather than stopping along active park roads.

What hours is Ann Morrison Park open? +

Most of the park is open from sunrise to sunset. Lighted ballfields may operate until 11:00 p.m. when games are scheduled; that extension does not keep the whole park open late.

Is there a large playground at Ann Morrison Park? +

Yes. Ann Morrison has one of Boise's largest playgrounds for children 12 and under, along with broad lawns, sports courts, disc golf, paths, and nearby restroom access.

Where can dogs go off leash at Ann Morrison Park? +

Together Treasure Valley Dog Island is a 5.4-acre, year-round off-leash area within the park. Outside the designated area, follow Boise leash rules and protect river, sports, and family-use spaces.

Where does the Boise River float end? +

The official Barber Park-to-Ann Morrison float ends at Ann Morrison Park during float season. A paid shuttle normally departs from Ann Morrison for Barber Park on a seasonal schedule; confirm river conditions and shuttle operations on the day of the trip.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Are there restrooms at Ann Morrison Park? +

Yes. The city's Greenbelt guide lists restrooms among the park's core amenities. Temporary river-float operations and reserved events may add facilities, but do not assume those are available outside their season.

Can I reserve the Ann Morrison pavilion? +

Yes. Old Timer's Pavilion is reservable in half-day or full-day blocks and supports large groups. Current fees, permits, event rules, and availability are managed by Boise Parks and Recreation.

What changes seasonally at Ann Morrison Park? +

River floating, shuttle service, fountains or water features, field schedules, concessions, and special events are seasonal. High or hazardous river flows can suspend floating even while the general park remains open.

Where should I park at Ann Morrison Park? +

Free general parking is distributed inside the park near the playground, sports areas, pavilion, Dog Island, and river-float takeout. Choose the lot nearest your activity and follow temporary signs during float season and major events, when roads, shuttle stops, general parking, and accessible spaces may be reorganized.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Boise ↗
Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Ann Morrison Park? +

Old Timer's Pavilion is the park's main reservable covered gathering space and holds up to 500 people; Dog Island also has smaller shelters, and mature trees shade portions of the Greenbelt and picnic landscape. Sports fields, disc golf, river-float areas, playgrounds, courts, and broad lawns are exposed. The pavilion is not a designated storm shelter, so use an open substantial building or vehicle during lightning, river flooding, extreme heat, or high wind.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Boise ↗
What are the walking and biking surfaces at Ann Morrison Park? +

The paved Boise River Greenbelt provides the park's most dependable flat route and connects major entrances, river access, nearby parks, and downtown. Paved and developed internal walks reach playgrounds, shelters, sports areas, parking, and Dog Island, while disc-golf fairways, lawns, gravel edges, and riverbank approaches can be uneven, muddy, flooded, or icy. The park is large and Greenbelt users move at different speeds, so keep right, announce passes, and check river or construction closures before expecting a continuous route.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Boise ↗
Does Ann Morrison Park have a splash pad or place to swim? +

Ann Morrison Park now has an interactive seasonal fountain with water jets reaching nearly seven feet, but it is not a supervised swimming pool. Fountain operation can change with weather, maintenance, and seasonal schedules. The Boise River float also ends at the park during the official season; that moving-water activity requires current river-condition checks, a properly fitted life jacket, and use of designated entry and exit points.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Boise ↗
What is the landmark fountain at Ann Morrison Park? +

The park's central landmark is the large fountain honoring Ann Morrison, whose husband Harry W. Morrison donated the park in her memory when it opened in 1959. The fountain is now an interactive seasonal feature, making it both the park's visual centerpiece and a place for water play. Treat the operating schedule separately from general park hours and use the broad fountain area as an orientation point for the playground, fields, and Greenbelt connections.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Boise ↗
How accessible is Ann Morrison Park? +

The park has paved roads and Greenbelt paths, accessible parking and restrooms, and a large inclusive playground with ramped and ground-level play. Distances across the 153-acre park are substantial, so navigate to the exact playground, Dog Island, field, pavilion, fountain, or river-float destination rather than relying on the general park pin.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 8, 2026
City of Boise Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Ann Morrison Park free? +

General park entry, playground use, paths, Dog Island, fountain viewing, disc golf, and ordinary drop-in recreation are free. Pavilion reservations, organized field use, permits, river-float shuttle service, concessions, and special events can charge separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 8, 2026
