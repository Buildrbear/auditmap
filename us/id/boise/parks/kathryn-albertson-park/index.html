What people say

Kathryn Albertson Park is a 41-acre Boise urban wildlife habitat with two ponds, natural areas, paved walking paths, open lawns, shelters, gazebos, restrooms, and frequent waterfowl and wildlife viewing. Interior paths prohibit dogs and bicycles to protect habitat.

Where is the entrance to Kathryn Albertson Park? +

Use 1001 South Americana Boulevard. The park is adjacent to Ann Morrison Park but has its own quieter wildlife-focused entrance, parking, and internal path system.

The principal internal walking routes are paved and wind around ponds, natural habitat, lawns, gazebos, and viewing areas. Conditions can still include wet leaves, winter ice, temporary wildlife closures, and short grades.

Are dogs allowed at Kathryn Albertson Park? +

Dogs are not allowed from March 1 through June 30 during waterfowl nesting season. At other times dogs must remain leashed. Bicycling, skating, organized sports, swimming, boating, and fishing are prohibited inside this wildlife-focused park.

Is Kathryn Albertson Park good for wildlife viewing? +

Yes. The park was designed as an urban wildlife habitat around ponds, wet areas, mature vegetation, and quieter paths. Visit calmly near dawn or dusk, remain on paths, and do not approach or feed wildlife.

Why might paths close at Kathryn Albertson Park? +

Boise can close park sections for wildlife, maintenance, or safety. Waterfowl nesting protections are especially important in spring; obey temporary barriers and give nesting birds a wide distance.

Can I reserve Kathryn Albertson Park for a wedding or gathering? +

The park may be reserved for formal ceremonies only, not receptions. Chairs are discouraged; up to 10 may be hand-carried for guests with mobility needs and must remain on paved surfaces rather than turf. The Rookery and Eyrie gazebos are the principal ceremony locations.

Is there parking at Kathryn Albertson Park? +

Yes. The official facility map identifies on-site parking near the Americana Boulevard entrance, along with drinking fountains and restrooms. Park here for the interior walking loops rather than assuming the adjacent Ann Morrison Park lots are the same destination.

Is there a playground at Kathryn Albertson Park? +

No conventional playground is listed at Kathryn Albertson Park. Boise describes it as a quiet wildlife and contemplation park with wide paved footpaths, ponds, interpretive signs, seating, and reservable outdoor gazebos; organized sports, bicycles, skating, swimming, boating, and fishing are prohibited. For equipment-based play, use a different Boise park such as nearby Ann Morrison or Julia Davis. At Kathryn Albertson, help children stay on paths and avoid disturbing nesting birds, ponds, and sensitive habitat.

Can I take a bus to Kathryn Albertson Park? +

Yes. Valley Regional Transit Route 4 Franklin stops on Americana Boulevard at Kathryn Albertson Park and connects with Main Street Station. The park entrance and loop are west of Americana, so allow a short walk from the stop. Route frequency is limited compared with a major-city trunk line; check the live schedule and return time before entering the park, especially evenings and weekends.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Is there a splash pad or swimming at Kathryn Albertson Park? +

No. Kathryn Albertson is a wildlife and quiet-use park with decorative and habitat ponds, fountains, overlooks, boardwalks, and walking paths, but no splash pad or pool. Boise explicitly prohibits swimming, boating, and fishing there. Keep children on developed paths and viewing decks rather than approaching pond edges. For water play, choose a separately listed Boise splash pad such as Borah or another current City aquatic facility.

What are the main landmarks in Kathryn Albertson Park? +

The park is a quiet wildlife and landscape destination rather than a public-art park. Its two named orientation landmarks are Rookery Gazebo and Eyrie Gazebo, set among ponds, paths, fountains, and viewing areas. Use the official facility map to choose a gazebo; formal ceremonies may be reserved, but receptions and ordinary recreation such as biking, skating, swimming, boating, and fishing are prohibited.

How much does Kathryn Albertson Park cost? +

General park entry, parking, wildlife viewing, paths, shelters, and gazebo use are free unless reserved or permitted for an organized activity.
