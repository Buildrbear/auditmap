Park

Water Works Park

2201 George Flagg Parkway, Des Moines, IA 50321
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Water Works Park is open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Concerts, Biergarten, stages, rentals, and special events use separate schedules and can change road or parking access.
Navigate

What people say

Des Moines Water Works Park is a 1,500-acre Raccoon River landscape with extensive paved trail connections, Reichardt Family Natural Play Area, picnic tables and shelter, two enclosed restroom facilities, Lauridsen Amphitheater, Killinger Family Stage, Des Moines Biergarten, bike-share pad, broad lawns, wooded and river areas, and event spaces connecting toward Gray's Lake.

What address should I use for Water Works Park? +

Use 2201 George Flagg Parkway, Des Moines, IA 50321. For concerts, route to Lauridsen Amphitheater or the event instructions because temporary entrances, traffic control, and parking reservations can differ from normal park access.

Where should I park at Water Works Park? +

Multiple on-site lots serve the park, with paved and accessible parking near Lauridsen Amphitheater, Killinger Family Stage, and the Biergarten. Concert parking can be reserved and lots may open only two hours before showtime.

Can I bike through Water Works Park? +

Yes. Paved regional trails cross the park and connect toward Gray's Lake and central Des Moines. Stay on trails after normal park hours, and expect event detours, river flooding, or maintenance to affect individual segments.

Are there restrooms at Water Works Park? +

Yes. The park has two enclosed restroom facilities serving developed recreation and event areas. They are not distributed across all 1,500 acres, and event or winter operations may affect access.

Is there a playground at Water Works Park? +

The Reichardt Family Natural Play Area offers nature-based family play rather than a conventional neighborhood playground. It sits within the broader park near developed visitor amenities and trail connections.

Can I picnic at Water Works Park? +

Yes. Picnic tables and a shelter are available, with the shelter also offered for private rentals. Tailgating and camping are prohibited, and concert events may restrict outside food, alcohol, or access to reserved areas.

What accessible facilities are at Water Works Park? +

Paved lots with accessible spaces serve the amphitheater, stage, and Biergarten, and paved trails link major developed areas. The vast natural landscape includes longer distances and variable surfaces away from those hubs.

Is Water Works Park free? +

General entry, normal public parking, trails, natural play area, picnic areas, and open landscape access are free. Concerts, event parking, food, shelter rentals, bike share, and private programs can charge.

What can close roads or trails at Water Works Park? +

Concerts, festivals, reserved parking, Raccoon River flooding, storms, utility operations, and trail work can change access. Event lots may open two hours before a show and close one hour afterward, so normal parking guidance may not apply.

Where can visitors find shade or weather shelter at Water Works Park? +

Wooded sections and mature trees provide seasonal shade, and a picnic shelter serves the developed family area. The Lauridsen Amphitheater and Killinger Family Stage are event structures with separate schedules and should not be assumed available as general shelter. Broad lawns, play areas, trails, and river edges remain exposed; during flooding, lightning, or dangerous wind, leave low areas and use a substantial building or vehicle rather than a picnic roof or tree.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Water Works Park Foundation ↗
Where can dogs go at Water Works Park, and what rules apply? +

Dogs may use Water Works Park when controlled on a leash no longer than 6 feet; animals may not run at large except in an area specifically designated for off-leash use. No off-leash area is identified for this park, so keep the leash attached throughout the visit and remove waste. Ticketed concerts prohibit pets inside the concert venue except service animals, even though the surrounding public park ordinarily allows leashed dogs.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Is there a splash pad or swimming at Des Moines Water Works Park? +

No splash pad, public pool, or designated swimming beach is listed at Des Moines Water Works Park. The 1,500-acre park is designed around trails, woodland, natural play, events, picnics, and the Raccoon River; the river is not a staffed family swimming area and can rise or flood rapidly. Do not confuse this park with Waterworks Park Splash Pad in Grimes, which is a different city and destination.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Water Works Park Foundation ↗
Can I take DART to Water Works Park? +

Do not assume special-event buses are regular daily service. DART has provided dedicated Water Works Park shuttles for events such as RAGBRAI, but those trips operate only when announced. Use DART's current trip planner with 2201 George Flagg Parkway and verify whether a normal route and safe final walk are available for your date; otherwise arrange a ride, and confirm event-specific transit separately when attending a concert or festival.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Des Moines Area Regional Transit Authority ↗
What landmarks should I find in Water Works Park? +

The Lauridsen Amphitheater, Manatt Great Lawn, and Killinger Family Stage form the main event core, with the Des Moines Biergarten under trees to the east and the Reichardt Family Natural Play Area nearby. River Constellation is the park's defining public artwork: 45 illuminated poles create a walk-through light-and-sound sculpture near the amphitheater. The wider 1,500-acre riverside park has trails and natural areas, so route to the amphitheater or play area for developed amenities.
