Gray's Lake Park

Des Moines

Use 2101 Fleur Drive, Des Moines, IA 50321. The park has multiple parking lots serving the beach and playground side and the terrace and trail side, so follow signs for the feature you plan to use.

Pappajohn Sculpture Park

Des Moines

The park is at 1330 Grand Avenue in downtown Des Moines, within Western Gateway Park. It is an open landscape with access from Grand Avenue and surrounding streets rather than a ticketed building entrance.

Water Works Park

Des Moines

Use 2201 George Flagg Parkway, Des Moines, IA 50321. For concerts, route to Lauridsen Amphitheater or the event instructions because temporary entrances, traffic control, and parking reservations can differ from normal park access.
