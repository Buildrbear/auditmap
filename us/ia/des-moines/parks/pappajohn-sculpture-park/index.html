1330 Grand Avenue, Des Moines, IA 50309
Hours ⌄

What people say

Pappajohn Sculpture Park is Des Moines' free 4.4-acre downtown art landscape, displaying more than 30 works by internationally known artists in an accessible pedestrian setting within Western Gateway Park, with lawns, paths, a mobile visitor guide, virtual tour, bike access, transit connections, and metered street parking.

Where is Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

The park is at 1330 Grand Avenue in downtown Des Moines, within Western Gateway Park. It is an open landscape with access from Grand Avenue and surrounding streets rather than a ticketed building entrance.

Where should I park for Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

Ample metered street parking surrounds the park. Read current space signs and payment hours; downtown events can remove spaces or change rates, so use a nearby public garage if curb parking is full.

Can I reach Pappajohn Sculpture Park by transit or bike? +

Yes. Grand Avenue bike lanes serve the park, and DART routes connect Western Gateway with downtown. The park is also walkable from central downtown hotels, offices, library, and nearby cultural destinations.

How do I walk through Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

Pedestrian paths and surrounding sidewalks cross the compact 4.4-acre landscape, while broad lawns provide additional viewing space. Use the Art Center's mobile visitor guide to identify works and artists without a fixed tour route.

Is Pappajohn Sculpture Park accessible? +

Yes. The Art Center describes it as an accessible, pedestrian-friendly setting. Street crossings, lawn conditions, temporary events, and winter weather can still affect the easiest route between individual works.

Can I touch the sculptures at Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

No. Touching, climbing, or sitting on sculptures is prohibited because it damages the work, except for Scott Burton's Seating for Eight, which is designed for use. Photography is allowed.

Is Pappajohn Sculpture Park free? +

Yes. The outdoor park, artwork viewing, mobile guide, and virtual tour are free. Metered street parking, organized events, commercial activity, and nearby businesses have separate costs.

What can restrict access at Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

Art conservation, lawn restoration, permitted events, severe weather, snow or ice, and downtown street closures can affect individual works or paths. Never cross barriers or touch artwork even when a sculpture appears durable.

Can I picnic at Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

Yes. Picnicking is allowed, but food and drinks must stay away from the sculptures and waste belongs in receptacles around the park perimeter. Glass bottles are prohibited, and alcohol requires a permit.

Is Pappajohn Sculpture Park good for children? +

Families are welcome, and the Art Center provides a printable exploration guide with movement, observation, and discussion activities for all ages. This is an art park rather than a playground: children must not climb, sit on, or touch sculptures except Scott Burton's Seating for Eight.

Are there public restrooms at Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

Do not plan on a dedicated public restroom inside the open-air sculpture park; the official visitor page does not list one. The adjacent Central Library and nearby businesses have their own hours and access rules, so use a staffed downtown facility before entering the park if restroom access is important.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Des Moines Art Center ↗
Is there shade or weather shelter at Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

The 4.4-acre sculpture park is an open outdoor gallery of lawns, berms, paths, and monumental art with only limited young-tree shade. The official visitor guide identifies no pavilion, restroom, or indoor visitor building inside the park. The adjacent Central Library and nearby businesses have separate hours and access, while the Des Moines Art Center museum is several miles west; during dangerous heat, lightning, hail, or high wind, leave the park and use a confirmed-open downtown building rather than standing near trees or sculpture.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Des Moines Art Center ↗
Are dogs allowed at Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

Leashed dogs may accompany visitors in the outdoor city sculpture park, but it is not an off-leash area. Keep pets away from every artwork, pedestal, planting bed, and food setup, remove waste at the perimeter receptacles, and never allow climbing or contact with a sculpture. The separate Des Moines Art Center museum permits only service dogs indoors.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Des Moines Parks and Recreation ↗
Is there a splash pad or swimming area at Pappajohn Sculpture Park? +

No. Pappajohn is a 4.4-acre outdoor sculpture and landscape park with no listed pool, splash pad, or public water-play feature. Plan for art viewing, photography, a family exploration guide, or a picnic while keeping food, children, and pets away from the artworks. Use a separately listed Des Moines aquatic facility when cooling off in water is the purpose of the outing.
