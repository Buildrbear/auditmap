Park

Gray's Lake Park

2101 Fleur Drive, Des Moines, IA 50321
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Gray's Lake Park generally uses extended hours: 5:00 a.m. to midnight April through October and 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. during the rest of the year. Beach, concessions, rentals, and water play use seasonal schedules.

What people say

Gray's Lake Park is Des Moines' 166.6-acre central lake park, with a lighted walking and bike loop, pedestrian bridge, accessible playground and seasonal sprayground, sandy beach with weekly summer water testing, boat and bike rentals, concessions, terrace, restrooms, fountains, parking lots, open lawns, and trail connections toward downtown and Water Works Park.

What address should I use for Gray's Lake Park? +

Use 2101 Fleur Drive, Des Moines, IA 50321. The park has multiple parking lots serving the beach and playground side and the terrace and trail side, so follow signs for the feature you plan to use.

Is parking free at Gray's Lake Park? +

Yes. The City lists public parking lots at the park, with access on different sides of the lake. Summer weekends and organized runs can fill the closest lots, so use the alternate lot rather than parking illegally on nearby roads.

Can I walk or bike around Gray's Lake? +

Yes. A paved shared walking and bike trail loops the lake and crosses the signature pedestrian bridge, with connections toward downtown and Water Works Park. It is heavily used, so keep right and expect congestion near family areas.

Are there restrooms at Gray's Lake Park? +

Yes. Public restrooms serve the developed beach, terrace, parking, and recreation areas. Seasonal winterization and concession schedules can affect nearby indoor services, so do not assume every building is open year-round.

Is there an accessible playground at Gray's Lake? +

Yes. The park's accessible playground includes two toddler bucket swings and is beside the newer sprayground near the beach-side parking area.

Is there a sprayground at Gray's Lake Park? +

Yes. A new sprayground opened beside the accessible playground near the beach parking lot in May 2026. Water operation is seasonal and can pause for weather, maintenance, or water-system issues.

What is accessible at Gray's Lake Park? +

The park lists an accessible playground, paved trail and bridge, parking, restrooms, terrace, and developed beach facilities. Watercraft and concession operations have separate accessibility and seasonal conditions.

Is Gray's Lake Park free? +

General entry, parking, trail, bridge, playground, sprayground, beach, and open space are free. Boat and bike rentals, concessions, private reservations, and organized trail events charge separately.

How do I check if the Gray's Lake beach is safe? +

From Memorial Day through Labor Day, Des Moines conducts weekly beach water-quality monitoring. Advisories appear on the official park page, at the beach, and on City social channels; the Iowa DNR hotline is 515-725-3434.

Where can visitors find shade or weather shelter at Gray's Lake Park? +

The Christine Hensley Terrace includes a purpose-built shade structure beside the concessions and restroom building, and scattered trees shade portions of the grounds. The beach, bridge, lake loop, sprayground, and broad lawns are mostly exposed. The terrace cover is useful for sun or ordinary rain but not lightning or dangerous wind; leave the waterfront and use an open substantial building or vehicle during severe weather.

Where can dogs go at Gray's Lake Park, and what rules apply? +

Leashed pets may use the lake loop and ordinary park grounds, and owners must clean up and remove waste. Dogs are not allowed on the beach or on watercraft rented from the City, and Gray's Lake has no designated off-leash area. Keep the leash attached for the entire park visit and use another facility for off-leash exercise.

Can I reserve a gathering space at Gray's Lake Park? +

Yes. The park's named reservable gathering space is Christine Hensley Terrace and its Terrace building; the City also rents designated open-space areas. For a casual picnic, use available park seating or lawn without claiming an event area, obstructing the lake loop, or bringing an organized setup. Reserve the Terrace for guaranteed use, and obtain the required permit for alcohol, large groups, trail activities, equipment, or a structured event; the beach, boat-rental area, bridges, and playground are separate public amenities.

Can I take a bus to Gray's Lake Park? +

Yes, with a short final walk. DART Route 8 travels between DART Central Station and the south side along Thomas Beck Road and Fleur Drive, passing the east side of Gray's Lake. Use the live planner for 2101 Fleur Drive or Christine Hensley Terrace and confirm the closest active stop, because DART replaced its network and moved many stops in June 2026. The park loop and beach lie beyond Fleur Drive, so allow time to reach the exact amenity.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Des Moines Area Regional Transit Authority ↗
What is the signature landmark at Gray's Lake Park? +

The pedestrian bridge across Gray's Lake is the park's signature visual anchor and part of the illuminated paved loop used by walkers and cyclists. For family amenities, use the beach-side area with the accessible playground and seasonal sprayground. For gatherings and concessions, navigate to Christine Hensley Terrace. The trail network also connects toward downtown, Gray's Station, and Water Works Park, so the bridge is a useful orientation point rather than the only destination.
