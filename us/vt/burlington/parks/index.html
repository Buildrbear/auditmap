Oakledge Park

Burlington

Admission is free when arriving on foot or by bicycle. Vehicle parking costs $3 per hour from May through October 31 and is free from November through April; shelter reservations and programs are separate.

Waterfront Park

Burlington

Use the City of Burlington's waterfront parking map and posted lot signs. Paid public lots are distributed along Lake Street and the waterfront, and event days can change availability or access.
