Current city pages

Brackenridge Park

San Antonio

The general park is free and open daily 5:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; individual attractions keep separate hours.

Hemisfair

San Antonio

Open daily 5:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; splash pads operate 9:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. March-October, subject to weather and maintenance.

Pearsall Park

San Antonio

Open daily 5:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; splash pads operate 9:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. March-October, subject to weather and maintenance.

Phil Hardberger Park

San Antonio

Open sunrise to sunset daily; both dog parks are also open sunrise to sunset.

San Antonio Missions National Historical Park

San Antonio

Park facilities are generally open 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m.; Mission San Juan and Mission Espada contact stations are 10:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Grounds are sunrise to sunset and close on Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day.

San Antonio River Walk

San Antonio

Downtown River Walk and Museum Reach are open 24 hours; Mission Reach is open daylight hours only. Businesses, boats, restrooms, parks, and attractions keep separate schedules.

San Pedro Springs Park

San Antonio

Park hours are 5:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. daily. During the current 2026 outdoor season, San Pedro Springs Pool is posted open 1:00-8:00 p.m. daily except Tuesday, subject to staffing, weather, maintenance, and seasonal changes.

Woodlawn Lake Park

San Antonio

Open daily 5:00 a.m.-midnight. Pool hours are seasonal; current posted open swim is 1:00-8:00 p.m. daily except Monday, with weekday morning lap swim.
