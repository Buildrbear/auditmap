Woodlawn Lake walking trail

1103 Cincinnati Avenue, San Antonio, TX 78201
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open daily 5:00 a.m.-midnight. Pool hours are seasonal; current posted open swim is 1:00-8:00 p.m. daily except Monday, with weekday morning lap swim.
Navigate

What people say

The paved lake loop connects the playground, pool, dog park, gym, Island House, fitness stations, sports facilities, fishing, and neighborhood entrances. Swimming is allowed only in the staffed pool, not the lake. Verify seasonal pool status and expect extreme crowding and access changes during major civic events.
