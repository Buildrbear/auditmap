What people say

The 1.5-acre dog park uses a combined large- and small-dog area rather than fully separated enclosures; leash dogs immediately outside it. Shade is limited in many major activity zones. Bring water, verify splash-pad status, check surface heat, and do not assume the playground, skate park, and fitness areas share one parking lot.
