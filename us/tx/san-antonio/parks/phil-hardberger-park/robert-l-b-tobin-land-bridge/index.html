destination

Robert L.B. Tobin Land Bridge

13203 Blanco Road and 8400 NW Military Highway, San Antonio, TX
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open sunrise to sunset daily; both dog parks are also open sunrise to sunset.
Navigate

What people say

The broad vegetated Tobin Land Bridge carries people and wildlife over Wurzbach Parkway and connects the east and west trail networks. Treat Hardberger as a natural-area park, not a water-play destination. Stay on designated trails, carry water, avoid midday heat, and leave before sunset.

Where should I park for Robert L.B. Tobin Land Bridge? +

Choose the east entrance at 13203 Blanco Road or west entrance at 8400 NW Military Highway based on the feature you want. The Land Bridge connects the two sides on foot, not by moving your parked car.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

When is Robert L.B. Tobin Land Bridge open? +

Open sunrise to sunset daily; both dog parks are also open sunrise to sunset.

Are there restrooms near Robert L.B. Tobin Land Bridge? +

Restrooms, portable toilets, fountains, and parking are available at developed nodes, not along every natural trail.

Is Robert L.B. Tobin Land Bridge free? +

General park, trails, Land Bridge, Skywalk, playgrounds, dog parks, and picnic use are free; reservations and programs may charge.

How accessible is Robert L.B. Tobin Land Bridge? +

Developed areas and the Land Bridge have accessible routes, while natural trails vary in width, slope, rock, roots, and weather impacts.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Robert L.B. Tobin Land Bridge? +

Dogs may use designated off-leash parks on both sides and must be leashed elsewhere. Separate small- and all-dog areas are provided.

Is Robert L.B. Tobin Land Bridge good for children? +

Nature play, two playgrounds, Land Bridge wildlife crossings, Skywalk, short trails, picnics, and ecology programs suit families; there is no splash pad or pool.

How do you reach Robert L.B. Tobin Land Bridge? +

This is primarily a driving or biking destination. Select east or west before routing and account for the long internal trail connection.
