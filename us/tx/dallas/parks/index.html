Dallas

Park and trail hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. daily; the recreation center, pool, pavilion, and programs keep shorter schedules.

Dallas

Dallas

Grounds are generally open 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. through Gates 5 or 6; museums, attractions, events, and buildings keep separate schedules.
