What people say

The Victory Park end connects the trail to downtown destinations, American Airlines Center, transit, and busy event-day streets. The trail is linear and has no single parking destination. Peak-hour crowding, heat, darkness, street crossings, and mixed walking/running/cycling speeds require awareness.
