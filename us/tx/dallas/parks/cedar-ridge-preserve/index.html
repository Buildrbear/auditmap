Cedar Ridge Preserve

7171 Mountain Creek Parkway, Dallas, TX 75249
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open Tuesday-Sunday 6:30 a.m. to dusk and closed every Monday; special holiday and weather closures are posted separately.

What people say

A rugged North Texas nature preserve with escarpment forest, prairie, ponds, overlooks, elevation changes, native plants, wildlife, and a branching hiking-trail network.

When is Cedar Ridge Preserve open? +

Open Tuesday-Sunday 6:30 a.m. to dusk and closed every Monday; special holiday and weather closures are posted separately.

Where should I park for Cedar Ridge Preserve? +

The gravel lot and marked spaces fill quickly. Carpool on weekends; official overflow parking is at Park in the Woods Recreation Center, 6801 Mountain Creek Parkway, subject to current signs.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Audubon Dallas ↗
What is the best entrance for Cedar Ridge Preserve? +

Start at 7171 Mountain Creek Parkway and photograph the trail map before hiking. Multiple loops and connectors make a planned route essential.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Audubon Dallas ↗
Are there restrooms at Cedar Ridge Preserve? +

Basic visitor facilities are near the entrance; trail sections have no restrooms or water. Carry enough water and return before dusk.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Audubon Dallas ↗
Is Cedar Ridge Preserve free? +

Admission is free; a $5 or greater donation is suggested to support volunteer maintenance and conservation.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Audubon Dallas ↗
How accessible is Cedar Ridge Preserve? +

This is a nature preserve rather than a conventional park. Many trails are rocky, steep, narrow, muddy, or rooted and should be matched to each visitor's ability.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Audubon Dallas ↗
Are dogs allowed at Cedar Ridge Preserve? +

Follow the preserve's current pet rules and all posted habitat restrictions. Wildlife protection and trail congestion take priority over recreation.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Audubon Dallas ↗
Is Cedar Ridge Preserve good for children? +

Older children who can manage hills and heat may enjoy ponds, wildlife, fossils, and overlooks; this is not a playground or stroller-oriented destination.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Audubon Dallas ↗
How do I reach Cedar Ridge Preserve without a car? +

The preserve is primarily reached by car. Arrange return transportation and do not rely on informal neighborhood parking.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Audubon Dallas ↗
What should I know before visiting Cedar Ridge Preserve? +

Closed Mondays. Parking, heat, steep rock, snakes, ticks, mud, and early darkness are the main planning issues; check the preserve's live banner before leaving home.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Audubon Dallas ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Cedar Ridge Preserve? +

Check Dallas's hourly heat index, lightning, severe-storm and heavy-rain alerts, flash-flood risk, air quality, and current facility notices. Pavement, play equipment, rock, and exposed trails become dangerously hot, while low trails and creeks may flood quickly.
