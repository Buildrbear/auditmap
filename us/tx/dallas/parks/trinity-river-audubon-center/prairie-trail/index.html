What people say

Prairie paths cross open habitat with strong sun, grasses, birds, and seasonal wildflowers; carry water and sun protection. Reserve or confirm admission, check rental closures and weather, bring water, and leave trails before the gate locks. Trails close for unsafe mud, flooding, storms, heat, maintenance, or habitat protection.

Where should I park for Prairie Trail? +

Use the gated on-site lot at 6500 Great Trinity Forest Way, also searchable as 6500 South Loop 12. The vehicle gate closes with the center, so return before closing.

Restrooms and water are centered at the visitor building; carry water on trails and do not expect facilities at every overlook.

Admission and ticket requirements can change. Reserve or confirm current general-admission tickets and program fees before driving to the gated site.

How accessible is Prairie Trail? +

The center has accessible architecture and trail options, but natural routes include heat, mud, uneven surfaces, seasonal water, and wildlife habitat.

Are dogs allowed at Prairie Trail? +

Pets are prohibited; approved service animals are permitted under center policy. This protects wildlife and other visitors.

Is Prairie Trail good for children? +

Short trails, ponds, birds, hands-on exhibits, and seasonal programs work well for families. The center expects an adult-to-child ratio of at least 1:6 for groups.
