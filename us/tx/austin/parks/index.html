Ann and Roy Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail

Austin

The Boardwalk is open 5:00 a.m.-midnight daily, with a midnight-5:00 a.m. curfew. Connected parks and facilities may close earlier.

Butler Metro Park

Austin

General park and Alliance Children's Garden hours are 5:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. unless posted otherwise; splash-pad and event operations are seasonal or separate.

Emma Long Metropolitan Park

Austin

Open year-round 7:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m.; the on-site office keeps shorter seasonal hours.

McKinney Falls State Park

Austin

Open daily; gate hours are 8:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. and the office is generally 8:00 a.m.-4:15 p.m. Visitor Center hours are shorter.

Mueller Lake Park

Austin

Open during posted public park hours; events, the farmers market, and managed facilities use separate schedules.

Pease District Park

Austin

Public areas follow posted park hours; Kingsbury Commons facilities, splash pad, reservations, and programs can use shorter or seasonal schedules.

Waterloo Park

Austin

The park is generally open daily during posted hours; Moody Amphitheater events and production closures use separate schedules and entry rules.

Zilker Metropolitan Park

Austin

General park hours are 5:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m.; Barton Springs Pool and individual attractions keep separate schedules.
