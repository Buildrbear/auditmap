What people say

Lebermann Plaza is a flexible paved gathering area near the amphitheater used for arrivals, programs, and event circulation. Check the park event calendar before visiting. Concert setup can close lawns or paths, and summer heat is strongest on exposed slopes despite substantial garden shade.
