Mueller Lake amphitheater

4550 Mueller Boulevard, Austin, TX 78723
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open during posted public park hours; events, the farmers market, and managed facilities use separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

The lakeside amphitheater lawn supports informal seating and programmed events, with little separation from paths and the water. The lake is visual habitat, not a swimming area. Shade is uneven, event days change parking and lawn access, and the nearby Bartholomew Splash Pad is a separate destination.

Where should I park for Mueller Lake amphitheater? +

Use marked street spaces and public parking near Mueller Boulevard, Simond Avenue, Aldrich Street, or Browning Hangar. Event and market days increase demand; do not use reserved residential or business spaces.
