What people say

Utility campsites provide water and electric service with vehicle and equipment limits; planned utility work can temporarily remove water service. Buy peak-season passes before travel, check boat-ramp and utility alerts, and confirm lake, storm, and water-quality conditions. Passes have no in/out privileges and capacity rules are enforced.

Where should I park for Emma Long utility campground? +

A paid entry pass is required. Peak-season Friday-Sunday and holiday visits require advance passes, often sell out, and do not allow roadside parking or entry without a valid pass.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

When is Emma Long utility campground open? +

Open year-round 7:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m.; the on-site office keeps shorter seasonal hours. This destination may have shorter, seasonal, event, maintenance, water-condition, or weather-dependent hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are there restrooms near Emma Long utility campground? +

Developed areas normally include toilets, showers, sinks, and water. Water utilities are planned to be shut off during August-October 2026 maintenance, leaving portable toilets while showers, sinks, fountains, and hose bibs are unavailable.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Emma Long utility campground free? +

Current day-use fees are generally $5 per vehicle Monday-Thursday, $10 Friday-Sunday and holidays, and $1 for walk/bike entry; camping and reservations cost extra.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Emma Long utility campground? +

Developed picnic, beach, parking, and restroom areas provide accessible features, but shorelines, campsites, slopes, and Turkey Creek trails vary substantially.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Emma Long utility campground? +

Leashed dogs may use allowed outdoor areas, but swimming-beach and facility restrictions may apply. Protect paws from heat and keep pets away from wildlife and unsafe water.

Is Emma Long utility campground good for children? +

The designated beach, picnic sites, shoreline, and camping support full-day family visits. There is no substitute for close water supervision, sun protection, and advance pass planning.
