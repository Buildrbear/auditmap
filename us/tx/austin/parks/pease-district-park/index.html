What people say

A Shoal Creek greenway park anchored by Kingsbury Commons, with imaginative play, a seasonal splash pad, Treehouse observation pod, nature play, basketball, lawns, Tudor Cottage, trails, and historic landscapes.

When is Pease District Park open? +

Public areas follow posted park hours; Kingsbury Commons facilities, splash pad, reservations, and programs can use shorter or seasonal schedules.

Where should I park for Pease District Park? +

Parking is extremely limited. Walk, bike, use transit, or follow Pease Park's current parking guidance; do not assume a space will be available beside Kingsbury Commons.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation and Pease Park Conservancy ↗
What is the best entrance for Pease District Park? +

Use 1100 Kingsbury Street for Kingsbury Commons. Trail users can enter from multiple Shoal Creek connections, but feature access and parking differ along the long park.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation and Pease Park Conservancy ↗
Are there restrooms at Pease District Park? +

Improved public restrooms are located at Kingsbury Commons. Northern trail sections have fewer facilities.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation and Pease Park Conservancy ↗
Is Pease District Park free? +

General park entry, play areas, and the city splash pad are free. Reservations and ticketed programs may cost extra.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation and Pease Park Conservancy ↗
How accessible is Pease District Park? +

Kingsbury Commons includes accessible routes and inclusive play elements. Shoal Creek trails, slopes, wet surfaces, and natural areas vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation and Pease Park Conservancy ↗
Are dogs allowed at Pease District Park? +

Leashed dogs may use ordinary park and trail areas. Keep pets out of designated play and splash-pad zones and under close control near children and creek habitat.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation and Pease Park Conservancy ↗
Is Pease District Park good for children? +

Kingsbury Commons concentrates the playground, Treehouse, nature play, splash pad, restrooms, picnic space, and open lawn into the easiest family visit.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation and Pease Park Conservancy ↗
How do I reach Pease District Park without a car? +

Walking, biking, and public transportation are strongly encouraged because parking is scarce. Check current CapMetro routing and Pease Park access guidance.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation and Pease Park Conservancy ↗
What should I know before visiting Pease District Park? +

Check Austin's facility-closures page before a splash or nature-play visit. As of the latest city notice, portions of the western trail, splash pad, and nature play area are affected by a plumbing break and runoff until further notice.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Austin Parks and Recreation and Pease Park Conservancy ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Pease District Park? +

Check Austin's hourly heat index, lightning and heavy-rain alerts, flash-flood risk, air quality, and current facility notices. Limestone, pavement, play equipment, and exposed trails become dangerously hot, while creeks can rise quickly after upstream rain.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
