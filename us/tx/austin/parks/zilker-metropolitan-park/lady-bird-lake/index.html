Lady Bird Lake

Austin, Austin, TX
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Zilker Metropolitan Park is generally open daily from 5:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Barton Springs Pool, gardens, museums, theater, train, rentals, events, and other facilities use separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

Lady Bird Lake is the Colorado River reservoir bordering Zilker Park, used for paddling and surrounded by the Butler Hike-and-Bike Trail. Zilker touches the lake along its northern edge near Barton Creek. Choose an exact trailhead, launch, or rental destination rather than routing only to the lake name. This is not a designated swimming destination; use Barton Springs Pool for guarded swimming. Paddling access, rentals, trail conditions, and parking vary by launch point. Keep dogs leashed where required, watch for fast trail traffic, and check City water-quality or closure notices after storms before water contact.

When does Zilker Park charge for parking? +

From the first Saturday of Spring Break through Labor Day, parking is $3 per hour on Saturdays, Sundays, and official holidays. Spaces are first-come, first-served, and major events can replace normal access and rates.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can I reach Zilker Park without parking there? +

Yes. CapMetro bus 30 travels through the park, while routes 3 and 803 stop on South Lamar. The seasonal 2026 Zilker Loop shuttle runs weekends and holidays through September 7 from noon-6 p.m., with four major park stops.

How much does Lady Bird Lake cost? +

General park, playscape, trails, lawns, and ordinary recreation are free. Seasonal weekend and holiday parking is $3 per hour; pool, gardens, museums, rentals, train, reservations, and events may charge.
