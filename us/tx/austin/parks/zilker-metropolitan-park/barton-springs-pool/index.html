What people say

The spring-fed three-acre pool has guarded and swim-at-your-own-risk periods, paid admission, separate entrances, and a Thursday cleaning closure. Confirm pool and attraction hours separately, check live park closures, and expect major access changes for ACL Festival, Trail of Lights, holidays, flooding, maintenance, and large events. Barton Creek can rise quickly after rain.

Where should I park for Barton Springs Pool? +

Parking is spread across several lots. Weekends, holidays, festivals, and swim days fill quickly; seasonal paid parking and event traffic plans may apply. Match the lot to your destination rather than entering 'Zilker Park' alone.

Is Barton Springs Pool free? +

The general park is free. Barton Springs Pool, Zilker Botanical Garden, train rides, rentals, reservations, parking periods, and ticketed events can charge fees.

Accessible parking and routes serve major destinations, but the campus is large and includes slopes, gravel, grass, creek banks, and event detours. Choose the exact feature before navigating.

Leashed dogs may use ordinary park areas; pets are restricted from Barton Springs Pool, some attractions, play areas, and event zones. Observe posted rules.
