Discovery Green

Houston

Open daily 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Restrooms operate 9:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; fountain changing rooms 9:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m.; events and seasonal attractions vary.

Emancipation Park

Houston

Outdoor areas and staffed facilities use posted schedules; pool, spraygrounds, recreation center, gym, cultural center, and programs may open or close independently.

Gerald D. Hines Waterwall Park

Houston

Hermann Park

Houston

Open daily 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; gardens, railroad, carousel, pedal boats, zoo, museums, cafes, theatre, and golf use separate schedules.

Levy Park

Houston

General outdoor areas follow posted park hours; the Children's Park operates 8:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. and closes the first Tuesday monthly from 8:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m. for maintenance.

Memorial Park

Houston

Open daily 5:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; natural areas, staffed buildings, lots, pool, golf, tennis, and sports facilities use separate schedules.

Smither Park

Houston

Open daily from dawn until dusk. Saturday artist activity and scheduled programs are weather dependent; the adjacent Orange Show Monument has separate hours and may be closed for restoration.
