Discovery Green

1500 McKinney Street, Houston, TX 77010
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open daily 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Restrooms operate 9:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; fountain changing rooms 9:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m.; events and seasonal attractions vary.
Navigate

What people say

Downtown Houston's 12-acre village green combines a major playground, free interactive fountain, Kinder Lake, separate dog runs, event lawns, performance spaces, public art, restaurants, gardens, and frequent free programming.

When is Discovery Green open? +

Open daily 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Restrooms operate 9:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; fountain changing rooms 9:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m.; events and seasonal attractions vary.

Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
Where should I park for Discovery Green? +

Use metered perimeter streets or paid downtown garages, including Avenida Central beneath the park. Convention, arena, festival, and event traffic can sharply affect price and availability.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
What is the best entrance for Discovery Green? +

Use the Alkek Building and Gateway Fountain on the northwest side for information, restrooms, playground, and family water play; select the lake, stage, or dog-run edge for those destinations.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
Are there restrooms at Discovery Green? +

Public restrooms are on the south side of the Alkek Building near the playground and generally operate 9:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; a women's restroom is also in Avenida Central Garage.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
Is Discovery Green free? +

Admission, playground, fountain, lawns, dog runs, public art, and most public programming are free. Parking, food, boats, skating, rentals, and selected events cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
How accessible is Discovery Green? +

Broad paved paths and level plazas serve the compact park; wheelchair users can participate in some seasonal rink experiences under posted rules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
Are dogs allowed at Discovery Green? +

Pets must be leashed outside the separate large- and small-dog runs. Dog-run users must remain with dogs and follow size, vaccination, and 45-minute rules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
Is Discovery Green good for children? +

Excellent compact family destination with a refreshed hill playground, free Gateway Fountain, lake, lawns, restrooms, changing rooms, and frequent programs.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
How do I reach Discovery Green without a car? +

METRORail, downtown buses, convention-district walking routes, and nearby bike facilities make transit practical; Convention District is the closest rail stop.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
What should I know before visiting Discovery Green? +

Gateway Fountain generally runs 9:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. and shuts off for thunder or lightning. Private events and maintenance can close portions of the compact park.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Discovery Green Conservancy ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Discovery Green? +

Check Houston's hourly heat index, lightning, heavy-rain and flood alerts, air quality, and current facility notices. Outdoor surfaces and play equipment can become dangerously hot, and open pavilions are not safe lightning shelters.
