What people say

The skatepark is a dedicated wheeled-sports facility near Sabine Street with separate rules, busy sessions, and limited shade. Low trails, underpasses, paddling access, and the dog park can close after storms, high water, sediment, or contamination. Obey barriers and use upper routes.

Where should I park for Lee and Joe Jamail Skatepark? +

Metered spaces between Sabine and Taft are $1 for up to three hours. Additional signed parking is near City Lot H and Allen Parkway; never park on grass or flood-control access.

Are there restrooms near Lee and Joe Jamail Skatepark? +

The Water Works restrooms are generally open 8:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m.; Lost Lake restrooms generally operate 8:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Long trail sections have no nearby restroom.

Is Lee and Joe Jamail Skatepark free? +

General park, trails, playground, dog park, skatepark, lawns, gardens, and public art are free. Parking, Cistern tours, rentals, food, permits, and events may charge.

How accessible is Lee and Joe Jamail Skatepark? +

Paved trails and plazas serve major destinations, while sand, grass, slopes, wet steps, and flood-prone low routes vary substantially.

Are dogs allowed at Lee and Joe Jamail Skatepark? +

Leashed dogs are allowed generally; Johnny Steele Dog Park is the designated off-leash area and may close independently after flooding or water-quality concerns.

Is Lee and Joe Jamail Skatepark good for children? +

The nature play area and designed stream are strong family draws, but Buffalo Bayou itself is active floodwater and is not a swimming or wading area.
