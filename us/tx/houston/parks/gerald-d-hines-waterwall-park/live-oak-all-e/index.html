Live oak allée

2800 Post Oak Boulevard, Houston, TX 77056
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open daily 9:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m.; fountain operation can pause for maintenance, severe weather, freezing conditions, or special work.
Navigate

What people say

The live-oak allée creates the shaded approach between Williams Tower and the Waterwall. Expect heavy mist and wet pavement close to the Waterwall. Supervise children, protect cameras and phones, and never enter or climb the fountain structure.

Where should I park for Live oak allée? +

The most convenient official recommendation is paid visitor parking at Williams Tower, followed by a short walk. Do not assume adjacent curbs or private business lots permit park parking.

Are there restrooms near Live oak allée? +

There is no dependable dedicated public restroom inside the small outdoor park; use a confirmed open nearby business or facility rather than assuming Williams Tower access.

Is Live oak allée free? +

The park and Waterwall are free. Garage parking and nearby food or shopping cost separately.

How accessible is Live oak allée? +

Level paved approaches reach the fountain plaza and lawns, but spray can make surfaces wet and slick; use care with mobility devices near the basin.

Are dogs allowed at Live oak allée? +

Leashed dogs may use the outdoor lawn and paths under posted rules; keep pets out of the fountain basin and away from crowded photo areas.

The roar, mist, scale, and live-oak lawn make a memorable short family stop, but the fountain basin is not a splash pad or swimming area.
