Children's water feature

3801 Eastside Street, Houston, TX 77098
Hours ⌄
Published schedule General outdoor areas follow posted park hours; the Children's Park operates 8:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. and closes the first Tuesday monthly from 8:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m. for maintenance.
Navigate

What people say

The three-tier water feature is inside the Children's Park; swim diapers are required under age two and monthly maintenance closures apply. Children under 12 require an accompanying adult, flat shoes are required, surfaces can become hot, and the water feature requires swim diapers for children under two.

Where should I park for Children's water feature? +

Free parking is available adjacent to the park and along Eastside Street and Wakeforest Avenue. Paid overflow is available in the Kirby Grove garage at Richmond and Wakeforest.

Is Children's water feature free? +

Park entry, playground, water feature, dog park, reading room, lawns, games, and most daily activities are free. Garage parking, concessions, rentals, and selected events cost separately.

How accessible is Children's water feature? +

Paved paths, gradual ramps, the Treehouse Overlook boardwalk, family restrooms, and compact layout support accessible visits; some play elements require transfer or climbing.

Are dogs allowed at Children's water feature? +

Dogs are allowed in the designated large- and small-dog areas under posted rules; pets are not allowed inside the Children's Park or its water feature.
