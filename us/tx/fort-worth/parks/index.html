Fort Worth Water Gardens

Fort Worth

The Water Gardens are open daily from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Water features, lighting, rentals, and individual areas can be interrupted by maintenance, freezing weather, events, or safety operations.

Gateway Park

Fort Worth

Gateway Park is open daily from 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. Athletic fields, Fort Woof, events, concessions, and reserved facilities can follow separate operational schedules.

Trinity Park

Fort Worth

Route to Dream Park for the all-abilities playground, Trinity Park Duck Pond for waterfowl and nearby parking, or Forest Park Miniature Railroad for the train station. The park stretches along the river beside the zoo and cultural district.
