What people say

The Trinity Park Depot is the north boarding platform for the Forest Park Miniature Railroad, a weather-dependent five-mile family train ride that crosses the Trinity River, follows the park corridor, and includes a midway snack stop. Check the operator's live calendar on the day of the trip and arrive about 15 minutes before the desired departure. Trains normally leave hourly, seats are first come, and the roughly 45-60 minute ride includes a midpoint stop. The Trinity Park Depot is distinct from the main Forest Park depot at 1700 Colonial Parkway; use this exact depot pin, and use the main depot if you need the operator's primary ticket-and-arrival location. Kiosks accept cash, major cards other than American Express and Discover, and tap payments. Do not rely on the train as transportation back to a parked car because weather or maintenance can end service early.

Where should I park for Forest Park Miniature Railroad Trinity Park Depot? +

Route to the named Trinity Park Depot at 2001 University Drive and use only signed park spaces that are open on arrival. Do not route to the railroad's main Forest Park depot at 1700 Colonial Parkway unless that is where you intend to board. Trinity Park events and University Drive closures can change the final approach, so follow current signs and never leave a vehicle on grass, trail shoulders, or railroad access areas.

Are there restrooms near Forest Park Miniature Railroad Trinity Park Depot? +

The railroad operator lists a Trinity Park Snack Shack at the midpoint platform but does not promise a public restroom at this depot. Trinity Park has restrooms in selected developed areas, yet schedules and events can limit them. Identify an open restroom before boarding, especially with children, because the ride normally lasts 45-60 minutes.

What fees apply at Forest Park Miniature Railroad Trinity Park Depot? +

Current operator pricing is $10 for riders age 13 and older, $8 for children ages 2-12 and seniors age 65 and older, and free for children age 1 and younger. Tickets use ride credits purchased at an on-site kiosk; food at the Trinity Park Snack Shack is paid separately and train credits are not accepted there.

How accessible is Forest Park Miniature Railroad Trinity Park Depot? +

The operator's current visitor pages do not document wheelchair boarding dimensions, transfer requirements, securement, or step-free access for the train cars. Contact the railroad before a mobility-sensitive trip rather than assuming the paved depot approach makes the ride accessible. The City can address access to Trinity Park itself, but the separately operated train controls boarding.

Are dogs allowed at Forest Park Miniature Railroad Trinity Park Depot? +

Fort Worth permits leashed dogs in Trinity Park, but the railroad operator does not publish a general pet-boarding policy on its current visitor pages. Do not assume a non-service animal can board; contact the operator before arriving and be prepared to enjoy the park without taking the train.

Is Forest Park Miniature Railroad Trinity Park Depot useful for a family visit? +

This is a family attraction, but plan for a 45-60 minute seated ride, hourly departures, first-come capacity, a midway stop, outdoor weather exposure, and possible same-day cancellation. Children age 1 and younger currently ride free; ages 2-12 use the child fare. Arrive early enough for tickets and a restroom stop rather than promising a particular departure.
