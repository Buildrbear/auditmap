What people say

Trinity Park is Fort Worth's 252-acre river and cultural-district park, with accessible Dream Park playground, duck pond, WPA shelter, picnic tables and grills, basketball, observation areas, parking, water features, Forest Park Miniature Railroad station, and direct connections to the 100-plus-mile Trinity Trails network.

Where should I start at Trinity Park? +

Route to Dream Park for the all-abilities playground, Trinity Park Duck Pond for waterfowl and nearby parking, or Forest Park Miniature Railroad for the train station. The park stretches along the river beside the zoo and cultural district.

Where should I park at Trinity Park? +

Parking lots and roadside spaces are distributed near Dream Park, the duck pond, train station, shelters, and trail access. Choose a destination before entering because the 252-acre park is long and major events can close sections.

Are there restrooms at Trinity Park? +

Restrooms and water fountains are available in selected developed areas and near major family or picnic destinations, not along every trail segment. Train, zoo, and nearby attraction facilities follow separate admission hours.

Can I bike from Trinity Park onto Trinity Trails? +

Yes. Paved walking and cycling paths through the park connect directly to the regional Trinity Trails network, which extends more than 100 miles. Watch for event detours, river flooding, and shared-use traffic.

What is Dream Park at Trinity Park? +

Dream Park is an inclusive playground designed for children with varied needs, mobility, and abilities. It is a destination within Trinity Park rather than a small neighborhood play area, so route directly to Dream Park parking.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Is Trinity Park free? +

General park access, parking, Dream Park, duck pond, trails, picnic areas, and open space are free. The miniature railroad, reserved shelters, vendors, special events, zoo, gardens, and nearby museums may charge.

What can close parts of Trinity Park? +

Mayfest and other major events, river flooding, trail construction, railroad operations, maintenance, and severe weather can close roads, lots, lawns, or paths independently. Check Fort Worth park notices before a feature-specific visit.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

What time does Trinity Park open and close? +

Trinity Park is open every day from 5:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. The miniature railroad, restrooms, reserved pavilions, events, and nearby attractions have separate schedules, so park hours do not guarantee every feature is operating.

Are dogs allowed at Trinity Park? +

Yes, but dogs must remain on a leash and visitors must remove pet waste. Trinity Park is not an off-leash dog park; use a designated Fort Worth dog park when off-leash recreation is the goal.

Can I picnic or reserve a pavilion at Trinity Park? +

Yes. The park has tables, grills, shelters, and pavilions, and reservable spaces are available through Fort Worth. Public events, runs, and exclusive gatherings require advance approval; alcohol is prohibited and bounce houses are specifically prohibited at Trinity Park.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Trinity Park? +

Trinity Park has pavilions and mature trees along portions of the river corridor, but Dream Park, lawns, trails, the duck pond, event areas, and many river-edge sections are exposed. Pavilion 1 is the only reservable pavilion where restrooms are offered as an added reservation service. Covered picnic structures are not severe-weather shelters; during lightning, flooding, extreme heat, or high wind, leave the river and use an open substantial building or vehicle.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Fort Worth ↗
Is there a splash pad or swimming at Trinity Park? +

No operating public pool or splash pad is listed at Trinity Park. The City's park page lists a water feature, duck pond, and the Trinity River, but none is identified as a designated swimming facility. Do not use the pond or river as family water play. Forest Park Pool is a separate seasonal aquatic facility nearby, and Fort Worth also lists Marine Park Pool and Sycamore Spray Ground; confirm the chosen facility's current schedule and admission before going.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Fort Worth ↗
Which bus goes to Trinity Park in Fort Worth? +

Trinity Metro Routes 2 and 4 serve the Cultural District and University Drive area beside Trinity Park, and the former DASH corridor also connected downtown with 7th and University near the park. Use Trinity Metro's live planner for Dream Park, the duck pond, Forest Park Pool, the miniature train, trail access, or a specific event because Trinity Park is long and road closures frequently affect the University Drive side.

Trinity Metro ↗
What are the main landmarks inside Trinity Park? +

Trinity Park's clearest family anchors are Frank Kent's Dream Park, the duck pond, the historic WPA shelter, and the station for the Forest Park Miniature Railroad. Paved paths also connect directly to the extensive Trinity Trails river network. These destinations are spread through the 252-acre park, and the zoo, Botanic Garden, and Cultural District museums are nearby but separately operated, so route to the exact park feature you want rather than a neighboring attraction.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

How accessible is Trinity Park? +

Accessibility varies by entrance, route, terrain, and destination. Review the exact mapped subsite and its official source before relying on a specific accessible route.
