Park directory

Current city pages

Al Lopez Park

Tampa

The outdoor park is open daily from sunrise to sunset. Hunt Community Center normally closes earlier and uses its own weekly schedule, including Sunday closure.

Ballast Point Park

Tampa

The park is open daily from 6:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m. The current featured-park page lists splash-pad hours as 8:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m.; the citywide seasonal schedule may vary, so confirm before a water-play visit.

Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park

Tampa

Park grounds are open daily from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. The Louvre splash fountain normally runs April-October from 9:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. and November-March from 10:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m.; water features can stop for cold weather, maintenance or events.

Julian B. Lane Riverfront Park

Tampa

Outdoor park areas generally follow posted City park hours. The splash pad normally runs April-October from 9:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. and November-March from 10:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m.; event spaces and organized athletics keep separate schedules.

Lettuce Lake Park

Tampa

Open daily from 8:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. in spring and summer and 8:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. in fall and winter. Gates and individual facilities may close earlier for weather or construction.
