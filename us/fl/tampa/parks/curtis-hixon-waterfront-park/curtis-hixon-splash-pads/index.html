What people say

Curtis Hixon Splash Pads is a seasonal, weather-dependent water feature at Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park; verify same-day operation and supervision rules. The Great Lawn is a major event venue, so concerts and festivals can fence sections, limit ordinary access and change parking. Check the City event calendar and fountain status before a water-play trip.

Where should I park for Curtis Hixon Splash Pads? +

The William F. Poe Garage at 800 North Ashley Drive is the most practical general option, with metered street spaces and other downtown garages nearby. Event days can fill the closest facilities and change rates.

Are there restrooms near Curtis Hixon Splash Pads? +

Is Curtis Hixon Splash Pads free? +

The park, playground, splash fountains, dog areas and Riverwalk are free. Parking, water taxi rides, museums, food and ticketed events cost separately.

How accessible is Curtis Hixon Splash Pads? +

The main lawn edges, Riverwalk, playground approach and museum connections are paved and broadly level. Use Poe Garage accessible parking or the nearest legal drop-off for the shortest route.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Curtis Hixon Splash Pads? +

Dogs are welcome on leash outside the designated dog areas. Separate small- and large-dog parks opened in 2026; dogs remain excluded from playground and splash-water areas under posted rules.

Is Curtis Hixon Splash Pads good for children? +

The shaded playground and seasonal fountains sit close together, with museums immediately beside the park. Bring water and sun protection because the Great Lawn is exposed despite shaded seating near play areas.
