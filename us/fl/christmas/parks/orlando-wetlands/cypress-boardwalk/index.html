Where exactly is Cypress Boardwalk? +

Cypress Boardwalk is a distinct visitor destination within Orlando Wetlands. Start at the Visitors Center and map board near the entrance. Choose the 2,200-foot Cypress Boardwalk, a short berm loop, or a longer trail before leaving the developed area.

Where should I park for Cypress Boardwalk? +

Use the free entrance lot on Wheeler Road. Restrooms, picnic pavilions, and the Visitors Center are near this lot; private vehicles cannot continue onto the wetland berm roads.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Orlando ↗
When is Cypress Boardwalk open? +

Open Tuesday-Sunday from sunrise to sunset and closed Monday. The Visitors Center is open Tuesday-Saturday 9 a.m.-4 p.m. and closes on city-observed holidays; tram tours use a limited seasonal schedule.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Orlando ↗
Are there restrooms near Cypress Boardwalk? +

Restrooms are just north of the parking lot and at the Visitors Center. There are no restrooms across the remote 1,650-acre trail system.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Orlando ↗
Is Cypress Boardwalk free? +

Admission, parking, and public volunteer tram tours are free; tram donations are welcome and reserved group-tour rates vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Orlando ↗
How accessible is Cypress Boardwalk? +

A sidewalk connects accessible parking with restrooms, picnic pavilion, Visitors Center, and Cypress Boardwalk. Compacted sand-shell berms are generally wheelchair accessible only when dry.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Orlando ↗
Are dogs allowed at Cypress Boardwalk? +

Pets are prohibited because of alligators and birds of prey. ADA-defined service animals are permitted; keep them close and away from water edges.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Orlando ↗
Is Cypress Boardwalk good for children? +

The boardwalk and Visitors Center are the easiest family visit. Keep children close, stay atop berms, carry water, and never approach alligators, snakes, or other wildlife.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Orlando ↗
What should I know before visiting Cypress Boardwalk? +

Expect extreme heat, minimal shade, fast lightning storms, mosquitoes, soft trails after rain, and wildlife at close range. The automatic gate closes at sunset and vehicles can be locked in.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Orlando ↗
How do you reach Cypress Boardwalk? +

There is no practical public transit from Orlando. Driving is the reliable approach; confirm a rideshare return before depending on one in rural Christmas.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
