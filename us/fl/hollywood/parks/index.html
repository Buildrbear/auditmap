Anne Kolb Nature Center

Hollywood

The Broward County exhibit-hall page currently lists nature centers and their exhibit halls open daily from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Tower, trail, gate, event, and paddling access can close separately for weather, maintenance, or programming, so call 954-357-5161 before a time-sensitive visit.

Hollywood Beach and Broadwalk

Hollywood

The city lists its parks as open from sunrise to sunset. Lifeguard coverage is normally 9:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. year-round and extends to 7:00 p.m. on holidays and special events. Restaurants, garages, restrooms, Charnow Park, and the Beach Theatre can keep different schedules.
