Battle of the Bulge Memorial

Orlando, Orlando, FL
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Lake Eola Park is open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 11:59 p.m. Swan boats normally operate Tuesday-Sunday 10:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. and are closed Mondays; amphitheater, market, events, restaurants, and rentals use separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

The Battle of the Bulge Memorial honors World War II veterans and was dedicated on the battle's 55th anniversary in 1999. The outdoor monument is on the Lake Eola promenade; use the exact pin while walking the paved loop rather than routing to a separate street address. The memorial is outdoors, free, and accessible during park hours, with no ticket desk or dedicated parking. It is modeled after a monument in Clervaux, Luxembourg. Treat it as a short stop on the lake loop. Do not climb on the memorial, and use Lake Eola's public restrooms rather than expecting facilities at the monument.

Use 512 East Washington Street, Orlando, FL 32801. The park surrounds the entire lake, so choose the closest block for swan boats, playgrounds, amphitheater, farmers market, fountain, or your event entrance.

Where should I park for Lake Eola? +

Use legal metered street parking or a public downtown garage closest to the side of the lake you plan to visit. Markets, festivals, performances, and downtown events can close streets or fill nearby spaces.

How much are Lake Eola swan boats? +

The city operates pedal-powered swan boats Tuesday-Sunday from 10 a.m.-7 p.m. and closes them Mondays. Rental pricing and operation can change with weather, maintenance, events, or lake conditions, so check the current rental page or call 407-246-4484.
