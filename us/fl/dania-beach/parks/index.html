Dr. Von D. Mizell-Eula Johnson State Park

Dania Beach

The park is open 8:00 a.m. to sunset, 365 days a year. The boat ramp opens one hour before official sunrise and follows separate closing rules, while concessions, rentals, restrooms, and pavilions can keep shorter schedules or close independently.
