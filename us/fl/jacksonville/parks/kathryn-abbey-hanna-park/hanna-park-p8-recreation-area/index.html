What people say

Hanna Park P8 Recreation Area is a mapped arrival zone within Kathryn Abbey Hanna Park; use this pin instead of the broad park center and follow the gate, fee, and posted parking rules. Swimming is prohibited in the freshwater lake. The splash park normally operates from the first weekend in May through the last weekend in October, 10:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m., but closes temporarily for maintenance or lightning within ten miles and has no lifeguards.

Where should I park for Hanna Park P8 Recreation Area? +

Enter from Wonderwood Drive and pay at the gate, then follow signs to beach access, lake, splash park, campground, trails, or a reserved pavilion. The park is large; choose the destination before entering.

Does it cost money to visit Hanna Park P8 Recreation Area? +

$5 per car with up to six people or $3 per pedestrian or bicyclist from 8:00 a.m. to closing. Camping, cabins, rentals, reservations, early-bird access, and annual passes cost separately; verify current tax and rates.

The city lists accessible amenities and an adaptive kayak launch. Beach sand, natural trails, campsites, lake edges, and surf access still vary substantially by route and conditions.

Are dogs allowed at Hanna Park P8 Recreation Area? +

Pets are permitted only in designated areas, must be attended, well behaved, and on a leash eight feet or shorter. Follow signs around beach, water-play, campground, and natural areas.
