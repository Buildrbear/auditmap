Dreher Park

West Palm Beach

Outdoor park and pavilion use is generally sunrise to sunset. Palm Beach Zoo, Cox Science Center and Aquarium, reserved shelters, and special programs use their own admission hours and holiday schedules.

Okeeheelee Park

West Palm Beach

The park is open daily from sunrise to sunset. Lighted courts and permitted athletic fields may operate until 10 p.m.; the Nature Center is open Wednesday–Friday 1–4:30 p.m. and Saturday 10 a.m.–4:30 p.m., excluding County holidays.
