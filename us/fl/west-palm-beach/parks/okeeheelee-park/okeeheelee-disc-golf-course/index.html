Okeeheelee Disc Golf Course

7715 Forest Hill Blvd, West Palm Beach, FL 33413
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The public disc-golf course is open daily from sunrise to sunset. It does not inherit the later hours used by permitted lighted fields or courts elsewhere in Okeeheelee Park.

What people say

Okeeheelee Disc Golf Course is the park's 18-hole public course, laid out around the Pooch Pines activity area with open fairways, mature trees, paved park paths, lakes, and water-edge holes. Navigate to the exact course pin and use the county's April 2026 park map to find Hole 1 near Pooch Pines. Bring discs, drinking water, sun and rain protection, and shoes suited to turf. Fairways run near lakes and shared park paths, so watch for water hazards, pedestrians, storms, and lightning.

Okeeheelee Canoe and Kayak Area is the central freshwater-lake launch and rental zone for non-motorized paddling, with a mapped boat ramp, nearby parking, restrooms, picnic areas, and a separately operated rental concession.

Okeeheelee Nature Center

Okeeheelee Nature Center is a free visitor and environmental-education destination inside Okeeheelee Park, with hands-on ecosystem exhibits, live animal ambassadors, a preschool Discovery Zone, a wildlife-viewing window, and 2.5 miles of trails through 90 acres of pine flatwoods and wetlands.

Okeeheelee Golf Course

Okeeheelee Golf Course is Palm Beach County's 27-hole public golf facility, arranged as three nine-hole courses with a driving range, practice areas, Golf Shop, and Okee Grill on a lake-and-palm landscape inside Okeeheelee Park.

Where should I park for Okeeheelee Disc Golf Course? +

The county's April 2026 map marks Disc Golf Course Hole 1 near Pooch Pines and the surrounding north-side activity parking. Use the exact course pin rather than the park's general street address, then follow posted park signs because Okeeheelee has separate north and south entrances and many distributed lots.

Are there restrooms near Okeeheelee Disc Golf Course? +

The county map marks restrooms in the north recreation district, but not at every disc-golf hole. Use the mapped activity-area facilities before starting and do not assume a restroom or drinking-water stop will be immediately available mid-round.

What fees apply at Okeeheelee Disc Golf Course? +

Palm Beach County invites the public to play and does not publish a separate daily course-admission fee. Bring your own discs; leagues, tournaments, instruction, or special events can require separate registration or charges.

How accessible is Okeeheelee Disc Golf Course? +

The official ADA map shows accessible parking and paved circulation in the activity district. The disc-golf round itself crosses turf and water-edge fairways, so visitors who need a step-free route, firm surface, or shortened play option should ask Palm Beach County Parks about current course conditions before arriving.

Are dogs allowed at Okeeheelee Disc Golf Course? +

Pooch Pines Dog Park is adjacent to the course area, but off-leash access applies only inside the designated dog-park enclosure. Keep dogs controlled outside that enclosure and away from active fairways, players, wildlife, and water edges.

Is Okeeheelee Disc Golf Course useful for a family visit? +

This is a full 18-hole outdoor course rather than a compact playground activity. Families should bring their own discs, supervise children around throws and lake edges, allow time for a complete round, and pause play for pedestrians or severe weather. The nearby playground and Pooch Pines are separate destinations with their own use rules.
