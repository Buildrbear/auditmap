What people say

Cox Science Center and Aquarium is Dreher Park's ticketed science museum and aquarium, with hands-on exhibits, aquarium programs, planetarium and observatory experiences, outdoor science areas, and sensory-access resources. Enter Dreher Park from Southern Boulevard, turn onto Dreher Trail North, and continue to the exact science-center pin. Parking throughout Dreher Park is free. The operator is expanding the campus, so use its current construction link before visiting and expect route, exhibit, or bag-storage changes. Average exhibit time is about two hours; weekday field trips commonly leave by 1:30 p.m., and children under 13 must remain with an adult age 18 or older.

Are there restrooms near Cox Science Center and Aquarium? +

Wheelchair-accessible, sensory-friendly restrooms are located throughout the science-center building; they do not use hand dryers or automatic flush mechanisms. Ask Guest Services for the nearest facility after entry because construction can change circulation.

What fees apply at Cox Science Center and Aquarium? +

Current general admission is $26 for adults, $22 for children ages 3-12, $24 for teens ages 13-17, and $24 for seniors age 60 and older; members enter free. Discounts, special programs, planetarium access, events, and future price changes can follow separate rules, so the live ticket page controls.

How accessible is Cox Science Center and Aquarium? +

The operator states that all exhibits and spaces are wheelchair accessible except the Observatory, where alternative accommodations are available. Complimentary wheelchairs are available by request in limited quantity. Sensory resources include quieter-time guidance, sensory-friendly restrooms, monthly Sensory Saturdays, and advice to bring noise-reducing headphones for the Planetarium or Theater.

Are dogs allowed at Cox Science Center and Aquarium? +

Non-service animals are not permitted inside the science center. Trained service dogs are welcome when controlled under the operator's ADA policy; review the detailed animal policy or contact Guest Services before a visit involving specific access needs.

Is Cox Science Center and Aquarium useful for a family visit? +

The average visit is about two hours, and children under 13 must remain with an adult age 18 or older. Check the live program schedule because aquarium interactions, demonstrations, observatory access, outdoor activities, and the splash pad can be weather-, event-, or construction-dependent. Weekday afternoons after 2:00 p.m. and weekend mornings from 10:00-11:00 a.m. are often quieter during the school year, but crowd levels are never guaranteed.
