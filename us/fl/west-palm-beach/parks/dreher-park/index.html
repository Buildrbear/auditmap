What people say

Dreher Park is a large West Palm Beach cultural and recreation campus surrounding Palm Beach Zoo and Cox Science Center and Aquarium, with walking and biking trails, fitness stations, dog park, fishing dock, playgrounds, restrooms, disc golf, sand volleyball, open fields, grills, public art, and ten reservable picnic shelters.

What hours is Dreher Park open? +

Outdoor park and pavilion use is generally sunrise to sunset. Palm Beach Zoo, Cox Science Center and Aquarium, reserved shelters, and special programs use their own admission hours and holiday schedules.

What address should I use for Dreher Park? +

Use 5701 Dreher Trail South, West Palm Beach, FL 33405 for the general park. Route directly to Palm Beach Zoo or Cox Science Center when either ticketed attraction is your destination because each has its own entrance and parking flow.

Where should I park at Dreher Park? +

Parking is distributed among the general park, Palm Beach Zoo, Cox Science Center, playgrounds, shelters, and recreation areas. Follow destination signs and do not assume a museum or zoo lot is the closest choice for a free park feature.

Yes. The City lists restrooms among the outdoor park amenities. Zoo and science-center restrooms are within ticketed facilities and follow their operating hours, so use a confirmed park facility for a free visit.

Is there a dog park at Dreher Park? +

Yes. Dreher Park is one of West Palm Beach's designated dog-park locations. Dogs should remain leashed outside the off-leash enclosure and are subject to separate rules at the zoo and science-center properties.

Yes. The City lists walking and biking trails plus outdoor fitness stations. The campus is spread around major ticketed attractions, roads, ponds, and recreation areas, so a park map is useful for choosing a loop.

Zoo and science-center events, shelter reservations, field use, storms, maintenance, and special events can affect lots or individual areas. Check the City park page and the ticketed attraction's own notice before a feature-specific visit.

Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Dreher Park? +

Dreher Park has ten reservable picnic shelters in large, medium, and small sizes, with additional tree shade along portions of its trails, lakes, and picnic landscape. Open fields, playgrounds, fishing docks, disc-golf areas, and parking can be exposed. Palm Beach Zoo and Cox Science Center are separately ticketed destinations and should not be treated as free park shelter. Picnic roofs are not safe during lightning or hurricane-strength wind; use an open substantial building or enclosed vehicle.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can I take public transit to Dreher Park? +

Yes, but expect a walk within the large campus. Palm Tran Route 60 stops at Summit Boulevard and Dreher Trail North near the park's north edge. Use Palm Tran's live planner for 5701 Dreher Trail South, Palm Beach Zoo, or Cox Science Center because each destination has a different best approach, and confirm the return trip before entering the park.

Is there a splash pad or swimming at Dreher Park? +

No. West Palm Beach lists a decorative fountain, fishing pier, playgrounds, trails, fields, pavilions, and other dry recreation at Dreher Park, but it does not list a splash pad or public pool. The fountain and ponds are not water-play or swimming facilities. For splash play, use a separately designated City site such as Bill Moss Hillcrest Paseo, Coleman Park, or South Olive Park and verify its current operating or construction status first.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of West Palm Beach ↗
What are the main attractions and landmarks in Dreher Park? +

Palm Beach Zoo and the Cox Science Center and Aquarium are the park's two major destination anchors, each with its own entrance, hours, admission, and parking guidance. The free municipal park around them includes a decorative fountain, listed public art, fishing dock, disc golf, dog park, trails, playgrounds, and picnic pavilions. Navigate directly to the zoo or science center for a ticketed visit; use 5701 Dreher Trail South for the general park and pavilion area.
