Hugh Taylor Birch State Park

Fort Lauderdale

The park is open from 8 a.m. to sunset every day of the year. The Terramar Visitor Center, concession, rentals, tours, and beach access may use shorter schedules.

Snyder Park

Fort Lauderdale

The park is open 7 a.m.–7 p.m. from March through October and 7 a.m.–6 p.m. from November through February. It is closed on Christmas and New Year's Day; dog-park hours differ in summer.
