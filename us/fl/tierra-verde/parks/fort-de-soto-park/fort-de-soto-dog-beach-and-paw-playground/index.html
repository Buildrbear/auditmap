What people say

Fort De Soto Dog Beach and Paw Playground is a named shoreline destination at Fort De Soto Park; check water status, lifeguard coverage, wind and posted beach rules before entering. A burn ban has been in effect since February 2026, and construction or maintenance can affect facilities. Check rip-current, lightning, red-tide/water-quality, nesting-wildlife and County closure notices on the day of the trip.

Where should I park for Fort De Soto Dog Beach and Paw Playground? +

A $6 daily vehicle parking fee applies and is paid by app, pay-by-text or onsite terminals; mobile payment adds a $0.35 convenience fee. Pay stations are distributed near the beaches, piers, dog park, fort and headquarters.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Fort De Soto Dog Beach and Paw Playground may keep shorter, seasonal, staffed, event-dependent or weather-dependent hours; verify its current status before relying on the parent schedule.

Are there restrooms near Fort De Soto Dog Beach and Paw Playground? +

Restrooms and outdoor rinse showers are distributed near major beaches, picnic areas, campground and developed facilities. Remote trail and shoreline stretches have fewer services.

Is Fort De Soto Dog Beach and Paw Playground free? +

The daily parking fee is $6 per vehicle. Camping, boat launching, concessions, rentals and reservations cost separately; ordinary beach, fort and trail access do not add a general admission fee.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Pinellas County Parks and Conservation Resources ↗
How accessible is Fort De Soto Dog Beach and Paw Playground? +

A 2,200-foot Barrier-Free Nature Trail, accessible facilities and beach-access features serve visitors with mobility needs. Sand, tides and storm damage can still alter practical access.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Pinellas County Parks and Conservation Resources ↗
Are dogs allowed at Fort De Soto Dog Beach and Paw Playground? +

Dogs are allowed on the designated dog beach and in separate fenced large- and small-dog areas with water stations. Outside designated areas, leash and exclusion rules apply; dogs are not allowed on ordinary swimming beaches.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Pinellas County Parks and Conservation Resources ↗
Is Fort De Soto Dog Beach and Paw Playground good for children? +

North Beach, playgrounds, shallow-water areas, fort exploration and picnic shelters support a full family day. Conditions can change rapidly, so check flags, tides, heat and storms and bring substantial water and sun protection.

How do you reach Fort De Soto Dog Beach and Paw Playground? +

There is no practical frequent transit for most visitors. Driving, cycling for experienced riders or a prearranged ride are the usual options; verify return transportation before being dropped off.
