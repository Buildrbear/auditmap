What people say

Terry Tomalin Campground is a distinct mapped destination within Boyd Hill Nature Preserve; navigate to this point instead of the general park pin and check posted conditions on arrival. The nature center's exhibits are currently closed for renovation, although the preserve and trails remain open. Mondays are closed, trail entry ends before facility closing, and heat or lightning can shorten a safe visit.

Free onsite parking is available near the environmental education center entrance. Arrive early for cool-weather weekends and scheduled programs, when the closest spaces can fill.

Are there restrooms near Terry Tomalin Campground? +

Restrooms are available near the education-center and developed entrance area during operating hours. Use them before longer loops; interior trail facilities are limited.

Is Terry Tomalin Campground free? +

Daily admission is $3 per adult, $1.50 for children ages 3-16 and free under age 3. Annual passes, camps, tours and special programs have separate pricing.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of St. Petersburg Parks and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Terry Tomalin Campground? +

The entrance facilities and some routes support mobility access, while natural trails include sand, roots, uneven surfaces and exposed sections. Ask staff for the most suitable current route.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of St. Petersburg Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Terry Tomalin Campground? +

Pets are not appropriate on preserve trails because of protected wildlife; follow posted preserve restrictions. Qualified service animals are handled under applicable access rules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of St. Petersburg Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Terry Tomalin Campground good for children? +

The raptor aviary, habitat trails and programs create a strong wildlife-learning visit. Bring water, insect repellent and sun protection, and never approach or feed alligators or other wildlife.
