Where should I park for Bending Arc? +

More than 500 paid onsite spaces are available in the Dolphin and Pelican lots and along Pier Drive, but parking is limited to six hours. Peak demand is typically Friday 4:00-9:00 p.m. and Saturday 1:00-8:00 p.m.; downtown garages are useful for longer visits.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of St. Petersburg ↗
When is Bending Arc open? +

Temporarily closed after hurricane damage. Check the official Pier notice before planning to see it.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of St. Petersburg ↗
Are there restrooms near Bending Arc? +

Public restrooms are available at multiple developed Pier locations, including family-oriented areas. Use the posted Pier map because the district is much longer than it appears from the entrance.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of St. Petersburg ↗
Is Bending Arc free? +

Pier entry, playground, splash pad, Spa Beach, lawns and public art are free. Parking, food, discovery-center admission, retail and some events cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of St. Petersburg ↗
How accessible is Bending Arc? +

The main promenade is paved, an accessible tram carries wheelchairs, and developed attractions include step-free routes. Wheelchairs and strollers are not rented onsite.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of St. Petersburg ↗
Are dogs allowed at Bending Arc? +

Leashed dogs are allowed in ordinary outdoor Pier areas but not on Spa Beach or in the Majeed Foundation Splash Pad. Businesses may set separate pet rules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of St. Petersburg ↗
Is Bending Arc good for children? +

The playground, splash pad, beach, Discovery Center and food options can fill several hours. Bring sun protection and dry clothes; water-play supervision and beach conditions remain the caregiver's responsibility.

How do you reach Bending Arc? +

The free Downtown Looper stops at the Pier entrance about every 15 minutes, and the Central Avenue Trolley, SunRunner connections, bicycles and pedestrian routes serve downtown.
