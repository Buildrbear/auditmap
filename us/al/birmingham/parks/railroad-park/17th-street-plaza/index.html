What people say

17th Street Plaza is Railroad Park's central covered gathering area and the location of the ranger station, Foundation office, public restrooms, and Boxcar Cafe. This is the most useful first-time arrival pin when restrooms or staff help matter. Sections A and B include covered space, but reservations and events can change how much is open. Bicycles, skateboards, and roller skates should use Powell Avenue rather than riding across the plaza.

Where should I park for 17th Street Plaza? +

Use metered street parking along First Avenue South or within three to four blocks. Do not use spaces reserved for neighboring businesses because vehicles may be ticketed or towed; meters are free after 6 p.m. and on weekends under the published policy. Are there restrooms near 17th Street Plaza? +

Restrooms are at the 17th Street Plaza behind the Railroad Park Foundation office, near the ranger station and café. They are more central than the park's outer lawns and trail ends.

What fees apply at 17th Street Plaza? +

Walking through and using public amenities during ordinary park operations is free. Reserved plaza sections, organized events, and purchases from the cafe cost separately.

How accessible is 17th Street Plaza? +

The outer loop is just under three-quarters of a mile. Paved and hard-surface paths wind through the park, while the Rail Trail runs its length and rises to skyline viewpoints.

Are dogs allowed at 17th Street Plaza? +

Yes, but dogs must remain leashed and under the owner's control throughout the park. Owners must clean up after them; there is no designated off-leash dog area. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.
