Current city pages

Railroad Park

Birmingham

Railroad Park is open daily from 7 a.m. to 11 p.m., 365 days a year. Rangers patrol the park and a security system monitors it around the clock, but visitors must leave by closing.

Red Mountain Park

Birmingham

Red Mountain Park is open daily from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., with separate holiday closings. Visitors should return to the trailhead before the gate closes.

Ruffner Mountain

Birmingham

Trails, preserve, and pavilion are open Tuesday–Sunday 7 a.m.–5 p.m. November–February and 7 a.m.–7 p.m. March–October; closed Mondays. The Nature Center is generally open Thursday–Sunday 9 a.m.–5 p.m., staffing permitting.
