Where should I park for Shawnee Mission Park Beach? +

Free parking is distributed around the lake, beach, marina, dog area, theatre and trailheads. Choose a destination before entering from Renner Road or Ogg Road.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Johnson County Park and Recreation District ↗
When is Shawnee Mission Park Beach open? +

The 2026 beach season is May 23-August 9: noon-4:00 p.m. Monday, Wednesday and Friday; noon-5:00 p.m. Tuesday and Thursday; and noon-6:00 p.m. Saturday and Sunday. It opens at 1:00 p.m. August 2, closes August 5, and opens at 1:00 p.m. August 8-9. Weather, water conditions and staffing can change the schedule.

Are there restrooms near Shawnee Mission Park Beach? +

Restrooms serve the beach, marina, shelters and developed recreation areas; most park restrooms are winterized between roughly November 1 and April 15.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Johnson County Park and Recreation District ↗
Is Shawnee Mission Park Beach free? +

General park entry is free. Beach admission, marina rentals, permits, programs, camping and reservations may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Johnson County Park and Recreation District ↗
How accessible is Shawnee Mission Park Beach? +

Developed facilities and several shelters have accessible parking and routes; natural shoreline and single-track trails vary substantially.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Johnson County Park and Recreation District ↗
Are dogs allowed at Shawnee Mission Park Beach? +

The 44-acre off-leash area includes woods, trails and pet-friendly lake access. It closes Tuesday and Thursday 5:00-9:00 a.m. for maintenance; pets are not allowed at the staffed swimming beach.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Johnson County Park and Recreation District ↗

The staffed beach is open noon-4:00 p.m. Monday, Wednesday and Friday; noon-5:00 p.m. Tuesday and Thursday; and noon-6:00 p.m. weekends during its 2026 season, with listed date modifications.
