What people say

Wichita's Riverside Parks form a historic three-part riverfront district. Central Riverside Park at 720 Nims is the principal family destination, with the free Kansas Wildlife Exhibit, playground, seasonal Celebration Plaza splash pad, half-mile historic walking loop, restrooms, picnic tables, gazebo, lake, self-service recreation rentals, public art, memorials, and mature shade trees.

Which Riverside Park should I visit in Wichita? +

Use Central Riverside Park at 720 Nims for the wildlife exhibit, playground, splash pad, restrooms, gazebo, walking loop, and public art. North Riverside is at 900 N. Bitting and South Riverside at 551 W. Central, with different recreation facilities.

Is there parking at Central Riverside Park? +

Yes. The City lists dedicated parking and internal drives serving Central Riverside Park. Additional lots serve North and South Riverside; choose the section before routing because the three park areas span both sides of the river.

Are there restrooms at Riverside Park? +

Yes. Central Riverside lists public restrooms near its developed family amenities. North and South Riverside also have restroom facilities, but the reservable Riverside Stage itself is listed without a restroom.

Is there a playground at Central Riverside Park? +

Yes. Central Riverside has a playground with sand safety surfacing near the splash pad, picnic areas, parking, restrooms, and Kansas Wildlife Exhibit.

When is the Riverside Park splash pad open? +

Celebration Plaza Splash Pad at Central Riverside is free and normally operates May through September, daily from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. It can close unexpectedly for safety or mechanical issues, so check Wichita's live splash-pad status before leaving.

Is there a walking path at Central Riverside Park? +

Yes. A half-mile oval walking path follows the historic park layout and includes twelve interpretive markers with archival images and local history. Sidewalks also connect the major Central Riverside amenities.

What can I see at Central Riverside Park? +

Highlights include the free Kansas Wildlife Exhibit with more than 25 species, The Hiker bronze, Solar Calendar stone installation, Spanish-American War memorial cannon, gazebo and lily-pad sculptures, and twelve historical markers along the loop.

Is Riverside Park shaded? +

Yes. The Riverside parks are known for mature shade and ornamental trees, with picnic areas and paths beneath the canopy. Splash-pad and playground exposure still varies by time of day, so summer visitors should bring sun protection.

Is Riverside Park in Wichita free? +

General park entry, parking, playground, seasonal splash pad, Kansas Wildlife Exhibit, walking loop, public art, picnic areas, and gazebo are free. Equipment rentals, reserved venues, events, and nearby attractions may charge.

Use Wichita's splash-pad status page on the day of your visit. The City updates unexpected safety or mechanical closures there; weather, special events, wildlife care, and maintenance can also affect individual park features.

Can I bring a dog to Riverside Park in Wichita? +

Yes, if the dog remains leashed, monitored, and under control and you remove all waste. Riverside Park is not on Wichita's official list of designated off-leash dog parks, so open lawns and paths are not off-leash areas. Keep pets away from the Kansas Wildlife Exhibit, splash pad, playground equipment, and any signed restricted space; use one of the City's named dog parks for off-leash exercise.

Where can I picnic at Riverside Park in Wichita? +

Central Riverside Park is the most practical family picnic destination, with picnic tables, restrooms, parking, playground, splash pad, gazebo, and extensive shade near 720 Nims. Most open shelters are first come, first served; Riverside Stage is one of the locations that can be rented for guaranteed group use. The Riverside system includes separate North, Central, and South sections, so route to Central Riverside when the wildlife exhibit, splash pad, and family amenities are the priority.

Which Wichita bus serves Riverside Park? +

Wichita Transit Route 17, the Park Line, serves Central Riverside Park with stops on Central Avenue at Nims and on Nims near Franklin. Use the Central-and-Nims stops for the south and central park or the Nims-and-Franklin stops for the north side, then walk to the wildlife exhibit, playground, splash pad, or river path. Check InfoPoint for live departures and direction before boarding.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Wichita Transit ↗
How accessible is Central Riverside Park in Wichita? +

Convenient sidewalks reach the park's major areas, and the City lists parking and restrooms among its facilities. Individual historic structures, lawn routes, the sand-surface playground, wildlife exhibit, splash pad, bridge approaches, and event setups can differ, so use the closest destination entrance and follow posted accessible routes.
