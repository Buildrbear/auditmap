Where should I park for Orpheus Statue? +

Free parking is available onsite, including accessible spaces, but capacity is limited during peak seasons and major programs. Parking is for park visitors and cannot be used for unrelated all-day waterfront parking.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are there restrooms near Orpheus Statue? +

Accessible restrooms and water fountains are available at the visitor center during building hours. Facilities outside the staffed area are limited.

Is Orpheus Statue free? +

Most grounds, trails, visitor center and orientation film are free. Entry to the historic zone and Star Fort is $15 per adult age 16 and older, valid for seven days; the site is cashless and accepts federal passes.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
How accessible is Orpheus Statue? +

Parking lots, visitor facilities, exhibits, restrooms and many paved routes are accessible. Wheelchairs are available to borrow, and exhibits include captioned and tactile resources.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Are dogs allowed at Orpheus Statue? +

Leashed pets are allowed on outdoor grounds but not inside buildings or the historic Star Fort. Service animals are permitted in buildings under applicable rules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Is Orpheus Statue good for children? +

The orientation film, ranger talks, flag programs and Junior Ranger activity make this a strong family history visit. Program schedules vary, so check the calendar before arrival.

How do you reach Orpheus Statue? +

MTA bus service reaches the Locust Point area, but the final walk can be substantial. Bicycles are allowed on paved park areas but not inside the historic zone.
