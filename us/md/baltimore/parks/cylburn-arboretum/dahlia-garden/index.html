destination

Dahlia Garden

4915 Greenspring Avenue, Baltimore, MD 21209
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Outdoor grounds are open Tuesday-Sunday, currently 8:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m., and closed Mondays. Building schedules change independently; the Nature Education Center and Mansion have limited posted hours, and the Vollmer Center is temporarily closed for maintenance.
Navigate

What people say

Dahlia Garden is a distinct landscaped or habitat area within Cylburn Arboretum; stay on established routes and respect plantings and wildlife. There is no food service onsite. Picnics are limited to groups of ten or fewer, tables and tents are prohibited, and posed photography or proposals require advance permission.

Where should I park for Dahlia Garden? +

Free onsite parking is available near the main entrance and Vollmer Center. Accessible spaces are in the upper lot; special events can change parking or use overflow areas.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Baltimore City Recreation and Parks ↗
When is Dahlia Garden open? +

Outdoor grounds are open Tuesday-Sunday, currently 8:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m., and closed Mondays. Building schedules change independently; the Nature Education Center and Mansion have limited posted hours, and the Vollmer Center is temporarily closed for maintenance.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Baltimore City Recreation and Parks ↗
Are there restrooms near Dahlia Garden? +

Restrooms are available in the Greenhouse Classroom during arboretum hours; staffed buildings provide additional facilities only when open.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Baltimore City Recreation and Parks ↗
Is Dahlia Garden free? +

General arboretum and garden entry is free. Classes, special events, rentals and permitted photography may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Baltimore City Recreation and Parks ↗
How accessible is Dahlia Garden? +

Paved routes connect parking, major buildings and formal gardens, with accessible parking near Vollmer Center. Woodland trails have natural surfaces and are not recommended for strollers.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Baltimore City Recreation and Parks ↗
Are dogs allowed at Dahlia Garden? +

Leashed dogs are welcome on outdoor grounds; clean-up bags are provided. Pets are not ordinarily allowed inside buildings, and service-animal rules apply.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Baltimore City Recreation and Parks ↗
Is Dahlia Garden good for children? +

Gardens, the children's garden and Nature Education Center are family-friendly. Balls, lawn games, kites and climbing trees are prohibited to protect the collection.
