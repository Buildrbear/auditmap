General Samuel Smith Monument is a specific landmark within Federal Hill Park; use this mapped point for the shortest practical approach and follow edge or crossing warnings. This is a viewpoint rather than a full-service park: bring water, plan a restroom stop and expect wind on the exposed summit. Fireworks and major events can radically change access.

Where should I park for General Samuel Smith Monument? +

The park has no dependable dedicated visitor lot. Use legal neighborhood street parking or a paid Inner Harbor garage, and expect tighter availability during weekends, festivals and stadium events.

Is General Samuel Smith Monument free? +

Park access, overlooks, playground and monuments are free. Nearby garages, attractions, permits and events cost separately.

How accessible is General Samuel Smith Monument? +

The upper plateau is largely level once reached, but the harbor-side staircase and surrounding grades are steep. Approach from Warren Avenue for the most practical step-free route.

Are dogs allowed at General Samuel Smith Monument? +

Leashed dogs are welcome in ordinary outdoor areas. There is no off-leash dog enclosure, and dogs should stay out of play equipment and posted event areas.

Is General Samuel Smith Monument good for children? +

The harbor view and ship-and-tower playground make a memorable short family stop. Closely supervise children around slopes, walls and busy bordering streets.
