Baltimore Waterfront Promenade

Baltimore

The outdoor promenade is generally available daily, but individual parks, bridges, attractions, fountains and construction zones keep separate schedules or may close temporarily.

Cylburn Arboretum

Baltimore

Outdoor grounds are open Tuesday-Sunday, currently 8:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m., and closed Mondays. Building schedules change independently; the Nature Education Center and Mansion have limited posted hours, and the Vollmer Center is temporarily closed for maintenance.

Druid Hill Park

Baltimore

Outdoor park areas are generally available from dawn to dusk. Rawlings Conservatory, the Maryland Zoo, pool, recreation facilities and permitted events keep separate schedules; check the exact destination before visiting.

Federal Hill Park

Baltimore

Outdoor park areas are generally available from dawn to dusk, subject to events, maintenance and posted restrictions.

Fort McHenry National Monument and Historic Shrine

Baltimore

Summer grounds open at 7:00 a.m. and close at 5:00 p.m. weekdays and 6:00 p.m. weekends; visitor center and Star Fort open at 9:00 a.m. Winter grounds are 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Seasonal transitions and holiday closures apply, so confirm the NPS hours page.

Gwynns Falls Leakin Park

Baltimore

Outdoor park areas are generally used from dawn to dusk. Carrie Murray Nature Center, Orianda Mansion, programs and special events have separate limited schedules; confirm before making them the purpose of a trip.

Lake Roland Park

Baltimore

Park grounds are open daily from sunrise to sunset. The Nature Center is generally open Tuesday-Sunday from 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m.; programs and seasonal operations vary.

Patterson Park

Baltimore

Park grounds are open daily from dawn to dusk. The Observatory is typically open Sundays from 11:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m. from mid-April through mid-October; pool, rink and recreation facilities keep separate seasonal hours.
