Where should I park for Falls Road Light Rail Entrance? +

Free parking is available at the Lakeside Drive lot and the Falls Road Light Rail lot. The light-rail lot connects to the park by a roughly 0.3-mile boardwalk and can be useful when the main lot is busy.

Are there restrooms near Falls Road Light Rail Entrance? +

The Nature Center provides the most dependable restrooms during building hours. Trail areas and Paw Point do not guarantee nearby year-round facilities.

Is Falls Road Light Rail Entrance free? +

General park, boardwalk and trail access are free. Programs may charge, and Paw Point is a code-controlled membership off-leash area with a separate annual fee.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Baltimore County Department of Recreation and Parks ↗
How accessible is Falls Road Light Rail Entrance? +

A wheelchair-accessible boardwalk links the Falls Road Light Rail lot to the central park area. Natural trails include roots, rocks, mud and grades; use the paved central routes for the most predictable surface.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Baltimore County Department of Recreation and Parks ↗
Are dogs allowed at Falls Road Light Rail Entrance? +

Dogs must be leashed in the general park. Paw Point is a fenced membership-only off-leash swim-and-play area; water access can close independently based on testing or conditions.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Baltimore County Department of Recreation and Parks ↗
Is Falls Road Light Rail Entrance good for children? +

The boardwalk, Nature Center, playground and short central trails form the easiest family visit. Longer colored trails vary in surface and can become muddy after rain.

How do you reach Falls Road Light Rail Entrance? +

MTA Light RailLink stops at Falls Road, where a boardwalk leads directly into the park. This is one of the region's most practical transit-to-trail connections.
