Black Rock Mill

11950 Clopper Road, Gaithersburg, MD 20878

What people say

The roofless stone remains of a historic mill stand beside Seneca Creek in a separate western park area, offering a cultural-landscape stop distinct from Clopper Lake's day-use facilities. Do not use the Clopper Lake entrance for a mill-only visit. The Black Rock parking lot is currently closed for streambank rehabilitation, so normal vehicle arrival is not available and visitors should not improvise roadside parking. The park alert currently closes the Black Rock parking lot until streambank repairs are complete. Treat the site as unavailable for ordinary vehicle arrival and check the official alert before attempting any route.
