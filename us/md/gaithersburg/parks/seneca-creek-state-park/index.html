Seneca Creek State Park

11950 Clopper Road, Gaithersburg, MD 20878
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Park hours are 8 a.m. to sunset March through October and 10 a.m. to sunset November through February. The Boat Center, programs, offices, events, and reserved facilities keep separate seasonal schedules.
Navigate

What people say

A 6,300-acre state park extending along 14 miles of Seneca Creek, with the 90-acre Clopper Lake day-use area, more than 50 miles of trails, seasonal boat rentals, playgrounds, picnic areas, fishing, historic sites, and varied natural landscapes.

When is Seneca Creek State Park open? +

Park hours are 8 a.m. to sunset March through October and 10 a.m. to sunset November through February. The Boat Center, programs, offices, events, and reserved facilities keep separate seasonal schedules.

Where should I park for Seneca Creek State Park? +

Parking is distributed among the park office, lake, Boat Center, playground, picnic, and trail areas. The Black Rock parking lot is currently closed for streambank rehabilitation, so do not navigate there expecting normal vehicle access.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Maryland Department of Natural Resources ↗
What is the best entrance for Seneca Creek State Park? +

Use 11950 Clopper Road for the Clopper Day-Use Area, lake, playground, picnics, and Boat Center. The park stretches for miles along Seneca Creek, and Black Rock Mill, trailheads, and other access points are separate from the main lake entrance.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Maryland Department of Natural Resources ↗
Are there restrooms at Seneca Creek State Park? +

The park reports 11 restrooms, seven reached by paved walkways, with service concentrated in developed day-use and picnic areas. Building and seasonal restroom availability can differ from the broader park schedule.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

Is Seneca Creek State Park free? +

Most weekday day use is free. On weekends and holidays from April through October, the posted service charge is $3 per person for Maryland residents and $5 per person for out-of-state visitors; children in car or booster seats and qualifying pass holders enter free.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Maryland Department of Natural Resources ↗
How accessible is Seneca Creek State Park? +

The park has an accessible office, six accessible pavilions, accessible family picnic sites, seven restrooms reached by paved walkways, and a paved lakefront fishing route at the Boat Center. Hiking trails are unpaved and may include roots, steps, and steep slopes.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Maryland Department of Natural Resources ↗
Are dogs allowed at Seneca Creek State Park? +

Leashed pets are allowed in permitted park areas, but pets other than service animals are not permitted in rental boats. Keep pets away from wildlife, active launching, playground equipment, and any posted restricted area.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Maryland Department of Natural Resources ↗
Is Seneca Creek State Park good for children? +

Clopper Lake views, the Tire Playground, picnics, fishing access, seasonal boats, and shorter trails can support a full family day. Swimming and wading are prohibited in Clopper Lake, and children need close supervision at every shoreline and dock.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Maryland Department of Natural Resources ↗
How do I reach Seneca Creek State Park without a car? +

The park is primarily vehicle-oriented and spread across a large landscape. If using rideshare, select the exact day-use destination and arrange a return before entering a trail or remote historic-site area.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Maryland Department of Natural Resources ↗
What should I know before visiting Seneca Creek State Park? +

Swimming and wading are prohibited in Clopper Lake. Current alerts also close the Black Rock parking lot and the bridge connection between Long Draft and Mink Hollow trails; boat operations, storm closures, trail conditions, and park alerts should be checked the same day.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Maryland Department of Natural Resources ↗
Should I bring food and water to Seneca Creek State Park? +

Bring water and a backup snack unless a currently open concession is confirmed near the exact destination. Food availability, vending, and event vendors are not consistent across this large public place.
