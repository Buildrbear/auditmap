Park

Woodward Park

2435 S Peoria Ave, Tulsa, OK 74114
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Woodward Park is open daily from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. The Tulsa Garden Center mansion, Teaching Garden, Visitor Center, Conservatory Greenhouse, and private events use shorter and seasonally different schedules.

What people say

Woodward Park is Tulsa's historic public garden and arboretum campus, with the Tulsa Municipal Rose Garden, Tulsa Arboretum, seasonal Linnaeus Teaching Garden, Conservatory Greenhouse, Tulsa Garden Center mansion, fountains, pocket gardens, mature trees, walking paths, lawns, horticultural programs, and event spaces.

Teaching Garden

What address should I use for Woodward Park? +

Route to Tulsa Garden Center at 2435 S. Peoria Avenue for the mansion, Teaching Garden, rose garden, and central campus. The park extends around several garden areas, so use the official map when seeking the arboretum, greenhouse, or a private event entrance.

Where should I park at Woodward Park? +

Use designated roads and parking areas around the Tulsa Garden Center and garden campus. Motor vehicles are prohibited on lawns, garden paths, and other non-road areas; private events can reserve or redirect the closest spaces.

What kind of walking is available at Woodward Park? +

Short garden walks connect the rose garden, arboretum, mansion, Teaching Garden, greenhouse, fountains, and lawns. Expect a mix of paved paths, steps, slopes, and garden surfaces rather than a long-distance recreation trail.

What should I see at Woodward Park? +

The principal destinations are the historic Tulsa Garden Center mansion, Tulsa Municipal Rose Garden, Arboretum, Linnaeus Teaching Garden, Conservatory Greenhouse, fountains, pocket gardens, and historic barn. Bloom and greenhouse conditions vary by season.

Are dogs allowed at Woodward Park? +

Leashed pets are allowed in general City park areas, but the Teaching Garden prohibits pets other than service animals. Keep animals out of planted beds and confirm building-specific policies before entering indoor facilities.

Is Woodward Park accessible? +

The developed garden campus includes road-accessible entrances and paths, but this historic landscape has varied slopes, surfaces, and building access. Contact Tulsa Garden Center before a mobility-specific visit or event to confirm the best entrance and currently accessible route.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Is Woodward Park free? +

General park, rose garden, arboretum, paths, lawns, and outdoor garden viewing are free. The Teaching Garden is pay-as-you-wish with a suggested $5 donation; classes, rentals, and private events may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

When is the Teaching Garden at Woodward Park open? +

The Teaching Garden generally runs from the first weekend in March through the second weekend in December, Tuesday-Saturday 9 a.m.-4 p.m., with weather-dependent March access and occasional private-event closures. The broader park remains open 5 a.m.-11 p.m.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Tulsa Garden Center ↗
What hours is Woodward Park open? +

The outdoor park is open daily from 5 a.m.-11 p.m. The Mansion, Teaching Garden, visitor center, and conservatory have much shorter schedules and can close for private events or seasonal changes.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Tulsa Garden Center ↗
Can I rely on restrooms at Woodward Park late at night? +

Do not assume staffed-building restrooms follow the park's 5 a.m.-11 p.m. schedule. The Mansion, visitor center, Teaching Garden, and conservatory close much earlier; use facilities during their published hours when restroom access is essential.

Tulsa Garden Center ↗
Can I hold a gathering at Woodward Park? +

Casual park use is allowed, but the Tulsa Garden Center requires an event permit for gatherings of 10 or more people. Permitted venues and fees differ, and the Teaching Garden prohibits pets except service animals.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Tulsa Garden Center ↗
Where can I find shade or weather cover at Woodward Park? +

Mature trees shade much of Woodward Park's garden landscape, and the park includes a modernist shelter and restroom building. The Conservatory provides indoor garden viewing only during posted seasonal hours, while the Tulsa Garden Center mansion and event spaces follow separate access rules. Gardens, lawns, rose beds, and paths can still be exposed. Use an open substantial building or enclosed vehicle rather than trees, glasshouses, or the open shelter during lightning, hail, or severe wind.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Tulsa Garden Center ↗
Is there a playground at Woodward Park? +

Woodward Park's official visitor information does not list conventional playground equipment. This destination is centered on the rose garden, arboretum, conservatory, historic mansion, lawns, trees, and quiet garden walks rather than swings or slides. Choose another Tulsa park for equipment play. Families can still make a short nature-focused visit, but children should stay on paths and avoid climbing garden structures, entering planted beds, or disturbing conservatory displays; building access and programs use separate hours.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Tulsa Garden Center ↗
Does Woodward Park have a splash pad or swimming pool? +

No public pool or splash pad is listed at Woodward Park. Its stream, pond, and other garden water features are ornamental and are not places to wade, swim, or play. Tulsa operates pools and water playgrounds at separately named parks, and Woodward is not among the City's current aquatic locations; check the current aquatics list before choosing an alternative.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Tulsa ↗
Can I take Tulsa Transit to Woodward Park? +

Use MetroLink Tulsa's trip planner with Woodward Park, the Tulsa Garden Center, or Linnaeus Teaching Gardens as the destination; the best final stop depends on which part of the grounds you need. Target the Peoria Avenue side for the main garden complex and allow for a walk through a sloped, multi-block landscape. Confirm live service and the return trip before leaving, particularly on weekends or for evening events when frequencies may be limited.
