What people say

Myriad Botanical Gardens is Oklahoma City's free 15-acre downtown garden and family space, with the ticketed Crystal Bridge Conservatory, Children's Garden and adaptive playground, seasonal Thunder Fountain and water features, off-leash dog park, Mo's Carousel, ornamental collections, lake, public art, lawns, event spaces, and winter ice rink.

What address should I use for Myriad Botanical Gardens? +

Use 301 W. Reno Avenue, Oklahoma City, OK 73102 for the main gardens and Crystal Bridge Conservatory. The grounds fill a downtown block, so approach from the side closest to the Children's Garden, dog park, carousel, or event destination.

Where should I park for Myriad Botanical Gardens? +

Use legal metered street parking around the gardens or a nearby downtown garage. Myriad's parking guidance notes that vehicles with a valid disability placard or plate may use downtown meters free for the posted time limit; event demand can fill nearby spaces.

Are there public restrooms at Myriad Botanical Gardens? +

Yes. Visitor restrooms are available in developed garden facilities around the Crystal Bridge and public-use areas. Conservatory access, events, and late-evening grounds hours can affect which indoor facilities are open, so use restrooms before facilities close.

Is there a children's playground at Myriad Gardens? +

Yes. The Children's Garden includes a playground with adaptive equipment, nature and story areas, and nearby seasonal water play. Pets are not permitted inside the Children's Garden, though service animals are allowed.

Is the Thunder Fountain open at Myriad Gardens? +

The outdoor water features, including the family-favorite Thunder Fountain area, are listed daily through Labor Day from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Weather, water quality, maintenance, and seasonal changes can interrupt operation.

Where is the dog park at Myriad Botanical Gardens? +

The fenced off-leash dog park is east of the Crystal Bridge Conservatory. Leashed pets are welcome on most exterior grounds but not inside the conservatory, Children's Garden, or water features; only service animals may enter the conservatory.

Is Myriad Botanical Gardens wheelchair accessible? +

The renovated Crystal Bridge includes an elevator, and a complimentary wheelchair is available first come with a driver's license held by guest services. Outdoor paths and adaptive playground features support accessible visits, though event layouts can alter routes.

What art can I see at Myriad Botanical Gardens? +

Outdoor works include Hans Van de Bovenkamp's red Gateway sculpture, Kendall Howerton's limestone Goldfish, Don Narcomey's Storyteller's Chair, and Nancy Schön's Friendship prairie-dog sculpture. These pieces are on the free exterior grounds.

Is Myriad Botanical Gardens free? +

The outdoor grounds, ornamental gardens, Children's Garden, playground, water features, dog park, paths, and public art are free. Crystal Bridge Conservatory requires admission; carousel, ice rink, classes, food, parking, and some events have separate costs.

What has separate hours at Myriad Gardens? +

The outdoor grounds stay open 6 a.m.-11 p.m., but Crystal Bridge, water features, carousel, ice rink, shops, classes, and events use separate or seasonal schedules. Crystal Bridge closes on Thanksgiving and Christmas, and water features are seasonal through Labor Day.

Where can I cool off or find shade at Myriad Botanical Gardens? +

Tanenbaum Reflection Garden is the most clearly designated shady outdoor respite, and the Children's Garden has a covered pavilion. Crystal Bridge Conservatory is climate controlled but ticketed and only available during its posted hours. Devon Lawn, Seasonal Plaza, and other event spaces can be exposed; in severe weather use an open substantial building rather than relying on a garden pavilion.

What are the walking surfaces and slopes at Myriad Botanical Gardens? +

The 15-acre outdoor garden combines paved accessible perimeter routes with winding stone paths, meandering children's-garden walks, terraces, lawns, bridges, and stairways around the lake. Accessible paths reach the water's edge without using the stair fountain, while Meinders Terrace and planted slopes can involve grades and less direct routes. Seasonal ice-rink construction, fountains, wet stone, events, and dense plantings can narrow circulation; use the official map and select the accessible perimeter route when avoiding stairs.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Where can I picnic at Myriad Botanical Gardens? +

Sheridan Lawn is the most practical casual picnic area, and visitors may bring a blanket, chairs, and prepared food for open-lawn programs; glass is prohibited. Keep all food and drinks out of Thunder Fountain and the seasonal splash plaza, and do not take food into the Crystal Bridge Conservatory. Tables can be limited or added only for events, so bring a blanket. Reserve a Gardens venue or the Children's Garden Pavilion for a birthday, guaranteed seating, or organized group setup.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

How do I reach Myriad Botanical Gardens without a car? +

The Gardens sit in the walkable downtown transit area between Reno Avenue, Hudson Avenue, Sheridan Avenue, and Robinson Avenue. Use EMBARK's live planner for the nearest bus or OKC Streetcar stop to your starting point, then walk to the garden entrance that best matches the Crystal Bridge, Children's Garden, or event lawn. Downtown construction and event closures can shift stops, so verify the final block before leaving.
