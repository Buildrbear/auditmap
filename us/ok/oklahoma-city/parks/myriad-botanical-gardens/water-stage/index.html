What people say

Water Stage is Myriad Botanical Gardens' outdoor amphitheater and floating-looking stage on the lagoon, paired with the West Plaza for performances, ceremonies, and gatherings. This is an active event venue beside open water, not a guaranteed public seating area at all times. Check the Gardens calendar and on-site signs before planning a quiet stop, keep children back from the lagoon edge, and use the exact pin to distinguish Water Stage from other lawns and performance spaces.

Are there restrooms near Water Stage? +

Yes. Visitor restrooms are available in developed garden facilities around the Crystal Bridge and public-use areas. Conservatory access, events, and late-evening grounds hours can affect which indoor facilities are open, so use restrooms before facilities close.

What fees apply at Water Stage? +

Viewing the Water Stage during ordinary garden access is free. Reserving the venue, attending a ticketed event, nearby parking, food, and other services can cost separately.

How accessible is Water Stage? +

The renovated Crystal Bridge includes an elevator, and a complimentary wheelchair is available first come with a driver's license held by guest services. Outdoor paths and adaptive playground features support accessible visits, though event layouts can alter routes.

Are dogs allowed at Water Stage? +

The fenced off-leash dog park is east of the Crystal Bridge Conservatory. Leashed pets are welcome on most exterior grounds but not inside the conservatory, Children's Garden, or water features; only service animals may enter the conservatory. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.
