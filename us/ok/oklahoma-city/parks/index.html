Lake Hefner Park

Oklahoma City

Choose a destination before routing: Stars and Stripes Park for south-side recreation and views, Lions Children's Park for the playground, East Wharf for restaurants, the north lot off Hefner Road for north trail access, or a named marina, fishing dock, or golf facility.

Myriad Botanical Gardens

Oklahoma City

Use 301 W. Reno Avenue, Oklahoma City, OK 73102 for the main gardens and Crystal Bridge Conservatory. The grounds fill a downtown block, so approach from the side closest to the Children's Garden, dog park, carousel, or event destination.

Scissortail Park

Oklahoma City

Use 300 SW 7th Street, Oklahoma City, OK 73109 for the general Upper Park. Route to 650 S. Hudson Avenue for the Boathouse, 655 S. Robinson Avenue for the playground, or 403 SW 10th Street for Lower Park's Sports Pavilion.
