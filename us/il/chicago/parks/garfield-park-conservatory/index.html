Park

Garfield Park Conservatory

300 N. Central Park Avenue, Chicago, IL 60624
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open Wednesday 10 a.m.-8 p.m. and Thursday-Sunday 10 a.m.-5 p.m.; closed Monday and Tuesday. Last entry is 45 minutes before closing, and holidays can change hours.
Navigate

What people say

A landmark glass conservatory with Palm, Fern, Desert, Aroid, Show, and Sugar from the Sun houses, outdoor gardens, seasonal displays, and a reservable children's garden.

When is Garfield Park Conservatory open? +

Open Wednesday 10 a.m.-8 p.m. and Thursday-Sunday 10 a.m.-5 p.m.; closed Monday and Tuesday. Last entry is 45 minutes before closing, and holidays can change hours.

Where should I park for Garfield Park Conservatory? +

Free visitor parking is available in the Conservatory lot and on legal nearby streets, but reservations and special events can fill the closest spaces.

What is the best entrance for Garfield Park Conservatory? +

Use the main entrance at 300 N. Central Park Avenue near Lake Street. The CTA Green Line Conservatory station is immediately beside the grounds.

Are there restrooms at Garfield Park Conservatory? +

Is Garfield Park Conservatory free? +

Chicago residents receive free admission with residency information; current nonresident admission is $10 adults and $5 seniors, with child and other categories shown during reservation. Timed entry is required.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

How accessible is Garfield Park Conservatory? +

The Conservatory is wheelchair accessible except for the historic Fern Room. The new children's garden includes a wheelchair-accessible spiral ramp and sensory-friendly area.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Garfield Park Conservatory? +

Pets are not allowed inside the Conservatory or children's garden. Service animals follow applicable access rules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

Is Garfield Park Conservatory good for children? +

The indoor houses and new Elizabeth Morse Genius Children's Garden are strong year-round family options. The children's garden requires its own 30-minute reservation in addition to Conservatory entry.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

How do I reach Garfield Park Conservatory without a car? +

The CTA Green Line Conservatory station is the most direct option, with buses on Lake Street and Central Park Avenue.

What should I know before visiting Garfield Park Conservatory? +

Reserve both general timed admission and a separate children's-garden slot when needed. Arrivals are accepted within 30 minutes of the reservation, and last entry precedes closing.

What weather check matters before visiting Garfield Park Conservatory? +

Check current heat, air quality, storms, wind, lake or river conditions, and operator alerts. Chicago weather can close water, beach, climbing, skating, garden, and event areas independently of general park hours.
