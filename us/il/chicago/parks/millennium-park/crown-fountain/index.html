Crown Fountain

201 E. Randolph Street, Chicago, IL 60602
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Free during park hours when operating. Water can be off for weather, maintenance, winter, or events.
Navigate

What people say

Two video towers and a shallow reflecting pool create interactive seasonal public art and popular free water play. Enter from Michigan Avenue near Madison Street; the fountain is south of Cloud Gate. Free during park hours when operating. Water can be off for weather, maintenance, winter, or events.

Where exactly is Crown Fountain? +

Enter from Michigan Avenue near Madison Street; the fountain is south of Cloud Gate.

Where should I park for Crown Fountain? +

Paid Millennium Park and Grant Park garages are directly below or nearby. CTA and Metra are usually more predictable than downtown street parking.

Is Crown Fountain free? +

Park admission, public art, garden access, and many concerts are free. Garages, food, tours, and selected events charge.

How accessible is Crown Fountain? +

Major paths, Cloud Gate plaza, Crown Fountain, Lurie Garden, and pavilion seating areas have developed accessible routes; crowds and event barricades can alter circulation.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026
City of Chicago ↗
Is Crown Fountain good for children? +

Cloud Gate reflections, Crown Fountain water play, lawns, and the pedestrian bridge to Maggie Daley Park make an easy family cluster. Bring dry clothes when the fountain is operating.

How do you reach Crown Fountain? +

Washington/Wabash, Lake, Monroe, Millennium Station, and downtown buses serve different approaches. Avoid driving when a major festival or concert is scheduled.
