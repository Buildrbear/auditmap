Chicago Lakefront Trail

Chicago

The Lakefront Trail follows normal Chicago Park District operating hours, generally 6 a.m.-11 p.m. Construction, storms, waves, ice, races, and special events can close segments.

Chicago Riverwalk

Chicago

The Chicago Riverwalk is generally open daily 6 a.m.-11 p.m. Vendors, museums, tours, boat docks, elevators, and restrooms follow separate seasonal hours.

Garfield Park Conservatory

Chicago

Open Wednesday 10 a.m.-8 p.m. and Thursday-Sunday 10 a.m.-5 p.m.; closed Monday and Tuesday. Last entry is 45 minutes before closing, and holidays can change hours.

Grant Park

Chicago

Grant Park is generally open daily 6 a.m.-11 p.m. Buckingham Fountain, Museum Campus institutions, fields, concessions, and major events follow separate schedules.

Jackson Park

Chicago

Jackson Park is open daily 6 a.m.-9 p.m. The fieldhouse, beaches, golf, harbors, concessions, and programs follow separate schedules.

Lincoln Park

Chicago

The outdoor park is generally open daily 6 a.m.-11 p.m. The zoo, conservatory, beaches, fieldhouses, harbors, and concessions follow separate schedules.

Maggie Daley Park

Chicago

The park is open daily 6 a.m.-10 p.m. The Play Garden is generally 6 a.m.-9 p.m.; skating, climbing, mini golf, rentals, cafe, and fieldhouse use seasonal schedules.

Millennium Park

Chicago

Millennium Park is generally open daily 6 a.m.-11 p.m. Individual art areas, fountains, gardens, concerts, security perimeters, and services can use separate schedules.
