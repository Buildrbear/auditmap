Promontory Point Trail Access

Ardmore Avenue to 71st Street, Chicago, IL
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Park access is free. Lake steps are not a guarded beach, waves can wash over rocks, and events or preservation work can limit sections.
Navigate

What people say

A historic limestone lakefront peninsula in Burnham Park with skyline views, lawns, fire circles, steps, and trail connections. Leave the main trail near 55th Street and follow the marked park path onto the peninsula. Park access is free. Lake steps are not a guarded beach, waves can wash over rocks, and events or preservation work can limit sections.

How accessible is Promontory Point Trail Access? +

The paved trail is broadly accessible, but entrance ramps, underpasses, construction, crowding, windblown sand, standing water, and distance between exits vary.

Is Promontory Point Trail Access good for children? +

Separated pedestrian and bike routes improve safety, but the corridor is fast and crowded. Select a short segment with a known restroom, turnaround, and low-stress access.
