Como Regional Park

St. Paul

The outdoor regional park is open sunrise-11:00 p.m. Zoo, conservatory, Japanese garden, Como Town, carousel, pool, pavilion restaurant, golf, rentals, and ski center all keep separate schedules.

Harriet Island Regional Park

St. Paul

General Saint Paul park hours are sunrise-11:00 p.m.; reserved pavilions, stages, docks, cruises, restrooms, event enclosures, and adjacent island spaces follow separate schedules.

Phalen Regional Park

St. Paul

The park is open sunrise-11:00 p.m. In 2026 the guarded beach is weather permitting through September 7, generally noon-8:00 p.m. before August and noon-7:00 p.m. later; splash pad runs 9:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. June 13-September 7.
