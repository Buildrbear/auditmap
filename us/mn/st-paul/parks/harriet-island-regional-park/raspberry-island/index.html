What people say

Raspberry Island sits under the Wabasha Bridge with a bandshell, trails, and downtown access; it is a related riverfront destination across the channel. Harriet Island is both a daily park and major event venue. Check the event calendar, pavilion rentals, flood or river conditions, dock access, road closures, and seasonal restrooms before a specific visit.

Where should I park for Raspberry Island? +

Surface lots and event parking serve Harriet Island, but festivals, concerts, floods, and private rentals can close roads or reserve spaces. Use the exact event or facility entrance.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

When is Raspberry Island open? +

General Saint Paul park hours are sunrise-11:00 p.m.; reserved pavilions, stages, docks, cruises, restrooms, event enclosures, and adjacent island spaces follow separate schedules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Saint Paul Parks and Recreation ↗
Are there restrooms near Raspberry Island? +

Restrooms are seasonal, with additional facilities during events or inside reserved buildings. Do not expect public access inside a private pavilion rental.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Saint Paul Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Raspberry Island free? +

Park lawns, riverwalk, skyline views, play area, and ordinary picnics are free. Parking during some events, pavilion rentals, cruises, festivals, food, and programs cost.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Saint Paul Parks and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Raspberry Island? +

Paved riverwalk and developed event facilities provide accessible routes, but flood closures, grass event fields, docks, snow, and bridge grades can affect access.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Saint Paul Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Raspberry Island? +

Leash dogs and keep them out of private events, play equipment, food areas, and posted docks or facilities. Crowds, fireworks, heat, and river edges add risk.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Saint Paul Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Raspberry Island good for children? +

Skyline views, boats, lawns, play area, picnics, riverwalk, and festivals can suit families. Supervise continuously near docks and the Mississippi River.

How do you reach Raspberry Island? +

Biking via regional trails, walking from downtown bridges, Metro Transit, rideshare, and driving are options. Bridge grades and event street closures matter.
