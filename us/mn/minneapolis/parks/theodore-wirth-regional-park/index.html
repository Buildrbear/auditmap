What people say

A 700-plus-acre year-round recreation landscape with The Trailhead, Wirth Lake Beach, wildflower garden, quaking bog, children's garden, golf, disc golf, mountain biking, skiing, tubing, snowshoeing, trails, and skyline views.

When is Theodore Wirth Regional Park open? +

Developed areas are generally open 6:00 a.m.-midnight and undeveloped areas 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. The Trailhead, gardens, beach, golf, rentals, ski trails, and winter operations keep separate schedules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
Where should I park for Theodore Wirth Regional Park? +

Use The Trailhead lots for central recreation, Wirth Beach for lake and cycling access, or the small metered garden and bog lots. Event and winter demand can fill lots; parkway shoulder parking is restricted.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
What is the best entrance for Theodore Wirth Regional Park? +

Theodore Wirth is larger than many visitors expect. Choose The Trailhead, beach, Eloise Butler garden, Quaking Bog, golf, or a winter activity before driving.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
Are there restrooms at Theodore Wirth Regional Park? +

Restrooms are most dependable at The Trailhead and staffed seasonal facilities. Natural trails, bog paths, garden areas, and remote loops have limited services.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
Is Theodore Wirth Regional Park free? +

General park and many trails are free. Golf, ski passes, rentals, tubing, lessons, food, parking in selected lots, and events cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
How accessible is Theodore Wirth Regional Park? +

The Trailhead and developed facilities provide accessible access; natural trails, boardwalks, snow, sand, hills, and off-road cycling terrain vary widely.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
Are dogs allowed at Theodore Wirth Regional Park? +

Leash dogs under park rules and keep them out of beaches, gardens, groomed ski-only routes, and posted facilities. Activity-specific restrictions apply.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
Is Theodore Wirth Regional Park good for children? +

Beach, children's garden, easy paths, tubing, skiing, disc golf, and Trailhead facilities offer year-round choices. Match terrain and winter conditions to the youngest participant.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
How do I reach Theodore Wirth Regional Park without a car? +

Metro Transit, biking via Luce Line and parkway trails, rideshare, and driving reach different park sections; no single stop serves every destination.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
What should I know before visiting Theodore Wirth Regional Park? +

Off-road trails close to all users when wet; if a foot or tire leaves a mark, stay off. Verify ski or tubing operations, beach status, garden season, trail conditions, and current Trailhead food service.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Theodore Wirth Regional Park? +

Check the Twin Cities forecast for thunderstorms, heat or air-quality alerts, wind, snow, ice, extreme cold, flooding, and current park notices. Beaches, water features, trails, ski routes, river access, concessions, and outdoor attractions can close independently as conditions change.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board ↗
