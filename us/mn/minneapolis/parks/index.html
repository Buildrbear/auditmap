Chain of Lakes Regional Park

Minneapolis

Developed park areas are generally open 6:00 a.m.-midnight and undeveloped areas 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Beaches, rentals, concessions, concerts, streetcar, ice rinks, and gardens keep seasonal schedules.

Minneapolis Sculpture Garden

Minneapolis

The Sculpture Garden is free and open daily 6:00 a.m.-midnight year-round. Walker galleries, cafe, restrooms, tours, programs, and indoor services keep shorter schedules and holiday closures.

Minnehaha Regional Park

Minneapolis

Developed areas are open 6:00 a.m.-midnight and undeveloped areas 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Pergola Garden is 7:30 a.m.-10:00 p.m.; food, wading pool, historic sites, and rentals have separate seasonal hours.

Mississippi Gorge Regional Park

Minneapolis

Developed areas generally follow 6:00 a.m.-midnight hours and undeveloped natural areas 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Trail, stair, beach, flood, and construction closures may be more restrictive.

Theodore Wirth Regional Park

Minneapolis

Developed areas are generally open 6:00 a.m.-midnight and undeveloped areas 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. The Trailhead, gardens, beach, golf, rentals, ski trails, and winter operations keep separate schedules.
