What people say

Norfolk's maritime discovery center combines hands-on harbor exhibits, the free Hampton Roads Naval Museum and self-guided access aboard the massive Iowa-class Battleship Wisconsin.

When is Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin open? +

Nauticus is open Tuesday-Sunday 10:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. and closed Monday. Holiday, maintenance, weather and special-event schedules can vary.

Where should I park for Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin? +

Use the adjacent Town Point Garage or another downtown city garage. Five onsite accessible spaces are first-come and are unavailable on cruise-ship days.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Nauticus ↗
What is the best entrance for Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin? +

Enter Nauticus at 1 Waterside Drive for ticketing before boarding Wisconsin. Victory Rover cruises depart nearby but require their own time and ticket; arrive early enough to park and walk from the garage.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Nauticus ↗
Are there restrooms at Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin? +

Restrooms and elevators are available inside Nauticus. Shipboard facilities and circulation are limited, so use the museum facilities before climbing into less accessible sections.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Nauticus ↗
Is Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin free? +

General admission is currently $19.95 for ages 13+, $16 for ages 3-12 and free for ages 0-2 with a reserved ticket. Guided ship tours and Victory Rover cruises cost extra; the Hampton Roads Naval Museum is free.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Nauticus ↗
How accessible is Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin? +

Nauticus and Wisconsin's main deck are wheelchair accessible. Most interior ship routes involve steep ladders, narrow passages and high thresholds; the Ship Experience Access Room provides a virtual alternative.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Nauticus ↗
Are dogs allowed at Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin? +

Pets are not permitted inside Nauticus or aboard the ship; trained service animals follow accessibility and safety procedures. Confirm accommodations before arrival because ship spaces are unusual.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Nauticus ↗
Is Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin good for children? +

Hands-on maritime exhibits and the battleship work well for school-age children. Toddlers need close supervision, and adults should assess whether steep ladders and confined spaces are appropriate.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Nauticus ↗
How do I reach Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin without a car? +

The Tide light rail, buses, ferry, downtown sidewalks and Elizabeth River Trail reach the waterfront. The MacArthur Square light-rail area is a practical downtown approach.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Nauticus ↗
What should I know before visiting Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin? +

The general ticket includes Nauticus and standard Battleship Wisconsin access, but specialized tours add fees. Wear secure shoes, keep both hands available on ladders and use the accessible main deck or virtual access room if climbing is unsafe.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Nauticus ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin? +

Check current Hampton Roads weather, surf and rip-current forecasts, tides, wind, heat index, air quality and operator alerts. Thunderstorms, coastal flooding, high water, extreme heat and special events can independently affect roads, beaches, trails, bridges or facilities.
