Lafayette Park

Norfolk

Use 3500 Granby Street, Norfolk, VA 23504 for the general park. Reservable shelters are accessed from LaVallette Avenue, while smaller first-come shelters are reached from Zoo Drive beside the Virginia Zoo and Ernie Morgan Center.

Nauticus and Battleship Wisconsin

Norfolk

Nauticus is open Tuesday-Sunday 10:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. and closed Monday. Holiday, maintenance, weather and special-event schedules can vary.

Norfolk Botanical Garden

Norfolk

The Garden is currently open daily 9:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. through September 30. Seasonal daytime hours and evening-event schedules change later in the year, so check the dated admission calendar before traveling.

Town Point Park

Norfolk

The public waterfront is generally available daily outside posted closures. Festivals, fountain operation, cruise-ship security and private permits can change access and hours independently.
