What people say

17th Street Park is a distinct mapped destination within Virginia Beach Oceanfront and Boardwalk; navigate to this point instead of the general park pin and check posted conditions on arrival. There is no single best Oceanfront entrance. Glass, public alcohol consumption and many disruptive activities are prohibited; storms, rip currents, special events and military aircraft noise can change the experience quickly. Check flags and lifeguard direction before swimming.

Where should I park for 17th Street Park? +

Use a city garage, municipal surface lot or legal metered space in the Resort Area. Rates are dynamic in 2026, so compare the live city parking map rather than relying on an old flat rate; garages are usually simpler for a longer family visit.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Virginia Beach Resort Management ↗
When is 17th Street Park open? +

The beach and boardwalk are publicly accessible throughout the day, subject to emergency and event closures. Lifeguards generally staff the Oceanfront from 9:30 a.m. to at least 6:00 p.m. between Memorial Day weekend and Labor Day weekend. This feature may have shorter, seasonal or daylight-only hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Virginia Beach Resort Management ↗
Are there restrooms near 17th Street Park? +

Permanent public restrooms are located at 2nd, 17th, 20th, 24th, 30th and 31st streets. Seasonal operating conditions and maintenance can affect individual facilities.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Virginia Beach Resort Management ↗
Is 17th Street Park free? +

Beach, boardwalk, bike path, monuments and city parks are free. Parking, rentals, fishing-pier access, food, concerts and ticketed events charge separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Virginia Beach Resort Management ↗
How accessible is 17th Street Park? +

The boardwalk is paved and Grommet Island provides an accessible beach playground and access route. Beach wheelchairs and access mats are available at selected locations; sand conditions still vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Virginia Beach Resort Management ↗
Are dogs allowed at 17th Street Park? +

From Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day, leashed dogs may use the boardwalk from 6:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m., but dogs are not allowed on the Resort Area sandy beach from Rudee Loop to 42nd Street. North and south beaches allow dogs before 10:00 a.m. or after 6:00 p.m. Off-season, dogs may use the beach and boardwalk throughout the day; boardwalk leashes and cleanup rules still apply.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Virginia Beach Resort Management ↗
Is 17th Street Park good for children? +

Lifeguarded summer zones, Grommet Island, street-end playgrounds and frequent restrooms make the central Oceanfront practical for families. Keep children within arm's reach in surf and separate from the bicycle path.

How do you reach 17th Street Park? +

Seasonal WAVE trolleys and Hampton Roads Transit serve the Resort Area. Walking and biking are effective once parked, but bicycles and surreys belong on the separate bike path, not the boardwalk.
