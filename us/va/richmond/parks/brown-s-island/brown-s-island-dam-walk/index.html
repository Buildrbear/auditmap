What people say

Where should I park for Brown's Island Dam Walk? +

Downtown surface lots and decks serve Tredegar, Canal and Cary streets. Event and construction closures change availability; use Venture Richmond's current parking list and never assume the small Belle Isle lot is available.

Ordinary public riverfront access is free when open. Museums, parking, concerts, festivals and special events can charge separately.

The improvement project is adding accessible entrances, bathrooms and terraces. During construction, detours and closed paths can change step-free connections.

Brown's Island itself is closed during construction. For independently open adjacent routes, follow the posted rule at that route and do not enter fenced work areas.
