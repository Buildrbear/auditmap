Park directory

Current city pages

Brown's Island

Richmond

Brown's Island is closed to public access during construction. Venture Richmond plans to reopen it for the Richmond Folk Festival October 9-11, 2026, with full project completion anticipated in early 2027. Adjacent routes and museums use separate conditions.

Byrd Park

Richmond

Open daily from sunrise to sunset, except the self-lighted tennis complex may operate later. Concessions, pedal boats, events and facilities use seasonal schedules.

Forest Hill Park

Richmond

Open daily from sunrise to sunset. Market, concert, Stone House rental and court schedules are separate.

James River Park System

Richmond

Park areas are open daily from sunrise to sunset. River conditions, high water, construction and emergency response can close individual access points independently.

Libby Hill Park

Richmond

Open daily from sunrise to sunset under Richmond city park rules. Special events and maintenance can affect individual lawns or streets.

Maymont

Richmond

Grounds, gardens and farm are generally open daily 10:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. April-September and 10:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. October-March. Mansion, Nature Center, tours and events use separate schedules.

Richmond National Battlefield Park

Richmond

Battlefield grounds and trails are open daily sunrise to sunset. Chimborazo and Cold Harbor visitor centers are open Wednesday-Sunday 9:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m.; Fort Harrison Visitor Center is closed for 2026.

Virginia Museum of Fine Arts

Richmond

Open 365 days a year, 10:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. daily and until 9:00 p.m. Wednesday-Friday. Restaurants, library, shop and programs use separate schedules.
