Where should I park for Dogwood Dell Amphitheater? +

Use the lot or legal roadside spaces nearest Fountain Lake, Shields Lake picnic shelters, Dogwood Dell or Barker Field. The park spans both sides of major roads and event traffic can change access.

Is Dogwood Dell Amphitheater free? +

General park, paths, playground, courts and dog park access is free. Pedal boats, concessions, reservations and programmed activities may charge; fishing requires a Virginia license.

How accessible is Dogwood Dell Amphitheater? +

Sidewalks connect the three-lake area and developed recreation features. Grass slopes, older paths and event layouts vary, so use the closest accessible parking.

Are dogs allowed at Dogwood Dell Amphitheater? +

Dogs are allowed on leash in Byrd Park, with off-leash use confined to Barker Field Dog Park. Keep dogs away from wildlife and never feed waterfowl.

Is Dogwood Dell Amphitheater good for children? +

The playground, summer boats, lakes and free Dogwood Dell programming support a full family visit. Children need close supervision beside all three lakes and road crossings.
