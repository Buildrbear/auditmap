Dorrs Pond

East Side, Manchester, NH
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Livingston Park is open daily from sunrise to sunset. Livingston Pool and the Dorrs Pond warming hut use separate seasonal schedules.

What people say

Dorrs Pond is an 18-acre pond within Livingston Park, bordered by woods, wetland boardwalks, and an approximately 0.9-mile unpaved walking and running loop. Use the onsite parking reached from Red Coat Lane off Hooksett Road; the trail circles the pond and connects to the boat-launch and warming-hut area. The City lists paddling, fishing, and seasonal ice skating, but conditions and safe ice access can change. The park is open sunrise to sunset, while the warming hut has separate seasonal hours. The loop is unpaved and includes elevated wetland boardwalks, so expect a natural surface and check local notices before planning water or winter activities.

What address should I use for Dorrs Pond? +

Use 156 Hooksett Road, Manchester, NH. The park spans 131 acres, so choose the north Beech Street lot, Hooksett Road tennis-court lot, or a Red Coat Lane lot based on whether you need the playground, pool, fields, trail, or Dorrs Pond.

Where should I park at Dorrs Pond? +

Official on-site lots are at the north end of Beech Street, beside the tennis courts off Hooksett Road, and at several points reached from Red Coat Lane. Sports, pool demand, and events can make the closest lot busy.

Are there restrooms at Dorrs Pond? +

Yes. The City's reservation listing includes restrooms, and staffed seasonal facilities may provide additional access. Availability can depend on pool, field, warming-hut, and event operations, so do not assume every building is open all day.

How much does Dorrs Pond cost? +

General park, playground, trail, pond, fields, track, and court access is free. Pool access, reservations, leagues, and organized programs may charge separately.
