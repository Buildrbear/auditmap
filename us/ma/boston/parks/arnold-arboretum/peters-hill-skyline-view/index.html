destination

Peters Hill skyline view

125 Arborway, Boston, MA 02130
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The landscape is open daily sunrise to sunset. The Hunnewell Visitor Center is generally open daily 10 a.m.-4 p.m.; programs and facilities can differ.
Navigate

What people say

Peters Hill is the Arboretum's highest point and requires a sustained climb. Use Peters Hill Gate for the shortest approach and bring water because the summit is exposed. Blooms and foliage are seasonal, hills are steeper than the map suggests, and paths can be icy or muddy. Check storms, heat, wind, ticks, restroom hours, and gate location.

Hunnewell Visitor Center

The Visitor Center is at Arborway Gate, 125 Arborway, and is the best first stop for maps, staff help, accessible restrooms, and wheelchair loans during its hours.

2 destination

Bussey Hill and lilac collection

Bussey Hill holds the celebrated lilac collection and is busiest around spring bloom and Lilac Sunday. Roads climb steadily and bloom timing varies each year.
View guide →
Photo: Lauren A Siegel
3 destination

Hemlock Hill and woodland collections

Hemlock Hill is a shaded, natural-feeling collection area with steeper and less even routes than the main Arborway landscape. Wet leaves, roots, ticks, and winter ice matter.
View guide →
Photo: Lauren A Siegel
4 destination

Larz Anderson Bonsai Collection

The historic bonsai and penjing collection is displayed seasonally near the Dana Greenhouses. Confirm exhibit dates and hours before making it the purpose of a visit; the surrounding Arboretum remains open sunrise to sunset.
View guide →
Photo: Daderot
5 destination

Bradley Rosaceous Collection

This five-acre rose-family collection sits where Meadow, Bussey Hill, and Forest Hills roads meet. Grass paths between beds are not wheelchair accessible, and a focused visit usually takes 20 to 60 minutes.
View guide →
Photo: Percival Kestreltail
6 destination

Explorers Garden

Explorers Garden occupies a high, sheltered slope near Bussey Hill and highlights plants collected from expeditions. Reaching it involves climbing, and paths can be slippery with wet leaves, snow, or ice.
View guide →
Photo: Lauren A Siegel
7 destination

Conifer Path

Conifer Path links evergreen collections near the Arborway side of the Arboretum. It is useful for a quieter walk and winter interest, but natural surfaces and grades vary from the main paved roads.

How accessible is Peters Hill skyline view? +

Paved roads provide many routes, but natural terrain and trails vary. Reserved weekday driving permits and free manual wheelchairs support visitors with access needs.

Are dogs allowed at Peters Hill skyline view? +

Leashed dogs are welcome in the landscape under visitor guidelines; service animals are welcome in the landscape and Visitor Center. Protect collections and clean up waste.

Free family activities, labeled trees, wildlife, hills, and open exploration work well for children. Bring water and choose a shorter loop for strollers or young walkers.
