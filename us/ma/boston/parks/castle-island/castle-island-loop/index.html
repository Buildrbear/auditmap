What people say

The short paved loop circles Fort Independence with harbor, ship, and airport views. Seawalls are exposed to wind and have immediate water edges. Check water quality, lifeguard status, tide, wind, heat, thunderstorms, fort-tour hours, event security, and parking. Swimming is only safe when conditions permit.

1 destination

Fort Independence

Fort Independence occupies the center of Castle Island. The exterior loop is generally available during park hours, but interior tours are seasonal and event closures are common.
View guide →
Photo: Chris Wood
2 destination

Pleasure Bay beach and loop

Pleasure Bay lies west of the fort and supports a longer accessible loop plus seasonal swimming. Check lifeguards, water quality, beach mats, tide, and wind.
View guide →
Photo: 4300streetcar
3 destination

Sullivan's and family picnic area

Sullivan's is the seasonal food counter near the fort parking lot, playground, picnic space, and restrooms. Lines and parking peak on warm weekends.
View guide →
Photo: Robert Linsdell
4 destination

Castle Island Playground

The playground is beside the fort-side family area near parking, food, and seasonal restrooms. It is exposed to harbor wind and sun, and the nearby seawall requires close supervision.
View guide →
Photo: Chris Wood
5 destination

Fishing pier and harbor seawall

Fishing is popular along the harbor-facing pier and seawall near Fort Independence. Bring required gear and licenses, yield to walkers, watch hooks and slippery edges, and check wind and marine conditions.
View guide →
Photo: Paul Keleher
6 destination

Head Island Causeway

The causeway is the narrow connection between the Pleasure Bay loop and the fort-side island. It is paved and scenic but exposed to wind, waves, runners, bicycles, and immediate water edges.
View guide →
Photo: 4300streetcar
7 destination

Carson Beach

Carson Beach is the western beach segment of the larger DCR reservation, well beyond the fort loop. It has its own access, parking, seasonal lifeguard and beach-service conditions, so navigate directly to the beach.

Where exactly is Castle Island Loop? +

The short paved loop circles Fort Independence with harbor, ship, and airport views. Seawalls are exposed to wind and have immediate water edges.

Where should I park for Castle Island Loop? +

A free lot is near Fort Independence, with metered street parking along the beaches. Summer weekends and special events fill early and may use traffic controls.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation ↗
When is Castle Island Loop open? +

The reservation is open sunrise to sunset. Fort tours, lifeguards, restrooms, showers, concessions, and events use seasonal or separate schedules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation ↗
Are there restrooms near Castle Island Loop? +

Accessible restrooms are available at Castle Island and Carson Beach; rinsing showers and first-aid or lifeguard facilities are seasonal.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation ↗
Is Castle Island Loop free? +

General park, beach, loop, playground, and fort exterior access is free. Food, bus parking, and some organized activities cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Castle Island Loop? +

The site has accessible restrooms, an accessible Pleasure Bay trail, seasonal beach mats and beach wheelchairs, and accessible picnicking at Carson Beach.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Castle Island Loop? +

Dogs are allowed under DCR rules but are prohibited on beaches May 1 through September 15. Leash and cleanup requirements still apply elsewhere.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation ↗
Is Castle Island Loop good for children? +

The short fort loop, playground, planes and ships, beach, picnic space, and food make an easy family outing. Supervise closely at seawalls and water.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation ↗
What should I know before visiting Castle Island Loop? +

The short paved loop circles Fort Independence with harbor, ship, and airport views. Seawalls are exposed to wind and have immediate water edges. Check water quality, lifeguard status, tide, wind, heat, thunderstorms, fort-tour hours, event security, and parking. Swimming is only safe when conditions permit.

How do you reach Castle Island Loop? +

The area is transit friendly, but the final walk can be exposed. Cycling and rideshare are useful when the lot is full; confirm return service after events.
