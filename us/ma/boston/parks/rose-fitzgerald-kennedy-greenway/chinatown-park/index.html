What people say

The southern Greenway park at Chinatown provides gardens, seating, and a gateway near Chinatown and South Station. Events and maintenance can change usable space, and public restrooms are not guaranteed in the park. Check the live calendar and exact parcel. Fountains, Carousel, vendors, markets, public art, and drinkeries are seasonal; events can occupy lawns and plazas.

Armenian Heritage Park

Harbor Fog sculpture

Where should I park for Chinatown Park? +

There is no public parking on the Greenway. Use nearby garages, Atlantic Avenue meters, transit, ferries, walking, or cycling; rates and event restrictions vary.

Is Chinatown Park free? +

Park, gardens, fountains, public art, and most Conservancy events are free. Carousel rides are currently $4; food, drinks, parking, and special programs cost separately.

How accessible is Chinatown Park? +

All Greenway paths are maintained as ADA-compliant, and the Carousel uses universal design for physical, sensory, and cognitive access.

Are dogs allowed at Chinatown Park? +

Leashed dogs are allowed; clean up waste and keep pets out of garden beds. Smoking, alcohol outside permitted operations, and wheeled recreation on park paths are restricted.

Seven water attractions, the native-animal Carousel, lawns, art, food, and frequent free programs are family highlights. Fountain and event operations change daily.
