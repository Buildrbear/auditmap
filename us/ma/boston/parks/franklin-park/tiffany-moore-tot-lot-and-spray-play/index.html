What people say

Use 155 Seaver Street for the accessible playground and documented spray play. The City lists drinking water and shade but no restroom at the playground itself. White Stadium and pathway projects can alter roads, parking, and paths. Check construction, field events, zoo traffic, heat, thunderstorms, snow, ice, and woodland conditions.

Franklin Park Zoo

Franklin Park Zoo is a separately operated, ticketed attraction on the northeast side of the park. Use the zoo entrance and current Zoo New England hours, admission, parking, accessibility, and animal-care notices rather than general park rules.

5 destination

William J. Devine Golf Course

The municipal golf course occupies much of Franklin Park's eastern landscape. Tee times, fees, clubhouse hours, course conditions, and player access are separate from free general park access.

6 destination

Schoolmaster Hill

Schoolmaster Hill is a historic high point and ruin reached by interior park paths. Expect a walk from distributed parking, limited services, uneven ground, and fewer people than at the zoo or Playstead.

7 destination

Ellicott Arch and 99 Steps

Ellicott Arch and the nearby 99 Steps are historic stone landscape features in Franklin Park's interior. Routes include grades and stairs, masonry may be restricted for preservation, and this is not a staffed destination.
