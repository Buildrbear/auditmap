Current city pages

Arnold Arboretum

Boston

The landscape is open daily sunrise to sunset. The Hunnewell Visitor Center is generally open daily 10 a.m.-4 p.m.; programs and facilities can differ.

Boston Common

Boston

Boston parks generally close from 11:30 p.m. to 6 a.m. Frog Pond, the Visitor Information Center, restrooms, programs, and events have separate seasonal schedules.

Castle Island

Boston

The reservation is open sunrise to sunset. Fort tours, lifeguards, restrooms, showers, concessions, and events use seasonal or separate schedules.

Charles River Esplanade

Boston

The Charles River Reservation is officially open sunrise to sunset. Playgrounds, boating, restrooms, concessions, events, and the Hatch Shell use separate schedules.

Franklin Park

Boston

Boston parks generally close 11:30 p.m.-6 a.m. The zoo, golf course, White Stadium, playground spray features, restrooms, events, and construction zones have separate schedules.

Jamaica Pond

Boston

Boston parks generally close 11:30 p.m.-6 a.m. Boat rentals, boathouse, nature programs, restrooms, concerts, and seasonal operations have separate schedules.

Public Garden

Boston

The garden follows Boston park closure hours, generally closing 11:30 p.m.-6 a.m. Swan Boats run seasonally and weather permitting on their own daily schedule.

Rose Fitzgerald Kennedy Greenway

Boston

The Greenway is open 7 a.m.-11 p.m. Carousel, fountains, food vendors, drinkeries, markets, and events use separate seasonal schedules.
