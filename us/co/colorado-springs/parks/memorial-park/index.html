Park

Memorial Park

1605 E Pikes Peak Avenue, Colorado Springs, CO 80910
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Memorial Park is open 5:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. November 1 through April 30 and 5:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. May 1 through October 31. Facility and water-activity schedules vary.

What people say

Memorial Park is a large Colorado Springs recreation park around Prospect Lake, with a 1.25-mile paved lake trail, accessible beach access, three playgrounds, sports fields and courts, skate park, recreation center, ice center, velodrome, reservable picnic area, fitness stations, and seasonal water activities.

Where should I enter Memorial Park in Colorado Springs? +

Use 1605 East Pikes Peak Avenue for the general park. Route to 619 Prospect Lake Drive when your destination is the Beach House or accessible beach; large sports, ice, recreation, and playground areas have different closest approaches around the park.

Where should I park at Memorial Park? +

Choose the lot closest to your destination around Prospect Lake rather than circling from the main address. Accessible parking and a compliant route serve the Swing High playground, and the Beach House side provides the closest approach to the accessible beach.

What hours is Memorial Park open? +

Park hours are 5:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. from November 1-April 30 and 5:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. from May 1-October 31. Vehicle gates secure access during closed hours, and individual facilities use separate schedules.

How many playgrounds are at Memorial Park? +

The city lists three playgrounds, including the Phil Long/Denver Broncos community playground with accessible features. Select a precise playground destination before navigating because the park is large.

What accessible recreation is available at Memorial Park? +

The park includes the universally accessible Swing High playground with compliant parking and a route connected toward a bus stop, an accessible route to Prospect Lake beach, and accessible restrooms at Sertich Ice Center. Conditions at individual fields and older paths vary.

Is there a paved trail around Prospect Lake? +

Yes. A 1.25-mile paved fitness trail circles Prospect Lake and includes exercise stations. The city also lists a 0.6-mile criterium trail and a roughly 2.2-mile perimeter jogging trail with destination-specific surfaces.

Are there restrooms at Memorial Park? +

Yes. The city lists restrooms among park amenities, with additional facilities associated with the recreation center, ice center, Beach House, and other staffed destinations. Building hours and seasonal availability differ from general park hours.

Can I swim or boat in Prospect Lake any day? +

No. Swimming is at your own risk with no lifeguard and is limited to designated swimming days, listed by the city as Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday. Fishing and all motorized or non-motorized watercraft require permits, and water quality, weather, or events can suspend access.

Where can visitors find shade or weather shelter at Memorial Park? +

Three reservable pavilions are the park's main covered outdoor spaces, each seating about 50 people. The Beach House and recreation facilities provide indoor space only during their own operating or rental hours. Prospect Lake, sports fields, skatepark, and perimeter paths are exposed; during lightning or dangerous wind, leave the water and fields and use a substantial building or vehicle rather than a pavilion or tree.

Where can dogs go at Memorial Park in Colorado Springs? +

Dogs may use Memorial Park's ordinary paths and open park areas only while leashed, and owners must immediately collect and properly dispose of waste. The City does not list Memorial Park among its designated off-leash dog parks, so the lake loop, fields, playground approaches, and picnic areas are not informal off-leash zones. Keep pets out of posted restricted facilities and away from Prospect Lake water activities unless a rule expressly allows them.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can I reserve a picnic area at Memorial Park in Colorado Springs? +

Yes. The City lists a large-group picnic area at Memorial Park as reservation-only and also lists three reservable covered pavilions that seat about 50 people each. Book through the City's online pavilion portal for guaranteed group use. Unlisted pavilion spaces are first come, first served only for groups under 50; larger groups or setups such as tents, stages, or inflatables require the separate park special-event process. Seasonal park restrooms generally close for winterization from October through April.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Colorado Springs ↗
Can visitors swim at Memorial Park's Prospect Lake? +

Yes, but only in the roped swimming area in front of the Prospect Lake Beach House on designated swim days. The City's published water-play guidance lists Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday, with free access and no lifeguard, so swimming is at your own risk. Do not swim elsewhere in the lake or on motorized-use days, and check current lake status before entering because weather, lightning, events, and harmful-algae conditions can close water recreation. Panorama Park, not Memorial Park, has the City's named splash pad.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Which bus goes to Memorial Park in Colorado Springs? +

Mountain Metro Transit Routes 1 and 7 serve Memorial Park, and the City identifies a bus stop east of Swing High Playground along Prospect Lake Drive. Use the live planner for the Beach House, playground, lake, skatepark, velodrome, ice center, or sports fields because this is a large park and the nearest stop differs by destination; confirm evening and weekend return service.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Colorado Springs ↗
What are the main landmarks in Colorado Springs Memorial Park? +

Prospect Lake is the park's central landmark, with a 1.25-mile paved fitness trail and the Beach House at 619 Prospect Lake Drive for accessible beach access, programs, and gatherings. Separate destinations include three playgrounds, the skatepark, velodrome, Sertich Ice Center, sports fields, criterium, and large-group picnic area. Route to the named facility rather than a generic Memorial Park pin because the park spans several blocks around the lake.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Colorado Springs ↗
How much does Memorial Park cost? +

General park entry, paths, playgrounds, courts, skate park, and open recreation areas are free. Ice skating, recreation programs, reservations, concessions, fishing, and watercraft permits may charge.
