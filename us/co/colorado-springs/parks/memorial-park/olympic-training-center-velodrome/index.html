What people say

The Olympic Training Center Velodrome is a 333.3-meter banked concrete cycling track on Memorial Park's east side with scheduled public programming and races. Do not enter the track because the parent park is open. Track cycling requires controlled sessions, suitable equipment, and operator permission; verify the public calendar and entry instructions before making it your destination.

How accessible is Olympic Training Center Velodrome? +

The park includes the universally accessible Swing High playground with compliant parking and a route connected toward a bus stop, an accessible route to Prospect Lake beach, and accessible restrooms at Sertich Ice Center. Conditions at individual fields and older paths vary.

Are dogs allowed at Olympic Training Center Velodrome? +

Dogs may use Memorial Park's ordinary paths and open park areas only while leashed, and owners must immediately collect and properly dispose of waste. The City does not list Memorial Park among its designated off-leash dog parks, so the lake loop, fields, playground approaches, and picnic areas are not informal off-leash zones. Keep pets out of posted restricted facilities and away from Prospect Lake water activities unless a rule expressly allows them. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.
