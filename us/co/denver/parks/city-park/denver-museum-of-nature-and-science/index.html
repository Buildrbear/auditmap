What people say

Denver Museum of Nature and Science is a separately operated or reservable destination within City Park; its access, admission, restrooms, and hours may differ from the surrounding park. Ferril Lake is not a swimming area. Summer storms, heat, large events, and attraction traffic can change parking and path access quickly.
