destination

Commons Park wetlands

2101 15th Street, Denver, CO 80202
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Denver urban park hours are 5:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; weather and temporary closures can still affect this destination.
Navigate

What people say

The river-edge wetlands manage stormwater and provide habitat; they are observation areas rather than swimming or off-trail play space. The restroom is seasonal and closes well before the park. River edges, bikes, scooters, heat, and limited shade require attention.
