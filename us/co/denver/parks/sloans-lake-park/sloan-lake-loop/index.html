What people say

Sloan Lake loop is a mapped water-edge destination within Sloan's Lake Park. It is unsupervised natural water unless officially posted otherwise; conditions, permits, ice, flow, and water quality can change. Boating and fishing follow separate rules and permits. The lake can be windy, icy, or affected by water-quality notices, and the loop has little shelter.
