Riverfront Park

Little Rock

Use 202 La Harpe Boulevard, Little Rock, AR 72201 for a general arrival. For Peabody Playground and its splash pad, route near Ottenheimer Plaza and Junction Bridge; the toddler area is west of the Marriott.

Two Rivers Park

Little Rock

Use 6900 Two Rivers Park Road, Little Rock, AR 72223 for the park-side lots, playground, pavilion, trails, and bridge approach. A separate River Mountain trailhead on the east side provides access from the Arkansas River Trail network.
