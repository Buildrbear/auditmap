Finlay Park

Columbia

Finlay Park is open daily from 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. The Landing café and rental hub is normally open 11 a.m.–7 p.m., and the splash pad uses separate seasonal hours.

Riverfront Park

Columbia

The City lists Riverfront Park as open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Construction, flooding, maintenance, and special events can restrict individual sections.
