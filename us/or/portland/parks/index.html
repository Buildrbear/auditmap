Park directory

Current city pages

Cathedral Park

Portland

The park is open 5:00 a.m.-midnight. Boat-ramp parking, docks, events, construction, and off-leash use have separate posted conditions; no overnight parking or moorage is allowed.

Forest Park

Portland

The natural area is open 5:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Trail hazards, storms, landslides, fallen trees, fire conditions, and maintenance can close individual access points at any time.

Laurelhurst Park

Portland

The park is open 5:00 a.m.-10:30 p.m. Off-leash hours change by season, weekday, and weekend; reservations and volunteer events use separate schedules.

Mt. Tabor Park

Portland

The park is open 5:00 a.m.-11:59 p.m.; vehicle access is 5:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Roads close to motor vehicles all day Wednesday and from 10:00 p.m.-5:00 a.m. otherwise.

Peninsula Park

Portland

The park is open 5:00 a.m.-midnight; tennis lights turn off at 10:00 p.m. The community center, outdoor pool, splash pad, rose-garden services, and reservations keep seasonal schedules.

Powell Butte Nature Park

Portland

The park is open 5:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Main road and lot hours are seasonal: generally 7:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. in winter, 8:00 p.m. in spring and fall, and 10:00 p.m. in summer.

Tom McCall Waterfront Park

Portland

The park is open 5:00 a.m.-midnight. Interactive fountains, docks, restrooms, markets, festivals, construction zones, and temporary event enclosures use separate hours.

Washington Park

Portland

The outdoor park is open 5:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. and closes to vehicles at 10:00 p.m.; every garden, zoo, museum, visitor center, shuttle, and concession keeps its own schedule.
