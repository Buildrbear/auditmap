Japanese Arts Learning Center

Arlington Heights, Portland, OR
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Washington Park is open daily from 5:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.; vehicles must leave by 10:00 p.m. The zoo, gardens, arboretum, museums, visitor services, shuttle, playground, restrooms, and events use separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

Jordan Schnitzer Japanese Arts Learning Center is the educational and cultural hub inside Portland Japanese Garden's Cultural Village, with a gallery, library, classroom, and performance space. The Learning Center is inside the ticketed Portland Japanese Garden Cultural Village, reached from the 611 SW Kingston Avenue Welcome Center and Garden shuttle. This is not a separate free attraction: regular Garden admission and Garden hours apply unless an event states otherwise. Exhibitions, talks, and demonstrations follow their own schedules and may need reservations. The Cultural Village and Learning Center are ADA accessible via the free Garden shuttle, but other garden areas may not be. Check the event calendar before traveling for a specific program.

Use the South Entrance and Washington Park MAX station for the zoo, World Forestry Center, and free shuttle; use 400 SW Kingston Avenue for the Rose Garden side. Route directly to Hoyt Arboretum, Japanese Garden, memorial, playground, or trailhead when that is the goal.

How much is parking at Japanese Arts Learning Center? +

Paid parking is enforced daily from 9:30 a.m.-8 p.m. at $2.40 per hour, up to $9.60 per day. It is pay-by-plate and spaces fill quickly on sunny weekends. Disabled placards generally still require payment unless they meet specific Oregon exemptions.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Can I visit Japanese Arts Learning Center without driving? +

Yes. MAX Red and Blue lines serve Washington Park station about every 15 minutes. The free ADA-accessible shuttle loops among all major attractions, typically every 15-30 minutes depending on season.

How much does Japanese Arts Learning Center cost? +

General park, accessible playground, trails, memorials, public landscapes, and free shuttle are free. Parking is $2.40 per hour up to $9.60 daily; zoo, Japanese Garden, attractions, food, and events charge separately.
