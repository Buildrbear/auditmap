destination

Waterfront Park Bowl

Naito Parkway between S Harrison Street and NW Glisan Street, Portland, OR 97204
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The park is open 5:00 a.m.-midnight. Interactive fountains, docks, restrooms, markets, festivals, construction zones, and temporary event enclosures use separate hours.
Navigate

What people say

Waterfront Park Bowl is the broad southern lawn between Hawthorne Bridge and RiverPlace, used for recreation and major festivals that can fence or close the area. Event calendars matter here more than at most parks. Waterfront Blues Festival, Rose Festival, races, demonstrations, Saturday Market, construction, and fountain maintenance can transform access from one day to the next.

Where should I park for Waterfront Park Bowl? +

Use downtown garages, metered streets, transit, biking, walking, or rideshare. Major festivals and races close streets and remove ordinary curb access; do not expect park-side parking.

Are there restrooms near Waterfront Park Bowl? +

An accessible restroom is listed, and temporary facilities appear during major events. Availability is not continuous along the entire waterfront, so identify the nearest open facility before walking far.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Portland Parks & Recreation ↗
Is Waterfront Park Bowl free? +

Ordinary park access, paths, lawns, memorials, and fountains are free. Parking, food, market purchases, cruises, rentals, and ticketed or fenced events cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Portland Parks & Recreation ↗
How accessible is Waterfront Park Bowl? +

The riverfront has long paved, generally level routes, accessible transit, and an accessible restroom, though bridge approaches, crowds, event fencing, and construction can change circulation.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Waterfront Park Bowl? +

Dogs must remain leashed throughout the park. Keep pets away from interactive fountain jets, food areas, crowded events, docks, and posted restricted spaces.

Is Waterfront Park Bowl good for children? +

Fountains, river views, boats, bridges, markets, lawns, and festivals can make an easy downtown family outing. Supervise closely beside water, bikes, traffic crossings, and crowded events.
