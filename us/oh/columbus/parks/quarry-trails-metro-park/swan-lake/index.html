Where should I park for Swan Lake? +

Use 2158 Old Dublin Road for Millikin Falls, 2600 Dublin Road for the north area and mountain biking, or 2319 Quarry Trails Drive for the lake area.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

When is Swan Lake open? +

Open year-round from 6:30 a.m. until dark. Programs, climbing access and construction areas may use separate schedules.

Are there restrooms near Swan Lake? +

Restrooms and portable facilities are available in developed areas, but coverage varies while the park continues to develop.

Is Swan Lake free? +

Park, parking, trails, waterfall, dog park and ordinary lake access are free. Via ferrata requires a guided program reservation; climbers provide required gear for sport routes.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Columbus and Franklin County Metro Parks ↗
How accessible is Swan Lake? +

The paved North-South Connector includes an ADA boardwalk from Lake Area parking. Waterfall steps, bike routes and climbing areas are not universally accessible.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Columbus and Franklin County Metro Parks ↗
Are dogs allowed at Swan Lake? +

Leashed pets are welcome on designated paths, and a two-acre fenced dog park separates small and large dogs; running water is not guaranteed there.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Columbus and Franklin County Metro Parks ↗
Is Swan Lake good for children? +

The waterfall overlook, lakes and paved paths work for general visits. Climbing and mountain-bike areas require age, skill, gear and supervision appropriate to the activity.
