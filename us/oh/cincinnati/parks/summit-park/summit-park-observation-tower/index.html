Summit Park Observation Tower is a distinct mapped destination within Summit Park; navigate to this point instead of the general park pin and check posted conditions on arrival. Check the tower, rink and event schedules independently. Park rules prohibit grills and open flames, and only designated restaurant-purchased DORA drinks may leave restaurant areas.
