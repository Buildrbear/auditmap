What people say

Wendy Park's sand volleyball courts are the active recreation area beside the main Whiskey Island lawn, with league play most evenings and first-come public use when no event is scheduled. Do not assume an empty-looking court is available later in the evening. Check the Wendy Park event schedule, expect league use Sunday through Thursday after 5:00 p.m. and choose Edgewater or another park when playground equipment is the priority.

Where exactly is Wendy Park Volleyball Courts? +

Navigate to the exact courts pin inside the main Wendy Park area. They are west of Wendy Park Bridge and near the main Whiskey Island arrival, not at Edgewater Beach's volleyball area.

Where should I park for Wendy Park Volleyball Courts? +

Use the Wendy Park lot from Whiskey Island Drive. League nights and events can fill the closest spaces, so arrive before scheduled play and keep boat, marina and emergency access clear.

How accessible is Wendy Park Volleyball Courts? +

Paved park routes reach the court area, but the playing surface is deep sand. Spectators may have a firmer approach than players; confirm event setup when an exact accessible viewing location is needed.

Are dogs allowed at Wendy Park Volleyball Courts? +

Keep leashed dogs out of active courts and away from league or event operations. Use ordinary park areas instead and maintain control around crowds and balls.

Is Wendy Park Volleyball Courts good for children? +

The courts can work for older children and family play when open, but there is no guarantee of an available court and this is not a playground. Bring water, sun protection and footwear for hot sand.
