Where should I park for Beaver Marsh? +

Parking is free but limited at popular sites. Start at Boston Mill Visitor Center; its closest spaces are limited-mobility parking, while general visitors use Boston Trailhead or Boston Mill lot.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
When is Beaver Marsh open? +

The park is open every day; most outdoor areas are accessible 24 hours, but Brandywine Falls, Ledges, Octagon, Kendall Lake and several developed areas close at dusk. Buildings keep separate hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Are there restrooms near Beaver Marsh? +

Restrooms are available at the visitor center and selected developed trailheads, but not continuously along trails. Seasonal and construction closures apply.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Is Beaver Marsh free? +

The national park charges no entrance fee and requires no entry reservation. Scenic railroad, rentals, programs and neighboring attractions may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
How accessible is Beaver Marsh? +

Accessible experiences include portions of the Towpath and Beaver Marsh, Canal Exploration Center, Everett Covered Bridge and Brandywine's upper boardwalk.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Are dogs allowed at Beaver Marsh? +

Leashed pets are allowed on many trails but not in buildings or on the scenic railroad; check route-specific restrictions and clean up waste.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
National Park Service ↗
Is Beaver Marsh good for children? +

Brandywine's upper boardwalk, Beaver Marsh, Boston Mill and short Towpath segments work well for families. Keep children away from cliff and waterfall edges.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

How do you reach Beaver Marsh? +

The park is spread across a large valley. Seasonal railroad and limited regional options exist, but most visitors drive or bicycle between selected stops.
