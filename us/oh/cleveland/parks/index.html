Current city pages

Brecksville Reservation

Cleveland

Reservation hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Brecksville Nature Center is generally open daily 9:30 a.m.-5:00 p.m. except listed holidays.

Cleveland Lakefront Nature Preserve

Cleveland

Open to the public during daylight hours only.

Cuyahoga Valley National Park

Cleveland

The park is open every day; most outdoor areas are accessible 24 hours, but Brandywine Falls, Ledges, Octagon, Kendall Lake and several developed areas close at dusk. Buildings keep separate hours.

Edgewater Park

Cleveland

Lakefront Reservation is generally open 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; Edgewater Beach closes at dusk and swimming, concessions and facilities have seasonal hours.

Public Square

Cleveland

Park hours are 5:00 a.m.-midnight. The splash fountain normally operates daily 6:00 a.m.-midnight April through October; cafe, kiosk, monument and rink keep separate schedules.

Rockefeller Park and Cultural Gardens

Cleveland

The Cultural Gardens are open year-round 6:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m.; the park closes after sunset and the Greenhouse has separate daytime hours.

Rocky River Reservation

Cleveland

Reservation hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Rocky River Nature Center is generally open daily 9:30 a.m.-5:00 p.m. except listed holidays.

Wendy Park

Cleveland

Lakefront Reservation is generally open 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; use trails, water access and wildlife areas in safe daylight conditions.
