What people say

Dike 14 Peninsula Point is a distinct mapped destination within Cleveland Lakefront Nature Preserve; navigate to this point instead of the general park pin and check posted conditions on arrival. Bring water, sun and wind protection, binoculars and insect repellent. Stay on marked trails and do not disturb wildlife, nests or habitat.
