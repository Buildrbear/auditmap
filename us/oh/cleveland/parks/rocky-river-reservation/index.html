Rocky River Reservation

24000 Valley Parkway, North Olmsted, OH 44070, Cleveland
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Reservation hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Rocky River Nature Center is generally open daily 9:30 a.m.-5:00 p.m. except listed holidays.

What people say

A long Rocky River gorge reservation with nature center, shale cliffs, waterfalls, marina, fishing, picnic areas, golf, stairs and an all-purpose trail through multiple suburbs.

Rocky River Nature Center

Rocky River Nature Center is the staffed visitor center above the Rocky River, with indoor geology and wildlife exhibits, a river-view deck, HideAway Hollow children's play, restrooms and direct access to more than five miles of trails and Fort Hill Stairs.

Berea Falls Scenic Overlook

Berea Falls Scenic Overlook is the named roadside and trail viewpoint above the East Branch Rocky River waterfall, with a small lot that also stages cyclists for the reservation's all-purpose trail.

When is Rocky River Reservation open? +

Reservation hours are 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Rocky River Nature Center is generally open daily 9:30 a.m.-5:00 p.m. except listed holidays.

Where should I park for Rocky River Reservation? +

Numerous free lots serve separate trailheads and facilities. Route to the exact destination rather than a generic reservation pin.

What is the best entrance for Rocky River Reservation? +

Valley Parkway follows the long valley, but Berea Falls, Nature Center, Fort Hill, marina and Stinchcomb-Groth are miles apart.

Are there restrooms at Rocky River Reservation? +

Restrooms are available at the Nature Center, marina and selected picnic and golf facilities; trailhead availability varies seasonally.

Is Rocky River Reservation free? +

General park, trails, nature center and picnic areas are free. Golf, marina services, rentals and reservations cost separately.

How accessible is Rocky River Reservation? +

Developed facilities and portions of the all-purpose trail are accessible. Fort Hill has many stairs and natural trails can be steep, muddy or flooded.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Rocky River Reservation? +

Leashed dogs are welcome in ordinary park areas; keep them out of buildings, golf play and posted habitat zones.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

Is Rocky River Reservation good for children? +

Nature Center exhibits and HideAway Hollow, Berea Falls and gentle all-purpose trail sections are strong family choices.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

How do I reach Rocky River Reservation without a car? +

The reservation spans several communities and is difficult to cover by transit; driving or cycling to a chosen destination is most practical.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

What should I know before visiting Rocky River Reservation? +

Check river level and trail alerts after rain. The Rocky River is a major steelhead stream, but currents, cold water and slippery shale demand caution.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026
Cleveland Metroparks ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Rocky River Reservation? +

Check current Cleveland-area weather and operator alerts. Lake Erie wind and waves, heat, snow, ice and storms can close water access, paths or facilities independently.
