Delaney Park Strip

Anchorage

The park runs for roughly 13 downtown blocks between 9th and 10th avenues, from A Street on the east to P Street on the west. Navigate to the cross street nearest your field, court, rink, memorial, or event area.

Far North Bicentennial Park

Anchorage

Choose a named trailhead rather than navigating to the park center. The Service and Abbott Road area at 5577 Abbott Road serves the newer Hillside singletrack system; other entrances serve Campbell Creek, BLM Campbell Tract, ski, mushing, and multi-use routes.

Kincaid Park

Anchorage

Use the Raspberry Road entrance and Kincaid Chalet at the west end of Raspberry Road for the main trail, stadium, and visitor hub. Other trail connections exist, so choose the trailhead shown on the official map.
