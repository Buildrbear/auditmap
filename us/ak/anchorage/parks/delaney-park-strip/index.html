What people say

Delaney Park Strip is Anchorage's long downtown civic green between 9th and 10th avenues, extending from A Street to P Street. Its open lawns, paths, memorials, sports fields, tennis and volleyball courts, seasonal outdoor rink, play and gathering areas support casual recreation, civic events, festivals, and downtown connections.

Where is Delaney Park Strip? +

The park runs for roughly 13 downtown blocks between 9th and 10th avenues, from A Street on the east to P Street on the west. Navigate to the cross street nearest your field, court, rink, memorial, or event area.

Where should I park for Delaney Park Strip? +

Use legal downtown street parking or a nearby public lot closest to the block you plan to visit. The park is linear, and festivals, sports, ceremonies, or winter operations can restrict adjacent spaces.

The park contains a collection of civic monuments and memorials distributed along its downtown blocks. Use on-site markers to understand each site and do not treat one general park pin as the location of every memorial.

Yes. General walking, lawn, memorial, and ordinary recreation access are free. Reserved fields, leagues, permitted gatherings, commercial activity, and special events may have fees or controlled access.

Can I bring a dog to Delaney Park Strip? +

Dogs may use the park under Anchorage control and cleanup rules, but Delaney is not one of the municipality's designated off-leash areas. Keep the dog leashed, avoid active fields and rink surfaces, and follow any temporary event restriction.

Is Delaney Park Strip accessible? +

The park's mostly level downtown blocks are bordered and crossed by paved sidewalks, making many lawns, memorials, courts, and gathering areas approachable without stairs. Snow, ice, turf, temporary fencing, and cross-street conditions can change the best route.

Is there shade or weather shelter at Delaney Park Strip? +

Delaney Park Strip is intentionally a long, open, grassy downtown park. Scattered trees provide limited seasonal shade, but sports fields, courts, lawns, memorials, and event areas are broadly exposed, and the Municipality does not identify a dependable public indoor park building or general storm shelter there. Temporary tents may appear for permitted events but should not be assumed. During lightning, high wind, wildfire smoke, extreme cold, or heavy snow, use a confirmed-open nearby substantial building or enclosed vehicle.

Is there a splash pad or swimming at Delaney Park Strip? +

No splash pad, pool, or designated swimming area is listed at Delaney Park Strip. The long downtown green is used for sports fields, courts, memorials, events, walking, winter skating, and open recreation rather than water play. Do not confuse references to Anchorage municipal pools with facilities inside this park. Choose a separately listed aquatic center and confirm its current public-swim schedule before going.

Which People Mover bus goes to Delaney Park Strip? +

Many downtown People Mover routes pass the Park Strip or stop within a short walk, including routes 10, 11, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 65, and 85. Because the park spans 11 blocks between Ninth and Tenth avenues, enter the exact cross street, field, memorial, or event area in the live planner rather than using only the park name.
