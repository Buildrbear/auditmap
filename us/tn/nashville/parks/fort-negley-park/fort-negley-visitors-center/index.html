destination

Fort Negley Visitors Center

1100 Fort Negley Boulevard, Nashville, TN 37203
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Park open dawn to dusk. Visitor Center: Tuesday-Friday noon-4:00 p.m. and Saturday 9:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m.; closed Sunday and Monday.
Navigate

What people say

The free Visitor Center provides exhibits, films, maps, restrooms, and current construction guidance during shorter Tuesday-through-Saturday hours. Phase-one construction is active in 2026. The half-mile trail and Visitor Center remain open, but access to the historic hilltop fort is closed until the announced reopening; check current notices before visiting.

Are dogs allowed at Fort Negley Visitors Center? +

Leashed dogs are welcome on open outdoor paths. Keep pets out of the Visitor Center, construction zones, fragile stonework, and posted restricted areas.
