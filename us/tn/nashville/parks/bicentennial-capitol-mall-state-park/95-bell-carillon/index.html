What people say

The 95-bell carillon represents Tennessee's counties and sounds on a programmed schedule near the Capitol-facing end of the park. The park is highly exposed to heat, storms, and event changes. Confirm fountain operation, tours, carillon programs, Farmers Market hours, road closures, and current state-park notices.

General park monuments, map, history walk, fountains when operating, lawns, and ordinary programs are free. Food, parking, private events, and selected programs cost.
