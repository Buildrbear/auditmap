What people say

The designated mountain-bike trail uses one-way natural-surface riding rules and closes when wet enough for tires to damage the route. Download maps before entering because service can be limited. Are there restrooms near Warner Mountain Bike Trail? +

Restrooms cluster near the Nature Center and selected developed recreation or picnic areas, not throughout primitive trail loops.

Is Warner Mountain Bike Trail free? +

General park, trails, Nature Center, dog park, and ordinary picnics are free. Golf, equestrian services, reservations, permits, and some events cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

How accessible is Warner Mountain Bike Trail? +

Nature Center grounds and paved multi-use routes offer accessible choices; primitive trails are rooted, hilly, muddy, and sometimes crossed by drainage.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Metro Nashville Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Warner Mountain Bike Trail? +

Leash dogs in ordinary areas and use the fenced dog park for off-leash activity. Pets are prohibited in Burch Reserve and on posted horse-and-pedestrian routes.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Metro Nashville Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Warner Mountain Bike Trail good for children? +

Nature Play, Little Acorn, Hungry Hawk, the Nature Center, paved routes, picnics, and wildlife observation are strong family options; there is no managed swim or splash area.
