Current city pages

Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park

Nashville

Outdoor park access is generally available daily during posted state-park hours; fountains, carillon programming, tours, market vendors, events, and restrooms use separate schedules.

Centennial Park

Nashville

Open daily from dawn to 11:00 p.m. The Parthenon, Sportsplex, Arts Center, dog park, concessions, programs, and events use separate schedules.

Fort Negley Park

Nashville

Park open dawn to dusk. Visitor Center: Tuesday-Friday noon-4:00 p.m. and Saturday 9:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m.; closed Sunday and Monday.

Radnor Lake State Natural Area

Nashville

Natural-area gates and trails follow posted daylight-based hours. The visitor center, aviary, ranger programs, and road access use shorter schedules and can close independently.

Shelby Bottoms and Shelby Park

Nashville

Shelby Bottoms Greenway is open dawn to dusk daily; Shelby Park is open dawn to 11:00 p.m. The Nature Center has shorter weekday and Saturday hours and is closed Sunday and Monday.

Two Rivers Park

Nashville

Open daily from dawn to 11:00 p.m. Wave Country, golf, mansion tours, dog park, facilities, rentals, and events use separate or seasonal schedules.

Warner Parks

Nashville

Parks are open daily from dawn to 11:00 p.m. The Nature Center is open Tuesday-Saturday 9:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m. and closed Sunday and Monday; other facilities differ.

Wasioto Park

Nashville

Open daily from dawn to 11:00 p.m. Spray-ground operation is seasonal and maintenance dependent; events, elevator access, and reservable spaces can differ.
