Overton Park

Memphis

The public park is open daily from dawn to dusk. Memphis Zoo, Brooks Museum, the golf clubhouse, Levitt Shell, and other institutions have separate admission hours and event schedules.

Shelby Farms Park

Memphis

The park and Greenline are open sunrise to sunset every day of the year. Woodland Discovery Playground opens at 9 a.m. and closes one hour before sunset; rentals, restaurants, shops, and activities use separate schedules.

Tom Lee Park

Memphis

Tom Lee Park is open daily from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Sunset Canopy courts and Hanover Welcome Center are generally open 8 a.m.–8 p.m.; café and vendor hours are shorter and vary by day.
