How accessible is Shelby Farms Greenline? +

The park has more than 40 miles of paved and unpaved routes. The 10.65-mile paved Greenline is the main regional walking and cycling route, while natural trails provide hiking and mountain-bike experiences with roots, mud, and changing terrain.

The Outback is a 120-acre off-leash area with meadows, woods, trails, and lakes. Dogs must be leashed elsewhere.
