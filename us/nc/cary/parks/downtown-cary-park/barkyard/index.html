Barkyard

Downtown Cary Park, NC
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Barkyard is normally accessible 7 a.m.-9 p.m. It closes for scheduled Wednesday maintenance and may close for weather, turf, or water-feature work.
Navigate

What people say

A pass-controlled off-leash dog park with separate small- and large-dog areas, tunnels, interactive water, turf, logs, mounds, hills, and shade. Barkyard fills the northeast corner of Downtown Cary Park along Walker Street, immediately beside The Bark Bar. A Cary dog-park membership or day pass is required. Bring proof of the pass, keep the leash on until inside the enclosure, and check maintenance closures before driving downtown.

Where should I park for Barkyard? +

Use downtown public parking and enter from Walker Street or Park Street. There is no dedicated dog-park lot inside the seven-acre park.

The closest public restrooms are in Academy Pavilion or Guest Services near The Nest. Plan the restroom stop before entering the gated dog area.

What accessibility details should I know about Barkyard? +

The entrance is on an accessible park route, but play terrain includes slopes, logs, turf, tunnels, and wet areas. Separate enclosures reduce size conflicts.

How much does Barkyard cost? +

Free general access; rides, food, programs, rentals, passes, or ticketed events may cost extra.

How do you reach Barkyard? +

GoCary buses are fare-free and connect at Cary Depot, less than half a mile from the park. The Downtown Loop is suspended until further notice, but other fixed routes continue serving most downtown stops.
