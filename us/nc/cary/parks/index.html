Black Creek Greenway

Cary

Choose a trailhead for the section you plan to use. Fred G. Bond Metro Park serves the southern hub; Black Creek and Godbold Park trailheads serve the middle; Old Reedy Creek Road normally serves the north but its connecting Phase 3 segment is currently closed.

Downtown Cary Park

Downtown Cary

Downtown Cary has more than 2,000 free public parking spaces. Closest official options include the Academy Street Parking Deck at 206 S. Academy Street, the accessible deck behind Cary Regional Library, the Charlie Gaddy Lane lot, labeled street parking, and Town Hall Deck at 121 Wilkinson Avenue. A passenger and rideshare drop-off is on S. Walker Street near The Walker building.

Fred G. Bond Metro Park

Cary

801 High House Road, Cary, NC 27513 is the main park address. Use 197 Bond Park Drive for the boathouse and waterfront; choose the exact entrance for the community center, senior center, fields, shelters, or challenge course.

Hemlock Bluffs Nature Preserve

Cary

2616 Kildaire Farm Road, Cary, NC 27518. Use the Stevens Nature Center entrance for exhibits, restrooms, trail information, and the primary trail network.

Jack Smith Park

Cary

9725 Penny Road, Cary, NC 27518, near Holly Springs Road. Follow internal signs for the playground and sprayground, dog park, climbing boulder, shelter, restrooms, or greenway.
