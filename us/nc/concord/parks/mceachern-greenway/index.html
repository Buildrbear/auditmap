What people say

McEachern Greenway is a 2.2-mile paved central-Concord route following Three-Mile Branch between Les Myers Park and the area north of Wilson Park, with access at McGee and Wilson Street parks, a Downtown Connector, bridges, underpasses, and LifeTrail fitness stations.

What address or entrance should I use for McEachern Greenway? +

For a practical southern trailhead use Les Myers Park, 338 Lawndale Avenue; J.W. Mickey McGee Park and Wilson Street Park provide additional official access. The 2.2-mile route follows Three-Mile Branch through central Concord.

Where should I park for McEachern Greenway? +

Official trail parking is available at Les Myers Park, J.W. Mickey McGee Park, and Wilson Street Park. Choose a trailhead for your planned segment instead of assuming the full route returns to your vehicle.

When is McEachern Greenway open? +

Are there restrooms at McEachern Greenway? +

Restrooms are available at Les Myers and McGee parks during operating conditions. Wilson Street Park has had restroom and trailhead construction, so verify current availability before relying on that stop.

What accessibility information should I know about McEachern Greenway? +

The developed route is intended for shared walking, rolling, and cycling access, but grades, bridges, underpasses, road crossings, flooding, construction, and park connections vary by segment.

What are the paths or trails like at McEachern Greenway? +

The route is about 2.2 miles: roughly one mile from McGee Park to Les Myers Park and 1.2 miles north from McGee to the turnaround beyond Wilson Street. The Downtown Connector and sidewalks create an approximately four-mile loop.

What can families do at McEachern Greenway? +

A short out-and-back from Les Myers or McGee Park pairs trail time with playgrounds, restrooms, and recreation. Supervise children at road crossings, underpasses, bridges, water, and faster bicycle traffic.

Can I bring a dog to McEachern Greenway? +

Leashed dogs may use the greenway where posted rules permit. Keep leashes short around cyclists and crossings, remove waste, and follow separate rules inside connected parks.

Can I picnic at McEachern Greenway? +

Use designated picnic facilities at connected parks rather than stopping across the shared trail. Reserve shelters for guaranteed group use.

What landmarks or areas should I look for at McEachern Greenway? +

Key connections include Les Myers Park, J.W. Mickey McGee Park, Wilson Street Park, Three-Mile Branch, the Downtown Connector, LifeTrail fitness stations, Corban Avenue and Cabarrus Avenue underpasses, and the Downtown Greenway Loop.

What closures or conditions can affect McEachern Greenway? +

Flooding, high water, ice, storms, bridge or underpass work, Wilson Street construction, utility projects, and park closures can interrupt sections. Check current City notices after severe weather.
