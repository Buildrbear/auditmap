Brown Mill Mountain Bike Trail Park

Concord

Use the official trail parking on 2nd Street SW in Concord. Confirm the live open or closed status before loading bikes because trail conditions can change after rain, snow, ice, or maintenance.

James L. Dorton Park

Concord

5790 Poplar Tent Road, Concord, NC. Use the main park drive and follow internal signs for playgrounds, shelters, soccer, tennis and pickleball, disc golf, restrooms, or the walking trail.

Lake Fisher

Concord

5099 Lake Fisher Road, Kannapolis, NC. This is a City of Concord water-supply lake with staff-operated jon-boat rentals, not a general swimming, shoreline-fishing, kayak, or private-boat access site.

Les Myers Park

Concord

338 Lawndale Avenue, Concord, NC. Follow internal signs for the playgrounds, courts, ballfield, amphitheater, community building, shelters and gazebos, restrooms, or McEachern Greenway parking.

McEachern Greenway

Concord

For a practical southern trailhead use Les Myers Park, 338 Lawndale Avenue; J.W. Mickey McGee Park and Wilson Street Park provide additional official access. The 2.2-mile route follows Three-Mile Branch through central Concord.

Concord
