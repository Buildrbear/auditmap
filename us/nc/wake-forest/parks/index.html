Ailey Young Park

Wake Forest

Ailey Young Park is mapped at 800 East Juniper Avenue, Wake Forest, NC 27587 in Wake Forest, North Carolina. Use the map pin for the park itself rather than a similarly named municipal office or neighborhood.

E. Carroll Joyner Park

Wake Forest

701 Harris Road, Wake Forest, NC 27587. Follow park signs for the restored farm buildings, gardens, amphitheater, community center, trails, fishing area, and event parking.

Holding Park

Wake Forest

133 W Owen Avenue, Wake Forest, NC 27587. The compact five-acre campus includes the inclusive playground, Holding Park Aquatic Center, Wake Forest Community House, basketball court, picnic area, rain garden, and historic pool structures.

J.B. Flaherty Park

Wake Forest

1100 N White Street, Wake Forest, NC 27587. The community center uses 1226 N White Street; follow internal signs for fields, playground, ponds, courts, fitness court, dog park, shelter, trail, and restrooms.

Smith Creek Greenway

Wake Forest

The southern 1.15-mile segment extends from Burlington Mills Road to the Neuse River Greenway. Official parking is reached through the Neuse network at WRAL Soccer Park, 7700 Perry Creek Road; Horseshoe Nature Preserve, 2900 Horse Shoe Farm Road; or Thornton Road access, 6100 Thornton Road.
