Cornelius Road Park

Mooresville

247 Cornelius Road, Mooresville, NC 28117. Use the signed park entrance and then follow internal wayfinding for the turf fields, baseball fields, playground, dog park, courts, disc golf, shelters, or concessions.

Liberty Park

Mooresville

217 E Iredell Avenue, Mooresville, NC 28115. The park is immediately east of downtown; use the signed public entrance and parking serving the playground, splash pad, amphitheater, shelter, basketball court, and greenway connection.

Mazeppa Park

Mooresville

645 Mazeppa Road, Mooresville, NC 28115. This large sports and trail campus is north of downtown near the Motorplex; follow internal signs for the correct field, shelter, playground, or mountain-bike access.
