Avon and Catawba Creek Greenway

Gastonia

Use Lineberger Park at 632 East Garrison Boulevard for a developed central trailhead with parking and restrooms. The connected corridor runs along Avon and Catawba creeks toward Ferguson Park and the Robinwood Road area.

Lineberger Park

Gastonia

632 East Garrison Boulevard, Gastonia, NC 28054. Use Chestnut Street for the eastern playground, Vietnam War Memorial, shelters, restrooms, and greenway trailhead; use Avon Street for the pool, splash pad, basketball, shelters, and closed miniature-train area.

Martha Rivers Park

Gastonia

1515 Neal Hawkins Road, Gastonia, NC 28056. Follow internal signs for the one-acre castle playground, soccer, baseball and softball complexes, walking loops, shelters, volleyball, concessions, and restrooms.

Rankin Lake Park

Gastonia

1750 Rankin Lake Road, Gastonia, NC 28052. Use the main park drive for the clubhouse, picnic shelters, lake office, fishing piers, boat dock, playground, disc golf, StoryWalk, paved lake loop, and Highland Rail Trail connector.
