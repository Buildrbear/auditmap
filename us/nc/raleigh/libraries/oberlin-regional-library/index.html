Oberlin Regional Library is a large Wake County branch in the Village District with adult, teen, children's, picture-book, Spanish-language, and large-print collections, plus study space and public programs.

Where should I park for Oberlin Regional Library? +

Use the customer parking at Oberlin Regional Library, 1930 Clark Avenue. Published location data does not state the lot capacity or guarantee space availability, so allow extra arrival time during programs and other busy periods.

Which entrance should I use for Oberlin Regional Library? +

Navigate to Oberlin Regional Library at 1930 Clark Avenue and follow the branch signs from the customer parking area. Wake County does not publish a door-by-door entrance description, so call 919-856-6710 if you need the shortest accessible route or arrival assistance.

What accessibility details should visitors know at Oberlin Regional Library? +

Accessible parking is beyond the library building on the left, according to Wake County's official polling-place directions. The library's official page also links a KultureCity Social Story for visitors who benefit from sensory preparation. Door operation, interior routes, and restroom features are not documented in detail, so call 919-856-6710 for a specific accommodation.

Is Oberlin Regional Library open today? +

The regular schedule is Monday-Thursday 9 a.m.-9 p.m., Friday-Saturday 9 a.m.-6 p.m., and Sunday 1-5 p.m. The official branch record does not currently show a branch-specific closure. Holiday, weather, or maintenance closures can supersede normal hours, so check the Wake County branch page or call 919-856-6710 before a time-sensitive trip.

How can I reach Oberlin Regional Library by bus? +

GoRaleigh Route 16 serves the Oberlin and Village District corridor near the library and operates daily. Use GoRaleigh's current trip planner or live map for the nearest active stop to 1930 Clark Avenue because stop assignments can change. Route 16 connects the area with downtown and Crabtree Valley Mall.

Can I reserve a meeting room at Oberlin Regional Library? +

Yes. The library has three meeting spaces: one large room for adults age 18 and older with a maximum capacity of 45, plus two small rooms with a maximum capacity of six each. Reserve the large room through Wake County's LibCal system; call 919-856-6703 to reserve a small room.
