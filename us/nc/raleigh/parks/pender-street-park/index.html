Pender Street Park

What people say

A 0.09-acre memorial garden created through resident John Stokes's decades of care for a formerly overgrown lot, renovated in 2022 with a short walkway, seating, picnic space, public art, trees, shrubs, grasses, and perennials in Raleigh's College Park neighborhood.

Where should I park for Pender Street Park and John Stokes Garden? +

The City does not document a dedicated parking lot for this 0.09-acre garden. Use legal neighborhood street parking near 1600 Pender Street and North Carver Street, reading all signs and keeping driveways, corners, mail access, and residents clear. Do not use private residential spaces or assume nearby Fisher Street Park has overflow parking.

Where is the entrance to John Stokes Garden? +

Find the garden at the corner of Pender Street and North Carver Street, using 1600 Pender Street for navigation. The renovated layout provides a short garden path with approaches from the surrounding sidewalk area. This is not the fenced playground visible across Pender Street; that is Fisher Street Park.

Is there a restroom at Pender Street Park? +

No public restroom is documented at this tiny, unstaffed garden. The nearby Fisher Street Park page also does not list a restroom, so do not rely on crossing the street for one. Plan a restroom stop before arriving and never ask to use facilities in surrounding private homes.

What hours is Pender Street Park open? +

Raleigh parks are generally open dawn to dusk every day, and Pender Street Park has no staffed building or separate published hours. Visit only in daylight, respect nearby homes, and leave if City signs, maintenance work, severe weather, or a temporary closure restricts access.

Is John Stokes Garden free? +

Yes. This is a free public City garden with no documented admission, ticket, or reservation requirement for an ordinary visit. It is designed for brief neighborhood walking, sitting, reflection, and appreciation rather than private events or exclusive use.

Is Pender Street Park wheelchair accessible? +

The City renovation plan included a paved accessible entrance and route, along with seatwalls and a fixed sitting area. Because the garden is small and meets neighborhood sidewalks and curbs, current slope, curb-ramp, vegetation, and construction conditions can matter. Contact Raleigh Parks at 919-996-3285 for a route-specific check.

What is at Pender Street Park? +

The renovated garden has a short walkway, seatwalls, a small picnic or sitting area, public art honoring John Stokes, and new trees, shrubs, ornamental grasses, and flowering perennials. It is intentionally a quiet pocket garden and memorial, not a full recreation park.

Does Pender Street Park have a playground or basketball court? +

No. The playground, fenced basketball court, shelter, trellis swings, and larger active recreation area are at Fisher Street Park across Pender Street near North Fisher Street. John Stokes Garden is the smaller landscaped memorial at Pender and North Carver streets. The two parks are related but not interchangeable.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

Who was John Stokes and why is the garden named for him? +

John Stokes lived nearby and worked with College Park residents to clear trash and weeds from the formerly unmaintained lot, then spent years gardening and planting it. He also helped secure its dedication as a City park in the early 1980s. The renovated garden and public art preserve that community legacy.

What public art is at John Stokes Garden? +

The 2022 renovation added public art celebrating John Stokes's dedication to the garden and College Park community. The park is compact, so look along the paved sitting area and landscape rather than expecting a separate sculpture garden. Do not climb on or alter the work.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

When is John Stokes Garden most colorful? +

The garden includes flowering perennials, ornamental grasses, shrubs, and trees, so appearance changes throughout the year and as young landscaping matures. The City does not promise a peak-bloom calendar. Visit in different seasons and treat online photographs as a moment in time, not a guarantee of current flowers.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

How long should I plan for Pender Street Park? +

This is a 0.09-acre pocket garden with a short walkway, so a simple walk-through may take only a few minutes. Allow longer to sit, notice the plantings and public art, or combine it with Fisher Street Park across the street. It is best understood as a small neighborhood pause and memorial, not a half-day destination.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What is the park across from John Stokes Garden? +

Fisher Street Park is diagonally across Pender Street near North Fisher Street. It was relocated and rebuilt in 2022 with a playground, basketball court, picnic shelter, seating, trellis swings, landscaping, and public art. Cross only at a safe legal location and supervise children around neighborhood streets.
