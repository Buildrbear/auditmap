What people say

Forest Ridge Park is Raleigh's nearly 600-acre adventure park beside Falls Lake, combining a staffed Welcome Center, accessible playground and shelters, fishing, nature programs, a wildlife garden, paved and natural trails, mountain biking, a pump track, and seasonal disc golf.

Where should I park at Forest Ridge Park? +

Use the main park entrance at 2100 Old North Carolina 98 Highway. The Welcome Center lot has two accessible spaces, while the playground and picnic-shelter area has four. Park near the facility that matches your visit rather than assuming every attraction begins at the same trailhead.

What entrance should I use for Forest Ridge Park? +

Navigate to the City's listed address, 2100 Old North Carolina 98 Highway in Wake Forest, and enter through the signed park drive. Start at the Welcome Center for current trail conditions, maps, programs, and loaner equipment; continue to the separate playground and shelter parking area for the shortest family route.

Are there restrooms at Forest Ridge Park? +

Yes. Restrooms with accessible features are located at the Welcome Center and at the playground. Because this is a large 587-acre park, use them before starting a longer natural-surface trail or heading to a minimally developed fishing area.

What time does Forest Ridge Park close? +

Hours change seasonally: 8 a.m.-6 p.m. November through February; 8 a.m.-7 p.m. in March and October; and 7 a.m.-8 p.m. April through September. Plan to be back at your vehicle before closing and check current alerts when weather may affect trails.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Is Forest Ridge Park free? +

General entry, parking, playground use, the wildlife garden, trails, and designated bank-fishing access are free. Fishing equipment and other selected loaners are free with a RecLink account, while organized programs, meeting-room rentals, and reserved uses may charge.

How accessible is Forest Ridge Park? +

The park provides six accessible parking spaces: two at the Welcome Center and four at the playground/shelter area. The Welcome Center has push-button entry; accessible restrooms are at both primary facility areas; and shelters have accessible tables and grills. Natural trails and fishing banks vary considerably by surface and conditions.

What is the playground like at Forest Ridge Park? +

The adventure playground beside the picnic shelters is designed primarily for ages 5-12 and includes a zip line, slides, swings, climbing elements, and rubberized surfacing. Restrooms and accessible parking are in this area, making it the most convenient starting point for a family play visit.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What trails are at Forest Ridge Park? +

The City lists 3.25 miles of single-track mountain-bike trail, 1.85 miles of unpaved multi-use trail, 6.5 miles of natural-surface hiking trail, and a half-mile paved loop. Choose a route for your mobility and experience level, carry a map, and check status before using single-track after rain.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Are the mountain-bike trails at Forest Ridge Park open? +

The City's current alert lists the mountain-bike trail as closed. These trails frequently close during or after rain to prevent erosion and may reopen as conditions improve, so check Raleigh Parks Alerts or call 919-996-5800 on the day of your ride rather than relying on an older status.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh Parks Alerts ↗
Can I fish at Forest Ridge Park? +

Yes, from designated bank-fishing areas during park hours and under current North Carolina fishing regulations. Infrastructure at the banks is minimal and bait is not sold at the office. Free loaner fishing equipment is available first-come, first-served with a RecLink account; call ahead when shoreline conditions matter.

When is disc golf available at Forest Ridge Park? +

The 18-hole course is seasonal, normally operating from October 15 through March 22 during regular park hours, with the first tee south of the main parking lot. It closes for the growing season so vegetation can recover. Loaner discs are available at the park office when the course is operating.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I borrow equipment at Forest Ridge Park? +

Yes. First-come, first-served loaners at the Welcome Center include youth explorer and art backpacks, adult binoculars, a youth fort-building kit, balance bikes, fishing equipment, and seasonal disc-golf discs. A RecLink account is required for checkout, so create one before arriving if a loaner is central to your plan.
