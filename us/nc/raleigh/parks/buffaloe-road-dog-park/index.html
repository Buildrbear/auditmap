Where should I park for Buffaloe Road Dog Park? +

Navigate to Buffaloe Road Athletic Park at 5900 Buffaloe Road and use the marked public parking closest to the signed dog-park enclosure. The 5812 Buffaloe Road address appears in greenway parking guidance and is not the clearest dog-park destination. Tournament, aquatic, and league traffic can fill the shared campus, so arrive early during events.

How do I enter Buffaloe Road Dog Park safely? +

Keep your dog leashed from the vehicle through the athletic park and into the gated entry. Pause if dogs are crowding the gate, remove choke, prong, pinch, or spike collars, close the outer gate, then unleash before entering the play area. Reverse the process when leaving and never open both gates together.

Is there a restroom near Buffaloe Road Dog Park? +

Raleigh's playground directory identifies an in-park restroom at Buffaloe Road, but the dog enclosure itself does not contain a restroom. Outdoor facilities can close for maintenance or winterization, and Aquatic Center restrooms follow paid-facility hours and policies. Use an available public park restroom before entering and never leave a dog unattended.

What hours is Buffaloe Road Dog Park open? +

The dog park is open dawn to dusk daily, regardless of later field-light or Aquatic Center schedules. Posted closures for weather, saturated wood chips, maintenance, or safety take priority. Check Raleigh Parks Status after rain and leave before dusk.

Is Buffaloe Road Dog Park free? +

Yes. The fenced dog park is free with no day pass or public membership fee. The adjacent Aquatic Center, athletic programs, leagues, field rentals, and special events have separate admission or registration costs; those fees do not apply to ordinary dog-park use.

Is Buffaloe Road Dog Park wheelchair accessible? +

The athletic campus has developed parking and paved internal approaches, but Raleigh does not publish a dog-enclosure-specific accessible-route guarantee. Wood chips, mud, gates, slopes, and a busy vestibule can create barriers. Contact Raleigh Athletics at 919-996-6836 for current parking-to-gate and surface details.

Which Buffaloe Road dog area should I use? +

Use the small-dog enclosure for dogs weighing 30 pounds or less and the large-dog enclosure for dogs over 30 pounds. Follow posted gate signs rather than choosing by temperament alone. Size separation lowers mismatch risk, but handlers must still remove dogs that become aggressive, uncontrollable, or overwhelmed.

Is water available at Buffaloe Road Dog Park? +

Yes. The City lists a drinking fountain for both dogs and humans. Fountain availability can change because of freezing weather, maintenance, or damage, so carrying backup water and a portable bowl is still wise. Do not bring food, treats, or toys into the off-leash area.

What is the surface at Buffaloe Road Dog Park? +

The enclosure uses wood chips and includes shade and benches. Wood chips can shift, hide holes, or become wet and muddy after rain. Wear closed shoes, inspect play areas before running, stop your dog from digging and fill holes, and bring a towel or paw-cleaning supplies.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What dogs are allowed at Buffaloe Road Dog Park? +

Dogs must be healthy and fully vaccinated. Puppies younger than four months, females in season, and aggressive or uncontrollable dogs are prohibited. All breeds are welcome when rules are met. A handler may supervise no more than three dogs and remains responsible for their behavior.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I bring toys, treats, or a chair into Buffaloe Road Dog Park? +

No. Raleigh prohibits food, treats, toys, and outside furniture inside dog parks because they can provoke guarding or conflict. Remove choke, prong, pinch, and spike collars; pick up waste; stop digging; fill holes; and actively watch your dog throughout the visit.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can children enter Buffaloe Road Dog Park? +

Children under 12 must be closely supervised. The enclosure is not a playground: children should not run, carry food or toys, approach unfamiliar dogs, or stand in the gate vestibule. Buffaloe Road has separate playgrounds outside the dog park for child-centered activity.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What should I do about a dog bite at Buffaloe Road? +

Separate and remove dogs before conflict escalates. For an aggressive or uncontrollable dog, call Animal Control at 919-831-6311. After a bite, provide medical care, identify the dog and owner, and report the incident; call 911 for serious injury. Report enclosure or fountain maintenance problems at 919-996-4115.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

What else is near Buffaloe Road Dog Park? +

The larger 165.6-acre campus includes playgrounds, athletic fields, an outdoor track, the separately operated Buffaloe Road Aquatic Center, and access to the Neuse River Greenway. Dogs must be leashed outside the fenced enclosure and are prohibited on athletic fields and courts, even when those areas appear unused.
