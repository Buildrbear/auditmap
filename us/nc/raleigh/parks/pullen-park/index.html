Pullen Park

520 Ashe Ave, Raleigh, NC
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Park and restrooms: daily 7 a.m.-9 p.m.; ride hours vary by season and weather.
Navigate

What people say

Pullen Park is a 66.4-acre campus combining one of the country's oldest operating amusement parks with a historic carousel, miniature train, layered playgrounds, shelters, indoor swimming, arts, theatre, sports, and community programs. In 2026 the dependable family core is open while Lake Howell, pedal boats, and shoreline paths remain under construction.

Where is parking for Pullen Park? +

Parking is available at the park entrance at 520 Ashe Avenue, including accessible spaces. The upper parking lot is a useful landmark because several shelters sit beside it.

Do you need tickets at Pullen Park? +

General park access is free, but tickets are required for rides such as the carousel, train, pedal boats, and kiddie boats. Ride tickets are listed at $2 per person, with children under one riding free with a paying adult.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026

Is the playground open at Pullen Park? +

The main playground and park attractions reopened in spring 2026 while shoreline work continued near Lake Howell. Some lakeside paths and nearby areas may still have project impacts, so the playground is the more reliable family destination than the lake loop right now.

Where are the restrooms at Pullen Park? +

Restroom facilities are available in the park during listed operating hours, and both restroom locations include accessible facilities.

Which entrance should I use for Pullen Park? +

Use the main park and Welcome Center entrance at 520 Ashe Avenue for the amusements, playgrounds, cafe, and primary visitor parking. The City identifies this as the park entrance and says accessible parking is available there.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026

How accessible is Pullen Park? +

Pullen Park has paved sidewalks throughout that the City describes as accessible to wheelchairs, scooters, and ECVs. Accessible parking and two accessible restroom locations are available, and the playground includes accessible swings, slides, a sand digger, and a spring platform. Some rides and special-event areas have separate limitations; call 919-996-6468 for site-specific help or 919-996-2147 for an ADA program modification.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026
Pullen Park Amusements ↗
What is closed at Pullen Park right now? +

During the Lake Howell shoreline project, the paths around the lake, pedal boats, island gazebo, fountain terrace, and the shelter beside the carousel remain closed. The main entrance, playground, cafe, carousel, train, aquatic center, community center, and tennis courts are expected to remain open most days, although brief construction-related closures can occur. Construction is expected to continue through September 2026.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026

Can I take the bus to Pullen Park? +

GoRaleigh Route 11 Avent Ferry serves Pullen Park. Check GoRaleigh's live trip planner before leaving for the nearest active stop, direction, and arrival time because schedules and stop locations can change.

Are the paths at Pullen Park paved and stroller-friendly? +

The City describes the park's sidewalks as paved and accessible to wheelchairs, scooters, and ECVs, making the main visitor areas suitable for most strollers. The pathways around Lake Howell are temporarily closed for shoreline reconstruction, so do not plan on a complete lake loop during the project.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026
Pullen Park Amusements ↗
Is there a splash pad at Pullen Park? +

The City does not list an outdoor splash pad at Pullen Park. Water recreation is provided at the separate indoor Pullen Aquatic Center, which has an Olympic pool, a warm-water therapy pool, and scheduled lap and recreational swimming; admission and operating hours are separate from general park access.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026

Can I bring a dog to Pullen Park? +

Leashed dogs are welcome in Raleigh parks, and handlers must remove pet waste. Pullen Park is not listed among Raleigh's designated off-leash dog parks, so keep dogs leashed and do not expect a fenced dog run.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026
Raleigh Parks Know Before You Go ↗
Where can I have a picnic at Pullen Park? +

Pullen Park has six reservable picnic shelters with tables; Shelters 1 through 6 hold roughly 30 to 100 people, and most include charcoal grills. Shelters 3, 4, and 5 are beside the upper parking lot. Shelter 4 has electrical outlets. Reservations take priority, and current rates and availability are handled through RecLink.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026
Pullen Park ↗
Is there shade at the Pullen Park playground? +

Yes. The school-age climbing area has one shade structure, the sand-play area has two, and the enclosed preschool playground has two. The City does not publish hour-by-hour sun coverage, so actual shade shifts during the day.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026
Pullen Park Amusements ↗
What landmarks should I look for at Pullen Park? +

Easy-to-recognize landmarks in the amusement area include the historic carousel, the TV Land Andy and Opie statue, the children's amphitheater, and the caboose. During the Lake Howell project, the island gazebo and fountain terrace are closed.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026
Pullen Park Amusements ↗
What time does Pullen Park open and close? +

The park and public restrooms are open daily from 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. Amusement rides currently use shorter schedules and may close early for darkness, weather, maintenance, or the Lake Howell project. The aquatic center, arts center, community center, cafe, and Theatre in the Park each follow their own hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
How do I check which Pullen Park rides are running today? +

Call the official ride-status line at 919-996-6472 after about 10:30 a.m. Ride hours vary by season, the train closes around sunset, Kiddie Boats have limited days and hours, and weather or maintenance can change operations after the schedule is posted.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

Can I buy food at Pullen Park? +

The City lists Pullen Place Cafe beside the Welcome Center, but Raleigh sought a new cafe operator in 2026, so same-day service and menu availability should be confirmed before relying on it. Pack water and a backup snack for young children or dietary needs.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

What events are coming up at Pullen Park? +

AuditMap's Today section uses the current City of Raleigh event feed. Fitness programs, movies, Holiday Express, and other events can change parking, ride access, stage use, and crowd levels, so open the official event entry before leaving.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026
Raleigh Parks ↗
When will Lake Howell and the pedal boats reopen? +

The City says pedal boats will be unavailable until spring 2027. The lake is drained and the shoreline paths, island gazebo, fountain terrace, and the shelter beside the carousel remain closed during construction expected through September 2026.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

How does weather affect Pullen Park rides? +

Ride operations can pause or end early for rain, lightning, wind, heat, darkness, or maintenance even when the outdoor park remains open. Use the live National Weather Service panel, then call the ride-status line for the park's operating decision.
