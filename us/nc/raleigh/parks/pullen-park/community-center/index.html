Pullen Community Center

408 Ashe Avenue, Raleigh, NC
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Current hours are Monday-Friday 10 a.m.-9 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m.-3 p.m., and Sunday closed.
Navigate

What people say

A staffed community building with classes, programs, meeting rooms, a large hall, warming kitchen, and the Georges Le Chevallier 'Everyone is welcome' mural. The Community Center is on the east side of Ashe Avenue circulation beside the Aquatic Center and east of the amusement area. The building is a program and rental destination, not a general indoor lounge for amusement visitors. Confirm the class, reservation, or public-hours purpose before walking over.
