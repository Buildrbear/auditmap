What people say

WRAL Azalea Gardens is a free 1.5-acre public garden behind WRAL-TV, created by A.J. Fletcher and opened in 1959, with more than 60 azalea varieties, seasonal plantings, fountains, installations, lawns, paths, and shaded benches.

Where should I park for WRAL Azalea Gardens? +

Route to 2619 Western Boulevard and use the free visitor parking directly across from the garden entrance. The garden sits behind the WRAL studios near Western Boulevard and Avent Ferry Road and can also be approached from Centennial Parkway. Use garden visitor spaces rather than unrelated NC State or private business parking.

How do I find the entrance to WRAL Azalea Gardens? +

The garden is behind the WRAL television studios, not prominently exposed to Western Boulevard. Navigate to 2619 Western Boulevard, park in the free lot directly across from the garden entrance, and follow WRAL Gardens signs. Centennial Parkway provides another approach to the studio property.

Are there public restrooms at WRAL Azalea Gardens? +

No. WRAL's current FAQ says the garden restroom is closed to the public. The site is open for self-guided visits but is not a staffed public recreation center, so use a restroom before arriving and do not assume the television studio provides visitor facilities.

What hours is WRAL Azalea Gardens open? +

The gardens are open seven days a week from dawn until dusk, year-round. Because the schedule follows daylight rather than fixed clock times, arrive with enough light to walk back to the parking lot. Reserved ceremonies or maintenance may occupy part of the garden without turning it into an after-dark public venue.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Is WRAL Azalea Gardens free? +

Yes. Self-guided visits are free and do not require a reservation. Casual daylight photography is also free. Formal wedding ceremonies are a separate reserved use currently listed at $250, with specified weekend seasons, times, capacity, contracts, and payment requirements.

Is WRAL Azalea Gardens wheelchair accessible? +

Some paths are paved and wheelchair accessible, and benches provide rest points throughout the garden. WRAL does not claim that every lawn, planting path, or installation route is accessible. Use the paved network from the entrance and contact the garden before a visit that depends on a specific route or reserved area.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

When are the azaleas blooming at WRAL Gardens? +

Spring and fall are the best general periods for azalea bloom, while other flowers and plants continue through summer. The garden has more than 60 azalea varieties, including native deciduous and repeat-blooming Encore types, so bloom is staggered rather than one guaranteed peak day. Weather can shift timing substantially.

Are dogs allowed at WRAL Azalea Gardens? +

No. WRAL Gardens prohibits pets because of insurance-liability requirements. This applies even though the garden is free and outdoors. Choose another public park for a dog walk and do not leave an animal unattended in a vehicle while visiting.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Can I have a picnic at WRAL Azalea Gardens? +

Yes. Visitors may bring food and nonalcoholic drinks to enjoy on benches or the grass. Grilling, alcohol, and smoking are prohibited because of fire and property rules. Carry out everything you bring, since this is a small garden integrated with an active broadcasting campus.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
WRAL Gardens ↗
Do I need permission to take photos at WRAL Azalea Gardens? +

No reservation is required for ordinary daylight photography. Visitors should avoid blocking paths, respect other guests, and yield to reserved ceremonies or active garden work. Bridal portrait sessions can be reserved when a more controlled formal session is needed, while weddings require a paid contract.
