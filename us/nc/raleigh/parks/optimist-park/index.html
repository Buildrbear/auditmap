Optimist Park

5900 Whittier Drive, Raleigh, NC
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Community Center: 10 a.m.-9 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and 1-6 p.m. Sunday. Outdoor areas are generally dawn to dusk; tennis lights operate to 10 p.m.; pool hours vary with staffing.

What people say

Optimist Park is a 30.7-acre North Raleigh recreation hub behind Sanderson High School with a staffed Community Center, year-round covered Olympic pool, accessible playground, eight lighted tennis courts, three lighted ballfields, a small nature amphitheater, and trail access toward Shelley Lake.

Where should I park at Optimist Park? +

Navigate to 5900 Whittier Drive and use the signed City parking for the Community Center and park. Optimist Pool has the adjacent address 5902 Whittier Drive. Because the park sits behind Sanderson High School, follow park and pool signs and do not assume school spaces are available for ordinary park visits.

Where is the main entrance to Optimist Park? +

Use the Community Center at 5900 Whittier Drive as the main staffed starting point. The pool, playground, tennis courts, fields, amphitheater, and trail connection occupy different parts of the 30.7-acre campus, so confirm the specific facility in navigation when minimizing walking matters.

Are there restrooms at Optimist Park? +

Yes. The Community Center provides indoor facilities during posted hours, and Optimist Pool has a modern bathhouse and locker rooms for admitted swimmers. Outdoor tennis, fields, trails, and amphitheater use can extend beyond building access, so plan restroom needs around the facility you are actually visiting.

What hours is Optimist Park open? +

The Community Center is open 10 a.m.-9 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and 1-6 p.m. Sunday. Outdoor areas are generally dawn to dusk, and tennis lighting operates to 10 p.m. Pool lap, open-swim, wading, and dive-pool hours vary with staffing and events, so call before swimming.

Is Optimist Park free? +

General park admission, playground use, amphitheater use, trail access, and available casual tennis are free. Optimist Pool charges standard City admission. Programs, lessons, Community Center rentals, gym use, and organized field reservations may have separate fees and approval requirements.

How accessible is Optimist Park? +

The playground was designed for accessibility and combines rubberized surfacing with certified playground mulch. The paved developed campus, Community Center, pool locker rooms, indoor gym, and hard-surface tennis courts add predictable routes. Ask staff about pool entry equipment or the shortest route to a specific field or trail.

What is the playground at Optimist Park like? +

The accessible playground serves ages 2-5 and 5-12. Younger children have face-to-face 'expression swings' and a log tunnel; older children have a larger structure, spinner, climbing boulder, and nest swing. Surfacing combines rubberized material and certified playground mulch, so mobility conditions differ by feature.

Can I swim at Optimist Pool year-round? +

Yes. The main covered pool operates year-round and is an Olympic-size 50-meter-by-25-yard lap pool with lap and recreational swimming, a 12-foot-deep area and climbing wall. The outdoor wading pool is seasonal. Competitions and staffing can substantially change public lane and open-swim access, so call 919-996-2790 before traveling.

How much does Optimist Pool cost? +

Optimist uses Raleigh's standard public-pool admission. Current rates are $2 resident and $3 nonresident for ages 1-12; $5 resident and $8 nonresident for ages 13-54; and $4 resident and $6 nonresident for ages 55 and older. Children under one are free. Confirm rates and session availability before visiting.

How many tennis courts are at Optimist Park? +

There are eight outdoor courts with practice backboards and push-button lighting from dusk to 10 p.m. year-round. Courts are typically first-come, first-served unless a scheduled program, league, or maintenance closure has priority. Rotate fairly when others are waiting.

What baseball fields are at Optimist Park? +

The park has three lighted fields: an approximately 183-foot youth field, a 292-foot youth field, and a field for older youth and adults. Approved rentals, school or league use, and weather closures can override casual access even when a field appears empty.

Can visitors use the nature amphitheater at Optimist Park? +

Yes. The small outdoor amphitheater behind the Community Center near the walking paths is first-come, first-served, holds about 25 people, and has no electricity. Keep the space available for shared quiet use and verify with the Center before planning an organized performance or class.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Does Optimist Park connect to Shelley Lake Greenway? +

Yes. A park trail connects to Snelling Branch Greenway, which follows Snelling Branch between Optimist Park and the Mine Creek Greenway at Shelley Lake Park. This is a local Raleigh greenway connection, not a continuous trail to Charlotte. Check the Raleigh greenway map and closure notices before a longer ride.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Can I rent space at Optimist Community Center? +

Yes. The air-conditioned gym, classroom, and warming kitchen are rentable. The City currently requires a two-hour minimum, $15 application fee, $100 damage deposit, and $30 hourly supervision charge outside normal hours; nonresidents add 25 percent to hourly rental rates. Approval and payment confirmation are required.
