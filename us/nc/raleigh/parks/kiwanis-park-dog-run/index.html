Where should I park for Kiwanis Park Dog Run? +

Navigate to Kiwanis Park at 2525 Noble Road and use marked park parking. The same campus serves the neighborhood center, playground, fields, courts, and Crabtree Creek Greenway trailhead, so spaces may be busier during programs or games. Do not park in nearby private lots or block neighborhood streets.

City of Raleigh ↗
How do I enter Kiwanis Park Dog Run safely? +

Follow park signs from the Noble Road campus to the fenced dog run. Keep your dog leashed across the parking lot and park, wait if dogs crowd the gate, remove choke, prong, pinch, or spike collars, secure the outside gate, and unleash before entering the play enclosure. Leash again before opening the exit gate.

Is there a restroom near Kiwanis Park Dog Run? +

Kiwanis Neighborhood Center is on the same park campus, but indoor restroom access follows building hours: 9 a.m.-8 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and closed Sunday. The City does not list a dedicated dog-run restroom. Confirm building access and never leave a dog unattended in the enclosure.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What hours is Kiwanis Park Dog Run open? +

Raleigh lists its public dog parks as open dawn to dusk daily. The neighborhood center's later weekday hours do not extend dog-run hours. Posted closures for weather, mud, maintenance, or safety take priority, so check Raleigh Parks Status after heavy rain.

Is Kiwanis Park Dog Run free? +

Yes. Ordinary use of the dog run and public park is free. Neighborhood-center programs, room or field reservations, leagues, and other organized activities may charge separately.

Is Kiwanis Park Dog Run wheelchair accessible? +

The enclosure is in a developed public park, but the City does not publish a dog-run-specific accessible route. Wood chips, gate force, mud, slope, and crowding may create barriers. Contact Kiwanis Neighborhood Center at 919-996-6833 before visiting for current parking-to-gate and surface details.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

Does Kiwanis Dog Run separate small and large dogs? +

The official Kiwanis page describes one dog run and does not list separate small- and large-dog sections. Treat it as a shared enclosure unless current gate signs say otherwise. Small, shy, older, or mobility-limited dogs may be safer during a quiet period or at a size-separated Raleigh dog park.

Is water available at Kiwanis Park Dog Run? +

The City's dog-run amenity list does not include a dog or human water source. Bring enough water and a clean portable bowl, especially during warm weather. Do not assume the neighborhood center is open or that an outdoor tap is potable.

Is Kiwanis Dog Run shaded or muddy? +

The City confirms a wood-chip surface and benches but does not list shade as an enclosure amenity. Wood chips can shift, hide holes, and become wet or muddy after rain. Plan sun protection, wear suitable shoes, and bring towels or paw-cleaning supplies.

What dogs are allowed at Kiwanis Park Dog Run? +

Dogs must be healthy and fully vaccinated. Puppies under four months, females in season, and aggressive or uncontrollable dogs are prohibited. A handler may supervise no more than three dogs and remains responsible for each dog's behavior.

City of Raleigh ↗
Can I bring toys, treats, or a chair into Kiwanis Dog Run? +

No. Raleigh prohibits food, treats, toys, and outside furniture in dog parks because they can trigger guarding and conflict. Remove choke, prong, pinch, and spike collars; use the supplied waste bags; stop digging; fill holes; and actively supervise your dog.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can children enter Kiwanis Park Dog Run? +

Children under 12 must be closely supervised. The dog run is not a playground: children should not run, carry food or toys, approach unfamiliar dogs, or crowd the gates. Kiwanis Park has a separate playground with a rubber surface for ages 2-12.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What should I do about a bite or maintenance issue at Kiwanis Dog Run? +

Remove dogs before a conflict escalates. Call Animal Control at 919-831-6311 for an aggressive or uncontrollable dog; after a bite, get medical care, identify the dog and owner, and call 911 for serious injury. Report fence, gate, waste, or surface problems to Raleigh Parks maintenance at 919-996-4115.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What else is near Kiwanis Park Dog Run? +

Kiwanis Park includes a neighborhood center, playground for ages 2-12, athletic fields, courts, and a Crabtree Creek Greenway trailhead. Dogs must be leashed outside the enclosure and kept off playgrounds, athletic fields, and courts. The greenway can extend the outing, but it is shared and follows separate trail rules.
