Marsh Creek Park

3050 North New Hope Road, Raleigh, NC
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Community Center: 10 a.m.-9 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and 1-6 p.m. Sunday. Outdoor park facilities are generally open dawn to dusk unless separately scheduled or posted.

What people say

Marsh Creek Park is a 110.6-acre Northeast Raleigh recreation destination with the City's only permanent skatepark, an inline rink, staffed Community Center, two wheelchair-accessible playgrounds, indoor gym and fitness room, fields, a free first-come shelter, and a growing urban-farm and teaching-garden hub.

Where should I park at Marsh Creek Park? +

Navigate to 3050 North New Hope Road and use the signed park and Community Center lots. The 110.6-acre campus includes the Center, skatepark, inline rink, playgrounds, fields, shelter, and urban farm, so follow onsite signs toward the facility you need instead of assuming every area is beside the main doors.

Where is the main entrance to Marsh Creek Park? +

Use the Community Center at 3050 North New Hope Road as the staffed starting point. It provides the clearest orientation toward the skatepark, inline rink, lower deck, shelter, playgrounds, fields, garden, and indoor recreation spaces spread around the large campus.

Are there restrooms at Marsh Creek Park? +

Yes. Indoor visitor facilities are available in the Community Center during posted building hours. Outdoor facilities may remain active on different schedules, so families using the skatepark, rink, shelter, fields, or playgrounds should plan around Center hours and check onsite signs for any event-day facilities.

What hours is Marsh Creek Park open? +

The Community Center is open 10 a.m.-9 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and 1-6 p.m. Sunday. Outdoor park areas are generally used dawn to dusk, while classes, open gym, field reservations, skate activities, and private rentals can follow separate schedules.

Is Marsh Creek Park free? +

General park admission, the playgrounds, skatepark, garden viewing, first-come picnic shelter, and available casual outdoor use are free. Programs and indoor activities may charge. The inline rink is currently listed at $30 per hour, while room, gym, and covered-deck rentals have separate rates and approval requirements.

How accessible is Marsh Creek Park? +

The two playgrounds have rubber surfacing and wheelchair-accessible play structures. The developed Community Center, paved parking, indoor gym and restrooms, inline rink, and lower deck provide other firm-surface options. The skatepark is specialized terrain, and exact field or garden routes should be confirmed when mobility access is essential.

What is Marsh Creek Skate Park like? +

Built in 2009, Marsh Creek is Raleigh's only permanent skatepark and includes street, flow, and bowl elements. The City requires a helmet, elbow pads, and knee pads. Bring correctly fitted safety gear and inspect wetness, debris, crowding, and surface conditions before riding; never promise a beginner that every feature will be appropriate.

Is there an inline hockey rink at Marsh Creek Park? +

Yes. The outdoor rink measures approximately 185 by 85 feet and uses a Plexiflor inline-skating surface. The City lists a $30-per-hour rental rate. Scheduled rentals have priority, so check availability before organizing hockey, practice, or a group skate even if the rink appears empty.

What playgrounds are at Marsh Creek Park? +

Separate playgrounds serve ages 2-5 and 5-12. Both have rubber safety surfacing and a wheelchair-accessible play structure. Choose the age-appropriate area and supervise closely because the surrounding destination includes a skatepark, inline rink, parking, fields, and other active recreation uses.

What fields are at Marsh Creek Park? +

The park has a lighted 254-foot youth baseball field that can also support softball and a roughly 240-by-200-foot multipurpose field. Leagues and approved rentals have priority, and weather can close athletic fields while other park facilities remain open. Confirm field status before organized play.

Can I reserve the picnic shelter at Marsh Creek Park? +

No reservation is needed or accepted for the standalone picnic shelter. It is first-come, first-served, holds about 45 people, has eight tables and charcoal grills, no electricity, and sits near the skatepark and inline rink. For guaranteed event space, consider the separately rentable covered lower deck or indoor rooms.

What indoor recreation is available at Marsh Creek Community Center? +

The Center has an air-conditioned hardwood gym lined for full and small basketball courts plus volleyball, and a fitness room with treadmills, elliptical machines, a recumbent bike, universal machine, stability ball, and stretching station. Access depends on programs, open play, membership rules, or rentals rather than ordinary park admission.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can visitors see the garden and urban farm at Marsh Creek Park? +

Yes. The park's agricultural area includes raised beds, pollinator gardens, edible landscaping, a teaching garden, and a 1.5-acre urban-farm partnership with Raleigh City Farm. Self-guided viewing should not interfere with crops or classes; register through the Center for hands-on gardening and orchard programs.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Does the Marsh Creek Trail connect to Marsh Creek Park? +

Do not assume it does. The City-listed Marsh Creek Trail is a separate 0.3-mile greenway segment through Brentwood Park between Glenraven Drive and Ingram Drive, with parking near Vinson Court. This Marsh Creek Park listing refers to the recreation campus at 3050 North New Hope Road.
