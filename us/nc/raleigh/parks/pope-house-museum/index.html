Where should I park for the Pope House Museum? +

The museum has no onsite parking. The City recommends the City Center parking deck at 429 South Wilmington Street, which currently offers two free hours, or free weekend street parking where signs allow. Private lots beside the house may charge and can tow unauthorized vehicles, so do not assume an adjacent space belongs to the museum.

Where is the Pope House Museum entrance? +

Navigate to 511 South Wilmington Street and approach the historic house from Wilmington Street. Because the museum is a small house rather than a purpose-built gallery, arrive during published hours and follow onsite signs or staff directions for check-in and the next guided visit. There is no separate parking-lot entrance.

Is there a public restroom at the Pope House Museum? +

The current official visitor page does not identify a public restroom or describe its accessibility. Because this is a compact historic house with a guided experience, call 919-996-4190 before visiting if restroom access is essential, and use facilities at a confirmed nearby venue rather than assuming one will be available.

What hours is the Pope House Museum open? +

The City currently lists Tuesday-Saturday 9 a.m.-4 p.m. and Sunday 1-4 p.m.; Monday is closed. Tours, school programs, special events, First Friday evenings, and holiday closures can differ from normal building hours, so check the official page or call before a time-sensitive visit.

Is the Pope House Museum free? +

Yes. Ordinary museum admission is free, and donations are appreciated. Special programs can require registration or a fee; for example, seasonal Black history walking tours have separate tickets. Confirm the exact event rather than assuming every activity associated with the museum is free.

Is the Pope House Museum wheelchair accessible? +

Not fully. The historic house has raised thresholds, and its second floor is accessible only by stairs. Call 984-302-4304 to discuss the current first-floor route and how second-floor content can be interpreted. Raleigh Parks Inclusion Services can also arrange reasonable program modifications when requested in advance.

How do tours work at the Pope House Museum? +

The primary experience is a guided tour using the historic rooms, original furnishings, and artifacts to tell the Pope family's story. The City does not currently publish fixed departure times on the main page, so arrive with flexibility or call 919-996-4190 for that day's tour cadence, group availability, and any program interruption.

Why is the Pope House Museum important? +

Dr. Manassa Thomas Pope was one of North Carolina's first licensed African American physicians, a Leonard Medical School graduate, Spanish-American War officer, and the only African American known to have run for mayor of a Southern capital during the Jim Crow era. The home also preserves the stories of Lydia, Delia, Evelyn, and Ruth Pope.

Are the furnishings in the Pope House original? +

Many are. The museum interprets the family through original furnishings, documents, and historical objects rather than a fully recreated generic period interior. The collection totals more than 3,000 artifacts dating from the 1860s and helps show the daily, professional, and civic life of a prominent Black Raleigh family.

Is the Pope House Museum good for children? +

Children interested in history can visit, and the City offers educational programs tied to local history, African American studies, social studies, English, and art. This is a guided historic-house experience with fragile original objects, raised thresholds, and stairs rather than a hands-on children's museum, so prepare younger visitors accordingly.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Do Black history walking tours leave from the Pope House? +

Yes, selected City programs meet at the Pope House and explore the Third Ward, Black Wall Street, historic churches, and other downtown Black-history locations. These roughly 1.4-mile programs require registration and use dates and prices separate from free museum admission, so check the current event listing before arriving.

How much time should I allow for the Pope House Museum? +

Plan at least an hour for parking, walking from the deck, check-in, and a guided visit, with extra time if you want to examine artifacts or attend a program. A registered neighborhood walking tour lasts about one hour by itself and should be treated as a separate activity from touring the house.
