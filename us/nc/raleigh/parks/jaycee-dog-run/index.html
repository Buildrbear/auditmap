Where should I park for Jaycee Dog Run? +

Navigate to Jaycee Park at 2405 Wade Avenue. The dog run is just south of the Wade Avenue parking area, and the City says ample parking is available on both sides of the enclosure. The lot also serves playgrounds, tennis, volleyball, fields, a shelter, and the community center, so use marked spaces and expect event traffic.

How do I reach and enter Jaycee Dog Run? +

Use the accessible sidewalk from the Wade Avenue parking lot to the dog run. Keep your dog leashed through the park and gated entry, wait if the gate is crowded, remove choke, prong, pinch, or spike collars, secure the outer gate, then unleash before entering. Leash again before exiting.

Is there a restroom near Jaycee Dog Run? +

Jaycee Park lists restrooms near its picnic shelter, and indoor community-center restrooms follow building hours: 10 a.m.-9 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and 1-6 p.m. Sunday. Availability can change for maintenance or events. Never leave a dog unattended in the run while using a restroom.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

What hours is Jaycee Dog Run open? +

The City dog-park directory lists dawn to dusk daily. Later community-center hours or push-button tennis lights do not extend dog-run hours. Posted closures for weather, mud, maintenance, or safety take priority; check Raleigh Parks Status after rain.

Is Jaycee Dog Run free? +

Yes. The dog run and ordinary public park access are free. Community-center programs, weight room, gym, rooms, shelter reservations, leagues, and field rentals can charge separately.

Is Jaycee Dog Run wheelchair accessible? +

An accessible sidewalk connects the Wade Avenue parking area to the dog run, making the arrival route more clearly documented than at many enclosures. Wood chips, mud, gate force, slope, or crowding can still create barriers. Call Jaycee Park at 919-996-6833 for current parking, gate, and surface details.

Does Jaycee Dog Run have a separate small-dog area? +

No. Raleigh specifically describes Jaycee as one shared enclosure with no dog-size restriction, unlike Oakwood, Millbrook, Buffaloe Road, and Carolina Pines. Small, shy, older, or mobility-limited dogs may be safer at a size-separated park or during a quiet period. Leave immediately if play becomes overwhelming.

Is water available at Jaycee Dog Run? +

The City's current Jaycee amenity list does not include a dog or human water source. Bring enough water and a clean portable bowl, especially in hot weather. Do not bring food, treats, or toys into the enclosure, and do not assume community-center access or an outdoor tap will be available.

Is Jaycee Dog Run shaded or muddy? +

The City lists benches, natural shade, and a wood-chip surface. Wood chips can shift, conceal holes, or become wet and muddy after rain. Wear suitable shoes, inspect the enclosure, stop digging and fill holes, and bring towels or paw-cleaning supplies.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

What dogs are allowed at Jaycee Dog Run? +

Dogs must be healthy and fully vaccinated. Puppies under four months, females in season, and aggressive or uncontrollable dogs are prohibited. All breeds and sizes share the same enclosure when rules are met. A handler may supervise no more than three dogs and remains responsible for each one.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I bring toys, treats, or a chair into Jaycee Dog Run? +

No. Raleigh prohibits food, treats, toys, and outside furniture inside dog parks because they can trigger guarding and conflict. Remove choke, prong, pinch, and spike collars; pick up waste; stop digging; fill holes; and watch your dog continuously.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can children enter Jaycee Dog Run? +

Children under 12 must be closely supervised. This matters especially at Jaycee because all dog sizes share one enclosure and the run sits near a playground. Children should not run, carry food or toys, approach unfamiliar dogs, or crowd the entry. Use the separate playground for child-centered activity.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

What should I do about a dog bite at Jaycee Dog Run? +

Remove dogs before conflict escalates. For an aggressive or uncontrollable dog, call Animal Control at 919-831-6311. After a bite, provide medical care, identify the dog and owner, and report the incident; call 911 for serious injury. Report fence, gate, waste, or surface maintenance issues at 919-996-4115.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What else is near Jaycee Dog Run? +

Jaycee Park has a community center, playground, two tennis courts, six sand-volleyball courts, athletic fields, a picnic shelter with nearby restrooms, gym, rooms, and weight room. Dogs must be leashed outside the dog run and are prohibited on athletic fields and courts, even if those areas are empty.
