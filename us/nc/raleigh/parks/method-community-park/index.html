Method Community Park

514 Method Road, Raleigh, NC
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Community Center: 10 a.m.-9 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and 1-6 p.m. Sunday. Outdoor facilities are generally open dawn to dusk; lighted pickleball operates to 10 p.m.

What people say

Method Community Park is an 8.3-acre historic West Raleigh campus on the former Berry O'Kelly Training School grounds, combining a landmark Community Center and Pioneers Building exhibit with an accessible playground, six lighted pickleball courts, basketball, indoor recreation, fields, and a reservable shelter.

Where should I park at Method Community Park? +

Navigate to 514 Method Road and use the signed park and Community Center parking. The compact campus includes the Community Center, Pioneers Building, playground, pickleball and basketball courts, fields, and shelter. Follow accessible and reserved-space signs; future construction may temporarily change circulation or capacity.

Where is the main entrance to Method Community Park? +

Use 514 Method Road and begin at the Berry O'Kelly/Harveleigh White Community Center for staff help, restrooms, program information, and orientation. The separate Pioneers Building contains the historic exhibit and meeting spaces, so ask staff before assuming every building entrance is open for drop-in visits.

Are there restrooms at Method Community Park? +

Yes. Restrooms are available in the Community Center during posted building hours. The planned 2027-2028 improvement project includes new outdoor restrooms, but those future facilities should not be assumed available today. Handle restroom needs before the Center closes and watch for construction notices.

What hours is Method Community Park open? +

The Community Center is open 10 a.m.-9 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and 1-6 p.m. Sunday. Outdoor facilities are generally used dawn to dusk, while timer-controlled pickleball lights operate from dusk to 10 p.m. Programs, tours, rentals, and future construction can change access.

Is Method Community Park free? +

General park admission, playground use, outdoor basketball, and available casual pickleball are free. Programs, history tours, field reservations, shelter or room rentals, and indoor gym use may charge. The fitness room currently uses a $15 monthly City pass that can also be used at other participating Raleigh community centers.

How accessible is Method Community Park? +

The current Boundless Playground has bonded-rubber surfacing and a wheelchair-accessible play structure. The developed buildings, paved parking, gym, and hard-surface courts provide other firm routes. The planned renovation will add broader site-access improvements, but describe those as future work rather than current conditions.

What is the playground at Method Community Park like? +

The current Boundless Playground serves ages 2-12, uses recycled bonded-rubber surfacing, and includes a wheelchair-accessible play structure. A replacement playground is planned as part of 2027-2028 construction, so check project notices before visiting once work begins.

Does Method Community Park have a splash pad? +

Not yet. Raleigh has approved a new splash pad as part of the major park renovation, but construction is currently planned for 2027-2028. Do not travel here expecting water play today; use AuditMap or the City project page to recheck status as construction approaches completion.

How many pickleball courts are at Method Community Park? +

There are six outdoor pickleball courts with backboards and timer-controlled lighting from dusk to 10 p.m. Players must follow posted rotation rules so waiting groups can enter fairly. Scheduled programs, maintenance, and eventual renovation work may override casual availability.

Can I play basketball at Method Community Park? +

Yes. The park has a concrete outdoor basketball court. The Community Center gym also supports indoor sports, including three pickleball layouts and one volleyball court, but indoor access depends on open-play schedules, programs, or an approved rental rather than ordinary outdoor park access.

What fields are at Method Community Park? +

The park has a roughly 197-foot youth baseball field and a 300-by-175-foot multipurpose field. Approved reservations and scheduled programs have priority, and weather or future construction can close fields independently of other park areas. Confirm field status before organized play.

Can I reserve the picnic shelter at Method Community Park? +

Yes. The shelter is listed for approximately 60-100 people with eight tables, electricity, no grill, and a current rate of $20 per hour. Reservations require staff approval and payment confirmation, and a 25 percent nonresident surcharge may apply to the hourly rate.

Why is Method Community Park historically important? +

The park occupies the former Berry O'Kelly Training School grounds. Its Community Center honors educator and community leader Berry O'Kelly and Harveleigh Monte Rivera White. The campus and buildings are Raleigh Historic Landmarks within the National Register-listed Berry O'Kelly Historic District.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can visitors see the Berry O'Kelly School exhibit? +

Yes, when the Pioneers Building exhibit room is open or a history tour is arranged. The building was the old Agricultural Building of Berry O'Kelly High School, described by the City as North Carolina's first fully accredited and largest rural high school for African Americans. The exhibit includes a timeline and former-student artifacts.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

When will Method Community Park renovations happen? +

The current City schedule places design, permitting, and bidding in 2025-2027 and construction in 2027-2028. Planned work includes building renovations, a splash pad, replacement playground, outdoor restrooms, expanded parking, accessibility improvements, stormwater work, and public art. Dates can change, so recheck before a future visit.
