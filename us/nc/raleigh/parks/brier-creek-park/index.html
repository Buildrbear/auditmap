What people say

Brier Creek Park is a 10-acre City and Wake County Schools shared campus with a staffed Community Center, two age-specific rubber-surface playgrounds, two multipurpose fields, a shelter and comfort station, indoor gym, fitness room, programs, and a solar-powered EV charger.

Where should I park at Brier Creek Park? +

Navigate to 10810 Globe Road and use the designated Community Center and park parking. The campus is shared with Brier Creek Elementary School, so follow posted school-day traffic controls and do not treat school-only spaces, bus lanes, or fire lanes as park overflow. A solar-powered EV charging station is available.

Where is the public entrance to Brier Creek Park? +

Use the Brier Creek Community Center entrance at 10810 Globe Road for staff, programs, indoor recreation, and orientation to the outdoor playgrounds, fields, shelter, and comfort station. Because the park shares a school campus, remain within signed public-use areas rather than entering elementary-school spaces.

Are there restrooms at Brier Creek Park? +

Yes. The City lists a comfort station with restrooms beside the picnic shelter, tot playground, and Field 1, and the Community Center has indoor visitor facilities during operating hours. Check onsite signs if arriving for early outdoor use or a private event because building and comfort-station access may not always match.

What hours is Brier Creek Park open? +

The listed Community Center schedule is 7 a.m.-9 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and 1-6 p.m. Sunday. Outdoor fields, playgrounds, or school-shared areas may be affected by programs and campus schedules, so use posted signs rather than assuming every amenity is continuously available for the full building day.

Is Brier Creek Park free? +

Outdoor park entry and ordinary playground use are free. Indoor open play varies by activity: current City programming lists some free basketball and volleyball blocks and a small fee for adult pickleball. Fitness access, classes, camps, rentals, and reserved field use may charge; check RecLink for the exact session.

Is Brier Creek Park wheelchair and stroller accessible? +

Both playgrounds use rubberized safety surfacing, and the developed campus has paved approaches to the Community Center and main outdoor amenities. The indoor building and restrooms offer the most predictable access. Contact staff at 919-996-3301 when an exact field edge, transfer feature, fitness machine, or program accommodation is essential.

What ages are the Brier Creek Park playgrounds for? +

There are two separate playgrounds: one designed for ages 2-5 and one for ages 5-12. Both use rubberized safety surfacing, which is generally easier for strollers and mobility devices than loose woodchips. The tot lot sits near the shelter, comfort station, and Field 1.

What fields are at Brier Creek Park? +

The park has two open multipurpose fields used for recreation, practices, programs, and rentals. Reserved and school activities have priority over casual play. The City currently lists field rental at $45 per hour, but a request is not final until staff approval and payment confirmation.

Can I play basketball or volleyball at Brier Creek Park? +

Yes, primarily in the indoor air-conditioned gymnasium, which has six goals, a rubberized sports floor, and volleyball nets. Open-play blocks and programs change through the season. The City park page does not list public outdoor tennis courts here, so check the current indoor schedule before traveling for a specific sport.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What equipment is in the Brier Creek fitness room? +

The fitness room includes treadmills, recumbent and spin bikes, an elliptical, StairMaster, Smith machine, multi-stack weight machine, dumbbells from 5 to 80 pounds, benches, a preacher-curl bench, dip rack, and scales. Ask the front desk about current access rules, age limits, orientation, and fees.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I reserve the shelter at Brier Creek Park? +

Yes. The shelter is beside Field 1, the tot lot, and the comfort station and has eight tables, one grill, and capacity around 60-80. The current listing is $25 per hour with a two-hour minimum. Requests require staff approval and payment confirmation, so submitting RecLink information alone does not secure the date.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Is Brier Creek Park part of the elementary school? +

The park is a formal partnership between the City of Raleigh and Wake County Public School System, sharing school and community-use spaces on the Brier Creek Elementary campus. Public access is real, but school operations, student safety, and scheduled programs can control particular spaces at particular times.
