What people say

A 35-acre open meadow with flat areas, rolling hills, skyline views, mature oaks, a pine grove, hammocks, swings, and room for festivals, kites, games, and picnics. The Big Field fills the west-central side of Dix Park along Blair Drive, between the Dog Park and Adams Field. Route to 101 Blair Drive instead of the park's general address. This is the flexible lawn for flying kites, informal games, skyline photos, and major events. It is exposed in hot weather; use the oak edge or hammock areas for shade and bring water.

Where should I park for Big Field? +

Use marked Big Field parking along Blair Drive and nearby paved or gravel lots. Large events can close or reserve these spaces, so check the event listing before driving in.

Where are the nearest restrooms to Big Field? +

Temporary accessible restrooms are normally mapped near the Big Field. Indoor restrooms require a longer walk or relocation to the Stone Houses, Chapel when staffed, House of Many Porches, or Gipson Play Plaza.

What accessibility details should I know about Big Field? +

The meadow is grass with slopes and can be soft or muddy after rain. Some edges and parking approaches are paved, but reaching a specific lawn spot may require crossing uneven turf.
