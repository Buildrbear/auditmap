What people say

Paved connection from Dix Park to Raleigh's Rocky Branch Greenway, with access points near the Grove and Stone Houses and onward links toward the regional greenway network. The official map marks the trailhead at the lower east side of the park near the Grove; a second accessible connection rises toward the Stone Houses. This greenway stays within Raleigh and connects to the wider Capital Area Greenway system; it does not itself stretch to Charlotte. Check Raleigh's closure map for construction, flooding, or detours before a long ride.

Where should I park for Rocky Branch Greenway Trailhead? +

Use Grove or Stone Houses parking depending on the connection you need. Do not park on the greenway or block trail access.

No dedicated trailhead restroom is guaranteed. Stone Houses indoor restrooms are useful during weekend visitor-center hours; Gipson Play Plaza is farther uphill.

What accessibility details should I know about Rocky Branch Greenway Trailhead? +

The Stone Houses connection is documented as a fully accessible multi-use greenway connection. The Grove hillside itself is steep grass, so use the paved route rather than cutting uphill.
