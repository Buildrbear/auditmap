Baileywick Park

What people say

Baileywick Park is a 50-acre North Raleigh recreation hub with two age-specific playgrounds, restrooms, covered and uncovered multi-use courts, three athletic fields, two shelters, separate dog areas, a paved walking loop, and the 0.6-mile Baileywick Greenway.

Where should I park at Baileywick Park? +

Use the City lot at 9501 Baileywick Road for the playgrounds, restrooms, dog park, shelters, courts, fields, walking loop, and Baileywick Greenway. Parking can tighten during leagues and heavy spring programming, so arrive early for a scheduled game and use only marked park spaces rather than nearby school or neighborhood property.

Which entrance should I use for Baileywick Park? +

Navigate to 9501 Baileywick Road and enter from the signed park drive. This is also the official greenway parking point. The paved walking trail begins near the Baileywick Road entrance, loops behind the playground, and returns toward the parking lot, making the main entrance the simplest orientation point.

Are there restrooms at Baileywick Park? +

Yes. Baileywick Park has a public restroom facility serving the developed recreation area. Availability can be affected by maintenance or seasonal operations, so use posted signs and check Raleigh Parks Alerts before a visit that depends on an open restroom.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

What hours is Baileywick Park open? +

The paved Baileywick Greenway and ordinary park walking areas are open dawn to dusk. Covered and lighted courts have a posted activity schedule that can run through 9 p.m., and scheduled leagues have priority. Follow onsite gates, lighting, and program signs rather than assuming every amenity closes at the same time.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Is Baileywick Park free? +

Yes. General entry, parking, playground use, dog-park use, walking, greenway access, shelters when unoccupied, and available walk-up court play are free. League registration, organized programs, field reservations, and approved special uses may have separate fees.

Is Baileywick Park wheelchair and stroller accessible? +

Paved parking, a paved walking loop, the paved greenway, covered hard-surface courts, restrooms, and developed shelter routes provide the most predictable access. The playground uses woodchips, and field edges and informal natural areas vary. Contact Raleigh Parks when an accessible play component or exact field route is essential.

What ages are the Baileywick Park playgrounds for? +

Baileywick has separate play areas designed for ages 2-5 and 5-12, both with woodchip surfacing. This makes it useful for siblings in different age bands, but woodchips can be difficult for some wheelchairs or small stroller wheels. Restrooms and shelters are within the developed park area.

What is the dog park at Baileywick Park like? +

The fenced dog park has separate areas for small and large dogs and includes partially wooded space. Follow posted dog-park rules, remain with your dog, remove waste, and move to the appropriate size area. The dog park is public and not associated with the neighboring subdivisions.

Can I play basketball or pickleball at Baileywick Park? +

Yes, on three multi-use courts: A and B are covered and C is uncovered. Walk-up play is first-come only when a court is not scheduled. Current programming assigns different blocks to basketball, pickleball, youth leagues, and futsal, so review the posted weekly schedule before planning a specific sport.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What athletic fields are at Baileywick Park? +

Field 1 is a lighted older-youth and adult baseball/softball field with a 285-foot distance; Field 2 is a lighted youth baseball/softball field with a 250-foot distance; and Field 3 is an unlighted grass multipurpose field. Scheduled leagues and permitted users have priority over casual use.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Do Baileywick Park shelters require reservations? +

The two listed shelters are first-come, first-served. Shelter 1 has eight tables for roughly 36-60 people, no fixed grill, and electricity is not guaranteed. Shelter 2 has two tables, one grill, no electricity, and capacity around 15-30. An occupied shelter is not guaranteed solely because your group arrived with supplies.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Where does Baileywick Greenway go? +

The paved 0.6-mile Baileywick Greenway connects Baileywick Road and Strickland Road through the park and beside Baileywick Elementary School. It is a short neighborhood connector, not a continuous route to Charlotte or the main cross-city greenway corridors. The trail is open dawn to dusk.
