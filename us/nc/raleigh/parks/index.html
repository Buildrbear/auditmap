Anderson Point Park

East Raleigh

Route to 20 Anderson Point Drive. The main lot serves the shelters, playground, restrooms, fields, cottage, and greenway access, but the City warns that parking is limited when multiple areas are in use. Overflow parking is in the lot before crossing the bridge; a rental does not guarantee a main-lot space.

Annie Louise Wilkerson, MD Nature Preserve Park

North Raleigh / Falls Lake

Navigate to 5229 Awls Haven Drive and use one of the preserve's designated parking lots. Dog-waste dispensers are provided in both lots. Start near the Education Center when you need staff, exhibits, loaner equipment, or the comfort station; use the park map before choosing a loop from the other access area.

Baileywick Park

North Raleigh

Use the City lot at 9501 Baileywick Road for the playgrounds, restrooms, dog park, shelters, courts, fields, walking loop, and Baileywick Greenway. Parking can tighten during leagues and heavy spring programming, so arrive early for a scheduled game and use only marked park spaces rather than nearby school or neighborhood property.

Baileywick Park Dog Park

North Raleigh / Baileywick

Use Baileywick Park's public lot at 9501 Baileywick Road and follow park signs to the fenced dog area. The same park serves courts, fields, playgrounds, shelters, and the greenway, so parking can be busier during leagues and events. Use marked spaces and do not substitute school or neighborhood parking without permission.

Barwell Road Community Center

Southeast Raleigh

Navigate to 5857 Barwell Park Drive and use the community-center arrival area. The City does not publish the main lot's capacity, so allow extra time when programs or gym events overlap. For the community garden, two ADA-accessible spaces are provided along Barwell Park Road.

Biltmore Hills Park

Southeast Raleigh / Biltmore Hills

Use the main park and Community Center approach at 2615 Fitzgerald Drive. The seasonal pool has a separate address at 701 Crown Crossing Lane. Tennis construction expected to begin in August 2026 will close the lower lot and entrance and reconfigure accessible parking, so follow temporary signs and verify the active entrance before a sports visit.

Brier Creek Park

Navigate to 10810 Globe Road and use the designated Community Center and park parking. The campus is shared with Brier Creek Elementary School, so follow posted school-day traffic controls and do not treat school-only spaces, bus lanes, or fire lanes as park overflow. A solar-powered EV charging station is available.

Buffaloe Road Athletic Park

Large tournaments and swim sessions can fill nearby spaces, so follow event signs and do not block field or emergency access.

Buffaloe Road Dog Park

Northeast Raleigh / Buffaloe Road

Tournament, aquatic, and league traffic can fill the shared campus, so arrive early during events.

The Explorer also identifies restrooms, bike repair stations, parks, and other amenities. Never improvise parking in a neighborhood, business, or apartment lot.
