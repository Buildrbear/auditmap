What people say

Kiwanis Park is a renovated 24-acre neighborhood recreation campus in Raleigh's Five Points area, with an ages 2-12 playground, basketball court, baseball and multipurpose fields, picnic shelter, dog run, neighborhood center, and a trailhead on the Crabtree Creek Greenway.

Where should I park at Kiwanis Park in Raleigh? +

Route to 2525 Noble Road for the neighborhood center and main park entrance, then use marked on-site spaces. The park covers 24 acres, and the playground, shelter, fields, dog run, and Crabtree Creek Greenway trailhead are not all at the same curb. For a field rental or event, confirm the assigned arrival area with park staff rather than parking on neighborhood streets or service access.

Which entrance should I use for Kiwanis Park? +

Use 2525 Noble Road for the neighborhood center, playground, courts, shelter, and primary recreation campus. Greenway users can also enter from the Crabtree Creek Trail connection. Choose the Noble Road entrance when meeting someone at a specific facility because the trailhead does not function as the building entrance.

What ages is the Kiwanis Park playground for? +

The playground is designed for ages 2-12 and has a rubber safety surface, climbing structures, slides, and swings. It is an outdoor park amenity, so use it during daylight park hours and check Raleigh's Parks Status page after severe weather or maintenance closures.

Are there restrooms at Kiwanis Park? +

Plan around the neighborhood center rather than assuming a separate restroom stays open with the outdoor park. The center is open Monday-Friday 9 a.m.-8 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m.-3 p.m., and is closed Sunday. Call 919-996-3135 before a restroom-dependent visit because building access can change for programs, rentals, holidays, or maintenance.

Does Kiwanis Park have basketball courts and fields? +

Yes. Kiwanis has one full outdoor concrete basketball court, a lighted 185-foot baseball field, and two unlighted multipurpose fields. Courts are typically first come when not reserved. Field use and organized practices can require a reservation through Raleigh Athletics, so an empty-looking field may still be scheduled.

Can I reserve the picnic shelter at Kiwanis Park? +

Yes. The shelter holds about 60 people and has 10 tables, but no electricity or water. The City lists it at $15 per hour and requires approval and payment before a reservation is final. Unreserved casual use may be available, but a posted or confirmed reservation has priority.

Does Kiwanis Park have an off-leash dog park? +

Kiwanis has a fenced dog run with benches, wood chips, and a waste-bag dispenser. Unlike Raleigh dog parks that advertise separate small- and large-dog sections, the City does not list size-separated areas or a water source here. Dogs must be leashed outside the run, and toys, food, treats, and outside furniture are prohibited inside Raleigh off-leash areas.

Can I access the Raleigh greenway from Kiwanis Park? +

Yes. Kiwanis Park is a trailhead on Crabtree Creek Trail, one of the major paved corridors in Raleigh's Capital Area Greenway. The citywide system has more than 100 miles across 28 trails and is open dawn to dusk. Check the Greenway Explorer and current alerts before leaving because floods, construction, fallen trees, or maintenance can close an individual segment while the park remains open.

Is Kiwanis Park accessible for wheelchairs and strollers? +

The renovated playground uses a rubber surface, and developed paths connect the main recreation facilities. Conditions and grades can differ at the fields and greenway connection. For a route-specific accommodation, contact Kiwanis Park at 919-996-3135 or Raleigh Parks Inclusion Services before visiting.

What time does Kiwanis Park close? +

Use outdoor park amenities from dawn to dusk. The neighborhood center follows a separate schedule: Monday-Friday 9 a.m.-8 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m.-3 p.m., and closed Sunday. A lit baseball field does not mean the whole park or building remains available at night; reserved activities follow their confirmed schedule.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Is Kiwanis Park free? +

General entry, marked parking, playground use, the basketball court when available, dog run, and greenway access are free. Room rentals, the picnic shelter, organized field use, classes, and private programs can require reservations and fees. Non-Raleigh residents pay a 25 percent surcharge on eligible indoor rental rates.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Where can I find shade at Kiwanis Park? +

The picnic shelter provides reliable open-air shade, and trees provide changing shade around portions of the 24-acre park. The basketball court, baseball and multipurpose fields, playground areas, and greenway approaches can be exposed. The shelter has no water or electricity and is not safe severe-weather protection; during lightning or dangerous wind, use an open substantial building or enclosed vehicle.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

How do you reach Kiwanis Park? +

Use GoRaleigh's current trip planner for 2525 Noble Road. The park is also reachable from Crabtree Creek Greenway, but trail access and the neighborhood-center entrance are on different parts of the property.
