Where should I park for Reedy Creek Trail? +

The clearest official trailhead is the shared NCMA lot at 2110 Blue Ridge Road, where Museum Park parking is free. Raleigh also documents greenway parking at E. Stephen Stroud Way near PNC Arena. If starting from Umstead, use a legal State Park access area and verify gate hours; do not leave a vehicle in an unapproved campus, office, or roadside space.

What is the best entrance to Reedy Creek Trail? +

For a first visit, start at the NC Museum of Art trailhead at 2110 Blue Ridge Road. It provides documented free parking, Museum Park orientation, art, and access to the Blue Loop. Start near Umstead only when the state-park side is your destination and you have confirmed the correct entrance and gate schedule.

Are there restrooms on Reedy Creek Trail? +

NCMA's Welcome Center has restrooms open daily from dawn to dusk, making the museum side the most dependable service point. Restrooms are not continuous along the 5-mile route, and Meredith College or Umstead facilities have their own access and hours. Verify before relying on a facility at the far end.

What hours is Reedy Creek Trail open? +

Raleigh greenways are open dawn to dusk daily. NCMA Museum Park is also open dawn to dusk, but museum buildings, Welcome Center services, Meredith College routes, and Umstead State Park gates follow separate schedules. Plan the return around the earliest facility or gate closing you depend on.

Is Reedy Creek Trail free? +

Yes. The City trail, NCMA Museum Park, and NCMA visitor parking are free. Separately ticketed museum events, programs, rentals, transit, or services at other connected destinations may charge.

Is Reedy Creek Trail wheelchair accessible? +

The route is paved through developed sections and NCMA offers paved, mostly level all-access Eco Trail options near the museum. The complete 5-mile corridor is not uniform: grades, road interfaces, campus transitions, construction, and Umstead approaches vary. Visitors needing a predictable route should start with NCMA's shorter all-access options and verify any extension.

How long is Reedy Creek Trail? +

Raleigh lists Reedy Creek Trail as 5.0 miles. It follows Reedy Creek Road from Umstead State Park to Blue Ridge Road and continues through NCMA grounds and Meredith College. Connected museum loops and adjoining greenways add distance and should not be confused with the official 5-mile route.

Is Reedy Creek Trail a loop? +

No. It is a linear route between the Umstead side, NCMA, and Meredith College. For a simple visit, begin at NCMA, choose a turnaround, and return the same way. A full one-way outing needs a planned pickup or legal parking at both ends and enough time for the applicable gates and trail hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What does Reedy Creek Trail connect to? +

The route connects NCMA and its Blue Loop, Meredith College, Prairie Ridge Ecostation, Schenck Forest, Umstead State Park, Richland Creek Trail, House Creek Trail, and Rocky Branch Trail. It is also part of the East Coast Greenway route.

Can I bike Reedy Creek Trail? +

Yes. Bicycles are allowed on Reedy Creek Trail, which is a shared greenway and East Coast Greenway segment. Raleigh's greenway speed limit is 10 mph. Keep right, yield to pedestrians, and announce passes. Do not continue onto the connected Richland Creek route through Schenck Forest with a bicycle; that route prohibits bikes.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Are dogs allowed on Reedy Creek Trail? +

Dogs are allowed on Raleigh greenways when controlled on a leash no longer than six feet and owners remove waste. The connected Richland Creek Trail through Schenck Forest prohibits dogs, so handlers must turn around or choose a different connection at that boundary.

Can I see NCMA outdoor art from Reedy Creek Trail? +

Yes. Reedy Creek passes through the NCMA Museum Park and connects with the NCMA Blue Loop, providing access to outdoor art, meadows, gardens, and the Welcome Center. The 164-acre museum campus has multiple paths, so use the museum map if a particular artwork is the goal.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

How do I check Reedy Creek Trail closures? +

Check Raleigh Greenway Alerts before leaving and also review NCMA or Umstead notices when your route depends on those properties. Construction, storms, fallen trees, events, campus access, or state-park gate changes can affect a through trip. Never cross a closure or improvise through restricted research or campus land.
