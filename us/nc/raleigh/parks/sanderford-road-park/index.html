What people say

Sanderford Road Park is a 23.5-acre Southeast Raleigh recreation destination with a neighborhood center, outdoor restroom, age 2-12 playground, ten lighted pickleball courts, four-hoop basketball court, two lighted ballfields, a reservable shelter, and community programs.

Where should I park at Sanderford Road Park? +

Navigate to 2623 Sanderford Road and use the signed park and Neighborhood Center lot. The Center, playground, restroom, pickleball and basketball courts, fields, and shelter occupy different parts of the 23.5-acre campus, so follow onsite signs and leave accessible spaces and loading areas clear.

Where is the main entrance to Sanderford Road Park? +

Use the Neighborhood Center at 2623 Sanderford Road as the main staffed entrance during its limited hours. It is the best orientation point for programs, rentals, the game room, courts, fields, shelter, playground, and outdoor restroom. The proposed Creech Road trail should not yet be treated as a dependable entrance.

Are there restrooms at Sanderford Road Park? +

Yes. Raleigh's playground directory lists an outdoor restroom in the park, which is particularly useful because the Neighborhood Center opens at 2 p.m. on weekdays and is closed Sundays. Restrooms may still close for maintenance or winterization, so check onsite status before depending on access.

What hours is Sanderford Road Park open? +

The Neighborhood Center is open 2-8 p.m. Monday-Friday and 11 a.m.-5 p.m. Saturday, and is closed Sunday. Outdoor park areas are generally used dawn to dusk. Pickleball lighting operates on a timer from dusk to 10 p.m., while programs, rentals, fields, and restroom access can follow separate schedules.

Is Sanderford Road Park free? +

General park admission, playground use, outdoor basketball, and available casual pickleball are free. Programs, reserved pickleball time, rooms, shelter reservations, and organized field use may charge. A special event may also require registration even when admission is free.

How accessible is Sanderford Road Park? +

The developed Neighborhood Center, paved parking, outdoor restroom, concrete basketball area, and hard-surface pickleball complex provide firm access. The playground uses woodchips and sand, which can be difficult for wheelchairs and small stroller wheels. Contact staff when a shortest accessible route to a field or shelter is essential.

What is the playground at Sanderford Road Park like? +

The playground serves ages 2-12 and includes tot swings, slides, and climbing equipment. Raleigh's playground directory lists woodchips and sand as the primary surfaces rather than rubberized surfacing. An outdoor restroom is available in the park, subject to maintenance and seasonal access.

How many pickleball courts are at Sanderford Road Park? +

There are ten outdoor pickleball courts with timer-controlled lighting from dusk to 10 p.m. Courts have no practice backboards. Both reservations and casual play are supported, so follow posted rotation rules and yield to confirmed reservations rather than assuming every empty court is first-come.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Are there basketball courts at Sanderford Road Park? +

Yes. The outdoor concrete basketball area has four hoops, allowing more than one half-court game when conditions and attendance permit. It is separate from the ten-court pickleball complex. Scheduled programs, maintenance, and posted rules take priority over informal use.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What ballfields are at Sanderford Road Park? +

The park has a lighted 295-foot softball field and a lighted 200-foot youth baseball field. Approved rentals and league schedules have priority, and wet-weather closures can affect the fields independently of the courts, playground, and Neighborhood Center.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Can I reserve the picnic shelter at Sanderford Road Park? +

Yes. The shelter holds roughly 60-100 people and has nine tables, one grill, no electricity, and a current rate of $20 per hour. A request is not final until staff approves it and payment is confirmed; nonresident and application fees may also apply.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I rent rooms at Sanderford Road Neighborhood Center? +

Yes. Multipurpose Room A holds about 75 and currently costs $65 per hour; Room B holds 30 and costs $40; the warming kitchen is $25. Rentals require a two-hour minimum, $15 application fee, $100 deposit, possible after-hours supervision, and a 25 percent nonresident hourly surcharge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I enter Sanderford Road Park from Creech Road? +

A City project proposes a 10-foot asphalt connection from Creech Road to the park boundary, then an improved natural-surface path to the Neighborhood Center. The project page describes planned construction, not confirmed everyday access. Until completion is announced, use the main Sanderford Road entrance.
