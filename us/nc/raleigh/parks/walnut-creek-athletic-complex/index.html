What people say

Walnut Creek Athletic Complex is a renovated 204-acre regional tournament venue with nine fenced, lighted softball and baseball diamonds arranged in two wheels, covered dugouts and scorekeeping, control-tower restrooms, shaded spectator seating, food-truck plaza, and City adult softball leagues.

Where should I park at Walnut Creek Athletic Complex? +

Navigate to 1201 Sunnybrook Road and follow event signs toward your assigned field wheel. Wheel 1 contains five fields and Wheel 2 contains four, so confirm the field number before choosing a parking area. Tournament traffic and ticketing can change circulation; arrive early and keep emergency and accessible routes clear.

Which entrance should I use at Walnut Creek Athletic Complex? +

Use the signed event entrance from the 1201 Sunnybrook Road complex and follow directions for Wheel 1 or Wheel 2. Each wheel has its own control tower, bathrooms, referee area, and group of fields. A field assignment is more useful than the complex name alone when meeting a team or spectator group.

Are there restrooms at Walnut Creek Athletic Complex? +

Yes. Both Wheel 1 and Wheel 2 have control towers with bathrooms and referee lounges. Restroom access is tied to complex operations and scheduled events rather than a continuously staffed community-center schedule. Confirm the nearest control tower when arriving at your assigned field.

What hours is Walnut Creek Athletic Complex open? +

The City does not publish one dependable daily visitor schedule because access follows leagues, tournaments, rentals, maintenance, and field conditions. Confirm your event time, tournament schedule, or rental approval before traveling and call Raleigh Athletics when attending without a team-provided itinerary.

Is Walnut Creek Athletic Complex free? +

Admission varies. City league spectators may encounter free access, while tournaments can charge admission and teams pay registration or rental costs. Food trucks and concessions are separate purchases. Check the event organizer's information rather than assuming the complex itself sets one universal ticket price.

How accessible is Walnut Creek Athletic Complex? +

The renovated venue has paved entrances and internal walkways, shade-covered spectator bleachers, covered dugouts and scorekeeping areas, and restrooms in each wheel's control tower. Because nine fields span a large site, contact Athletics for the closest accessible parking and seating route to a specific assigned field.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

How many fields are at Walnut Creek Athletic Complex? +

There are nine fenced, halogen-lit diamonds with skinned infields and hybrid-grass outfields. Wheel 1 has five fields and Wheel 2 has four. Adjustable base and pitching distances plus portable mounds support youth and adult softball or baseball configurations, but field setup is controlled by the scheduled event.

How do I check whether Walnut Creek fields are open after rain? +

Use Raleigh's current athletic field-status page or call the Athletic Weather Line at 919-996-6575. Do not enter wet, muddy, frozen, or officially closed fields even when gates appear open; play in those conditions damages the surface and can be treated as a rules violation.

Can teams rent Walnut Creek Athletic Complex for a tournament? +

Yes. The complex is designed for tournament scheduling, but organizers must contact Raleigh Athletics, submit the current rental application, receive approval, and complete payment. Field configuration, lights, staffing, insurance, vendor needs, and schedule are coordinated rather than booked as casual first-come park use.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Can I bring a dog, bicycle, or cooler into Walnut Creek Athletic Complex? +

Pets are prohibited inside the complex, as are bicycles, skates, skateboards, rollerblades, alcohol, open flames, portable generators, and overnight camping. Smoking is prohibited in the plaza and surrounding areas and limited to the parking-lot area. Never leave children unattended.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Are food and concessions available at Walnut Creek Athletic Complex? +

A central food-truck plaza supports concessions during tournaments and City league play, but vendors are event-dependent and not guaranteed every day. Visitors with dietary, medical, or child-feeding needs should verify event rules and bring an allowed backup rather than relying on a particular truck.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
What emergency resources are at Walnut Creek Athletic Complex? +

The complex has basic first-aid supplies and AED equipment. The City notes that emergency medical services are approximately 1.5 miles away. For a serious emergency call 911 immediately and identify the complex, wheel, and field number so responders can find the patient quickly.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I walk to Walnut Creek Athletic Complex from Poole Road? +

The 0.3-mile Birch Ridge Connector is a wide sidewalk linking Poole Road with the complex, also called Walnut Creek North Park in greenway materials. It is a neighborhood pedestrian connection, not the 15.6-mile Walnut Creek Greenway Trail. Check crossings and event access before using it as an arrival route.
