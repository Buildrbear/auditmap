What people say

Carolina Pines Park is a 39-acre South Raleigh recreation hub with a staffed Community Center, new accessible playground, two traditional age-specific play areas, separate dog enclosures, lighted tennis and ballfields, sand volleyball, indoor gym and weight room, shelter, EV charging, and a GoRaleigh park-and-ride.

Where should I park at Carolina Pines Park? +

Use the City lot at 2305 Lake Wheeler Road for the Community Center, playgrounds, dog park, courts, fields, and shelter. The same address functions as a GoRaleigh park-and-ride, so some spaces serve transit riders. Follow marked park, accessible, EV-charging, and park-and-ride signs rather than blocking reserved uses.

Where is the main entrance to Carolina Pines Park? +

Navigate to the Community Center at 2305 Lake Wheeler Road. Starting there gives the clearest access to staff, restrooms, program information, indoor facilities, and orientation toward the playgrounds, dog park, tennis, volleyball, fields, and shelter spread across the 39-acre campus.

Are there restrooms at Carolina Pines Park? +

Yes. The staffed Community Center provides indoor visitor facilities during operating hours. Do not assume the building remains open solely because the lighted tennis courts are available later; handle restroom needs before the Center closes and ask staff about any outdoor event facilities.

What hours is Carolina Pines Park open? +

The Community Center is open 10 a.m.-9 p.m. Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Saturday, and 1-6 p.m. Sunday. Ordinary outdoor park areas are generally used dawn to dusk, while tennis push-button lighting operates from dusk until 10 p.m. Programs and reserved fields can follow separate schedules.

Is Carolina Pines Park free? +

General park admission, parking, playground use, dog-park use, tennis, volleyball, and available casual outdoor use are free. The weight room currently requires a $15 30-day membership. Classes, indoor open play, room rentals, shelter reservations, and field reservations may charge.

Is Carolina Pines Park wheelchair accessible? +

A new accessible playground opened in 2026, adding a more inclusive option beside the older sand-surface play areas. The Community Center, paved parking, indoor gym, restrooms, and hard-surface tennis courts provide other developed routes. Contact staff when an exact field, dog-park, or volleyball approach is essential.

What playgrounds are at Carolina Pines Park? +

The park has a newly renovated accessible playground plus traditional areas for ages 2-5 and 5-12. The two older age-banded playgrounds use sand surfacing, which is less predictable for wheelchairs and small stroller wheels. Choose the new inclusive area when firm access or adaptive play features matter.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

What is Carolina Pines Dog Park like? +

The free public dog park has separate fenced areas for large and small dogs, woodchip surfacing, trees, nighttime lighting, and water from faucets and buckets. Dogs may be off leash only inside the appropriate enclosure. Remain with your dog, remove waste, and bring a bowl if shared buckets are unsuitable.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Are there tennis and volleyball courts at Carolina Pines Park? +

Yes. Three tennis courts have push-button lights from dusk to 10 p.m. and no backboards. Two sand-volleyball courts are unlit and generally first-come, first-served. Scheduled City activities or maintenance take priority, so check court conditions before organizing a larger group.

What ballfields are at Carolina Pines Park? +

The park has two lighted softball fields measuring approximately 293 and 289 feet and one lighted 180-foot youth baseball field. Rentals require City approval and payment confirmation. Scheduled leagues and permitted users have priority over casual use, even when a field appears empty.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

What is in the Carolina Pines weight room? +

The room has two treadmills, a recumbent bike, elliptical, universal machine, and Life Fitness free weights. The City lists a $15 30-day membership. Ask the front desk about current age rules, required orientation, operating access, and whether membership includes any other Center privileges.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I reserve the shelter at Carolina Pines Park? +

Yes. The listed shelter has eight tables, two grills, no electricity, capacity around 36-60, and a current rate of $15 per hour. A request is not final until staff approval and payment; room and shelter rentals also carry minimums, application fees, deposits, and possible nonresident charges.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I take GoRaleigh to Carolina Pines Park? +

Yes. Carolina Pines Park is an official GoRaleigh park-and-ride served by Route 7L Carolina Pines. Verify the current schedule and stop location before traveling, and remember that State Fair shuttles do not use this park-and-ride despite its regular transit function.
