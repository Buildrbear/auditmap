Raleigh Rose Garden

301 Pogue Street, Raleigh, NC
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open dawn to dusk every day, year-round. Theatre buildings, performances, rentals, and special events use separate schedules.

What people say

A free historic City garden behind Raleigh Little Theatre with sixty beds and roughly 1,200 roses, trees, perennials, picnic space, a gazebo, arbor, and outdoor amphitheatre, typically blooming from around Mother's Day until the first hard freeze.

Where should I park for the Raleigh Rose Garden? +

Most garden visitors park in designated spaces along Pogue Street. Only a few spaces are next to the garden, including two for vehicles with disability placards. Do not park along the narrow road between Raleigh Little Theatre and the garden. The theatre lot has weekday permit and short-term markings, so read every sign unless attending an event with different instructions.

How do I find the Raleigh Rose Garden entrance? +

Navigate to Raleigh Little Theatre at 301 Pogue Street; the Rose Garden is behind the theatre rather than directly visible as a conventional roadside park. Follow the campus paths toward the outdoor garden and Stephenson Amphitheatre. The very limited garden-side parking is not a through road or general curb-parking area.

Are there public restrooms at the Raleigh Rose Garden? +

No. Raleigh Little Theatre explicitly states that the garden has no public restrooms. Theatre restrooms are not general park facilities and may be inaccessible when the building is closed or reserved. Plan a restroom stop before arrival, especially with children or during a longer photography visit.

What hours is the Raleigh Rose Garden open? +

The public garden is open dawn to dusk every day of the year. Raleigh Little Theatre buildings, box office, performances, and rentals have separate hours. During a rental or special event, portions of the garden may be restricted even though the wider park remains within normal daylight hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
Raleigh Little Theatre ↗
Is the Raleigh Rose Garden free? +

Yes. Ordinary garden visits are free and do not require a reservation. Tickets may be required for a Raleigh Little Theatre performance or special event, and organized private use may require a rental agreement. Free public access does not permit visitors to enter a reserved area or interrupt a production.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

Is the Raleigh Rose Garden wheelchair accessible? +

Two disability-parking spaces are available close to the garden, but the historic campus includes varied paths, grades, grass, and amphitheatre routes. Raleigh Little Theatre publishes a visual campus access guide showing parking and building approaches. Review it or call the theatre before visiting when a step-free route or specific seating access is essential.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

When are the roses blooming at the Raleigh Rose Garden? +

The usual bloom period begins around Mother's Day and continues until the first hard freeze, normally around mid-November. The City describes the strongest season as late May through autumn. Weather, pruning, pests, heat, and maintenance affect individual beds, while trees and perennials provide interest outside peak rose bloom.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

How can I identify roses in the Raleigh Rose Garden? +

Use the City's mobile-friendly Rose Garden Story Map or Raleigh Little Theatre's bed map and rose list. The collection includes hybrid teas, floribundas, grandifloras, miniatures, and antique roses across sixty beds, plus mapped trees and perennial plantings. Labels and bloom condition can vary as the collection is maintained.

Can I take photos at the Raleigh Rose Garden? +

Casual personal photography is compatible with an ordinary visit, but stay on visitor surfaces, do not block paths or reserved areas, and never cut or pick roses. Portrait sessions involving equipment, clients, staging, or extended use may conflict with rentals or City rules; contact Raleigh Little Theatre before planning a formal shoot.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
Raleigh Little Theatre ↗
Can I picnic at the Raleigh Rose Garden? +

The City lists picnic tables and an open play area, making a small leave-no-trace picnic practical when the space is not reserved. Bring water because no public restroom or guaranteed concession is provided. Keep food and belongings out of planting beds, pack out waste, and yield any section closed for a rental or performance.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I pick roses at the Raleigh Rose Garden? +

No. Visitors must not cut or pick roses. The beds are a maintained public collection supported by City horticulture staff and volunteers, so remain on appropriate routes, avoid stepping into beds, supervise children, and leave flowers and plant labels in place for the next visitor.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
Raleigh Little Theatre ↗
Is the amphitheatre included in a Raleigh Rose Garden visit? +

The Stephenson Amphitheatre is physically set within the garden campus and can be seen during an ordinary public visit when it is not restricted. Performances, rehearsals, rentals, and special events are separately managed by Raleigh Little Theatre and can limit access, require tickets, or change parking rules.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Can I take a bus to the Raleigh Rose Garden? +

Yes, with a walk. Raleigh Little Theatre says GoRaleigh routes 16 and 12 stop near Oberlin Road, while GoRaleigh 12 and 4 and GoTriangle 100 and 105 serve Hillsborough Street; listed stops are about a ten-minute walk from the campus. Verify current routes, sidewalks, and service alerts before leaving.
