John P. "Top" Greene African American Cultural Center

401 Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard, Raleigh, NC
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Tuesday-Saturday 11 a.m.-7 p.m. and Sunday 1-4 p.m.; closed Monday. Programs, rentals, construction, and holidays may alter access.

What people say

A City cultural center and 1.3-acre park preserving and sharing the history of Raleigh's South Park and East Raleigh communities through exhibits, more than 2,000 artifacts, classes, gatherings, local goods, and neighborhood programs, with a major expansion planned.

Where should I park for the Top Greene Cultural Center? +

Use the small onsite lot entered from South East Street; it includes reserved accessible spaces. Additional parking may be available on nearby streets where signs permit. Because programs and rentals can fill the small lot, allow time to read neighborhood restrictions and never block residences, driveways, bus stops, or construction access.

Where is the entrance to Top Greene Cultural Center? +

Navigate to 401 Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard, but enter the small park parking lot from South East Street. Follow onsite signs from the lot to the Cultural Center's public entrance. Construction phasing may alter the approach during the planned expansion, so obey temporary fencing and current staff directions.

Are public restrooms available at Top Greene Cultural Center? +

The existing center hosts public programs and rentals, but its current visitor page does not separately document ordinary drop-in restroom access. Restrooms follow building hours and may be affected by programs or construction. Call 919-996-6580 before visiting if restroom availability or an accessible fixture is essential.

What hours is Top Greene Cultural Center open? +

The current building schedule is Tuesday-Saturday 11 a.m.-7 p.m. and Sunday 1-4 p.m.; Monday is closed. Individual classes, exhibits, rentals, after-hours events, holidays, and the 2026-2028 construction project can use different times or affect access, so check the current City listing before leaving.

Is Top Greene Cultural Center free to visit? +

Ordinary entry to view the public cultural exhibits is free unless a special event states otherwise. Classes may require RecLink registration and a fee. Private use is not free: the banquet room currently has a two-hour minimum, hourly charge, application fee, refundable damage deposit, and possible after-hours supervisor fee.

Is Top Greene Cultural Center wheelchair accessible? +

Yes. The City states that the Cultural Center building is accessible throughout by wheelchair, scooter, or Electric Conveyance Vehicle, and the onsite lot has reserved accessible spaces. Contact staff at 919-996-6580 for a construction-era route check or Raleigh Parks Inclusion Services for a program modification.

What can I see at Top Greene Cultural Center? +

The center presents exhibits about African American culture and the East Raleigh-South Park Historic District. Its Centuries in the Making exhibit covers education, healthcare, businesses, and community contributions. Displays can rotate, so the exact objects and gallery availability may differ from online images.

Does Top Greene Cultural Center have historic artifacts? +

Yes. The City's Top Greene collection preserves more than 2,000 artifacts documenting life in East Raleigh and South Park. Only a selection can be displayed at once; additional records can be explored through the Historic Resources and Museum Program's online collection database.

Who was John P. "Top" Greene? +

John P. "Top" Greene was a community activist and South Park resident for whom the center and surrounding cultural park are named. The City formally adopted the expanded names in 2025 to reflect the site's mission and its relationship to Raleigh's largest historic African American neighborhood.

What programs are offered at Top Greene Cultural Center? +

Programs change throughout the year and include history, culture, art, creativity, learning, and community gatherings. Browse Raleigh RecLink and the City event calendar for current dates, ages, registration, fees, capacity, and accessibility. A normal center-hours listing does not guarantee that a class has walk-in space.

Can I take the bus to Top Greene Cultural Center? +

Yes. The City says GoRaleigh route 19, Martin Luther King/Sunnybrook, serves a stop in front of the center. Check the live route, direction, fare policy, and service alerts before traveling, especially during roadwork or construction around the campus.

Is Top Greene Cultural Center under renovation? +

A major renovation and expansion is scheduled as a 2024-2028 Parks Bond project, with construction planned during 2026-2028. The current City place page still publishes open hours, but access may change as work advances. Check the project and status pages for closures rather than assuming every future feature is already built.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
Does the South Park Heritage Trail start at Top Greene? +

The renovated center is planned as the trailhead for a new roughly two-mile South Park Heritage Trail linking downtown, greenway and park spaces, and the historic neighborhood through African American interpretation. It is a future project, not a reason to assume a completed signed loop exists today; check the project page before planning a trail visit.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

Can I rent a room at Top Greene Cultural Center? +

Yes, subject to staff approval. The current banquet room holds up to 99 people and includes use of a warming kitchen, where food may be warmed but not cooked from scratch. A request is not final until staff approve it and payment is confirmed; deposits, application charges, minimum hours, and after-hours staffing apply.
