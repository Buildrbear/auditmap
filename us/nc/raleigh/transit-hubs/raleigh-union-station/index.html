What people say

Raleigh Union Station is downtown Raleigh's staffed intercity rail station, with Amtrak and NC By Train service, checked baggage, accessible facilities, and a GoRaleigh stop at the entrance. On-site parking is intended for stays of three hours or less; longer trips should use a nearby deck.

Where should I park at Raleigh Union Station? +

Use the metered spaces at the station only for short visits of three hours or less. For a train trip or any stay longer than three hours, the City recommends The Dillon Parking Deck directly across West Street at 223 S. West Street. Other downtown decks are farther away. Parking is paid, and Amtrak does not guarantee space availability.

Where is passenger drop-off and the entrance at Raleigh Union Station? +

Approach from West Street and use the station traffic circle for passenger drop-off in front of the building. The main station entrance is at 510 W. Martin Street. From the drop-off area, an accessible barrier-free route continues through the station to the train platform.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026
NCDOT Raleigh Union Station Arrival Guide ↗
Are there public restrooms at Raleigh Union Station? +

Yes. Amtrak lists restrooms, including accessible restrooms, inside the staffed station. Restroom access follows station waiting-room hours rather than the outdoor plaza schedule, so do not assume the building remains open between all late-night or early-morning trips.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026
Amtrak Raleigh Union Station ↗
Is Raleigh Union Station open and are trains running normally? +

The station is operating as a staffed Amtrak stop, but a static place page cannot confirm a specific train's same-day status. Check Amtrak or NC By Train immediately before leaving for delays, cancellations, platform instructions, or temporary facility impacts. Arrive at least 30 minutes early, or 60 minutes early when checking baggage or needing ticketing or passenger assistance.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026

Station access is free; travel requires a fare

How accessible is Raleigh Union Station? +

Accessible parking, waiting room, ticket office, restrooms, water fountain, and a barrier-free route from passenger drop-off to the high-level platform are available. A station wheelchair can be requested.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 30, 2026
City of Raleigh ↗
How do you reach Raleigh Union Station? +

Amtrak and NC By Train intercity rail serve the station, and GoRaleigh stops in front of the building.
