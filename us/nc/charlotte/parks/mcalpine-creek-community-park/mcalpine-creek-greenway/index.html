What people say

McAlpine Creek Greenway is a distinct mapped destination within McAlpine Creek Community Park; navigate to this point instead of the general park pin and check posted conditions on arrival. Low areas and greenway segments can flood after heavy rain, and wetland insects are active in warm months. Check closures before a long run, carry water, and do not enter standing or moving floodwater.

Where should I park for McAlpine Creek Greenway? +

Free parking is available from Monroe Road in lots serving the lake, dog park and course, with roughly 285 spaces in the county inventory. Race days and peak dog-park periods can fill the closest sections.

Are there restrooms near McAlpine Creek Greenway? +

Developed park facilities include restrooms, but availability can vary by hour, event and maintenance. The longer greenway and wetland routes do not offer continuous restroom or water access.

Is McAlpine Creek Greenway free? +

General park, greenway, dog park, lake and course access are free. A North Carolina fishing license is required where applicable for anglers age 16 and older; organized events may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

How accessible is McAlpine Creek Greenway? +

Developed parking, the fishing pier and principal paved greenway support broader access, but the county inventory does not mark the overall park ADA accessible. Wetland edges, cross-country surfaces, flooding and unpaved connectors vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Mecklenburg County Park and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at McAlpine Creek Greenway? +

Ray's Fetching Meadow is the designated fenced off-leash area. Outside it, dogs must be leashed; follow posted small/large-dog arrangements, remove waste and avoid crowded gates.

Is McAlpine Creek Greenway good for children? +

Wildlife viewing, the fishing lake and paved greenway can work well for families, but this is not primarily a playground park. Closely supervise children near water, fast bicycles, dogs and active race routes.
