Where should I park for Romare Bearden Dining Courtyard? +

The park has no dedicated public lot. Use a legal Uptown street space or paid garage and compare rates before entry; baseball games, football games, concerts and conventions can sharply increase prices and demand.

Ordinary park, lawn, gardens, art and water-feature access are free. Parking, nearby attractions, food and ticketed events cost separately.

How accessible is Romare Bearden Dining Courtyard? +

Paved walks and curb ramps make the compact park broadly accessible. Crowds, temporary event barriers, wet sprayground surfaces and steep approaches from some Uptown blocks can affect the preferred route.

Are dogs allowed at Romare Bearden Dining Courtyard? +

Leashed dogs are allowed in general park areas. Keep them out of active sprayground water, remove waste and use extra control during crowded events.

The seasonal sprayground, chimes, open lawn and trains passing nearby can make a short family stop memorable. Shade is limited in parts of the park and restroom planning is important.
