Where should I park for Captain James Jack Statue? +

There is no single greenway lot. Choose a section first: Midtown uses nearby public or paid parking, Freedom and Park Road parks have free lots, and Tyvola Road has a dedicated southern access. Never assume private retail parking permits trail use.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Mecklenburg County Park and Recreation ↗
When is Captain James Jack Statue open? +

Use the greenway during posted daylight access hours. Construction, flooding, bridge work, events and the schedules of connecting parks can alter section access. This feature may have shorter, seasonal or daylight-only hours.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are there restrooms near Captain James Jack Statue? +

Restrooms are tied to selected connected parks and businesses rather than continuously available along the route. Plan a known open stop and carry water for longer trips.

Is Captain James Jack Statue free? +

Walking, running and bicycling on the public greenway are free. Parking, transit, food, rentals, adjacent attractions and events may charge.

How accessible is Captain James Jack Statue? +

Much of the principal route is paved and suitable for mobility devices, but grades, street crossings, bollards, flooding and construction detours vary by segment. Start from a mapped accessible parking point when that is essential.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Mecklenburg County Park and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Captain James Jack Statue? +

Leashed dogs may use the shared greenway. Keep leashes short around bicycles and runners, yield at narrow points and remove all waste.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Mecklenburg County Park and Recreation ↗
Is Captain James Jack Statue good for children? +

Short sections through Midtown, Freedom Park and Park Road Park combine trail time with play, lawns and restrooms. Children should stay to the right, stop before crossings and remain close beside the creek.

How do you reach Captain James Jack Statue? +
