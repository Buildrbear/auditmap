What people say

Wilson Bay Park is one of Jacksonville's busiest passive waterfront parks, offering Wilson Bay views, fishing access, a boardwalk, gazebo, playground, reservable shelter, picnic tables, grills, restrooms, benches, shade, and shoreline habitat near the New River.

What address or entrance should I use for Wilson Bay Park? +

5 West Bayshore Boulevard, Jacksonville, NC 28540. Parking is limited to the signed on-site lot; the City states that on-street parking is not permitted for shelter use.

Where should I park for Wilson Bay Park? +

Use the small on-site parking lot. Parking is limited, and the shelter rules prohibit on-street overflow parking, so arrive early or choose another waterfront park during peak periods.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Jacksonville ↗
When is Wilson Bay Park open? +

The park is open daily from 7 am to 9 pm. Shelter reservations are limited to four hours, and future seawall, boardwalk, or gazebo work may affect individual waterfront features.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Is Wilson Bay Park free? +

General park, playground, waterfront, and fishing access is free. The shelter is listed at $15 per hour with a maximum reservation occupancy of 30; fishing license requirements still apply.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

Are there restrooms at Wilson Bay Park? +

Restrooms are available near the shelter and playground. Hours and availability can be affected by maintenance or construction.

What accessibility information should I know about Wilson Bay Park? +

The City ADA assessment documented an accessible parking space, compliant approach, curb ramp, and shelter entrance. Boardwalk conditions, sand playground surfacing, shoreline work, and individual fishing positions may vary.

What are the paths or trails like at Wilson Bay Park? +

This is a compact waterfront park with a boardwalk rather than a long trail system. Use the shoreline route for bay views and fishing, but do not expect a continuous loop around Wilson Bay.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Jacksonville ↗
What can families do at Wilson Bay Park? +

The playground, shaded shelter, picnic tables, restrooms, bay views, and fishing make this a useful short family stop. Supervise children around the shoreline and boardwalk.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Jacksonville ↗
Can I bring a dog to Wilson Bay Park? +

Pets must remain on a leash no longer than six feet. Remove waste and keep dogs away from playground equipment, shelter food areas, fishing lines, and posted construction zones.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Jacksonville ↗
Can I picnic at Wilson Bay Park? +

The waterfront shelter includes picnic tables and serves groups up to 30 by reservation. Grills are available; extinguish coals fully, and note that amplified music is not allowed.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Jacksonville ↗
What landmarks or areas should I look for at Wilson Bay Park? +

Look for the waterfront shelter, playground, restrooms, Wilson Bay boardwalk, gazebo, fishing edge, benches, shade trees, and the Route 17/Buddy Phillips Bridge view.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Jacksonville ↗
What closures or conditions can affect Wilson Bay Park? +

A City capital project plans replacement of the aging seawall, boardwalk, and gazebo, with design and later construction capable of affecting waterfront access. Check City notices before a boardwalk- or fishing-dependent visit.
