What people say

Riverwalk Crossing Park is Jacksonville's landscaped downtown New River event park, with a public stage, waterfront paths, garden areas, vendor-ready streets, river views, and connections toward nearby waterfront parks, a gazebo, and Riverwalk Marina.

What address or entrance should I use for Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

421 Court Street, Jacksonville, NC 28540. The downtown waterfront route continues toward L.P. Willingham Park, Kerr Street Park, and Riverwalk Marina at 15 Kerr Street.

Where should I park for Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

Use legal downtown public parking near Court Street and walk into the park. Event days can close streets or repurpose nearby spaces; vehicles are not allowed on the grass or paved park areas.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026
City of Jacksonville ↗
When is Riverwalk Crossing Park open? +

The City lists Riverwalk Crossing Park as open daily from 7 am to 9 pm. Scheduled festivals, stage events, setup, weather, waterfront conditions, and neighboring marina operations can affect access.

Is Riverwalk Crossing Park free? +

General park and waterfront-walk access is free. Festivals may have vendors or paid activities, and stage, marina, shelter, or organized uses may require reservations and fees.

Are there restrooms at Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

Do not assume a restroom is immediately beside every section of the Riverwalk. Facilities may be available at nearby downtown recreation or marina nodes during their operating hours; plan ahead for large events.

What accessibility information should I know about Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

The core park uses paved downtown and waterfront approaches. Event barriers, lawn areas, curb transitions, marina grades, and temporary vendor layouts can change the easiest step-free route.

What are the paths or trails like at Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

Landscaped walkways lead toward the New River and connect with the broader downtown waterfront network. Treat this as an urban waterfront walk rather than a long natural trail.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 3, 2026

What can families do at Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

The river view, open lawn, gardens, gazebo connection, and seasonal festivals work well for a short family visit. There is no conventional playground listed at this park; supervise children closely at the water's edge.

Can I bring a dog to Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

Leashed pets are allowed where posted, with a maximum six-foot leash. Remove waste and avoid event crowds, stage areas, landscaped beds, and waterfront edges when conditions are unsafe.

Can I picnic at Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

Use designated seating and nearby waterfront gathering areas without blocking paths or event setup. Organized use of the stage or park requires City coordination.

What landmarks or areas should I look for at Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

Orientation points include the Court Street entrance, public stage, long garden area, New River waterfront, downstream gazebo, nearby L.P. Willingham Park, Kerr Street Park, and Riverwalk Marina.

What closures or conditions can affect Riverwalk Crossing Park? +

Oktoberfest, Winterfest, National Night Out, other festivals, stage reservations, storm conditions, flood or waterfront work, and event setup may limit normal access or parking.
