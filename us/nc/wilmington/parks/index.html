Gary Shell Cross-City Trail

Wilmington

For a central developed trail stop use Halyburton Park at 4099 South 17th Street or Empie Park at 3405 Park Avenue. The 15-mile route runs from Wade Park to the Heide-Trask Drawbridge, so choose access by trip segment.

Wilmington
