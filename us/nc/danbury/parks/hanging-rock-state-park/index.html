Hanging Rock State Park

1790 Hanging Rock Park Road, Danbury, NC
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The main gate opens at 7:00 a.m. and closes between 7:00 and 10:00 p.m. by month. The visitor center is open daily 9:00 a.m.-4:45 p.m. Seasonal swimming and boat rentals operate Thursday-Sunday between Memorial Day and Labor Day, subject to staffing.

What people say

Hanging Rock State Park is a mountain park north of Winston-Salem with 48 miles of hiking trails, 15 miles of mountain-bike trails, six miles of bridle trails, quartzite overlooks, waterfalls, a cave, seasonal lake swimming and boat rentals, camping, cabins, picnicking, climbing, fishing, and several separate trailhead parking areas.

What address should I use for Hanging Rock State Park? +

Use 1790 Hanging Rock Park Road for the main gate. For the signature Hanging Rock Trail, route inside the park to the visitor-center lot at 1005 Visitor Center Drive. The lake bathhouse and Lower Cascades use different parking, so choose the exact destination first.

Where should I park for the Hanging Rock overlook trail? +

Park at the visitor center, 1005 Visitor Center Drive, where the 1.8-mile one-way Hanging Rock Trail begins. Use the lake bathhouse lot for swimming and boating, and the separate Lower Cascades lot for that waterfall. Popular lots may fill on fall and fair-weather weekends.

What time does Hanging Rock State Park close? +

The main gate opens at 7 a.m. and closes at 7 p.m. January and December, 8 p.m. February and November, 9 p.m. March-April and October, and 10 p.m. May-September. Campers and cabin guests must arrive before the gate closes.

How difficult is the Hanging Rock Trail? +

The signature trail is 1.8 miles each way and rated moderate. It begins paved, then changes to gravel, dirt, rock, and wood and stone stairs while climbing to the overlook. Allow about an hour to reach the top and stay back from unstable cliff edges.

Where are restrooms at Hanging Rock State Park? +

Restrooms are available at major developed areas including the visitor center and, in season, the lake bathhouse and campgrounds. Remote waterfall and trail routes may not have facilities, so use the park map before leaving a developed area.

Does it cost to swim at Hanging Rock lake? +

Yes. In 2026, seasonal swim passes are $6 for adults and $4 for children ages 3-12. Swimming operates Thursday-Sunday, generally 10 a.m.-5:30 p.m. between Memorial Day and Labor Day, subject to staffing; free swim is listed from 5-5:30 p.m.

Are dogs allowed at Hanging Rock State Park? +

Yes. Pets must remain attended on a leash no longer than 6 feet. They are not allowed on the swim beach, inside buildings, or in vacation cabins; hikers with pets must yield to other trail users.

Are Hanging Rock trails and swimming open today? +

Trail categories and the swim lake can open or close independently. Check the live status at the top of the official park page: swimming and boats are seasonal Thursday-Sunday and may vary with staffing, while storms, ice, wildfire risk, or maintenance can close trails or facilities.

Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Hanging Rock State Park? +

The visitor center is the most dependable staffed indoor stop during its posted daily hours. Picnic shelters and the lake-area shelter provide roof cover, and forested trails offer shade, but overlooks, exposed rock, waterfalls, and ridgelines are unsafe during lightning or high wind. A picnic shelter is not a substitute for a substantial building or vehicle in a severe storm.

Is there a playground at Hanging Rock State Park? +

NC State Parks does not list a conventional playground among Hanging Rock's amenities. The family-focused alternatives are the visitor-center exhibits, seasonal swim lake, picnicking, ranger programs, and the 0.3-mile Kids in Parks TRACK Trail combining Upper Cascades and the paved Rock Garden Trail. The TRACK route is self-guided, but it crosses Hanging Rock Park Road and only the Rock Garden portion is listed as wheelchair accessible, so adults should supervise the road crossing and choose trails by ability.

Can I reserve a picnic shelter at Hanging Rock? +

Yes. Picnic shelters near the main lake and day-use area can be reserved through ReserveAmerica, and an unreserved shelter is free first come, first served. Accessible picnic tables and a paved connection are available near the lake bathhouse. Confirm the shelter's table count and hours before booking; weddings, races, festivals, and other organized activities may also require a special-activity permit.

When can visitors swim at Hanging Rock State Park? +

Hanging Rock has a staffed seasonal lake beach rather than a splash pad. For 2026, swimming is scheduled Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day, Thursday-Sunday from 10 a.m.-5:30 p.m., plus the two holiday Mondays; staffing can shorten operations. Daily swim admission is $6 for visitors 13 and older and $4 for ages 3-12, with free swimming from 5-5:30 p.m. Use only the designated swim area, and never enter waterfall pools or climb wet rocks.

Can I take public transit to Hanging Rock State Park? +

No practical fixed-route transit serves the park's visitor center, lake, campground, or trailheads. Hanging Rock is a rural mountain park outside Danbury, and its destinations are separated by steep roads and trails. Arrange a car, taxi, rideshare, shuttle, bicycle approach, or pickup before leaving, and do not plan to walk from a distant highway stop after hiking. Confirm the return trip because cellular service can be limited.

Which landmarks and waterfalls should I visit at Hanging Rock State Park? +

The namesake Hanging Rock overlook begins at the visitor-center lot and requires a moderate 1.8-mile one-way climb. From the same main access, short trails reach Upper Cascades, Hidden Falls, and Window Falls; Moore's Wall Loop reaches the park's highest observation tower from the lake area. Lower Cascades, Tory's Den and Falls, Dan River access, climbing access, and mountain-bike access use separate parking areas. Never climb around waterfall edges or leave marked overlooks, and choose only the destinations that fit your time and ability.

Accessibility varies by entrance, route, terrain, and destination. Review the exact mapped subsite and its official source before relying on a specific accessible route.
