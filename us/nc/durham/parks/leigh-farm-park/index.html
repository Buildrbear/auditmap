What people say

Leigh Farm Park is an 82.8-acre natural and historic Durham property with a seven-acre National Register core, 1835 Leigh House, historic outbuildings, visitor center, wooded trails, picnic tables, restrooms, and an 18-hole disc-golf course.

Where should I park for Leigh Farm Park? +

Use the entrance at 370 Leigh Farm Road. Parking is intentionally limited to protect the historic setting, and visitors should expect a walk from the entrance lot.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

When is Leigh Farm Park open? +

Park gates are open daily year-round from 8 a.m. to dark. Public tours are normally offered Saturdays from April through mid-December, 10 a.m.-2 p.m., subject to staffing and events.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

Is Leigh Farm Park free? +

General park, trail, historic-ground, picnic, and disc-golf access are free. Camps, programs, private tours, tournaments, and reservations may charge or require registration.

Are there restrooms at Leigh Farm Park? +

Restrooms and a water fountain are listed park features near developed visitor areas. Do not assume facilities are available deep within wooded acreage.

What accessibility information should I know about Leigh Farm Park? +

Limited parking and required walking are important constraints. Historic buildings, older surfaces, wooded trails, and the disc-golf course vary; contact DPR before relying on a specific step-free tour.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

What are the paths or trails like at Leigh Farm Park? +

Wooded park routes connect the historic core and broader property, while the 18-hole disc-golf course crosses recreation terrain. Expect natural surfaces beyond developed visitor areas.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
Durham Parks and Recreation ↗
What can families do at Leigh Farm Park? +

Families can explore historic grounds, scheduled tours, picnic tables, wooded areas, wildlife programs, and disc golf. Interpret the plantation and enslaved-person history thoughtfully with children.

Can I bring a dog to Leigh Farm Park? +

Keep dogs leashed and away from historic buildings, wildlife programs, camps, and active disc-golf play. Follow posted park and partner-program rules.

Can I picnic at Leigh Farm Park? +

Picnic tables support casual visits. Contact DPR for groups, programs, commercial activity, exclusive setups, or gatherings that could affect the historic site.

What landmarks or areas should I look for at Leigh Farm Park? +

The historic core includes Leigh House, dairy, enslaved peoples cabins, smokehouse, corn crib, well house, carriage house, cemetery, tobacco barn, and pack house.

What closures or conditions can affect Leigh Farm Park? +

Tours, visitor-center access, partner programs, historic interiors, disc-golf events, weather, and preservation work can change independently. Verify a building-specific visit before traveling.
