Bolin Creek Trail

Chapel Hill

Primary accessible arrival: Chapel Hill Community Center Park, 120 South Estes Drive, Chapel Hill, NC 27514. Additional parking is available at Umstead Park, 399 Umstead Drive, and along Bolinwood Drive where legal.

Cedar Falls Park

Chapel Hill

501 Weaver Dairy Road, Chapel Hill, NC 27514, across from East Chapel Hill High School. Use the main park lot for fields, playground, shelter, restrooms, tennis area, and Cedar Falls Trail.

Homestead Park

Chapel Hill

100 Aquatic Drive, Chapel Hill, NC 27516 for the outdoor park. Homestead Aquatic Center is a separate building at 300 Aquatic Drive with its own hours, admission, and pool schedule.
