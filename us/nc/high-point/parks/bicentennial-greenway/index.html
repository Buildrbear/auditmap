What people say

The 6.2-mile High Point section of Bicentennial Greenway runs from Piedmont Environmental Center north through Jamestown and Gibson Park to Gallimore Dairy Road, connecting with High Point Greenway for roughly 11.1 continuous miles and toward Greensboro's regional trail network.

What address or entrance should I use for Bicentennial Greenway? +

For the High Point end use Piedmont Environmental Center at 1220 Penny Road. Other official accesses include Jamestown Park at 7041 East Fork Road, Gibson Park at 5207 West Wendover Avenue, 4125 Piedmont Parkway, and Gallimore Dairy Road.

Where should I park for Bicentennial Greenway? +

Use PEC, Jamestown Park, Gibson Park, Piedmont Parkway, or Gallimore Dairy Road access for the segment you plan. Confirm legal parking and a return strategy for a long one-way trip.

When is Bicentennial Greenway open? +

Use the greenway during daylight and posted trailhead-park hours. Construction, road crossings, flooding, storms, maintenance, and events can alter access.

Is Bicentennial Greenway free? +

General greenway access is free. Programs, connected attractions, events, and parking restrictions may have separate costs.

Are there restrooms at Bicentennial Greenway? +

Restrooms are available at PEC from 9 a.m.-5 p.m. and at some connected parks during their operating conditions. Plan as though long intermediate stretches have no facilities.

What accessibility information should I know about Bicentennial Greenway? +

The shared-use route is developed, but steeper sections northeast of PEC include stairs with a bicycle wheel channel, and crossings, grades, surfaces, and trailhead routes vary. It is not uniformly step-free end to end.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of High Point ↗
What are the paths or trails like at Bicentennial Greenway? +

The High Point section is 6.2 miles: 1.1 miles PEC to East Fork Road, 2.2 to Gibson Park, 1.8 to Piedmont Parkway, and 1.1 to Gallimore Dairy Road.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of High Point ↗
What can families do at Bicentennial Greenway? +

Choose a short park-to-park out-and-back rather than the full 6.2 miles with children. Account for grades, stairs, road crossings, water, and faster bicycle traffic.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of High Point ↗
Can I bring a dog to Bicentennial Greenway? +

Leashed dogs may use the greenway where posted rules permit. Keep leashes short around bicycles and crossings, remove waste, and follow rules at each connected park.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026
City of High Point ↗
Can I picnic at Bicentennial Greenway? +

Use designated areas at PEC, Jamestown Park, Gibson Park, or other connected parks rather than stopping across the path. Reserve facilities for organized groups.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

What landmarks or areas should I look for at Bicentennial Greenway? +

Connections include PEC, High Point Greenway, Jamestown Park, East Fork Road bridge area, Gibson Park, Piedmont Parkway, Gallimore Dairy Road, and the broader Greensboro trail system.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 4, 2026

What closures or conditions can affect Bicentennial Greenway? +

Steep and stair sections, construction, road projects, flooding, storms, ice, events, and jurisdiction-specific maintenance can affect the route. Check both City and County notices before a through-trip.
