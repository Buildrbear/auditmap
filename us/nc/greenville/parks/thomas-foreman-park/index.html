Where is Thomas Foreman Park? +

Thomas Foreman Park is mapped at 400 Nash Street in Greenville, North Carolina. Use the map pin for the park itself rather than a similarly named municipal office or neighborhood.

What hours is Thomas Foreman Park open? +

Outdoor areas follow posted park hours; the recreation center, pool, programs, and reservations use separate and seasonal schedules. Check the official park page for holiday, weather, seasonal, or maintenance changes.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

Where should I park for Thomas Foreman Park? +

Navigate to 400 Nash Street and use designated facility parking. Pool and program schedules can make the shared lot busier; vehicles may not be driven to shelters without written permission.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026
Public map source ↗
What can I do at Thomas Foreman Park? +

Public records currently identify 25-seat picnic shelter, 100-seat picnic shelter, Eppes Recreation Center, Gymnasium, Seasonal swimming pool, Community programs. Check the official page for current availability.

What accessibility information is available for Thomas Foreman Park? +

Contact Eppes Recreation Center for the best current accessible parking and route to the pool, gym, shelter, or outdoor space. Confirm route-specific needs with the operator because surfaces and closures can change.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Thomas Foreman Park? +

Keep dogs leashed in permitted outdoor areas, remove waste, and keep pets outside the pool, buildings, playgrounds, and active recreation areas.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 5, 2026

How much does Thomas Foreman Park cost? +

General outdoor access is free. Pool admission, programs, building use, and shelter reservations may have fees.
