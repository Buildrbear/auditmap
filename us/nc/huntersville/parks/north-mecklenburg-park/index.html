What people say

North Mecklenburg Park is a large county-owned, Town-operated community sports park with baseball and softball fields, soccer or multipurpose space, tennis, basketball, playgrounds, picnic facilities, trails, and a wooded disc-golf course.

What address or entrance should I use for North Mecklenburg Park? +

16131 Old Statesville Road, Huntersville, NC 28078. The park entrance is along NC 115/Old Statesville Road; follow internal signs to the correct fields, courts, playground, or disc-golf area.

Where should I park for North Mecklenburg Park? +

Multiple activity areas are spread through the park. Follow internal signs and park near your specific field, court, playground, or disc-golf starting area rather than assuming one central lot serves everything equally.

When is North Mecklenburg Park open? +

Use posted Town park and facility hours; lighted courts and scheduled fields can operate differently from passive areas. Confirm current closing time and field status before an evening or league-dependent visit.

Is North Mecklenburg Park free? +

General park, playground, trail, and casual recreation access is free. Reserved fields, courts, leagues, tournaments, shelters, and organized uses may require Town approval or fees.

What accessibility information should I know about North Mecklenburg Park? +

Developed parking and paved approaches serve major sports and play areas. Field edges, older playground routes, wooded trails, and disc-golf terrain vary in grade and surface.

What are the paths or trails like at North Mecklenburg Park? +

The park includes walking connections and a disc-golf course through wooded terrain. Stay clear of active fairways, watch for flying discs, and use a current course or park map.

What can families do at North Mecklenburg Park? +

Playgrounds, ball fields, courts, open space, picnic areas, and short walks support mixed-age visits. Tournament activity can make the park busy, so check schedules when seeking casual play.

Can I bring a dog to North Mecklenburg Park? +

Keep dogs leashed where permitted, remove waste, and stay outside playgrounds, active fields, courts, buildings, and disc-golf throwing lanes unless signs say otherwise.

Can I picnic at North Mecklenburg Park? +

Use designated tables and shelters and reserve group facilities for guaranteed use. Keep grills and gatherings away from fields, trails, and play surfaces.

What landmarks or areas should I look for at North Mecklenburg Park? +

Key wayfinding points are the Old Statesville Road entrance, baseball and softball complexes, multipurpose fields, tennis and basketball courts, playgrounds, picnic facilities, wooded trail areas, and disc-golf course.

What closures or conditions can affect North Mecklenburg Park? +
