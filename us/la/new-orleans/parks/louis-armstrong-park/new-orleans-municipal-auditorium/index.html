What people say

The Municipal Auditorium is a major historic building within the landscape but should not be assumed open; follow current redevelopment and closure notices. Congo Square is a living cultural site, not simply an event lawn. Respect ceremonies and performances; verify gates and events, and do not assume the Municipal Auditorium or Perseverance Hall is open.
