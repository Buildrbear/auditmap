What people say

The 2026 Governor Nicholls expansion adds a free Louisiana marsh-themed playground, shaded seating, cooling misters, lawns, and direct St. Philip Street access. The riverfront changed substantially in April 2026. Use St. Philip Street for the new playground phase, watch children beside the working river, and check storms, heat, events, and ferry conditions.
