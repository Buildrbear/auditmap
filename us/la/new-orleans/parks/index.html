Park directory

Current city pages

Audubon Park

New Orleans

Public outdoor areas generally open daily around 5:00 a.m. and close at 10:00 p.m.; Audubon Zoo, golf, shelters, and events use separate hours.

Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge

New Orleans

Open daily during daylight hours only and closed at night. Seasonal hunting restrictions delay entry outside the Hurricane Protection Levee until noon during teal season and November 1-January 31.

Crescent Park

New Orleans

Crescent Park is generally open daily 6:00 a.m.-7:30 p.m.; gates, events, maintenance, storms, and river conditions may alter access, so confirm posted hours.

Goldring Woldenberg Riverfront Park

New Orleans

Open Sunday-Thursday 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. and Friday-Saturday 6:00 a.m.-midnight; Aquarium, ferry, events, food, and individual facilities keep separate schedules.

Jackson Square

New Orleans

Open daily 8:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. in winter and 8:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. during daylight saving time; ceremonies, maintenance, filming, and permitted events may alter access.

Lafitte Greenway

New Orleans

The public greenway is intended for daily transportation and recreation; developed facilities, playgrounds, courts, lighting, programs, and adjacent businesses use separate schedules.

Louis Armstrong Park

New Orleans

Open daily 8:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. in winter and 8:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. during daylight saving time; events may change gates and access.

New Orleans City Park

New Orleans

Park grounds are open sunrise to sunset every day; museums, gardens, amusement areas, food, rentals, sports facilities, and events keep separate schedules.
