Park

Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge

17160 Chef Menteur Highway, New Orleans, LA 70129
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open daily during daylight hours only and closed at night. Seasonal hunting restrictions delay entry outside the Hurricane Protection Levee until noon during teal season and November 1-January 31.
Navigate

What people say

A 27,190-acre urban national wildlife refuge of marshes, lagoons, forests, bayous, levees, fishing, paddling, birding, photography, and self-guided nature access in eastern New Orleans.

When is Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge open? +

Open daily during daylight hours only and closed at night. Seasonal hunting restrictions delay entry outside the Hurricane Protection Levee until noon during teal season and November 1-January 31.

Where should I park for Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge? +

Use designated parking only at the selected trail, launch, or access point. Do not rely on the general refuge address to reach every public-use area.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service ↗
What is the best entrance for Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge? +

Choose Ridge Trail, Highway 11 launch, Irish Bayou, Joe Madere Marsh, or another official access before driving; mobile service and navigation can be unreliable.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are there restrooms at Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge? +

Ridge Trail normally has restrooms and a picnic pavilion, but the Ridge Trail and boardwalk have been closed for reconstruction since March 2, 2026. Other remote access points may have no facilities.

U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service ↗
Is Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge free? +

General wildlife observation and approved public access are free. Louisiana licenses, permits, equipment, tours, and some consumptive uses may require fees or documentation.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service ↗
How accessible is Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge? +

The Ridge Trail boardwalk was the primary accessible developed route but is closed for reconstruction. Levees, launches, marsh edges, and remote terrain vary greatly.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service ↗
Are dogs allowed at Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge? +

Leashed dogs are allowed under refuge rules except during authorized hunting use; wildlife, alligators, heat, water, and rough access create serious pet risks.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service ↗
Is Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge good for children? +

Wildlife viewing and short developed routes can be memorable, but this is remote habitat, not a playground. Bring water, sun and insect protection, and maintain direct child supervision.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service ↗
How do I reach Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge without a car? +

A private vehicle is the practical way to reach most public access points. Confirm the exact route and tell someone your plan before entering remote areas.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service ↗
What should I know before visiting Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge? +

The Ridge Trail and Boardwalk remain closed for reconstruction as of this review. Never feed wildlife, expect alligators and insects, carry no expectation of shade or services, and obey seasonal closures and gate signs.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Bayou Sauvage Urban National Wildlife Refuge? +

Check New Orleans' hourly heat index, lightning, severe-storm and heavy-rain alerts, flash-flood risk, tropical weather, air quality, and current facility notices. Pavement, play equipment, levees, and exposed riverfront paths become dangerously hot, while low trails and streets may flood quickly.
