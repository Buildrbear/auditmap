Lake Mead National Recreation Area

Boulder City

The recreation area is open 24 hours daily. The Lake Mead Visitor Center is open 9:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m.; Boulder Beach vehicle access is generally 6:00 a.m. to dusk, and individual marinas, roads and campgrounds vary.
