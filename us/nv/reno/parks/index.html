Idlewild Park

Reno

Use 2055 Idlewild Drive for the central park office and general destination, or 75 Cowan Drive for the California Building and nearby rose garden. Parking is distributed along Idlewild Drive, Cowan Drive, Spoon Drive, and adjacent lots.

Rancho San Rafael Regional Park

Reno

Use 1595 North Sierra Street for the south park and one arboretum entrance. The Washington Street side is convenient for the Wilbur D. May Museum lot and the arboretum's second entrance. Trail users should choose the trailhead shown on the county map.
