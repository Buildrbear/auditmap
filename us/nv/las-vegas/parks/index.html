Current city pages

Clark County Wetlands Park

Las Vegas

Trails, trailhead parking and trailhead restrooms are open daily dawn to dusk. The Nature Center is open Tuesday-Sunday from 9:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m.

Floyd Lamb Park at Tule Springs

Las Vegas

Open 8:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m. April-September and 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. October-March. Special events and free-entry holidays can alter normal operations.

Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area

Las Vegas

The Scenic Drive is open 6:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m. April through September, 6:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m. in March and October, and 6:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. November through February. Gates close at the posted time.

Springs Preserve

Las Vegas

Open Thursday-Monday from 9:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m., with last entry at 3:00 p.m. Closed Tuesday and Wednesday, Thanksgiving and Christmas; outdoor areas may close early for extreme heat.

Sunset Park

Las Vegas

Open daily from 6:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m. Splash-pad, field, reservation and event operations may use shorter or seasonal schedules.

Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument

Las Vegas

Open during daylight only year-round. Alamo Road and other limited vehicle routes follow separate closure rules; do not remain after dark.
