Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open 8:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m. April-September and 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. October-March. Special events and free-entry holidays can alter normal operations. What people say

Floyd Lamb Pump Track is a distinct mapped destination within Floyd Lamb Park at Tule Springs; navigate to this point instead of the general park pin and check posted conditions on arrival. The entrance kiosk no longer accepts cash, and the park is distinct from the adjacent national monument and state fossil park. Do not feed peacocks or other wildlife, even when they approach picnic areas.

Where should I park for Floyd Lamb Pump Track? +

Enter at the Tule Springs Road gatehouse and pay by debit or credit card. Parking is distributed near the ranch, lakes and picnic areas; special events may redirect vehicles.

Are there restrooms near Floyd Lamb Pump Track? +

Is Floyd Lamb Pump Track free? +

Vehicle entry is $6 and the gatehouse is cashless. Annual household passes are available; fishing requires a Nevada license except on specifically announced free-fishing days.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Las Vegas Parks and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Floyd Lamb Pump Track? +

Developed ranch, lake and picnic areas have paved or firm approaches, while horse and bike routes use natural surfaces. Confirm the nearest accessible parking for an event.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Las Vegas Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Floyd Lamb Pump Track? +

Leashed dogs are allowed under posted City rules. Keep pets away from peacocks, waterfowl, horses and other wildlife, and never allow chasing or feeding.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Is Floyd Lamb Pump Track good for children? +

Lakes, historic ranch buildings, peacocks and shaded lawns make this a calmer family destination than exposed desert trails. Children still need supervision beside open water and animals.

How do you reach Floyd Lamb Pump Track? +

The park is difficult to reach efficiently by transit. Driving or a prearranged ride is usually necessary; confirm return service before a rideshare drop-off.
