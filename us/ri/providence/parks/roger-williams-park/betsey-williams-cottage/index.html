Betsey Williams Cottage

Providence, RI
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Roger Williams Park is open daily from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. The zoo, botanical center, museum, carousel, Gateway Center, restrooms, food, programs, and events use separate schedules.
Navigate

What people say

Betsey Williams Cottage is a restored 1773 house tied directly to the park's creation: Betsey Williams bequeathed the cottage and surrounding land to Providence in 1871. Look for the small red cottage and large sycamore near the Elmwood Avenue side of the park, connected toward the Casino by Lovers Retreat Bridge. The exterior and grounds can be viewed during park hours, but the cottage is not a daily walk-in museum. Interior access is offered through announced open houses and special programs, often with the Museum of Natural History. Check the park events calendar before making a trip specifically to go inside; do not assume an unlocked building or onsite visitor desk.
