What people say

The Roger Williams Park Casino is the historic 1896 Colonial Revival event building beside the lake and Victorian Rose Garden. Casino here means a social gathering house, not gambling. Route to the exact building near the Elmwood Avenue side, and do not rely on it for an open restroom, shelter, or interior visit without a scheduled event.

Is Roger Williams Park Casino useful for a family visit? +

The Roger Williams Park Casino is the historic 1896 Colonial Revival event building beside the lake and Victorian Rose Garden. Roger Williams Park has two public playground destinations. Hasbro Boundless Playground is fully enclosed at Carousel Village and includes a toddler area, swings, slides, drums, sensory-rich panels, climbing structures, and routes designed so children with physical disabilities can use at least 70% of activities without leaving support equipment. Bear Playground on Maple Avenue has a climbable bear, graduated challenges, an accessible transfer platform and ramp, slide, rope play, mature-tree shade, and picnic space. Carousel Village facilities and restrooms follow their own operating hours.
