Schenley Pool

What people say

Schenley Pool is Pittsburgh's seasonal outdoor municipal swimming pool at the bottom of Overlook Drive, with changing rooms, showers, and restrooms. Use Overlook Drive in Schenley Park; pedestrians approaching from Oakland can use the tunnel from Anderson Playground beneath Panther Hollow Road. The pool operates only during the City summer season and requires a daily admission or pool tag. Hours and weather closures can change, and Schenley cannot host lap swim because of its size and capacity. The pool building is listed as ADA compliant. Check the same-day City pool page before leaving, especially during storms, heat advisories, or late-season schedule reductions.

Where should I park at Schenley Pool? +

Lots and legal on-street spaces are distributed near Phipps, the visitor center, Schenley Oval, golf, pool, and other facilities. Park near the exact destination and avoid road shoulders without legal spaces; university events and major park events can fill Oakland parking.

Where are restrooms at Schenley Pool? +

Restrooms are associated with staffed or seasonal facilities such as the visitor center, pool, skating rink, sports complex, and Phipps. Their schedules differ, so confirm the facility nearest your route rather than assuming a trailhead restroom is open.

How much does Schenley Pool cost? +

General park, trails, overlooks, playgrounds, and picnic access is free. Phipps Conservatory, golf, pool, skating, rentals, reservations, and events may charge separately.
