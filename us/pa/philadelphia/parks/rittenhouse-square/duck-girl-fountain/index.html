Where should I park for Duck Girl fountain? +

There is no park lot. Use transit, walk, bike, or choose a paid Center City garage before arriving; legal curb spaces are scarce and loading or event restrictions change.

General park, lawn, seating, fountain-view, and public-art access is free. Market purchases, nearby food, parking, and special programs cost separately.

Primary paved paths are generally accessible, but historic paving, crowded entrances, lawn grades, wet leaves, snow, ice, markets, and event setups can narrow routes.
