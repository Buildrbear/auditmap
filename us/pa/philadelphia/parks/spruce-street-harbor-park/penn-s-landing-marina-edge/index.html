Penn's Landing marina edge

301 S. Christopher Columbus Boulevard, Philadelphia, PA 19106
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The park is seasonal and keeps a live operating calendar; hours, concessions, bar service, games, and events differ by day and weather. At least one concession operates during published park hours.
Navigate

What people say

The marina-facing edge provides boat and river views beside working docks; public access, charters, and dock gates vary, and the water edge needs close supervision. Confirm that the seasonal park is open, then check rain, heat, storms, river wind, event access, vendors, and hammock availability. Do not assume every amenity runs whenever the promenade is accessible.
