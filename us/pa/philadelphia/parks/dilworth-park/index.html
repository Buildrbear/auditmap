Dilworth Park

Center City, Philadelphia, PA
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Dilworth Park is generally open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 1:00 a.m.; individual fountains, rinks, cafés, markets, and events use separate hours.

What people say

Dilworth Park is Philadelphia's accessible City Hall plaza and transit gateway, with a 241-jet seasonal play fountain, winter ice rink, Greenfield Lawn and Wintergarden, shade trees, movable seating, public art, cafés, markets, events, and direct SEPTA access.

What hours is Dilworth Park open? +

The park is generally open daily from 6 a.m. to 1 a.m. The fountain, ice rink, cafés, Wintergarden, markets, performances, and other programmed areas each use their own seasonal schedules.

How do I reach Dilworth Park by transit? +

Dilworth Park sits directly over Philadelphia's largest transit hub at City Hall. Glass entrances connect to SEPTA subway and trolley lines, making transit the simplest arrival for many visitors.

When is the Dilworth Park fountain open for water play? +

The zero-depth fountain normally operates from April through October, with exact dates varying. Its 241 jets create a flat, accessible cooling and play surface; closures can occur for events, rink changeover, weather, or maintenance.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Is Dilworth Park wheelchair accessible? +

Yes. The park was designed with barrier-free accessible walkways from surrounding streets, a zero-depth fountain, movable seating, and accessible transit connections below. Rink sleds have also been offered by reservation during winter seasons.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Are there bathrooms at Dilworth Park? +

Bathrooms are available inside City Hall, but their hours vary and may be shorter than park hours. Cafés and seasonal facilities should not be assumed to provide unrestricted public restrooms.

Is Dilworth Park free? +

General park access, the lawn, seating, gardens, fountain play, and many public programs are free. Ice or roller skating, skate rental, food and drinks, market purchases, private rentals, and ticketed events charge separately.

What public art is at Dilworth Park? +

Janet Echelman's Pulse is integrated with the fountain and uses colored mist to evoke the subway routes below. City Hall, the glass transit headhouses, seasonal gardens, and the transformed rink plaza frame the installation.

Why might the Dilworth fountain or lawn be closed? +

The fountain and central plaza convert seasonally between water play and skating, while markets, performances, construction, maintenance, and private events can control individual areas. Check the current park calendar before a feature-specific visit.

What address should I use for Dilworth Park? +

Use 1 South 15th Street at Philadelphia City Hall. The park is at street level on City Hall's west side and has direct elevator and stair connections to SEPTA's Market-Frankford, Broad Street, and subway-surface trolley lines.

Where should I park for Dilworth Park? +

Dilworth Park has no dedicated surface visitor lot. Use a signed Center City garage or transit; direct SEPTA access is usually simpler, and events around City Hall can change curb access and garage demand.

Where can visitors find shade or weather shelter at Dilworth Park? +

Four tree groves provide the park's most reliable warm-season shade, with additional seasonal umbrellas or structures around programmed areas. Most of the fountain, lawn, rink, and plaza remains exposed, and the cafe or winter cabin follows separate seasonal hours. The glass transit entrances are not gathering shelters; during severe weather, use an open substantial building or enter the staffed transit concourse rather than remaining in the plaza.

What are the walking surfaces and accessibility at Dilworth Park? +

Dilworth Park is a compact, hard-surface civic plaza that is ADA accessible without stairs or barriers from street level. Broad paved routes cross the plaza and connect City Hall, transit entrances, lawn, cafe, fountain, and seasonal programming. The fountain creates wet and potentially slippery pavement, while the winter rink, event fencing, security activity, and dense commuter crowds can narrow ordinary circulation; follow the marked accessible route when a seasonal installation is operating.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Is there a playground or water play at Dilworth Park? +

Dilworth Park has no conventional playground equipment. Its seasonal centerpiece is a zero-depth play fountain with 241 programmable jets and three-foot waterspouts where children may splash when the fountain is operating; the same plaza changes to an ice rink in winter. Check the current park calendar and fountain status before promising water play, bring a change of clothes and water shoes, and supervise closely because the wet hard-surface plaza can be slippery and remains surrounded by City Hall, transit entrances, commuters, and events.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Dilworth Park? +

Yes, except on the lawn and inside the fountain. Dogs must stay on a leash no longer than six feet, owners must clean up after them, and pets must remain completely out of every water feature. Dilworth has no off-leash enclosure; the ice rink, café, markets, performances, and permitted events may apply additional access restrictions.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Center City District ↗
Can I bring food or picnic at Dilworth Park? +

Yes. Bring takeout or use the park's seasonal cafe, Patio, Starbucks kiosk, and authorized vendors, then use available outdoor seating or an open portion of the lawn. Personal barbecue grills and public food service are prohibited without a permit or license. Alcohol may be consumed only when purchased from an authorized vendor and kept within its designated area. Events, fountains, winter attractions, and lawn maintenance regularly change where seating is available, so check the current program before planning a group meal.
