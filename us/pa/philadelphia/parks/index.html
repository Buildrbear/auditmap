Current city pages

Dilworth Park

Philadelphia

The park is generally open daily from 6 a.m. to 1 a.m. The fountain, ice rink, cafés, Wintergarden, markets, performances, and other programmed areas each use their own seasonal schedules.

Fairmount Park

Philadelphia

Fairmount Park has no single useful entrance. Route to the exact destination, such as Lemon Hill, Belmont Plateau, Centennial District, Chamounix, Boathouse Row, or the Horticulture Center, because East and West Fairmount Park are separated by the river and major roads.

FDR Park

Philadelphia

Outdoor areas follow posted Philadelphia park hours. Playgrounds, museum, sports facilities, permits, restrooms, events, and construction zones use separate schedules.

Franklin Square

Philadelphia

Park and attraction hours change seasonally and are weather dependent. During the current summer schedule, daytime attractions and evening Chinese Lantern Festival access use separate sessions; check the live calendar.

Independence National Historical Park

Philadelphia

The Independence Visitor Center and major buildings generally open daily at 9 a.m.; individual closing times, holiday schedules, security access, and extended summer hours differ. Grounds are more broadly accessible.

Rittenhouse Square

Philadelphia

The square follows posted Philadelphia park hours. Farmers market, fountains, maintenance, performances, and permitted events use separate schedules.

Schuylkill Banks

Philadelphia

Open trail segments follow posted public hours. Boat tours, kayak programs, restrooms, dog run, construction areas, and individual parks use separate schedules.

Spruce Street Harbor Park

Philadelphia

The park is seasonal and keeps a live operating calendar; hours, concessions, bar service, games, and events differ by day and weather. At least one concession operates during published park hours.

Wissahickon Valley Park

Philadelphia

The park is open from 6 a.m. to 1 a.m. from April 1 through November 30 and from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. from December 1 through March 31. Trails or access points can close separately after storms or construction.
