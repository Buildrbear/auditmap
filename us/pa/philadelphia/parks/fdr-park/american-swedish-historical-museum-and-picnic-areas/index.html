What people say

The American Swedish Historical Museum and surrounding picnic groves are a separate cultural and gathering zone with their own hours, admission, and permit needs. Anna C. Verna Playground is closed pending a 2026 safety inspection. Also check flooding, construction, World Cup or stadium events, picnic permits, lake rules, heat, and thunderstorms. Swimming is not allowed.
