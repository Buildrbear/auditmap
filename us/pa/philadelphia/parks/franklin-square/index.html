What people say

Franklin Square is a free seven-acre historic square centered on an 1838 fountain, with a playground, Parx Liberty Carousel, Philly Mini Golf, SquareBurger, lawns, and seasonal light and lantern events.

When is Franklin Square open? +

Park and attraction hours change seasonally and are weather dependent. During the current summer schedule, daytime attractions and evening Chinese Lantern Festival access use separate sessions; check the live calendar.

Where should I park for Franklin Square? +

There is no dedicated free park lot. Transit is easiest; paid garages and legal street parking serve Old City and Chinatown but festival nights and events create heavy demand.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Historic Philadelphia ↗
What is the best entrance for Franklin Square? +

Use 6th and Race Streets. The square is two blocks north of the Independence Visitor Center; the fountain is central, with attractions arranged around the loop.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Historic Philadelphia ↗
Are there restrooms at Franklin Square? +

Restroom availability follows staffed attraction and event operations. Confirm the current facility before arriving with children, especially outside the main operating season.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Is Franklin Square free? +

Entrance to Franklin Square is free, but carousel, mini golf, festival admission, food, and some events require tickets. Carousel rides are currently $4 for ages three and older; mini golf is separately priced.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Historic Philadelphia ↗
How accessible is Franklin Square? +

Main park paths and attraction approaches are developed, and the compact loop limits walking distance. Ride transfer rules, crowds, temporary festival installations, and historic surfaces vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Historic Philadelphia ↗
Are dogs allowed at Franklin Square? +

Leashed dogs may use general park paths and lawns where posted, but should stay out of playground and ticketed attractions and away from food service and event installations.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Historic Philadelphia ↗
Is Franklin Square good for children? +

The playground, gentle carousel, mini golf, fountain, and food make Franklin Square one of Center City's strongest family stops. Attraction hours and tickets matter more than general square access.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Historic Philadelphia ↗
How do I reach Franklin Square without a car? +

The reopened Franklin Square PATCO station is beside the park, with SEPTA and Independence Mall connections nearby. Transit avoids difficult event parking.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Historic Philadelphia ↗
What should I know before visiting Franklin Square? +

Check the live calendar, weather, lantern-festival access, attraction tickets, fountain operation, and holiday closures. Daytime free access and evening ticketed event rules can differ.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Historic Philadelphia ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Franklin Square? +

Check heat index, thunderstorms, river or creek flooding, high wind, snow and ice, air quality, and daylight. Leave water edges, fields, playgrounds, and trees when thunder is heard.
