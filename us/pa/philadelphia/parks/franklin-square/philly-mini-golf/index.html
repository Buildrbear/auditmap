What people say

Philly Mini Golf is Franklin Square's 18-hole outdoor course built around miniature Philadelphia landmarks, with timed capacity, separate tickets and weather-sensitive play. Tickets are valid only during public operating hours. Check weather and festival transitions before starting a late round.

Where exactly is Philly Mini Golf? +

Find the gated mini-golf course inside the park loop. Check in at the attraction entrance rather than at the fountain or carousel queue.

Where should I park for Philly Mini Golf? +

There is no mini-golf lot. PATCO, SEPTA or a paid garage avoids circling the square.

Is Philly Mini Golf free? +

Current prices are $15 adults and $12 children ages 3-13; children two and under are free with a paid guardian. Art-Reach Access Card restrictions apply.

How accessible is Philly Mini Golf? +

The outdoor course has developed paths, but individual holes, obstacles, queues and temporary event layouts vary. Ask staff about the most workable route.

Is Philly Mini Golf good for children? +

The course works best for children able to wait turns and remain within the playing lane. Younger children need close supervision around clubs and neighboring groups.

How do you reach Philly Mini Golf? +
