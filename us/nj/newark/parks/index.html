Branch Brook Park

Newark

Choose an entrance by destination. The southern division near Park Avenue serves the lake, cathedral, concert grove, and roller rink; the middle and northern divisions extend through Newark into Belleville with cherry groves, fields, playgrounds, and trails. Use the official map rather than a center pin.

Weequahic Park

Newark

Use the Elizabeth Avenue and Meeker Avenue area in Newark, NJ 07114 for a general arrival. The 311-acre park has multiple activity zones, so route directly to the golf course, community center, playground, field, picnic area, or lake section when possible.
