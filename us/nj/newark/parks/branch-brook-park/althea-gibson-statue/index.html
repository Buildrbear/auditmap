What people say

The Althea Gibson Memorial is a bronze sculpture honoring the pioneering tennis and golf champion, installed beside the tennis center named for her. Find the statue beside the Althea Gibson Tennis Center on Heller Parkway, close to the Branch Brook Park Newark Light Rail station. The memorial is free and outdoors, with no ticket desk or dedicated visitor facilities. It is beside the tennis complex but does not represent a reservation for the courts. Use the Heller Parkway or light-rail approach rather than the park's distant southern attractions, and treat the sculpture as a short stop during a park or tennis-center visit.

Where should I park at Althea Gibson Statue? +

Parking is distributed along park drives and near major facilities. During peak cherry-blossom weekends, expect traffic controls, full lots, road restrictions, and long walks; arrive early or use transit and choose the exact destination before routing.

Can I reach Althea Gibson Statue by transit? +

Yes. Newark Light Rail serves the park, including Branch Brook Park station, and local buses serve perimeter streets. Select the stop closest to your planned park division because the landscape stretches roughly 3.5 miles.
