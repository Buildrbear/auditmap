Liberty State Park

1 Audrey Zapp Drive, Jersey City, NJ 07305
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Liberty State Park gates are open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. The park office and Nature Center are open daily from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.; CRRNJ Terminal hours vary seasonally.

What people say

Liberty State Park is New Jersey's Hudson waterfront gateway, with Manhattan, Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island views, a two-mile waterfront promenade, CRRNJ Terminal, Empty Sky 9/11 Memorial, playground and picnic areas, Nature Center, lawns, ferry service, marina, fishing, boat launches, and destination-specific parking.

What address should I use for Liberty State Park? +

Use 1 Audrey Zapp Drive, Jersey City, NJ for the CRRNJ Terminal and north waterfront. Route directly to Empty Sky, the playground and picnic areas, Nature Center, ferry terminal, boat launch, marina, or your event field because the park is large and linear.

What hours is Liberty State Park open? +

Park gates are open daily from 6 a.m.-10 p.m. The office and Nature Center are normally open 8 a.m.-4 p.m.; the CRRNJ Terminal closes between 4 and 5:30 p.m. depending on season and closes on Thanksgiving.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Where should I park at Liberty State Park? +

Use the official map to choose a lot near the terminal, Empty Sky, playgrounds, pavilions, Nature Center, ferry, boat launch, or marina. Parking policies and fees vary by lot and event; holidays and fireworks can sharply limit availability.

Can I reach the Statue of Liberty ferry from Liberty State Park? +

Yes. Statue City Cruises operates year-round service to Ellis Island and Liberty Island from the CRRNJ Terminal area. Tickets and security are separate from free park entry; confirm the current sailing schedule before arrival.

How long is the Liberty State Park waterfront walk? +

The developed Hudson River Waterfront Walkway promenade runs about two miles and links picnic and playground areas, the Nature Center, and CRRNJ Terminal. Construction, events, or waterfront weather can detour segments.

Are there playgrounds at Liberty State Park? +

Yes. Two picnic and playground areas are connected by the waterfront promenade. Use the official park map to select the correct lot; the pavilion area also has nearby restrooms and accessible parking.

Where are restrooms at Liberty State Park? +

Restrooms are available at several staffed and recreation destinations, including beside the reservable picnic pavilions and near terminal visitor services. Hours can follow the relevant building or seasonal facility.

Is Liberty State Park under construction? +

The State is carrying out a multi-phase revitalization program that can alter fields, paths, roads, lots, and terminal approaches. Check the current project and park advisory pages before relying on an older map or route.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
New Jersey DEP ↗
Where can visitors find shade or weather shelter at Liberty State Park? +

Two reservable picnic pavilions provide seasonal open-air cover on Pavilion Field, and the Nature Center offers indoor exhibits and restrooms daily from 8 a.m.-4 p.m. The waterfront promenade, lawns, memorial, ferry approaches, and playground areas are highly exposed to sun and harbor wind. Pavilions are not safe in lightning or dangerous wind; use an open substantial building or vehicle and follow waterfront closure notices during severe weather.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
New Jersey DEP ↗
Where can dogs go at Liberty State Park, and what rules apply? +

Pets may use Liberty State Park's paths, promenade, lawns, and ordinary outdoor areas while kept under control on a leash no longer than 6 feet. Owners must clean up after them, and the park has no designated off-leash area. Swimming is prohibited for all visitors, and individual buildings, ferry operations, events, playgrounds, and sensitive natural areas may have additional posted restrictions.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
New Jersey State Parks ↗
Do I need a reservation to picnic at Liberty State Park? +

Groups of 20 or fewer may use up to two first-come brown tables and one nearby grill, or picnic on the lawn, without a permit. Groups of 20-50 must reserve one of seven free open-air group sites; larger groups can rent Pavilion A for up to 120 or Pavilion B for up to 80. Alcohol, propane grills, wood fires, generators, and extra tables are prohibited.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
New Jersey DEP ↗
Can children swim or use a splash pad at Liberty State Park? +

No current splash pad or swimming area is open at Liberty State Park, and New Jersey's park rules explicitly prohibit swimming. A splash pad is part of the planned southern-jetties revitalization, but it is a future project and should not be treated as an existing amenity. The Hudson River, marina, launches, and shoreline are for views, boating, fishing, and crabbing under their specific rules, not wading or swimming.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
New Jersey DEP ↗
Is Liberty State Park free to visit? +

Yes. New Jersey lists no entrance fee for Liberty State Park. Ferry tickets, boat-launch permits, pavilion rentals, food, marina services, Liberty Science Center admission, and some transportation or parking options are separate costs.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 8, 2026
New Jersey State Park Service ↗
How accessible is Liberty State Park? +

The developed waterfront promenade links major visitor areas, and the picnic pavilions have adjacent ADA parking and accessible grills. Because the park is long, route to the exact destination and review current construction or shuttle notices rather than assuming every attraction is a short walk from one parking lot.
