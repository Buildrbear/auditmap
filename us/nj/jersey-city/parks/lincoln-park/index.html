What people say

Lincoln Park is Hudson County's 273-acre Jersey City park, split into active-recreation Lincoln Park East and nature-oriented Lincoln Park West, with playgrounds, spray pool, dog run, courts and fields, track, trails, Edgewood Lake, fishing and kayaking, golf, picnic areas, and monuments.

Which part of Lincoln Park should I navigate to? +

Use West Side Avenue for the main Lincoln Park East arrival and active recreation. Lincoln Park West is across Route 1/9 and contains nature trails, Edgewood Lake, golf, fishing, and kayaking, so route to the named destination rather than only the park name.

Are there playgrounds at Lincoln Park in Jersey City? +

Yes. Hudson County lists two playgrounds in Lincoln Park East along with a seasonal spray pool, sports courts, fields, track, picnic areas, and trails.

Is there water play at Lincoln Park? +

Yes. Lincoln Park East has a summer spray pool. It is seasonal, so check current county notices and on-site status before planning a trip specifically for water play.

Is there a dog run at Lincoln Park? +

Yes. Hudson County lists an enclosed dog run in Lincoln Park East. Dogs outside the designated run should follow current county leash and park rules.

Where are the walking trails at Lincoln Park? +

Full-length park trails run through Lincoln Park East, while Lincoln Park West has nature trails around its restored wetlands and Edgewood Lake area. Surfaces and conditions differ between active-park paths, cross-country routes, and nature sections.

Can I picnic or barbecue at Lincoln Park? +

Yes. Hudson County lists picnic and barbecue facilities. Use designated areas, follow posted fire and disposal rules, and verify whether a permit is required for a large group or organized event.

What accessible recreation is documented at Lincoln Park? +

Hudson County identifies fishing access at Edgewood Lake and extensive developed recreation in Lincoln Park East. Accessibility varies across the 273-acre East and West sections, so use the official map and confirm the exact parking-to-destination route needed.

Where should I park at Lincoln Park in Jersey City? +

Choose parking for Lincoln Park East's playgrounds, spray pool, track, courts, and fields or Lincoln Park West's golf, Edgewood Lake, paddling, and nature trails. Route 1/9 divides the sections, so parking on the wrong side can leave you far from the intended activity.

Where are restrooms at Lincoln Park? +

Restroom access is tied to developed recreation facilities and seasonal operations across the East and West sections rather than available beside every trail or lake area. Use the county map and choose the closest staffed facility before beginning a longer outing.

Is Lincoln Park free? +

General park, playground, spray-pool, trail, dog-run, court, field, picnic, and lakefront access is free unless reserved for an event. Skyway Golf Course, kayak rentals, permits, organized activities, and concessions charge separately.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Hudson County ↗
What can be closed or unavailable at Lincoln Park in Jersey City? +

Athletic fields, roads, and reserved areas can be unavailable during permitted leagues or events, and Hudson County may cancel, postpone, or move field use because of rain, fire, flooding, emergencies, or unsafe conditions. The spray pool, kayak rentals, golf course, restrooms, and concessions use separate or seasonal schedules, so confirm the exact facility rather than assuming all park amenities follow the same hours.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Hudson County Division of Parks ↗
Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Lincoln Park in Jersey City? +

Mature trees shade portions of Lincoln Park East's meandering paths, playground and picnic landscape, while covered picnic areas provide open-air shelter. Athletic fields, track, courts, golf course, riverfront areas, and Lincoln Park West's open restoration landscape can be exposed. The View at Lincoln Park is a privately operated event venue, not a general public refuge; during lightning, flooding, dangerous heat, or high wind, use a confirmed-open substantial building or enclosed vehicle rather than trees or a picnic roof.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Hudson County Parks ↗
Which bus goes to Lincoln Park in Jersey City? +

NJ Transit Route 1 serves the Lincoln Highway and Hackensack Avenue side of Lincoln Park and connects toward Journal Square and Exchange Place. Use the live NJ Transit planner for the park entrance or facility you need, because Lincoln Park East and West are divided by roads, river channels, and the golf course. Do not confuse this park with Lincoln Park in Newark or Lincoln Park station in Morris County when searching.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
NJ Transit ↗
What landmarks should I see in Lincoln Park in Jersey City? +

The County guide highlights the 1911 entrance fountain, Lincoln the Mystic, the Irish Famine Memorial, Civil War soldier statue, James T. Farrier Firefighter Memorial, historic cannons, and sun houses. These are concentrated mainly in Lincoln Park East; route to a specific landmark instead of the broad park pin.
