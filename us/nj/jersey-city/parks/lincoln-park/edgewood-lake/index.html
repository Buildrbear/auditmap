Where should I park for Edgewood Lake? +

Choose parking for Lincoln Park East's playgrounds, spray pool, track, courts, and fields or Lincoln Park West's golf, Edgewood Lake, paddling, and nature trails. Route 1/9 divides the sections, so parking on the wrong side can leave you far from the intended activity. Navigate to the exact Edgewood Lake pin rather than the general Lincoln Park marker. Are there restrooms near Edgewood Lake? +

Restroom access is tied to developed recreation facilities and seasonal operations across the East and West sections rather than available beside every trail or lake area. Use the county map and choose the closest staffed facility before beginning a longer outing.

What fees apply at Edgewood Lake? +

Viewing the lake and using ordinary public park paths are free; golf, permits, organized athletics, and reserved facilities can have separate charges.

How accessible is Edgewood Lake? +

Hudson County identifies fishing access at Edgewood Lake and extensive developed recreation in Lincoln Park East. Accessibility varies across the 273-acre East and West sections, so use the official map and confirm the exact parking-to-destination route needed.

Are dogs allowed at Edgewood Lake? +

Yes. Hudson County lists an enclosed dog run in Lincoln Park East. Dogs outside the designated run should follow current county leash and park rules. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.
