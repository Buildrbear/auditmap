What people say

Parham Bridges Park is a compact northeast Jackson recreation park anchored by a tennis center and pro shop, with a one-mile lighted fitness and jogging trail, outdoor exercise station, playground, picnic area, restrooms, and on-site parking.

Use 5055 Old Canton Road, Jackson, MS 39211. The entrance serves the tennis center, one-mile fitness trail, playground, picnic area, restrooms, and parking.

Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Parham Bridges Park? +

Trees and the picnic area provide the most likely relief from direct sun, while the tennis courts, ballfields, fitness stations, playground, and much of the one-mile loop can be exposed. Public records do not identify a general-use pavilion, and the tennis center or pro shop should not be treated as an always-open refuge. Carry water and plan around midday heat; for lightning, severe wind, or dangerous heat, use an open substantial building or enclosed vehicle.

Is Parham Bridges Park an off-leash dog park? +

No designated off-leash dog area is listed at Parham Bridges Park. Jackson's official property inventory identifies ballfields, a playground, and a one-mile exercise trail but does not identify a dog facility or park-specific pet allowance. Do not let a dog off leash here; follow entrance signs and current City animal-control rules, keep any permitted dog controlled, remove waste, and confirm with Parks before making a dog-specific trip.

Can I take JTRAN to Parham Bridges Park? +

JTRAN Route 25 serves Old Canton Road, but the published stops and system map do not show direct service into Parham Bridges Park at 5055 Old Canton Road. Use JTRAN's Transit app or trip planner to confirm the closest active stop and the remaining walk before committing to the trip. JTRAN currently warns of delays or route suspensions and does not operate on Sundays, so rideshare or a car may be more practical for games, equipment, or limited mobility.
