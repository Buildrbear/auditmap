Where should I park for Mayes Lake? +

Use 3315 Lakeland Terrace for the Mayes Lake state-park side, camping, pavilions, lake, disc golf, and two park playgrounds. The Museum District playground and museums use the separate Museum Boulevard and Riverside Drive campus with parking at both museums. Are there restrooms near Mayes Lake? +

Day-use facilities and the Museum District have destination-specific restrooms, while overnight guests use a campground bathhouse with hot showers. Current renovation projects can affect bathhouse or concession facilities, so confirm with the park before relying on one location.

What fees apply at Mayes Lake? +

The state park lists general entrance at $2 per person, with children age five and under free; camping, rentals, reservations, and other activities can charge separately.

How accessible is Mayes Lake? +

The park offers nature trails and an 18-hole disc golf course within its wooded urban landscape. Trail conditions change with rain and river or lake weather; use the interactive park map for current routing.

Are dogs allowed at Mayes Lake? +

Yes, but only in designated pet areas and on an adequate leash no longer than six feet. Pick up and dispose of waste, and remove any pet that disturbs other guests. Pets are prohibited in and around swimming-pool and lake-beach areas; museums, playgrounds, golf, events, lodging, and other separately managed facilities may apply additional restrictions. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.

Is Mayes Lake useful for a family visit? +

Mayes Lake is the fishing, non-motorized paddling, and electric-trolling-motor water destination inside LeFleur's Bluff State Park. The state park has two playground units between the pavilions in the Mayes Lake section. The larger LeFleur's Bluff playground complex near the museums is a separate destination with its own hours and holiday closures.
