What people say

LeFleur's Bluff State Park is a 305-acre urban state park in Jackson with Mayes Lake fishing and non-motorized paddling, boat launch, nature trails, 18-hole disc golf, two playground units, shaded picnic sites, five reservable pavilions, 28 developed campsites, 30 primitive tent sites, bathhouse, and direct connections to Mississippi Museum of Natural Science and LeFleur's Bluff playground complex.

What address should I use for LeFleur's Bluff State Park? +

Use 3315 Lakeland Terrace, Jackson, MS 39216 for the state park. Mississippi Museum of Natural Science uses 2148 Riverside Drive, so navigate to the exact entrance for camping, Mayes Lake, museum, playground, or golf.

What hours is LeFleur's Bluff State Park open? +

General day-use hours are 8 a.m.-5 p.m. daily. The museum, campground, playground complex, golf course, and reserved facilities maintain separate schedules and holiday closures.

How much does LeFleur's Bluff State Park cost? +

General entrance is $2 per person, with children age five and under free. The museum, camping, pavilions, golf, disc rental, and special activities charge separately.

Where are the playgrounds at LeFleur's Bluff? +

The state park has two playground units between the pavilions in the Mayes Lake section. The larger LeFleur's Bluff playground complex near the museums is a separate destination with its own hours and holiday closures.

Can I reserve a pavilion at LeFleur's Bluff? +

Yes. Five picnic pavilions can be reserved for gatherings. The shaded Riverside day-use area also has picnic tables, some charcoal grills, and views toward a large playground.

What trails are at LeFleur's Bluff State Park? +

The park offers nature trails and an 18-hole disc golf course within its wooded urban landscape. Trail conditions change with rain and river or lake weather; use the interactive park map for current routing.

Can I launch a boat on Mayes Lake? +

Yes. Visitors may launch their own boat, kayak, or paddleboard. Only electric trolling motors are allowed; jet boats are prohibited. Anglers ages 16-65 need a Mississippi fishing license.

Is LeFleur's Bluff golf course open? +

No. The official park page currently says the golf course is closed for maintenance until further notice. Confirm the status before traveling for golf; the rest of the state park may remain open.

Where should I park at LeFleur's Bluff? +

Use 3315 Lakeland Terrace for the Mayes Lake state-park side, camping, pavilions, lake, disc golf, and two park playgrounds. The Museum District playground and museums use the separate Museum Boulevard and Riverside Drive campus with parking at both museums.

Where are restrooms at LeFleur's Bluff? +

Day-use facilities and the Museum District have destination-specific restrooms, while overnight guests use a campground bathhouse with hot showers. Current renovation projects can affect bathhouse or concession facilities, so confirm with the park before relying on one location.

Is the LeFleur's Bluff Playground the same as the state park playgrounds? +

No. The inclusive LeFleur's Bluff Playground links the Natural Science and Children's museums on the Museum District campus and has its own hours and holiday closures. The Mayes Lake state-park section separately has two playground units between its pavilions.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Where can I find shade or weather shelter at LeFleur's Bluff State Park? +

The Riverside day-use area has a spacious shaded picnic section, and five reservable pavilions serve the park; wooded nature trails and campground areas also provide tree cover. Mayes Lake, playgrounds, disc golf, golf, boat launch, and open recreation areas can be exposed. The Museum of Natural Science and other complex buildings have separate admission and hours, and pavilions are not severe-weather shelters; during lightning, flooding, high wind, or dangerous heat, use a confirmed-open substantial building or enclosed vehicle.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Mississippi Department of Wildlife, Fisheries, and Parks ↗
Are dogs allowed at LeFleur's Bluff State Park? +

Yes, but only in designated pet areas and on an adequate leash no longer than six feet. Pick up and dispose of waste, and remove any pet that disturbs other guests. Pets are prohibited in and around swimming-pool and lake-beach areas; museums, playgrounds, golf, events, lodging, and other separately managed facilities may apply additional restrictions.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Mississippi Department of Wildlife, Fisheries, and Parks ↗
Can you swim or use a splash pad at LeFleur's Bluff State Park? +

No operating pool, splash pad, or designated swimming beach is listed for LeFleur's Bluff. Mayes Lake is presented for fishing, boat launching, kayaking, and paddleboarding with electric trolling motors only, not swimming. Use the lake only for the listed activities and choose another currently operating Jackson-area aquatic facility when swimming or water play is the goal.
