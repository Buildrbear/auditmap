Sugar House Park

1330 East 2100 South, Salt Lake City, UT 84106
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Open daily 7:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. May-October and 7:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. November-April.
Navigate

What people say

A 109-acre city park with a 1.38-mile loop, two playgrounds, three restroom buildings, seven pavilions, fields, courts, Sugar House Pond, Parley's Creek and Trail, gardens and a renowned sledding hill.

When is Sugar House Park open? +

Open daily 7:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. May-October and 7:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. November-April.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
Where should I park for Sugar House Park? +

The internal road runs one-way counter-clockwise. Use the lot nearest the intended pavilion or playground and keep the inside curb clear for walkers, runners and cyclists.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
What is the best entrance for Sugar House Park? +

Use 1330 East 2100 South for a general arrival, then follow the park map. Named pavilions are the easiest meeting points for the pond, playgrounds, courts and fields.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
Are there restrooms at Sugar House Park? +

Fabian Lakeside restroom is open year-round. Parley's Creek and Hidden Grove restroom buildings close in winter.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
Is Sugar House Park free? +

General park, paths, playgrounds, fields and pond overlooks are free. Pavilion and field reservations, permits and special events may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
How accessible is Sugar House Park? +

The paved inner lane loops 1.38 miles with about 92 feet of elevation gain. Developed pavilions, playgrounds and lots have firm approaches, while grass slopes and snow vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
Are dogs allowed at Sugar House Park? +

Dogs must remain leashed and controlled everywhere in Sugar House Park; there is no off-leash area.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
Is Sugar House Park good for children? +

Parley's Creek and Sugar Beet playgrounds serve different sides of the park. The pond and creek are not swimming or splash areas, and water entry is prohibited.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
How do I reach Sugar House Park without a car? +

UTA Route 21 serves 2100 South by the park; Route 220 may suit other approaches. Use live trip planning for the exact pavilion or entrance.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
What should I know before visiting Sugar House Park? +

Big Field remains open and continues to host permitted events. The separate Backstop Field is closed for softball construction through 2026; Hidden Grove and Mt. Olympus pavilions are not accepting 2026 reservations, and fireworks are prohibited year-round.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Sugar House Park? +

Check current Wasatch Front weather, air quality, road status and operator alerts. Heat, cold, snow, avalanche control, lightning, flash flooding, high wind, wildfire smoke and fire restrictions can independently close roads, trails or facilities.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Sugar House Park Authority ↗
