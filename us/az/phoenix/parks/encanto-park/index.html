When is Encanto Park open? +

The general park is open 5:30 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; pool, amusement rides, golf courses, sports facilities, clubhouse, and events keep separate schedules.

Where should I park for Encanto Park? +

Free park lots and curb approaches are spread around 15th Avenue, Encanto Boulevard, and facility edges. Route to the pool, Enchanted Island, lagoon, sports complex, or golf course.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
What is the best entrance for Encanto Park? +

The lagoon and amusement area, pool, athletic complex, and two golf courses occupy different zones. The first available lot may be far from the activity you need.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Are there restrooms at Encanto Park? +

Public restrooms and drinking fountains are listed, with additional facilities inside staffed pool, amusement, golf, and sports operations.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Encanto Park free? +

General park, playground, lagoon views, picnics, courts, and paths are free. Rides, pool admission for most adults, golf, concessions, rentals, fishing license, and reservations cost separately.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Encanto Park? +

The developed park lists ADA access and contains broad paved paths, accessible pool equipment, parking, shade, and level lawns; rides and older edges vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Encanto Park? +

Leashed dogs may use ordinary park areas but must stay out of pool, play equipment, rides, golf, sports fields, and posted restricted zones.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Encanto Park good for children? +

Enchanted Island, playground, lagoon, picnic shade, seasonal pool, ducks, and sports make this a full family day when schedules are checked in advance.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
How do I reach Encanto Park without a car? +

Bus, biking, rideshare, and driving serve this central park. Select the facility entrance rather than relying only on the park centroid.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
What should I know before visiting Encanto Park? +

Water in the lagoon is for fishing and boating activity, not swimming. Encanto Pool's 2026 open-swim schedule is seasonal, Enchanted Island may close for heat or weather, and summer equipment can still become dangerously hot.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Encanto Park? +

Check Phoenix's hourly temperature and heat index, National Weather Service Extreme Heat Warnings, trail restrictions, air quality, lightning, monsoon storms, flash-flood risk, and current park notices. Exposed rock, metal, pavement, play equipment, and pet paws can reach dangerous temperatures long before afternoon.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
