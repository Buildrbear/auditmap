What people say

The Phoenix Indian School Visitor Center interprets the campus and Native histories on a separately posted schedule. This is both a recreation park and a place of difficult Indigenous history. Treat memorial areas respectfully, verify visitor-center hours and event closures, and check dog-park maintenance before a pet-specific trip.
