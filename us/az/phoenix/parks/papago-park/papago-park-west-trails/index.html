destination

Papago Park West trails

625 North Galvin Parkway, Phoenix, AZ 85008
Hours ⌄
Published schedule East-side parking entrances are open 5:00 a.m.-7:00 p.m.; west-side entrances are open sunrise to sunset. Trails are open 5:00 a.m.-11:00 p.m.; individual attractions keep separate hours.
Navigate

What people say

West-side trails cross low sandstone and open desert from sunrise to sunset, with different gate hours from the east-side attraction district. Papago's trails are easier than Phoenix's steep summit hikes but desert heat remains dangerous. East and west parking close earlier than trail hours, ramadas require reservations, and each major attraction has independent admission and hours.

Is Papago Park West trails free? +

General park, trails, Hole-in-the-Rock, ponds, and ordinary picnics are free. Zoo, Botanical Garden, museums, golf, fishing licenses, ramada reservations, food, and events cost separately.

How accessible is Papago Park West trails? +

Several broad, low-elevation trails and developed areas are accessible or comparatively gentle, but sandstone, loose gravel, rock openings, heat, and attraction routes vary.

Are dogs allowed at Papago Park West trails? +

Leashed dogs are allowed on ordinary city park trails under posted rules; zoo, gardens, museums, golf, fishing edges, and extreme heat create separate restrictions and risks.
