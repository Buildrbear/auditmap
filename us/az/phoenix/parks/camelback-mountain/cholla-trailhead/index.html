What people say

Cholla Trailhead uses limited legal parallel parking and designated rideshare drop-offs on Invergordon Road with no conventional parking lot. Cholla Trail is scheduled to close August 10-21, 2026 for construction. Both routes are extremely difficult, trails close 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. during Extreme Heat Warnings, and rescues are common; turn around before half your water is gone.

Where should I park for Cholla Trailhead? +

Echo Canyon has a small lot that frequently fills and closes its gate; waiting or idling on McDonald or Tatum is prohibited. Cholla has no designated lot and only limited legal parallel parking and rideshare drop-offs.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Are there restrooms near Cholla Trailhead? +

Echo Canyon has restrooms and potable water at the trailhead. Cholla trailhead information warns that facilities and water cannot be relied upon; arrive self-sufficient.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

Is Cholla Trailhead free? +

Trail access is free. Transportation, parking violations, guided services, equipment, and emergency consequences are not.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026

How accessible is Cholla Trailhead? +

Both summit routes are rated extremely difficult with steep gain, uneven rock, exposed scrambling, and no accessible summit route. The base facilities do not make the trail accessible.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Cholla Trailhead? +

Dogs are prohibited year-round on every Camelback Mountain trail, regardless of leash, temperature, size, or hiking experience.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Cholla Trailhead good for children? +

These are not casual family walks. Only capable, prepared older participants should attempt the summit; families seeking easy red-rock scenery should choose Papago Park.

How do you reach Cholla Trailhead? +

Rideshare can avoid parking pressure but pickup staging is limited and may be redirected. Do not depend on phone service or an immediate ride after sunset.
