Camelback Mountain

Hours ⌄

What people say

Phoenix's internationally known 2,704-foot landmark with two extremely difficult summit routes, Echo Canyon and Cholla, steep scrambling, expansive views, severe heat exposure, and limited trailhead parking.

When is Camelback Mountain open? +

Echo Canyon and Cholla follow the changing daily daylight window shown by the City of Phoenix rather than fixed year-round clock times. Gates, construction, extreme-heat restrictions, rescues, and weather can close access.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Where should I park for Camelback Mountain? +

Echo Canyon has a small lot that frequently fills and closes its gate; waiting or idling on McDonald or Tatum is prohibited. Cholla has no designated lot and only limited legal parallel parking and rideshare drop-offs.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
What is the best entrance for Camelback Mountain? +

Choose Echo Canyon at 4925 East McDonald Drive or Cholla from Invergordon Road. They are not interchangeable parking areas and there is no shuttle across the mountain.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Are there restrooms at Camelback Mountain? +

Echo Canyon has restrooms and potable water at the trailhead. Cholla trailhead information warns that facilities and water cannot be relied upon; arrive self-sufficient.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Camelback Mountain free? +

Trail access is free. Transportation, parking violations, guided services, equipment, and emergency consequences are not.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Camelback Mountain? +

Both summit routes are rated extremely difficult with steep gain, uneven rock, exposed scrambling, and no accessible summit route. The base facilities do not make the trail accessible.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Camelback Mountain? +

Dogs are prohibited year-round on every Camelback Mountain trail, regardless of leash, temperature, size, or hiking experience.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Camelback Mountain good for children? +

These are not casual family walks. Only capable, prepared older participants should attempt the summit; families seeking easy red-rock scenery should choose Papago Park.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
How do I reach Camelback Mountain without a car? +

Rideshare can avoid parking pressure but pickup staging is limited and may be redirected. Do not depend on phone service or an immediate ride after sunset.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
What should I know before visiting Camelback Mountain? +

Cholla Trail is scheduled to close August 10-21, 2026 for construction. Both routes are extremely difficult, trails close 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. during Extreme Heat Warnings, and rescues are common; turn around before half your water is gone.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Camelback Mountain? +

Check Phoenix's hourly temperature and heat index, National Weather Service Extreme Heat Warnings, trail restrictions, air quality, lightning, monsoon storms, flash-flood risk, and current park notices. Exposed rock, metal, pavement, play equipment, and pet paws can reach dangerous temperatures long before afternoon.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation ↗
