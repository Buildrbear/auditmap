What people say

The northwest dog park opens 6:30 a.m.-9:00 p.m. and can close for annual May-June turf renovation. The park sits over a freeway and can feel like several separate campuses. Route to the correct edge, verify splash-pad and institution hours, and expect large festivals to transform access, noise, shade, and parking.
