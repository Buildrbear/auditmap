Reid Park

Tucson

Use 900 South Randolph Way for the general park. Use 800 South Concert Place for the outdoor performance center, 920 South Concert Place for the rose garden, 910 South Concert Place for Miko's Corner dog park, and 3400 East Camino Campestre for Hi Corbett Field.

Rillito River Park

Tucson

There is no single main entrance. Choose a trailhead near the section you want to use. Rillito Racetrack and Rio Vista Park are practical starting points with parking and paved connections; consult the official interactive map for north- or south-bank access.
