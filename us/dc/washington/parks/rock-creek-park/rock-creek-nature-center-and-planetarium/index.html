Rock Creek Nature Center & Planetarium

5200 Glover Road NW, Washington, DC 20015
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The Nature Center is generally open Wednesday-Sunday 9 a.m.-5 p.m. Planetarium programs have limited capacity and may require same-day registration.
Navigate

What people say

The park's primary orientation point offers exhibits, maps, ranger help, children's discovery activities, and free scheduled planetarium programs. Navigate to 5200 Glover Road NW and use the signed Nature Center lot; this is not the same entrance as Peirce Mill. The Nature Center is generally open Wednesday-Sunday 9 a.m.-5 p.m. Planetarium programs have limited capacity and may require same-day registration.

Where should I park for Rock Creek Nature Center & Planetarium? +

Use designated lots and marked spaces only. Parking is distributed and can fill near the Nature Center, picnic areas, trailheads, and events; roadside parking outside marked areas is prohibited.

Is Rock Creek Nature Center & Planetarium free? +

General park, trail, Nature Center, and Peirce Mill access is free. Horseback riding, permits, reservations, and some partner programs may charge.

How accessible is Rock Creek Nature Center & Planetarium? +

The Nature Center and selected developed areas have accessible routes, while many forest trails include hills, roots, rocks, creek crossings, and mud. Choose a destination-specific route.

Are dogs allowed at Rock Creek Nature Center & Planetarium? +

Dogs must remain leashed. Bring waste bags, protect wildlife, and do not let pets enter Rock Creek or interfere with horses and trail users.

Is Rock Creek Nature Center & Planetarium good for children? +

The Nature Center, planetarium programs, Peirce Mill, picnic areas, and shorter trails are strong family options. Match distance and terrain to the group before leaving the developed area.

How do you reach Rock Creek Nature Center & Planetarium? +

Bus and Metro access varies widely by destination. The Nature Center is not directly beside a Metro station; review the exact destination and final walking route before relying on transit.
