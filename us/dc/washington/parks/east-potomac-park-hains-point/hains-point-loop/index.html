Navigate

What people say

A roughly three-mile Ohio Drive circuit around the peninsula supports walking, running, cycling, river views, cherry trees, fishing access, and a low-speed scenic drive. Choose a legal lot before starting and travel in the posted loop direction. Do not use the road shoulder as overflow parking or block bicycle and emergency access. Loop access is free, but the road may close because of tides, flooding, snow, events, construction, or public safety. Expect mixed traffic and exposed weather.

Where should I park for Hains Point Loop? +

NPS lots A-C are along Ohio Drive and lot D serves the Buckeye Drive and tennis area. Metered parking is currently $2.30 per hour from 7 a.m.-8 p.m. daily except December 25, with pay-by-plate and posted three- or six-hour limits.

Is Hains Point Loop free? +

General park, shoreline, playground, and loop access are free. Golf, miniature golf, tennis, reservations, parking, rentals, and concessions charge separately.

How accessible is Hains Point Loop? +

Hains Point includes paved approaches, designated accessible parking, picnic space, and generally level waterfront routes. Facility entrances, older recreation areas, temporary flooding, and long distances still require destination-specific planning.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

Are dogs allowed at Hains Point Loop? +

Leashed pets are allowed in outdoor park areas under posted National Park Service rules. Keep pets off active golf and tennis areas, playground surfaces, and protected shoreline habitat, and remove waste.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 10, 2026

Is Hains Point Loop good for children? +

The playground, picnic grove, miniature golf, loop, and open lawns provide flexible family options. The peninsula is exposed to heat, storms, traffic, and water edges, so bring water and supervise closely.
