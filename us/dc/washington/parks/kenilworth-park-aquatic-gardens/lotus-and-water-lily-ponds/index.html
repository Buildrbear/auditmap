Lotus & Water Lily Ponds

1550 Anacostia Avenue NE, Washington, DC 20019
Hours ⌄
Published schedule The ponds are free during garden hours. Paths are gravel, dirt, and grass; keep children behind edges and never enter or pick from the ponds.
Navigate

What people say

A grid of historic cultivated ponds filled seasonally with lotus, hardy and tropical water lilies, wildlife, and changing bloom displays. Enter through the main garden gate, stop at the Visitor Center, and follow the pond paths; early morning offers the best flowers and cooler conditions. The ponds are free during garden hours. Paths are gravel, dirt, and grass; keep children behind edges and never enter or pick from the ponds.

How accessible is Lotus & Water Lily Ponds? +

Accessible parking and two loan wheelchairs are available. Main routes include gravel, dirt, grass, a wood boardwalk, and paved Anacostia River Trail; conditions and firmness vary.

Are dogs allowed at Lotus & Water Lily Ponds? +

Leashed pets are allowed outdoors under posted rules, but sensitive wetland habitat and narrow paths require close control. Pets generally cannot enter the Visitor Center.

The ponds, turtles, frogs, birds, and short boardwalk are excellent for curious children. Bring water, bug protection, and close supervision around open water.

How do you reach Lotus & Water Lily Ponds? +

Deanwood Metro on the Orange Line is roughly a half-mile walk. Review the pedestrian route in advance because the final neighborhood approach is less obvious than a downtown attraction.
