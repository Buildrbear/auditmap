Park

Gas Works Park

2101 N Northlake Way, Seattle, WA 98103
Hours ⌄
Published schedule Gas Works Park is open daily 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Major events can restrict lots, lawns, paths, and picnic areas.
Navigate

What people say

Gas Works Park combines preserved industrial structures, Kite Hill, a sundial, Play Barn, picnic lawns, skyline views, and Burke-Gilman Trail access on Lake Union.

When is Gas Works Park open? +

Gas Works Park is open daily 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Major events can restrict lots, lawns, paths, and picnic areas.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
Where should I park for Gas Works Park? +

The park lot is limited and fills quickly on sunny days and events. Cycling via the Burke-Gilman Trail, transit, walking, or rideshare are often easier.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
What is the best entrance for Gas Works Park? +

Use the Northlake Way lot for the Play Barn and central paths. Continue toward Kite Hill and the lake viewpoint on foot; event closures can alter circulation.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
Are there restrooms at Gas Works Park? +

Public restrooms and drinking fountains are listed park amenities near developed central areas; verify event access before relying on them.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Gas Works Park free? +

Park, viewpoints, play area, lawns, and trail connections are free. Reservations, permits, events, food, and transportation may charge.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
How accessible is Gas Works Park? +

Developed routes connect parking, lawns, viewpoints, and play areas, while Kite Hill grades, crowds, wet grass, and event fencing vary.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
Are dogs allowed at Gas Works Park? +

Keep dogs leashed and out of play areas and restricted shoreline. Pick up waste and control pets around crowded lawns and bicycle traffic.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Gas Works Park good for children? +

The Play Barn, Kite Hill, sundial, lawns, and skyline views are family-friendly, but preserved machinery is not general climbing equipment and the lake is off limits.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
How do I reach Gas Works Park without a car? +

The Burke-Gilman Trail runs past the parking lot, and buses serve Wallingford and Fremont approaches.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
What should I know before visiting Gas Works Park? +

No swimming, fishing, wading, boat launching, or water entry is allowed because lake sediment contains hazardous substances. Check the 2026 large-event calendar before visiting.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 6, 2026
Seattle Parks and Recreation ↗
What weather should I check before visiting Gas Works Park? +

Check Seattle's neighborhood forecast, rain, wind, heat, wildfire smoke, daylight, and water quality or tides where relevant. Shoreline and hilltop conditions can differ from downtown.
