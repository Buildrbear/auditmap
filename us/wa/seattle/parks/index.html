Current city pages

Discovery Park

Seattle

Discovery Park follows posted Seattle park hours. The Visitor Center is closed until summer 2027; parking, beach access, cultural facilities, restrooms, and shuttle use separate conditions.

Gas Works Park

Seattle

Gas Works Park is open daily 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. Major events can restrict lots, lawns, paths, and picnic areas.

Golden Gardens Park

Seattle

May through September park hours are 4 a.m. to 10:30 p.m.; October through April hours are 4 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. Parking gates open at 7 a.m.

Green Lake Park

Seattle

The park is open daily 4 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. Beaches, wading pool, Evans Pool, community center, boat programs, restrooms, and fields use separate schedules.

Kerry Park

Seattle

Kerry Park is open daily 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. The overlook has no staffed visitor facility.

Olympic Sculpture Park

Seattle

May through September hours are 6 a.m. to 9 p.m.; October through April hours are 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. Pavilion and programs use separate schedules.

Seward Park

Seattle

Park grounds follow posted Seattle park hours. Swimming beach, restrooms, art studio, permits, and events use separate schedules.

Volunteer Park

Seattle

Park grounds follow posted Seattle park hours. Conservatory, museum, water tower, wading pool, restrooms, and events use separate schedules.

Washington Park Arboretum

Seattle

Arboretum grounds are open daily from dawn to 8 p.m. Graham Visitors Center is generally Wednesday through Sunday 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.; exterior restrooms are open daily 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Waterfront Park

Seattle

Waterfront Park is open daily 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. Aquarium, vendors, ferries, elevators, restrooms, play areas, and events use separate schedules.
