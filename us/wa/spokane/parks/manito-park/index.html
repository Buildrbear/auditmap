What people say

Manito Park is a 98-acre Spokane showplace with five major garden areas, Gaiser Conservatory, Mirror Pond, two playgrounds, seasonal splash pad and cafe, picnic lawns, walking paths, Loop Drive, restrooms, and extensive native and cultivated landscapes.

Where should I start at Manito Park? +

Use the central Loop Drive area for Gaiser Conservatory and nearby formal gardens. The south playground and splash pad have a separate seasonal parking lot. Use the city walking-tour map to locate the Japanese, Duncan, Rose Hill, Lilac, and Ferris gardens.

What hours is Manito Park open? +

The park is open 6:00 a.m.-10:00 p.m. Gaiser Conservatory, the Japanese Garden, gated drives, seasonal parking areas, cafe, splash pad, and restrooms use separate schedules.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

Where should I park at Manito Park? +

Choose parking near your destination. The south playground and splash-pad lot operates 8:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m. April 1-October 31 and closes in winter. Portions of Loop Drive also have seasonal gates, so winter parking requires a different approach.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Spokane ↗
Are there playgrounds at Manito Park? +

Yes. Manito has upper and lower playgrounds. The south family area also includes a seasonal splash pad, but its parking lot closes November through March.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Spokane ↗
What gardens are at Manito Park? +

The five signature areas are Nishinomiya Tsutakawa Japanese Garden, Duncan Garden, Rose Hill, Lilac Garden, and Joel E. Ferris Perennial Garden. Gaiser Conservatory and Mirror Pond add indoor horticulture and waterfowl viewing.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Spokane ↗
Are Manito Park restrooms open outside summer? +

Manito is one of Spokane's exceptions to the standard first-weekend-in-May through third-weekend-in-September restroom season, but exact building and daily availability still varies by location and maintenance.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026

What closes seasonally at Manito Park? +

The south playground and splash-pad parking lot closes November 1-March 31, the loop between the Japanese and perennial gardens closes to vehicles in winter, and other drives use shortened winter hours. Gardens, cafe, conservatory, and splash pad also follow their own seasons.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Spokane ↗
When is the Manito Park splash pad open? +

For 2026, Manito Park's splash pad is scheduled May 22 through September 8 from 9 a.m.-8 p.m. It may close temporarily for maintenance; call Spokane's Aquatics Hotline for current status before making water play the purpose of the trip.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Spokane ↗
Can I reserve a shelter at Manito Park? +

Yes. Manito is one of the Spokane parks where shelter reservations are required during much of the year. Rotary Shelter has a picnic area and built-in barbecue pits; garden ceremonies and larger events use separate reservations and fees.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Spokane ↗
Can I bring a dog into Manito Park's gardens? +

Dogs may use ordinary outdoor park areas only under applicable leash and cleanup rules, but pets are prohibited inside Gaiser Conservatory and the Japanese Garden's event rules also prohibit pets. Service animals are allowed where required.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Spokane ↗
Where can I find shade or weather shelter at Manito Park? +

Mature trees shade many paths, picnic grounds, and garden edges, and the reservable Manito North and South shelters provide covered picnic space. Gaiser Conservatory offers indoor garden access only during its posted hours and may close for maintenance or events. Formal lawns, playgrounds, sports areas, open flower beds, and parking can be exposed; outdoor shelters and trees are unsafe during lightning or high wind, so use a confirmed-open substantial building or enclosed vehicle.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Spokane ↗
What are the walking surfaces and slopes at Manito Park? +

The City's walking map describes the primary Manito route as paved paths and sidewalks connecting Duncan Garden, Gaiser Conservatory, Rose Hill, Ferris Perennial Garden, playgrounds, pond, and picnic areas. The 90-acre park also includes lawns, garden beds, older steps, and informal edges that are less predictable, and snow, ice, leaves, roots, or irrigation can affect footing. A section of Loop Drive near the gardens is now pedestrian-only, while its parking lots remain accessible from 21st Avenue.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
City of Spokane Parks and Recreation ↗
Which Spokane Transit bus goes to Manito Park? +

Spokane Transit Route 4 Monroe-Regal serves the Manito Park area, and weekday South Express Route 144 stops near 14th and Bernard for the park's west side. Choose the stop closest to Duncan Garden, the conservatory, duck pond, playground, or picnic area and check snow detours during winter weather.

Sourced answer Researched Jul 31, 2026
Spokane Transit Authority ↗
How accessible is Manito Park? +

Major gardens, conservatory approaches, parking areas, and many park paths have paved or firm routes, but grades, gates, seasonal parking closures, and older garden layouts vary by destination. Use the City's park map and exact garden pin, and contact Parks before a visit that depends on a particular step-free route or accommodation.

Sourced answer Researched Aug 8, 2026
City of Spokane Parks and Recreation ↗
Is Manito Park free? +

General park entry, the major public gardens, playgrounds, paths, lawns, Mirror Pond viewing, and Gaiser Conservatory entry are free. Food, reservations, weddings, commercial photography, programs, and special events can charge separately.
