What people say

Duncan Garden is Manito Park's three-acre formal European Renaissance-style garden, with geometric annual beds, manicured turf, paths, and Davenport Fountain. The display changes with the planting season. Stay out of flower beds, do not pick blooms, and remember that weddings and wedding photography require reservations even when ordinary visitor photography does not.

Are dogs allowed at Duncan Garden? +

Dogs may use ordinary outdoor park areas only under applicable leash and cleanup rules, but pets are prohibited inside Gaiser Conservatory and the Japanese Garden's event rules also prohibit pets. Service animals are allowed where required. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.
