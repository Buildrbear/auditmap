Manito Park

Spokane

Use the central Loop Drive area for Gaiser Conservatory and nearby formal gardens. The south playground and splash pad have a separate seasonal parking lot. Use the city walking-tour map to locate the Japanese, Duncan, Rose Hill, Lilac, and Ferris gardens.

Riverfront Park

Spokane

Use 507 North Howard Street for the central park and Rotary Fountain, or 832 North Howard Street for the Ice Age Floods Playground. Navigate directly to the Pavilion, carousel, SkyRide, Skate Ribbon, Providence Playscape, or event entrance when that is your destination.
