Are there restrooms near Red Wagon? +

Playing on and viewing the Red Wagon are free; nearby attractions, parking, food, rentals, and events can cost separately.

How accessible is Red Wagon? +

Providence Playscape has wheelchair-friendly resilient surfacing, adaptive play elements, family restrooms with adult changing tables, seating with chair-charging receptacles, and nearby accessible parking. The SkyRide is also wheelchair accessible through its main entrance.

Are dogs allowed at Red Wagon? +

Yes, on a leash and with waste removed. Riverfront Park is not a general off-leash area, and pets can be prohibited during large events such as the Fourth of July under Spokane city rules. Keep dogs away from playground equipment, splash pads, rides, indoor attractions, and any temporarily restricted event zone; check the event calendar before a pet-specific visit. Separately operated buildings, beaches, gardens, and attractions can set stricter rules.

The Childhood Express Red Wagon is a 12-foot-high interactive sculpture whose handle doubles as a slide near Riverfront Park's south side.
