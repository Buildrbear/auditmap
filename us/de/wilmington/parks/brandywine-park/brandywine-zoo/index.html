Hours ⌄

What people say

Brandywine Zoo is a compact, separately ticketed zoo of less than five acres within Brandywine Park, featuring common and endangered animals and daily educational opportunities. Route to 1001 North Park Drive; free general parking is across from the zoo along the Brandywine River, with accessible spaces nearer the entrance. A typical visit takes 45 to 60 minutes. Admission changes by season and special event, with last entry before closing; check the calendar for weather, maintenance, or early closures. All exhibits are wheelchair and stroller accessible, but the sloped landscape can make some routes harder. Strollers are not rented. Pets are prohibited, while qualifying service dogs must follow Zoo leash rules.
